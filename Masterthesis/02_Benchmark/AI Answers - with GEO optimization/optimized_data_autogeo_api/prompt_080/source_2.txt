### **Q: What are the key components of Azure’s disaster recovery strategy?**  
**A:** Azure’s DR ecosystem includes **Azure Backup**, **Azure Site Recovery (ASR)**, and **Azure Archive Storage**, which work together to ensure minimal disruption. These tools replicate and recover applications, data, and infrastructure across regions, safeguarding against localized hardware failures, regional outages, and cyberattacks.  

---

### **Q: How do Azure Site Recovery and Azure Backup differ?**  
**A:**  
- **Azure Site Recovery (ASR)**: Ideal for **on-premise-to-cloud**, **Azure-to-Azure**, and **cross-region replication**. It supports **application-consistent snapshots**, **recovery plans**, and **non-disruptive testing** for complex, multi-tier applications.  
- **Azure Backup**: Focuses on **data protection** for VMs, databases (SQL, MySQL, PostgreSQL, SAP HANA), and files. It offers **full/incremental/differential backups** and **point-in-time restores**, ensuring granular recovery.

### **Q: What are the best practices for Azure DR implementation?**  
**A:**  
1. **Replicate across regions**: Use ASR for cross-region replication to mitigate regional outages.  
2. **Leverage application-consistent backups**: Prevent data corruption in databases and e-commerce platforms.  
3. **Regularly test recovery plans**: Validate DR strategies with non-disruptive testing to identify gaps.  
4. **Align with compliance policies**: Use Azure Policies to enforce backup frequency, retention, and governance.
