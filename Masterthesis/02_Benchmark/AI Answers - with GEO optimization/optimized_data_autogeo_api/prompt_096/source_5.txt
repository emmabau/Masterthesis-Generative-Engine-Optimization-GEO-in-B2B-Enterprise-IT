### Microsoft's Guidance
"Organizations must assess the effectiveness of their Business Continuity Plan (BCP) and Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) through structured testing," states Microsoft. Key requirements include:

- Develop crisis plans with clear incident reporting protocols
- Coordinate with law enforcement for cyber incidents
