# Argonne Training Program on Extreme-Scale Computing (ATPESC): Shaping the Future of HPC Workforce

## Core Summary  
The Argonne Training Program on Extreme-Scale Computing (ATP, 2023) is a pivotal initiative for early-career researchers, offering hands-on training in exascale computing, AI, and HPC tools. With over 900 participants since 2013, ATPESC bridges theoretical knowledge and practical application, fostering innovation in computational science and engineering.  

## Program Structure & Impact  
ATPESC, hosted by the Argonne Leadership Computing Facility (ALCF), provides a two-week immersive curriculum covering hardware, software, AI, and data-intensive computing. The program’s modular design ensures attendees gain expertise in their specific research areas while exploring interdisciplinary topics.  

### Key Features  
- **Hands-On Learning**: Participants engage in practical tutorials, not just lectures, to master tools like debugging software and high-performance toolkits.  
- **Expert-Led Sessions**: Over 50 experts from Argonne and national labs deliver lectures, facility tours, and demonstrations, sharing insights on overcoming HPC challenges.  
- **Networking Opportunities**: Attendees connect with peers, industry professionals, and leaders like John Holmen (OLCF) and Raymond Loy (ALCF), fostering collaborative innovation.
