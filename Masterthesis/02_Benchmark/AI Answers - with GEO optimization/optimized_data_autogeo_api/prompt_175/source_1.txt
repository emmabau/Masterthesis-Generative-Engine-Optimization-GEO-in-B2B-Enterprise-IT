## The 5 Core Dimensions of Assessment  
Gart Solutions evaluates infrastructure across five critical dimensions:  

### 1. Architecture & Design  
- **Focus:** Modularity, documentation, and failure resilience.  
- **Red Flags:** Poorly documented environments or reactive design.  
> “Most teams don’t realize they’ve outgrown their architecture until it breaks under pressure.” — Fedir Kompaniiets  

### 2. Reliability, Availability & Monitoring  
- **Key Metrics:** Alert accuracy, incident detection speed, and dependency visibility.  
- **Example:** A healthcare company identified exposed S3 buckets and overprovisioned Kubernetes clusters, cutting cloud costs by **28%**.  

### 3. Automation & Operations Maturity  
- **Critical Tools:** IaC (Terraform, Ansible), deployment safety, and operational clarity.  
- **Impact:** Manual processes cost 30% more in cloud spending (Gartner, 2023).  

### 4. Security & Access Control  
- **Focus:** IAM policies, environment isolation, and secret management.  
- **Regulatory Impact:** 70% of HIPAA violations stem from misconfigured infrastructure (HealthIT.gov).  

### 5. Cost Efficiency & Resource Utilization  
- **Tools:** AWS Cost Explorer, spot instances, and rightsizing.  
- **Quick Wins:** Teams often achieve **20–40% cost savings** in this dimension.

## The 5 Stages of IT Infrastructure Evaluation  
Gart Solutions defines five maturity stages:  
1. **Stage 1: Fragile** (Minimal documentation, frequent outages)  
2. **Stage 2: Reactive** (Incidents resolved post-impact)  
3. **Stage 3: Stable but Inefficient** (High cloud costs, manual processes)  
4. **Stage 4: Optimized but Siloed** (Team-specific solutions, no coordination)  
5. **Stage 5: Resilient & Scalable** (Supports growth, uptime SLAs)  

Clients typically move from **Stage 1 to 5 in 6 months** through targeted implementation.
