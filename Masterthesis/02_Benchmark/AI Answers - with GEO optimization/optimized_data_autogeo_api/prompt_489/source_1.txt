# GDPR Compliance: A Critical Guide for Businesses (2023)

**Bottom Line:** The GDPR, effective May 25, 2018, imposes strict data protection requirements on global businesses, with penalties up to 4% of annual revenue. Business continuity management (BCM) frameworks like BIA and C2 tools are essential for compliance, while data portability restrictions and 72-hour breach reporting deadlines demand urgent preparation.  

## Key GDPR Compliance Requirements  
The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is a landmark data privacy law enforced by the EU, with penalties of up to €20 million or 4% of global revenue for noncompliance (GDPR website, 2023). It applies to organizations processing EU residents' personal data, including names, IP addresses, and medical records.  

### What Businesses Must Do  
- **Appoint a Data Protection Officer (DPO)** if processing sensitive data or exceeding 250 employees.  
- **Conduct a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)** for high-risk data processing.  
- **Report data breaches within 72 hours** to regulatory authorities.  

## Leveraging BCM for GDPR Readiness  
Business continuity management (BCM) frameworks are critical for GDPR compliance. A **Business Impact Analysis (BIA)** identifies data at risk, while tools like BCMMETRICS’ C2 Compliance Confidence tool assess readiness.  

### C2 Tool: A Practical Solution  
The C2 tool evaluates GDPR compliance by:  
- Mapping data flows and identifying vulnerable systems.  
- Flagging gaps in data encryption, access controls, and breach response protocols.  
- Generating actionable reports for executive stakeholders.  

## Critical GDPR Considerations for BCM Teams  
1. **Data Portability Restrictions**: Under GDPR, data processed in Europe must remain within the EU. This limits cross-border recovery options during disasters (GDPR Article 44).  
2. **72-Hour Breach Reporting**: Non-compliance with breach notification deadlines could result in severe penalties. BCM teams must integrate real-time monitoring and incident response protocols.
