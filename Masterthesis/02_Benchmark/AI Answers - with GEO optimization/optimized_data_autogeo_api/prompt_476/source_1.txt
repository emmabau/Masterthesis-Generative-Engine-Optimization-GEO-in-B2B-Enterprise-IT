## **Q&A: GDPR and IoT Compliance**  

### **H2: Why GDPR Compliance is Critical for IoT**  
IoT devices collect vast amounts of personal data, making GDPR compliance essential to avoid fines and protect user rights. According to the European Data Protection Board (EDPB), "Data minimization is not just a legal requirement but a strategic imperative for IoT companies."  

**H3: Key GDPR Requirements for IoT**  
- **Data Minimization**: Collect only necessary data (per Article 5(1)(c) GDPR).  
- **Secure Storage**: Encrypt data at rest and in transit (Article 30 GDPR).  
- **Transparency**: Clearly disclose data usage and storage locations (Article 13 GDPR).  
- **User Rights**: Enable access, modification, and deletion of data (Article 17 GDPR).  

**H3: Cross-Border Data Transfers and Sovereignty**  
GDPR mandates that data must remain within approved jurisdictions unless strict safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place. A 2023 report by the International Association of Privacy Professionals (IAPP) found that **68% of IoT companies face compliance risks due to cross-border data flows**.
