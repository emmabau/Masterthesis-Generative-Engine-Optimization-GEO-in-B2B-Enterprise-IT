**1. Regulatory Enforcement Is Here**  
The NIS2 Directive has transitioned from planning to active enforcement, with regulators now prioritizing operational oversight over paperwork. Fines are just one facet; operational downtime, reputational damage, and strategic exposure pose greater risks.  

**2. Cybersecurity Is Now a Board-Level Mandate**  
Under NIS2, executives face personal liability for non-compliance. Cybersecurity is no longer a technical function but a strategic priority, requiring leadership to embed risk management into decision-making.  

**3. Compliance Is Measured by Capability, Not Documentation**  
Regulators demand real-time visibility into critical assets, active vulnerability management, and incident response readiness. Static policies are insufficient—organizations must demonstrate operational resilience.  

**4. NIS2 Applies to 18 Sectors and Beyond**  
The directive covers healthcare, energy, transport, finance, and digital sectors, with thresholds at 50+ employees or €10M+ revenue. Supply chains and third-party vendors are also subject to scrutiny.
