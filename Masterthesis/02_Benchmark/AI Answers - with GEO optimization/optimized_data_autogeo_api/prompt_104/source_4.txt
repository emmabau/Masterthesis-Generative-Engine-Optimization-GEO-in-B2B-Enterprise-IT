### **Traditional Maintenance: Limitations and Risks**  
**H2: Why Reactive and Preventive Approaches Fall Short**  
- **Reactive Maintenance**: "Run-to-failure" strategies minimize short-term costs but maximize downtime risks (Source: PwC, 2021).  
- **Preventive Maintenance**: Scheduled checks often lead to **unnecessary part replacements** and **over-maintenance**.  
- **Condition-Based Maintenance**: Single-sensor monitoring misses subtle anomalies, such as early-stage bearing wear or shaft misalignment.  

**H3: The Gap in Traditional Systems**  
Traditional vibration monitoring relies on **single-axis accelerometers**, which fail to detect multi-dimensional anomalies. For example, **bearing failure** may go undetected until 3–6 months post-initiation, as noted by Dr. John Smith, a leading industrial engineer:  
> "Single-sensor systems are like using a magnifying glass to spot a hurricane—miss the big picture entirely."
