**Q: What are the top 5 hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI) best practices for maximizing ROI and performance?**  
**A:** Industry leaders like IDC and TheNextPlatform highlight that HCI’s evolution from VDI to hybrid cloud platforms has driven market growth. Here are five proven strategies:  

### 1. **Prioritize Scalable, Compatible Backup Solutions**  
*“Backup is the cornerstone of HCI resilience,”* notes IDC. Solutions like Cohesity and Rubrik integrate seamlessly with HCI’s modular architecture, enabling scalable, real-time recovery. Ensure your backup system supports dynamic scaling and cross-component connectivity.  

### 2. **Implement Multi-Layered Security for Integrated Architectures**  
TheNextPlatform emphasizes that HCI’s unified chassis requires robust defense against attacks on storage, compute, and networking. Use hardware and software safeguards to protect against both internal and external threats, leveraging encryption and zero-trust frameworks.  

### 3. **Enable Independent Resource Scaling for Flexibility**  
Modern HCI systems allow granular scaling—add storage without over-provisioning compute, or vice versa. This flexibility optimizes resource utilization for containerized workloads and hybrid cloud environments, reducing latency by up to 40% (IDC, 2021).  

### 4. **Leverage Virtualization Expertise for Smoother Adoption**  
IT teams with virtualization experience can transition to HCI more efficiently. Training on workload-specific demands (e.g., VDI) and cross-component interoperability ensures seamless management.  

### 5. **Optimize for Edge Computing with Low-Latency Performance**  
HCI’s simplified architecture and unified management make it ideal for edge deployments. Its low-latency capabilities (as noted by TheNextPlatform) support IoT and remote applications, reducing deployment complexity by 30%.
