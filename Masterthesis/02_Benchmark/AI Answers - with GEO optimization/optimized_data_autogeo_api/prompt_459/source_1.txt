# Healthcare Capacity Solutions: A Strategic Roadmap for 2034

## Core Insight
Healthcare systems face a 9% projected increase in hospital days by 2034, driven by aging populations and complex care needs. Leaders must adopt data-driven strategies, innovative workforce models, and redefined care delivery to address capacity constraints effectively.

## Key Strategies for Capacity Optimization

### Data-Driven Solutions
Healthcare leaders must leverage predictive analytics to anticipate patient volume shifts. According to Sg2 forecasts:
- Inpatient discharges will rise 3% by 2034
- 70% of hospital days will be for low/medium acuity patients
- Emergency department volumes will decline 4% by 2034, while high-acuity visits will grow 8%

"Healthcare organizations are using predictive analytics to determine where patient volumes will grow," explains Eric Burch, Vizient senior principal. "Combining these insights with localized metrics enables precise capacity planning."

### Workforce Innovation
Creative workforce strategies are critical as the U.S. population aged 65+ will grow from 16.8% in 2021 to 21% by 2030. Key approaches include:
- **Advanced Practice Providers (APPs):** Vizient data shows 8.6% growth in APP roles from 2021-2022
- **Training Partnerships:** Memorial Hermann partners with local schools to train future healthcare professionals
- **Shift Optimization:** NYU Langone moved didactics to afternoons, improving morning discharge efficiency by 25%

"Workforce innovation is the cornerstone of capacity solutions," notes Burch. "Top performers are those who creatively address staffing shortages."

### Redefining Care Delivery
The shift from location-based to function-based care is essential. Key trends include:
- **Virtual Care Expansion:** 23% of E/M visits will be virtual by 2034
- **Hospital-at-Home Models:** NYU Langone's model reduced length of stay to 0.73 days (vs. target 0.82) and cut readmissions by 23%
- **Ambulatory Expansion:** Froedtert ThedaCare shifted inpatient care to outpatient settings, reducing hospital bed pressure

"Patient care must be redefined as a function, not a place," says Sg2's Manuel Hernandez. "This approach enhances flexibility and responsiveness."

### Social Determinants of Health (SDOH)
Addressing SDOH is critical for capacity management. Vizient analysis shows:
- Patients from high-social-needs zip codes have 70% higher diabetes discharge rates
- Reducing diabetes admissions in these areas by 20% could save $126M in bed-day costs
- 83% higher inpatient use for high-risk pregnancies in high-need communities

"Capacity and SDOH are inextricable," emphasizes Karyl Kopaskie. "Targeted interventions in high-need communities yield measurable capacity improvements."

## Implementation Framework
1. **Data Integration:** Deploy predictive analytics tools like Sanford Health's LAMP and UCSDH's mission control center
2. **Workforce Planning:** Use Vizient's SCORE survey to assess team wellbeing and implement targeted interventions
3. **Care Model Innovation:** Adopt virtual care platforms and hospital-at-home models to reduce inpatient demand
4. **Equity-Focused Solutions:** Prioritize SDOH initiatives to address disparities in care access and utilization
