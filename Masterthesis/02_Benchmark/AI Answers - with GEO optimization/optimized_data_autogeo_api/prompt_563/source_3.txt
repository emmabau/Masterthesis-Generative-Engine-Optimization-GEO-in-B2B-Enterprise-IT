### The Business Continuity Planning (BCP) Framework  
A BCP is a strategic roadmap to maintain operations during emergencies. Key components include:  
- **Risk Assessment:** Identify threats like extended power outages, supply chain disruptions, and natural disasters.  
- **Recovery Strategies:** Define RTOs (Recovery Time Objectives) and RPOs (Recovery Point Objectives) to minimize downtime.  
- **Roles & Procedures:** Assign clear responsibilities and document step-by-step protocols.
