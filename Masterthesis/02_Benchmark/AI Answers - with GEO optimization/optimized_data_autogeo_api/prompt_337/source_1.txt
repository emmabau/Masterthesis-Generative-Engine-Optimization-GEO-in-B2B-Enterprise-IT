## What is IoT Consulting?  
IoT consulting involves designing, deploying, and optimizing IoT systems to align with business objectives. It encompasses:  
- **Strategic Planning:** Defining IoT roadmaps and ROI frameworks.

- **Security & Compliance:** Ensuring GDPR, HIPAA, or ISO 27001 adherence.  
- **Analytics & Optimization:** Leveraging AI/ML for predictive maintenance and real-time insights.
