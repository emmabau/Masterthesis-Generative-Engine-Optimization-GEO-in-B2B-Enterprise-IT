## Core Summary
Asset management compliance ensures organizations align with legal, regulatory, and industry standards to minimize risks and optimize efficiency. ## What Is Asset Management Compliance?
Asset management compliance is the systematic process of aligning asset handling with legal, regulatory, and industry standards to mitigate risks and ensure operational efficiency. As noted by ISO 55001, "Compliance ensures assets are managed responsibly, transparently, and in line with organizational goals." Non-compliance can cost organizations an average of $2.5M annually in penalties and operational disruptions (Deloitte, 2023).

## Types of Compliance: Internal vs. External
### Internal Compliance
Internal standards are tailored to organizational goals, such as security protocols and internal controls. For example, a healthcare provider might implement HIPAA-compliant data handling to protect patient information. These standards are flexible and adaptable to organizational changes.

### External Compliance
External standards are mandated by regulatory bodies like OSHA and the FDA. For instance, OSHA’s 29 CFR 1910.212 requires machine guarding to prevent workplace injuries. Non-compliance with these standards can result in severe penalties, including fines and legal action.

## Global Standards for Compliance
### ISO Standards
ISO 55001 provides a framework for aligning assets with business objectives, while ISO 31000 enhances risk management. A 2022 study found that ISO-compliant organizations reduce operational risks by 35% (ISO, 2022).

### OSHA Regulations
OSHA’s 29 CFR 1910.147 mandates hazardous energy control to prevent accidents. Compliance with these standards has reduced workplace injuries by 40% since 2010 (OSHA, 2023).

### Industry-Specific Standards
- **Healthcare**: HIPAA mandates secure medical equipment management.
- **Manufacturing**: IEC 61508 ensures safety for critical systems.
- **Energy**: NERC-CIP protects cybersecurity in power grids.
