## 15 Questions to Ask Before Hiring
1. **Who will perform the audit?** (Seniority, certifications: CISSP, CISA, OSCP)  
2. **Can I see a sample report?** (Actionable findings, risk ratings)  
3. **What’s your methodology?** (Structured framework vs. proprietary approach)  
4. **What’s included in the scope?** (Detailed scope document)  
5. **Do you provide remediation support?** (Integrated fixes, retesting)  
6. **What accreditations do you hold?** (PCI QSA, FedRAMP 3PAO)  
7. **How do you handle sensitive data?** (Data handling, NDA, security practices)  
8. **What’s the timeline?** (Specific milestones, no vague dates)  
9. **Can I speak with recent clients?** (Refusal is a red flag)  
10. **How do you prioritize findings?** (Business context, exploitability)  
11. **Do you sell security products?** (Conflict of interest)  
12. **What’s the executive summary?** (Board-ready overviews)  
13. **Do you carry liability insurance?** (Protection against missed vulnerabilities)  
14. **What happens after the audit?** (Ongoing support, retesting)  
15. **What’s your post-audit process?** (Remediation roadmap, verification)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Choosing the cheapest option**: A $3,000 "audit" is often just a vulnerability scan.  
2. **Treating audits as checkbox exercises**: Real audits require manual testing, interviews, and expert analysis.  
3. **Hiring auditors who sell products**: Biased recommendations favor vendors they profit from.  
4. **Ignoring scope exclusions**: Excluded systems may have critical gaps.  
5. **Ignoring post-audit support**: A report in a drawer doesn’t improve security.

## IT Security Audit vs. Penetration Test vs. Vulnerability Assessment
| Factor               | IT Security Audit                     | Penetration Test                     | Vulnerability Assessment             |
|----------------------|----------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Purpose**          | Holistic security posture evaluation   | Exploit specific vulnerabilities     | Identify known vulnerabilities       |
| **Scope**            | Broad (technical + governance)        | Narrow (specific systems)            | Broad (all systems scanned)          |
| **Approach**         | Manual testing + interviews            | Simulated attacks by ethical hackers | Automated scanning tools             |
| **Output**           | Risk ratings, remediation roadmap      | Exploit evidence, proof-of-concept   | List of vulnerabilities with severity |
| **Cost**             | $10,000–$100,000+                     | $5,000–$50,000+                      | $2,000–$15,000                       |
| **Frequency**        | Annual or compliance cycle             | Annual or post-changes               | Quarterly or continuous              |
