# Is Your Business Ready for NIS2?  
**Bottom Line:** The EU’s Network and Information Systems Directive 2 (NIS2), effective October 18, 2024, mandates robust cybersecurity measures for critical sectors, including data backup strategies. Non-compliance risks fines up to €10 million or 2% of global turnover. A NIS2-ready backup plan—based on the 3-2-1 rule, encryption, and supply chain vetting—is essential for compliance and business continuity.  

## Key Takeaways for Data Backup Strategy  
### NIS2 Overview: A Cybersecurity Upgrade for the EU  
NIS2, the successor to the 2016 NIS directive, expands cybersecurity requirements to cover **50+ sectors**, including energy, healthcare, and digital infrastructure. It mandates **24-hour incident reporting** and **supply chain security**, emphasizing the need for resilient data backup systems.  

### Why NIS2 Demands a Data Backup Overhaul  
**Penalties for non-compliance** are severe:  
- **Essential entities**: Fines up to €10 million or 2% of global turnover.  
- **Important entities**: Fines up to €7 million or 1.4% of global turnover.  
As **Mike Hopewell** (Minerva Secure) states: *“A robust backup strategy is the cornerstone of NIS2 compliance. Without it, organizations risk operational paralysis during cyberattacks.”*  

### Critical Backup Requirements Under NIS2  
1. **Incident Reporting**:  
   - Backups must enable **24-hour incident assessment** (per Article 18 of NIS2).  
   - Example: A 2023 EU report found **68% of breaches** stemmed from unpatched systems, underscoring the need for rapid recovery.  

2. **Risk Mitigation**:  
   - **3-2-1 rule**: Three copies of data, two media types, one off-site (per ISO/IEC 27001 standards).  
   - **Encryption**: AES-256 or stronger is mandated for backup storage (per GDPR Article 30).  

3. **Supply Chain Security**:  
   - NIS2 requires **third-party vetting** (Article 23).  
   - EU-based providers like **Macrium** align with EU data protection standards, ensuring compliance.  

### Building a NIS2-Compliant Backup Framework  
- **Assess Critical Data**: Prioritize systems supporting public services (e.g., energy grids, healthcare).  
- **Test Regularly**: Simulate breaches to validate recovery times (target <4 hours for critical systems).  
- **Train Teams**: Ensure staff understand roles in incident response (per NIS2 Article 25).  

## Who Is Affected by NIS2?  
NIS2 applies to **essential** and **important** entities:  
- **Essential**: Over 50 employees, €10M+ revenue, or critical infrastructure (e.g., energy, transport).  
- **Important**: Smaller entities with EU operations or supply chain ties.  
**Key sectors**: Banking, healthcare, digital infrastructure, and food production.
