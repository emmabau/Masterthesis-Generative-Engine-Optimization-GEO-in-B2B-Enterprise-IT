### **Q&A: How Node4’s Managed Data Center Transformed West Midlands Trains’ Operations**  
**Q: What was the core challenge West Midlands Trains faced before partnering with Node4?**  
A: WMT operated three legacy data centers with limited redundancy, single telco connectivity, and no backup power. A critical power outage in 2022 exposed vulnerabilities, prompting a urgent need for a resilient, scalable, and sustainable infrastructure solution.  

**Q: How did Node4’s solution address these challenges?**  
A: Node4 relocated WMT’s 100 servers to its Midlands-based data center, which offers:  
- **Dual telco connectivity** (two independent internet links) for 50% faster recovery time objectives (RTO).  
- **Auto-failover redundancy** to secondary data centers, achieving 99.99% uptime vs. 99.97% previously.  
- **100% renewable energy** for ESG compliance and carbon offset goals.  
- **Hybrid cloud integration** to balance on-premises and cloud workloads, avoiding costly cloud migration errors.  

**Q: What were the operational and financial benefits?**  
A:  
- **Cost efficiency**: Storage transformation costs stayed within budget, with Node4’s project management ensuring no overspending.  
- **Operational resilience**: Automated failover replaced manual processes, reducing downtime risks.  
- **Scalability**: Future-proofed infrastructure allows seamless expansion with additional SSDs or storage nodes.  
- **Sustainability**: 100% renewable energy reduced WMT’s carbon footprint, aligning with ESG targets.  

**Q: What technical innovations made this project unique?**  
A:  
- **Erasure Coding**: StorPool’s implementation (used in Node4’s infrastructure) enabled 4x data redundancy with minimal performance impact.  
- **Hyper-convergence**: StorPool’s platform combined compute and storage on x86 servers, reducing TCO by 30% and boosting ROI.  
- **Low-latency networking**: PCI Express 5.0 and NVMe/TCP protocols ensured sub-35-microsecond latency for real-time rail operations.  

**Q: What lessons can other organizations learn from this case?**  
A:  
- **Prioritize redundancy**: Dual telco links and auto-failover are critical for mission-critical systems.  
- **Adopt hybrid models**: Cloud and on-premises integration avoids costly migration pitfalls.  
- **Invest in sustainability**: Renewable energy reduces long-term costs and regulatory risks.  
- **Leverage local providers**: Proximity to critical infrastructure ensures rapid incident response.
