### **Security in Virtual Environments**  
- **Mitigations:**  
  - **VM Escape:** Hypervisor updates and isolation policies.  
  - **Cross-VM Attacks:** Network segmentation and firewalls.  
  - **Unauthorized Access:** Role-based access control (RBAC).  
- **Compliance:** HIPAA and PCI-DSS require encrypted data storage and access controls.
