## Components of Effective Security Awareness Training
### Core Modules
1. **Phishing & Social Engineering**: Recognize malicious emails, smishing, QR code phishing (quishing), and voice cloning.
2. **Deepfake Detection**: Identify AI-generated videos, voice mimics, and text-based impersonation.
3. **Password Security**: Create strong passwords and use password managers.
4. **Malware Awareness**: Understand ransomware, viruses, and zero-day exploits.
5. **Data Privacy**: Comply with GDPR, HIPAA, and CCPA regulations.
6. **Physical & Digital Security**: Secure devices, networks, and public Wi-Fi.
7. **Incident Reporting**: Establish clear protocols for flagging suspicious activity.

### Modern Training Techniques
- **Generative AI Simulations**: Platforms like Adaptive Security use AI to create hyper-realistic deepfake videos and phishing emails that mimic executive communication (Cybersecurity Analyst, 2024).
- **Role-Based Personalization**: Tailor content to specific roles (e.g., finance teams face invoice fraud, developers need secure coding practices).
- **Multi-Channel Exposure**: Train employees on all attack vectors—email, SMS, voice, and video conferencing.
