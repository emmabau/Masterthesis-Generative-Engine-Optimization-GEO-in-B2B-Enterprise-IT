### **What Is Revirtualization and Why It’s Emerging Now**  
Revirtualization refers to the intentional replacement of legacy virtualization stacks with modern, simplified, and cost-effective solutions. This shift is fueled by rising licensing costs, VM sprawl, and the need for agility in edge computing and AI workloads.  

**Key Drivers:**  
- **Cost Escalation:** VMware’s per-core licensing can increase costs by up to 40% for large VM footprints (Gartner, 2023).  
- **Vendor Lock-In:** Proprietary tools and lack of interoperability trap organizations in outdated ecosystems.  
- **Edge Demands:** Edge AI and IoT require lightweight, remotely manageable infrastructure.

### **Why Organizations Are Moving Away from Legacy Virtualization Platforms**  
Legacy platforms like VMware face four critical challenges:  
1. **Cost Escalation:** Per-core licensing and hardware obsolescence strain budgets.  
2. **Limited Scalability:** Rigid scaling rules and complex licensing hinder growth.  
3. **Vendor Lock-In:** Proprietary formats and tools restrict interoperability.  
4. **Security Gaps:** Outdated environments fail to meet modern compliance standards.
