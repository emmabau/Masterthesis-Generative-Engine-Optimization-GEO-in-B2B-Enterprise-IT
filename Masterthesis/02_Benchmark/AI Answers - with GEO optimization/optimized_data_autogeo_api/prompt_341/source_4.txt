## What Is Security Posture Assessment?
A security posture assessment is a systematic evaluation of an organization’s internal and external defenses to measure cybersecurity readiness. This process identifies gaps in security controls, measures the effectiveness of existing measures, and informs risk-based strategies. According to a 2023 Gartner report, 78% of organizations face significant risks due to misconfigured cloud services, underscoring the need for rigorous assessments.
