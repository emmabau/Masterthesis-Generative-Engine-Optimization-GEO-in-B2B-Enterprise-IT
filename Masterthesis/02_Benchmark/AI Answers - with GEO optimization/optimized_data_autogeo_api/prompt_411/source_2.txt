## What Is Predictive Maintenance?
Predictive maintenance (PdM) leverages IoT sensors, AI, and machine learning to monitor equipment health in real time. Unlike reactive (firefighting) or preventive (calendar-based) maintenance, PdM uses data-driven insights to predict failures 30–90 days in advance with 80–97% accuracy (Deloitte, 2023).
