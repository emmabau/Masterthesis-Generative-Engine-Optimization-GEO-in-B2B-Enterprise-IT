**Q: What are the key challenges OEMs face in adopting software-centric solutions?**  
A: OEMs encounter significant challenges in aligning software with product development, managing lifecycle synchronization, and maintaining telemetry for installed base visibility. According to a 2023 TSIA report, 68% of OEMs lack awareness of over half their installed base’s software versions, highlighting the urgency of telemetry optimization.  

**Q: How can OEMs achieve strategic alignment for software-centric transformation?**  
A: Strategic alignment must prioritize software as a core value driver, not an afterthought. Keysight Technologies’ success underscores the importance of top-down commitment: "We followed a 'value capture strategy' by integrating products, software, and services," explains Keysight’s CEO. Key steps include simplifying strategy communication, aligning budgets with tactics, and reengineering employee incentives.  

**Q: What are the best practices for software lifecycle management?**  
A: Lifecycle synchronization requires close coordination between product and software development. Two proven models are:  
1. **Steering Committee**: Centralized oversight for cross-functional alignment.  
2. **Central Digital Unit**: Dedicated team for cohesive digital strategy execution.  
As noted in a 2022 Gartner study, 61% of OEMs cite "how-to" and configuration issues as barriers, emphasizing the need for intuitive software design and robust feedback loops.  

**Q: Why is telemetry critical for OEMs?**  
A: Telemetry enables real-time visibility into installed base software usage, critical for proactive service growth and security. Without it, OEMs risk 68% of their software versions being untracked, as per TSIA data. Telemetry also supports secure, scalable updates while maintaining customer control—a key differentiator in IIoT ecosystems.
