# Composable Infrastructure & Agentic AI: The Future of Enterprise Architecture

## What Is Composable Infrastructure?
Composable infrastructure represents a paradigm shift from monolithic systems to modular, API-driven components that AI agents can dynamically assemble. Traditional infrastructure relies on rigid, reactive provisioning, while composable systems enable real-time reconfiguration based on business needs.

**Key Insight**: "The future of IT operations is autonomous, policy-driven and hybrid by design," says IBM, which is investing heavily in agentic AI orchestration (IBM, 2025).
