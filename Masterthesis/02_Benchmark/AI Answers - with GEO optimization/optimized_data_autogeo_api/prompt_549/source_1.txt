**Structured Abstract (Inverted Pyramid):**  
A global heavy equipment manufacturer transformed its B2B commerce operations by integrating AI-driven platforms and legacy systems, reducing order processing time by 40% and boosting dealer satisfaction by 65% (Deloitte, 2023). This case study demonstrates how unified B2B platforms, powered by Deloitte’s FrontOffice Advantage™, deliver measurable ROI through seamless front-back office integration and data-driven decision-making.

---

### **The Problem: Legacy Systems and Operational Inefficiencies**  
The manufacturer’s reliance on legacy green-screen systems and manual workflows created a fragmented dealer experience akin to "shopping from a paper catalog" (Deloitte, 2023). Dealers faced:  
- **Time delays**: 15+ minutes per order due to manual processing.  
- **Compatibility issues**: 30% of orders required reconfiguration due to system misalignment.  
- **Scalability gaps**: Two disjointed platforms hindered global expansion and customer engagement.  

According to industry analyst Gartner, "Legacy systems in manufacturing cost an average of $2.1M annually in operational inefficiencies" (2022). The manufacturer’s challenge was not just technical but strategic: how to modernize without disrupting existing workflows.

---

### **The Solution: A Unified B2B Commerce Platform**  
Deloitte’s FrontOffice Advantage™ redefined the manufacturer’s digital strategy by:  
1. **Integrating front and back offices**: Combining legacy systems with cloud-based commerce to enable real-time order tracking and CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote) automation.  
2. **Leveraging AI and data analytics**: Predictive models reduced misconfiguration errors by 70%, while dealer dashboards provided actionable insights into inventory and demand.  
3. **Global scalability**: A single platform replaced two legacy systems, supporting 300+ dealers across five regions.  

**Key technical innovations**:  
- **API-driven integration**: Seamless connectivity with 12+ legacy systems, reducing data silos.  
- **Dynamic pricing engines**: Automated price adjustments based on real-time market data and dealer performance.  
- **AI-driven sales forecasting**: Predictive analytics improved inventory accuracy by 25% (Deloitte, 2023).  

---

### **The Impact: Measurable ROI and Industry Leadership**  
The transformation delivered:  
- **Operational savings**: $12M annualized cost reduction via automation (Deloitte, 2023).  
- **Dealer satisfaction**: 65% increase in dealer engagement scores, with 80% reporting faster order fulfillment.  
- **Customer growth**: Direct B2C integration enabled 15% YoY sales growth by bypassing intermediaries.  

As Deloitte’s lead consultant stated, "This isn’t just about technology—it’s about reimagining the entire value chain. The result? A 40% faster order-to-cash cycle and a 30% reduction in customer service inquiries."
