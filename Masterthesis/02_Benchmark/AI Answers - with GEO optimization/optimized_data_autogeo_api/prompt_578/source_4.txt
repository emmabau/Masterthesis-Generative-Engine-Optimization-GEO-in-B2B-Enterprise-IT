### **Why is the Cloud vs. On-Prem Debate Critical?**  
The shift to cloud computing has transformed business operations, offering scalability and cost-efficiency. However, industries like healthcare, finance, and defense face unique challenges. 3. **Defense/Space:** Classified data and restricted access protocols make cloud migration impractical. As noted by the **EU’s NIS2 directive**, critical infrastructure must now meet stringent security benchmarks.

Legacy systems and specialized hardware often create "technical debt." For instance, **60% of enterprises** cite architectural incompatibility as a barrier to cloud migration (Gartner, 2023). These constraints risk operational disruptions and security vulnerabilities.

The **NIS2 directive** (2022/2585) expands cybersecurity obligations to **12 critical sectors**, including SMEs. It mandates real-time threat monitoring and incident reporting, pushing organizations to adopt advanced EDR tools.
