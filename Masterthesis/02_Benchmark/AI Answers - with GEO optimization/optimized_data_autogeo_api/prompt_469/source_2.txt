Despite declining opportunities, worker preferences remain strong. FlexJobs reports that 65% of professionals prefer full-time remote work, while 32% opt for hybrid arrangements. "Remote work is no longer a perk—it's a necessity for many," states FlexJobs CEO, underscoring the enduring demand.

The Future Workforce Pulse Report projects that 22% of the U.S. workforce (32.6 million workers) will remain remote by 2025. This growth is attributed to advancements in collaboration tools and digital infrastructure, which are expected to expand remote and hybrid opportunities.
