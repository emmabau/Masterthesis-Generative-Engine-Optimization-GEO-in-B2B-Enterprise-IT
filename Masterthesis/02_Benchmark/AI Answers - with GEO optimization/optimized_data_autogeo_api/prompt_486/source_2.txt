### **H2: The Critical Role of Data in Customer Experience**

### **H2: Key Challenges in Data Management**  
1. **Fragmented Data Silos**  
   Disconnected systems create redundant data, increasing operational costs and reducing efficiency. As David Jones, Vice President at Nux, explains, *"Legacy systems trap data in silos, making it impossible to audit or secure effectively."*  

2. **Security and Compliance Risks**  
   With **65% of breaches originating from unsecured data** (IBM, 2023), outdated data maintenance practices expose organizations to regulatory penalties and reputational damage.  

3. **Obsolete Data Costs**  
   Maintaining redundant data consumes **15–20% of IT budgets** (Forrester, 2023), yet provides no business value.

### **H3: Strategies for Modern Data Solutions**  
**1. Extract Value from Unstructured Data**  
Leverage AI and Cloud platforms to automate data extraction, classification, and tagging. For example, Nuxeo’s Content Services enable real-time analysis of customer interactions, reducing manual effort by **40–60%** (Nuxeo Case Study, 2023).  

**2. Secure and Audit Your Data Ecosystem**  
Implement blockchain-based audit trails and AI-driven anomaly detection to ensure compliance. As Wren notes, *"Security isn’t a checkbox—it’s a continuous process powered by intelligent systems."*  

**3. Eliminate Obsolete Data**  
Use machine learning to identify and purge redundant data, cutting maintenance costs by **30–50%** while improving system performance.
