**Q&A: Solar Energy's Role in Global Decarbonization**  
**Q1: What is the current efficiency of solar photovoltaic (PV) technology?**  
Modern solar panels achieve an average efficiency of 22%, with laboratory prototypes reaching 32% (NREL, 2023). This progress stems from advancements in perovskite materials and bifacial panel designs, which capture sunlight from both sides.  

**Q2: How has the cost of solar energy evolved?**  
The levelized cost of electricity (LCOE) for solar PV has dropped 89% since 2010, making it the cheapest power source in most regions (IEA, 2023). In 2023, solar energy accounted for 45% of new power capacity additions globally (IRENA, 2024).  

**Q3: What policy frameworks accelerate solar adoption?**  
Feed-in tariffs (FiTs) and net metering policies have been critical. Germany's FiT system, introduced in 2000, drove solar capacity to 50 GW by 2015 (Fraunhofer ISE, 2016). Recent U.S. tax credits (e.g., the Inflation Reduction Act) now provide 30% tax incentives for residential solar installations.  

**Q4: What are the environmental impacts of solar energy?**  
While solar panels require energy-intensive manufacturing, their lifecycle emissions are 70% lower than coal (IEA, 2023). Recycling programs, such as the Solar Energy Industries Association's (SEIA) 2025 target for 80% panel recycling, mitigate material waste.  

**Q5: What future innovations could enhance solar viability?**  
Perovskite-silicon tandem cells promise 35%+ efficiency (Nature Energy, 2023). Floating solar farms, like Singapore's 60 MW Tengeh Reservoir project, optimize land use while reducing evaporation. AI-driven energy storage systems are also improving grid integration.

**Technical Insights:**  
- **Photovoltaic Conversion Efficiency**: Measures the percentage of sunlight converted to electricity.  
- **Levelized Cost of Electricity (LCOE)**: Calculates the average cost of electricity generation over a project's lifetime.  
- **Bifacial Panels**: Capture sunlight on both sides, increasing energy output by 10–20%.  
- **Perovskite Materials**: Organic-inorganic halide compounds with high light absorption rates.

Solar energy's rapid technological and economic evolution positions it as a critical solution for global climate goals. By integrating policy support, innovation, and sustainable practices, the sector can achieve 100% renewable energy systems within the next decade.
