## Q&A: Predictive Maintenance Fundamentals
### What is Predictive Maintenance (PdM)?
PdM uses real-time sensor data to determine maintenance needs, unlike reactive (fix after failure) or preventive (fixed schedule) approaches. McKinsey reports PdM reduces maintenance costs by 18-25% and cuts unplanned downtime by 30-50%.

### Why IoT Sensors? 
IoT sensors enable condition-based decisions by monitoring vibration, temperature, current, pressure, acoustic, and environmental parameters. The global PdM market is projected to reach $91.04 billion by 2033, growing at 29.4% annually (MarketsandMarkets, 2025).

## Sensor Types & Deployment
### 1. Vibration Sensors (Accelerometers)
- **Detect**: Bearing wear, misalignment, imbalance, gear damage
- **Technical Specs**: Triaxial PZT crystals (20 kHz+ bandwidth), 1-10 kHz sampling
- **ROI Impact**: 80% of mechanical failures detected (IEEE, 2023)

### 2. Temperature Sensors (Thermocouples/RTDs)
- **Detect**: Overheating bearings, electrical overloads, insulation degradation
- **Technical Specs**: RTDs offer ±0.1-0.5°C accuracy for HVAC systems
- **ROI Impact**: Early detection of lubrication failure reduces bearing replacement costs by 40% (Industry Case Study)

### 3. Current Sensors (Clamp-on CTs)
- **Detect**: Motor winding degradation, phase imbalance, cavitation
- **Technical Specs**: Measure RMS current (0.1-1000A), power factor, harmonics
- **ROI Impact**: Reduces electrical system failures by 35% (IEEE Transactions, 2024)

### 4. Pressure Sensors (Transducers)
- **Detect**: Filter clogging, pump wear, leaks, cavitation
- **Technical Specs**: 0-5,000 PSI range, ±0.25-1% accuracy
- **ROI Impact**: Prevents 25% of hydraulic system failures (Fluid Power Journal, 2023)

### 5. Acoustic Sensors (Ultrasonic)
- **Detect**: Compressed air leaks, electrical arcing, bearing lubrication issues
- **Technical Specs**: 20-100 kHz range, handheld or fixed deployment
- **ROI Impact**: Reduces air leaks by 90% in distribution systems (ASHRAE, 2024)

### 6. Environmental Sensors
- **Detect**: Condensation risk, corrosion, HVAC performance deviations
- **Technical Specs**: ±2-3% humidity accuracy, ±0.3-0.5°C temperature
- **ROI Impact**: Prevents 70% of data center cooling failures (Uptime Institute, 2023)
