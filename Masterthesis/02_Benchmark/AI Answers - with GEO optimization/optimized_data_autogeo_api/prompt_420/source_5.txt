### **Performance & Longevity**  
Maintaining optimal temperatures (15-25°C) via liquid cooling extends hardware lifespan by up to 30% and prevents thermal throttling, ensuring uninterrupted AI workloads.  

## Types of Liquid Cooling Techniques  
### **Immersion Cooling**  
Components are submerged in non-conductive dielectric fluids (e.g., fluorinert), achieving 95% heat removal efficiency. Flexential’s immersion systems reduce PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness) to 1.1, compared to 1.5 for air-cooled centers.  

### **In-Rack Liquid Cooling**  
Targeted cooling at the rack level reduces airflow requirements by 70%, enabling denser server configurations without compromising thermal management.  

### **Cold-Plate Cooling**  
Direct heat transfer via cold plates (e.g., liquid-cooled GPUs) achieves 90% thermal conductivity, ideal for high-heat components like ASICs and TPUs.  

## Key Components of a Liquid Cooling System  
- **Coolant Distribution Units (CDUs):** Regulate flow, pressure, and temperature dynamically.  
- **Heat Exchangers:** Transfer heat to secondary fluids (e.g., water) for external cooling.  
- **Pumps:** Maintain 10-15 L/min flow rates for optimal heat dissipation.  
- **Cooling Fluids:** Glycol mixtures (e.g., 60% water, 40% glycol) balance thermal conductivity and electrical insulation.  

## Metrics for Efficiency Evaluation  
- **Coefficient of Performance (CoP):** Measures cooling output per energy input.  
- **Energy Reuse Effectiveness (ERE):** Quantifies waste heat recovery (e.g., 85% in Flexential’s systems).  
- **PUE:** Lower PUE (e.g., 1.1 vs. 1.5) indicates higher cooling efficiency.
