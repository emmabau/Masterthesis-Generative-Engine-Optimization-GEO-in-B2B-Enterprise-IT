## What is Composable Infrastructure?  
Composable infrastructure redefines data center operations by abstracting physical resources into software-defined pools. As HPE explains: *“In composable infrastructure, compute, storage, and networking resources are abstracted from their physical locations and can be managed by software through a web-based interface.”* This paradigm shift enables enterprises to treat infrastructure like cloud services, achieving unprecedented agility.  

## Why Composable Infrastructure Matters  
### **1. Accelerating Business Innovation**  
Composable systems allow enterprises to scale individual resources (compute, storage, networking) independently, reducing deployment time by up to 30% (Gartner, 2023). This flexibility ensures organizations can rapidly adapt to market demands without hardware reconfiguration.  

### **2. Cost Optimization**  
By eliminating the need for physical hardware reconfiguration, composable infrastructure cuts operational costs by 40% (IDC, 2022). For example, HPE Synergy’s rack-scale multifabric connectivity reduces switch dependency, lowering CapEx and OpEx.  

### **3. Hybrid Cloud Readiness**  
Composable infrastructure serves as the foundation for private and hybrid cloud environments. HPE Synergy’s ability to compose physical/virtual resources into any workload configuration aligns with 85% of enterprises’ hybrid cloud strategies (Forrester, 2023).
