### **Key Components of a Business Continuity Plan**  
**Q: What are the core elements of a BCP?**  
A: A robust BCP includes:  
1. **Risk Assessment**: Identify threats (e.g., cyberattacks, supply chain disruptions) and analyze vulnerabilities.  
2. **Business Impact Analysis (BIA)**: Prioritize critical functions (e.g., customer service, IT systems) and determine recovery time objectives (RTOs).  
3. **Recovery Strategies**: Develop alternatives like remote work setups, backup infrastructure, and secure data replication.  
4. **Communication Protocols**: Define roles, emergency contacts, and escalation procedures.  
5. **Testing & Drills**: Conduct tabletop exercises and full-scale simulations to validate the plan.  

**Q: How does disaster recovery integrate with BCP?**  
A: Disaster Recovery Plans (DRPs) focus on restoring IT systems, while BCPs address broader operational continuity. Together, they form **BCDR (Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery)**, ensuring seamless recovery. For example, Corodata’s DRP templates help businesses recover data within 24 hours, minimizing downtime.
