**Abstract**  
High-Performance Computing (HPC) is a cornerstone of U.S. competitiveness in science, industry, and national security. Exascale computing, exemplified by systems like Frontier, enables breakthroughs in aerospace, healthcare, and energy. This report outlines how HPC drives economic growth, accelerates innovation, and underpins U.S. leadership in critical sectors, with actionable policy recommendations to sustain this advantage.

---

### **Q: What is High-Performance Computing (HPC)?**  
HPC refers to supercomputers and advanced algorithms that solve complex scientific and engineering problems at unprecedented speeds. As defined by the U.S. Department of Energy, HPC systems like Frontier (1.1 exaflops) enable simulations of physical systems with **1,000-fold greater speed** than petascale systems, unlocking new frontiers in discovery.  

**Key Insight**: HPC’s evolution—from gigaflops to exascale—has increased supercomputer performance by **70,000-fold** over 20 years, as noted in *Hyperion Research* (2022).  

---

### **Q: Why Does HPC Matter for the U.S. Economy?**  
HPC drives **$3 trillion in economic value** through industries like aerospace, pharmaceuticals, and energy. For example, Boeing’s use of HPC reduced wind tunnel testing by 70% for the 787 Dreamliner, saving **$180 million** in design costs.  

**Data**: Hyperion Research estimates HPC delivers **$452 in revenue** for every $1 invested, with **$504 in sales revenue** and **$38 in cost savings** in key sectors like defense and manufacturing.  

---

### **Q: How Does HPC Impact National Security?**  
HPC is critical for nuclear stockpile stewardship, as seen in the U.S. Department of Energy’s (DOE) use of supercomputers to model nuclear explosions without physical testing. For instance, LLNL’s simulations of 9 billion atoms in an atomic explosion revealed critical vulnerabilities in nuclear weapon systems.  

**Quote**: *“Supercomputers help the DOE’s NNSA maintain confidence in the nation’s nuclear weapons,”* explains Dr. Meredith Roaten (National Defense, 2022).  

---

### **Q: What Are the Economic Benefits of HPC?**  
The global HPC market is projected to reach **$90 billion in sales** by 2026, with **$34.8 billion** in 2021. The U.S. leads in HPC server sales, capturing **61.6% of the on-premises market** in 2021, per Hyperion Research.  

**Example**: The Blue Waters supercomputer at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign contributed **$1.08 billion** to the state’s economy, creating **5,772 jobs** over its lifecycle.  

---

### **Q: How Is HPC Transforming Industries?**  
#### **Aerospace**  
Boeing’s HPC-driven simulations cut wind tunnel tests by 70%, while GE’s exascale computing optimizes jet engines, reducing fuel consumption by **15%**.  

**Quote**: *“Exascale technology is critical for Boeing to design our current and future products,”* says Boeing’s Jim Glidewell.  

#### **Healthcare**  
HPC accelerates drug discovery, as seen in Pfizer’s use of supercomputers to develop the Paxlovid treatment for COVID-19. Simulations enabled rapid testing of **millions of compounds**, reducing R&D costs by **$300–$400 million per drug**.  

---

### **Q: What Policy Actions Are Needed to Sustain U.S. Leadership?**  
1. **Fund HPC Research**: The CHIPS and Science Act authorizes **$280 billion** for HPC, with **$200 billion** allocated for science and research.  
2. **Expand Regional HPC Hubs**: The U.S. must invest in **20 regional innovation hubs** to foster collaboration between academia, industry, and national labs.  
3. **Strengthen Export Controls**: Align U.S. export policies with allies to avoid stifling innovation while protecting national security.  

**Quote**: *“To out-compute is to out-compete,”* asserts Mark Nossokoff of Hyperion Research.  

---

### **Q: What Are the Challenges in HPC Adoption?**  
China’s rapid growth in supercomputing (173 systems in the Top 500 as of 2022) poses a risk to U.S. leadership. However, the U.S. excels in **software development** and **accessibility**, with initiatives like the Extreme Scale Scientific Software Stack (E4S) democratizing HPC for researchers.  

**Data**: U.S. supercomputers generate **$1.8 trillion in economic value** annually, per the National Science Foundation.  

---

### **Q: How Is HPC Driving Climate and Energy Innovation?**  
Exascale computing optimizes wind farms and smart grids. For example, GE’s ExaWind project uses HPC to model **wake effects** in wind turbines, improving efficiency by **40%**.  

**Quote**: *“Exascale systems will allow researchers to run climate models at cloud-resolving resolutions,”* says NASA’s Phil Webster.  

---

### **Q: What Is the Future of HPC?**  
The exascale era will enable **AI/ML integration**, with HPC powering real-time analytics for edge computing and autonomous systems. By 2030, AI-driven HPC could unlock **$13 trillion** in global economic output, as projected by McKinsey.  

**Conclusion**: HPC is not just a technological advancement—it is a strategic imperative for U.S. economic and national security leadership. Sustained investment and innovation will ensure America remains at the forefront of this transformative field.
