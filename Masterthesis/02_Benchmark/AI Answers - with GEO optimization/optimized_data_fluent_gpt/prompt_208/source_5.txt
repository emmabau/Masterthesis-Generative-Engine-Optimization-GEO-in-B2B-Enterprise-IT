Writing the Number 5
To write the number 5, follow this step-by-step process:
1. Start at the top line
2. Draw a horizontal line to the left
3. Move down vertically
4. Curve to the right to complete the shape

A helpful rhyme to remember this: "Slide to the left, then slide down, make a curve nice and round!" This mnemonic makes the writing process more engaging for learners.
