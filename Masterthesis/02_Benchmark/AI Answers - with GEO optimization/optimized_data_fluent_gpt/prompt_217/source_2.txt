Presented by Doug Kothe, Director of the Exascale Computing Project at Oak Ridge National Laboratory, and moderated by John West, Director of Strategic Initiatives at Texas Advanced Computing Center, this webinar highlights the US Department of Energy's Exascale Computing Project (ECP). Launched in 2016, the ECP is building the software infrastructure for next-generation supercomputers capable of over 10¹⁸ operations per second. The project unites the Office of Science Advanced Scientific Computing Research Program and the National Nuclear Security Administration Advanced Simulation and Computing Program, with over 100 teams developing applications, software, and hardware technologies for exascale systems.
