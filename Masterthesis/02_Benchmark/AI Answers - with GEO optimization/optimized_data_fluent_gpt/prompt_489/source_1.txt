**What is GDPR?**  
The GDPR is a strict data privacy standard enforced by the European Union, applying to companies with a presence in the EU or those processing EU residents' personal data. Penalties for noncompliance can be severe: up to **4% of annual global revenue** (or €20 million).  

**What Does GDPR Protect?**  
It safeguards personally identifiable information (PII) of EU residents, including:  
- Names, photos, email addresses  
- Bank details, medical records, political beliefs  
- Sexual orientation, IP addresses  

**Key GDPR Penalties:**  
- Fines up to 4% of global revenue or €20 million  
- Mandatory data breach reporting within **72 hours**  
- Restrictions on data portability (data stays in Europe post-disaster)
