**Key Criteria for Choosing the Right Technology Platform**  
Legacy systems, while familiar, come with significant limitations that hinder growth and competitiveness. Modern solutions offer scalable, flexible alternatives that address these pain points. To justify the investment in modern technology, decision-makers must conduct a thorough business value assessment, using TCO and ROI calculations to demonstrate cost-effectiveness and long-term value.

**Understanding TCO and ROI**  
TCO encompasses all expenses associated with acquiring, operating, and maintaining a system over its lifecycle. This includes initial setup, maintenance, operational costs, and hidden costs like technical debt. ROI measures the financial return from modern solutions, calculated as:  
**ROI = (Net Profit / Cost of Investment) × 100**  
By analyzing TCO and ROI, brands can quantify how modern commerce solutions reduce costs, drive revenue growth, and provide a competitive edge.

2. **Revenue Growth**  
   - Enhanced customer engagement through personalized, omnichannel experiences increases conversion rates.  
   - Improved inventory management reduces stockouts and ensures product availability.  
   - Integrated options like **Buy Online, Pick Up In-Store (BOPIS)** and **Buy Online, Return In-Store (BORIS)** create new revenue streams.  

3. **Competitive Advantage**  
   - Modern technology enables rapid feature rollouts, outpacing competitors reliant on outdated systems.  
   - Seamless integration with other platforms enhances efficiency and functionality.  
   - Proactive compliance minimizes risks and ensures regulatory readiness.  

**Selecting the Right Platform**  
To gain stakeholder buy-in, prioritize platforms that:  
- Align with your organization’s budget and long-term goals.  
- Offer seamless integration with existing systems, automating key processes (e.g., inventory updates, customer data management).  
- Provide ongoing support and collaborative vendor relationships for smooth implementation.  
- Scale with your business, handling increased transaction volumes and evolving needs.  
- Feature intuitive interfaces that minimize training time and enhance user experience.  
- Include robust security measures, continuous monitoring, and compliance certifications to protect customer data.
