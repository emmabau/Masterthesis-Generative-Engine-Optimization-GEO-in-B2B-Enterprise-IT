What is High-Performance Computing?  
High-performance computing (HPC) leverages powerful CPU or GPU-based systems to tackle complex mathematical problems. It enables industries to solve computationally intensive tasks efficiently, driving innovation across multiple sectors.

Key Industries Benefiting from HPC  
HPC is revolutionizing these critical fields:  
- **Genomics**: Accelerating DNA sequencing and analysis  
- **Oil & Gas**: Enhancing reservoir simulation and exploration  
- **Finance**: Optimizing risk modeling and algorithmic trading  
- **Semiconductors**: Streamlining chip design and simulation  
- **Engineering**: Improving structural analysis and CAD modeling  
- **Weather Forecasting**: Enhancing predictive accuracy and climate modeling  

Why Cloud-Based HPC Matters  
Cloud HPC solutions offer unparalleled flexibility. By dynamically scaling compute resources, organizations eliminate infrastructure bottlenecks and optimize costs. This scalability ensures that businesses can match computational power to real-time demands, making HPC accessible for projects of all scales.
