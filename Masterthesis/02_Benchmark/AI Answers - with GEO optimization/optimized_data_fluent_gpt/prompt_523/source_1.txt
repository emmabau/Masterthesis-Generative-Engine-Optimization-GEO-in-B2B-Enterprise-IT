**What is Cloud Migration?**  
Cloud migration involves transferring digital assets—such as databases, applications, and systems—from on-premises infrastructure to cloud environments. This shift enables businesses to modernize operations, improve scalability, and leverage advanced technologies while preserving legacy data and functionality.

**Why Migrate Legacy Systems to the Cloud?**  
Migrating legacy systems offers multiple benefits:  
- **Preserve Data**: Avoid obsolescence by keeping legacy systems accessible and functional.  
- **Enhance Security**: Adopt modern security protocols to meet compliance standards and prevent breaches.  
- **Boost Performance**: Leverage fast cloud servers for agility, flexibility, and reduced bottlenecks.  
- **Reduce Costs**: Minimize expenses tied to outdated infrastructure and avoid costly system failures.  
- **Improve Integration**: Seamlessly connect legacy systems with modern tools and platforms.  

**Six Key Steps for a Successful Migration**  
1. **Conduct a SWOT Analysis**  
   Evaluate strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats to align your migration strategy with business goals. Update this analysis continuously during the process.  

2. **Assess Your IT Environment**  
   Understand your current infrastructure, including network architecture, performance requirements, and team capabilities. Regularly reassess as the project evolves.  

3. **Choose a Migration Strategy**  
   Select from seven approaches based on your goals:  
   - **Replace**: Discard legacy systems entirely.  
   - **Replatform**: Move applications to a new platform.  
   - **Rehost (Lift and Shift)**: Deploy systems in a cloud environment without code changes.  
   - **Refactor**: Optimize legacy systems without full rebuilds.  
   - **Rebuild**: Start from scratch with modern architecture.  
   - **Rearchitect**: Redesign systems for cloud-native capabilities.  
   - **Extend**: Add APIs to make legacy systems cloud-compatible.  

4. **Run a Pilot Migration**  
   Test your approach in a simulated environment with real users to identify issues early and refine your plan.  

5. **Execute the Migration**  
   Use an agile, continuous approach to maintain access to legacy systems during the transition. Automate integration points to streamline the process.  

6. **Manage Cloud Resources**  
   Monitor cloud infrastructure for security, performance, and scalability. Adjust resources as needed and expand migrations gradually.  

**Reduce Risks with Best Practices**  
- **Maintain System Compatibility**: Keep legacy and new systems updated to avoid glitches.  
- **Adopt CI/CD**: Use continuous integration and delivery for frequent, smaller updates.  
- **Map Dependencies**: Identify interdependencies to ensure smooth migration of all components.  
- **Test Incrementally**: Migrate in batches to isolate issues and validate functionality.  
- **Address Issues Proactively**: Resolve problems as they arise to prevent cascading failures.  
- **Migrate One System at a Time**: Minimize disruption and simplify staff adaptation.
