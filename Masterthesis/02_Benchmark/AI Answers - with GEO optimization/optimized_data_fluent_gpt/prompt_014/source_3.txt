Government agencies are increasingly leveraging high-performance computing (HPC) to manage vast data volumes and address mission-critical challenges. From accelerating COVID-19 treatments to conducting military simulations and advancing scientific research, HPC has become a cornerstone of federal operations. As complexities grow, so does the demand for HPC, making it imperative for agencies to adopt robust strategies to manage these systems effectively.

**1. Prioritize Observability in Hybrid HPC Environments**  
Modern HPC workloads, such as genomic sequencing, require distributed computing resources to process data efficiently. Agencies often adopt hybrid HPC models, combining on-premise data centers with scalable public cloud resources. However, traditional monitoring tools fall short in hybrid environments spanning networks, regions, and software stacks. To ensure comprehensive visibility, agencies must implement observability solutions that provide real-time insights across multi-vendor infrastructure. This includes cross-domain analytics and actionable intelligence to automate remediation, optimize performance, and maintain reliability during peak loads.

**2. Strengthen Cybersecurity for HPC Systems**  
With HPC systems handling sensitive data, cybersecurity is critical. The Biden Administration’s Executive Order on Cybersecurity mandates encryption for data in transit and at rest, a non-negotiable measure to protect against breaches. Agencies must also adopt robust backup strategies, encrypting backup files and separating critical data before and after cyberattacks. Balancing workloads and staggering backups ensures operational continuity without compromising security.

**3. Ensure Consistent Workloads Across Hybrid Environments**  
Consistency is key to maintaining reliability in HPC operations. Whether on-premise or in the cloud, agencies must ensure simulations and computations align with established workflows. This flexibility allows seamless data migration during service outages, enabling efficient use of big data at scale while minimizing costs. Prioritizing interoperability ensures that software, data, and computations can transition smoothly between environments without disruption.

**Conclusion**  
As HPC becomes central to federal operations, agencies must balance complexity reduction with robust security and performance. By focusing on observability, cybersecurity, and workload consistency, they can harness HPC to address today’s challenges and prepare for future demands.
