**The Science of IMU Sensor Fusion for Predictive Maintenance**  
An Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) combines 3-axis accelerometers, gyroscopes, and magnetometers to measure motion and orientation in three dimensions. While individual sensors provide valuable data, sensor fusion algorithms unlock their full potential by analyzing multi-dimensional data.  

**The Fusion Advantage**  
Traditional vibration monitoring relies on single-axis accelerometers, which can miss subtle anomalies. IMU sensor fusion offers a comprehensive view by simultaneously tracking:  
- Vibration patterns across all axes  
- Rotational characteristics indicating misalignment or coupling issues  
- Magnetic field variations revealing electrical problems or material stress  
- Environmental factors like temperature  

221e’s MPE™ (Motion Processing Engine) processes this data using proprietary algorithms to eliminate drift and ensure accuracy in static and dynamic conditions.  

**Advanced Pattern Recognition Through AI**

This enables the system to distinguish between normal operational variations and genuine anomalies, identifying developing issues early.
