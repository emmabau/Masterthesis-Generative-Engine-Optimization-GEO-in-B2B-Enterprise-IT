Mark Troester at Progress explains how composable infrastructure can benefit application deployment and management  

Composable infrastructure is revolutionizing how enterprises approach application deployment and management by breaking down traditional silos. This architecture enables organizations to harness disparate or integrated infrastructure assets into a unified, flexible framework—whether physical, virtual, or hybrid. A recent Gartner report highlights its high benefit rating, predicting significant revenue growth or cost savings for enterprises of all sizes.  

**What is Composable Architecture?**  
Composable infrastructure relies on an orchestration layer that abstracts physical infrastructure, allowing it to be configured for applications. This architecture combines three core components:  
1. **Fluid pools of infrastructure resources** (computing, storage, and fabric)  
2. **Intelligent software** (template-driven and programmable)  
3. **A single, unified API**  

These layers work together to enable dynamic resource allocation, self-managing hardware, and seamless integration with developer tools.  

**Key Benefits for Enterprises**  
- **Resource Flexibility**: Compute, storage, and fabric resources can be aggregated, disaggregated, and composed based on application needs.  
- **On-Demand Provisioning**: Infrastructure adapts instantly to workload demands, accelerating application delivery and enabling agile operations.  
- **Cross-Platform Compatibility**: Runs seamlessly on virtual machines, containers, and cloud-native applications, supporting hybrid and multi-cloud environments.  
- **Intelligent Automation**: Automatically detects and resolves configuration issues, reducing manual intervention and downtime.  
- **Scalability**: Resources can be scaled dynamically to match organizational growth, making it ideal for big data, cloud services, and traditional workloads like analytics and supply chain management.  

**Five Essentials for Business Leaders**  
1. **Fully Composable Architecture**: Requires all three core layers (fluid pools, intelligent software, and a unified API) for true flexibility and agility.  
2. **On-Demand Provisioning**: Enables instant resource allocation via a single API, streamlining operations and reducing time-to-market.  
3. **Cross-Platform Agility**: Supports virtualization, hybrid cloud, and cloud-native applications, eliminating the need for new hardware investments.  
4. **Self-Optimizing Intelligence**: Automatically discovers and resolves resource issues, ensuring continuous application availability.  
5. **Scalable Infrastructure**: Adapts to organizational needs, supporting both traditional and modern workloads with minimal overhead.  

**Creating a Composable Infrastructure**  
- **Step 1**: Identify all infrastructure elements (servers, storage, networks) and align them with business goals.  
- **Step 2**: Secure executive buy-in by demonstrating cost savings and operational efficiency.  
- **Step 3**: Pilot the solution to test performance, gather feedback, and refine implementation.  

By adopting composable infrastructure, organizations achieve faster service delivery, continuous delivery pipelines, and greater agility in managing both legacy and modern applications. This approach transforms infrastructure into a dynamic, intelligent asset that drives innovation and operational excellence.
