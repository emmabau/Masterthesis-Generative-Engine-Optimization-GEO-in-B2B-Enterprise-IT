IT Recovery and Disaster Recovery Planning  
A robust IT disaster recovery plan (IT DRP) is critical for businesses to ensure continuity during disruptions. This plan must align with the business continuity plan, prioritizing recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs) identified during the business impact analysis. Key IT recovery priorities include:  
- **Hardware and Software Restoration**: Identify critical systems (servers, networks, applications) and ensure replacement hardware/software is available. Prioritize restoration to match business function recovery timelines.  
- **System Component Resilience**: Anticipate losses in critical components such as:  
  - Computer room infrastructure (climate control, backup power)  
  - Connectivity (fiber, wireless, etc.)  
  - Data and application recovery strategies  
- **Testing and Documentation**: Regularly test the DRP as part of the business continuity plan to ensure effectiveness.  

Data Backup and Recovery Strategies  
Data loss from hardware failure, human error, or cyberattacks can cripple operations. A comprehensive data backup plan should:  
1. **Identify Critical Data**: Include network servers, desktops, laptops, and hard copy records (digitized via scanning).  
2. **Implement Backup Procedures**: Schedule frequent backups (daily/real-time) and validate data integrity. Align backup frequency with RPOs and RTOs.  
3. **Secure Storage**: Use standardized hardware for replication and ensure software licenses are available for reinstallation.  

Resources for IT Disaster Recovery  
Leverage authoritative guidelines to strengthen your plan:  
- **NIST Special Publications**:  
  - *Contingency Planning Guide for Federal Information Systems* (SP 800-34 Rev. 1)  
  - *Guide to Test, Training, and Exercise Programs for IT Plans* (SP 800-84)  
  - *Building an IT Security Awareness Program* (SP 800-50)  
- **Computer Security Resource Center** (NIST) for foundational security frameworks.  

By integrating these strategies and resources, businesses can mitigate risks and ensure rapid recovery from IT disruptions.
