**What Is Hyper-Converged Infrastructure?**  
HCI integrates compute, storage, and networking into a single system managed via software. This consolidation reduces hardware complexity and footprint, enabling IT teams to deploy and scale resources more efficiently. Key components include:  
- Virtualized compute resources (servers)  
- Software-defined storage  
- Network virtualization  
- Centralized management tools  

The goal is to replace traditional three-tier architectures with a compact, scalable solution that supports cloud-native workloads, virtual desktops, and data-intensive applications.  

**Key Factors Influencing HCI Pricing**  
HCI costs depend on multiple variables beyond hardware. These include:  
- **Hardware specifications**: CPU cores, memory size, storage type (SSD vs. HDD), and network interfaces  
- **Software licensing**: Per-node, per-CPU, or per-virtual machine models  
- **Support and maintenance**: Annual fees for updates, patches, and technical assistance  
- **Scalability options**: Costs for adding nodes or expanding storage capacity  
- **Included features**: Backup, disaster recovery, security, and analytics tools (bundled or add-ons)

**Comparing Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)**  
Upfront pricing is only part of the equation. TCO includes:  
- **Support and maintenance**: ~15–25% of initial costs annually  
- **Scaling costs**: Vary by vendor (e.g., Nutanix’s higher upfront cost may offset lower management overhead)  
- **Operational savings**: Simplified management reduces IT labor expenses  
- **Energy and space**: Compact systems lower data center costs
