**Core Principles of Composable Infrastructure**  
Composable infrastructure redefines how enterprises leverage physical resources by abstracting compute, storage, and networking capabilities into software-managed pools. As HPE explains, "In composable infrastructure, compute, storage, and networking resources are abstracted from their physical locations and can be managed by software through a web-based interface." This model mirrors cloud service flexibility, making data center resources as accessible as cloud-based solutions. It serves as the foundation for private and hybrid cloud architectures, enabling dynamic resource allocation.

**Key Advantages of Composable Infrastructure**  
1. **Enhanced Flexibility**: Composable systems allow independent scaling of resources, enabling rapid deployment of workloads without infrastructure constraints.  
2. **Agility**: IT teams can configure hardware swiftly, aligning with business goals and accelerating innovation adoption.  
3. **Cost Efficiency**: By optimizing resource utilization and reducing hardware complexity, composable infrastructure lowers operational expenses.  
4. **Scalability**: Modular design supports seamless expansion to meet growing demands, ensuring long-term adaptability.
