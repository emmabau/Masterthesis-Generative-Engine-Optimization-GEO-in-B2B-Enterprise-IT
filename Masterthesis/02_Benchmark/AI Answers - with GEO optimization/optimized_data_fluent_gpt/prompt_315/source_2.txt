When combined with a hybrid cloud strategy—where workloads exist on-premise, in the cloud, and at the edge—it has revolutionized how enterprises manage IT resources. This approach enables faster innovation and adaptability in increasingly complex IT ecosystems.

Enterprises now face challenges like rising costs and the struggle to balance flexibility with operational stability. In this context, open-source solutions are emerging as a strategic choice from both commercial and procurement perspectives.
