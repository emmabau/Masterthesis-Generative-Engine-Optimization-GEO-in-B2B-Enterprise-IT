Compliance teams are facing unprecedented challenges as regulatory changes occur at an accelerating pace and massive volume, leaving many feeling overwhelmed by the demands of a high-stakes, fast-moving environment. The strain is compounded by an overabundance of projects, limited resources, and seemingly intractable issues—problems that have only intensified with the advent of Covid-19, which has disrupted traditional workflows and exposed gaps in data management across fragmented systems.  

This guide examines the critical challenges in compliance, risk, and regulatory adaptation shaping the financial services sector, including complexities in regulatory reporting, best execution, and market abuse surveillance. It then proposes a transformative approach to data governance and compliance, emphasizing the need to address root causes rather than surface-level symptoms. By leveraging digital transformation—particularly its focus on data quality—financial institutions can not only improve compliance effectiveness but also unlock new business value through smarter, more agile strategies.  

The conclusion is clear: current approaches to managing regulatory change are insufficient. A paradigm shift toward digital innovation is essential to navigate these challenges successfully and position compliance as a strategic enabler rather than a reactive burden.
