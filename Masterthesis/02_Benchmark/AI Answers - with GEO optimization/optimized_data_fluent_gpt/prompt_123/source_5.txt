**What is Hybrid Collaboration?**  
It’s teamwork across locations—some in-office, others remote. The focus is on seamless collaboration, not physical proximity. For example, a team might brainstorm in a conference room while a remote developer joins via video call, with a marketing specialist contributing to a shared document. Technology bridges this gap, ensuring distance doesn’t hinder productivity.
