Effective asset management involves tracking performance, ensuring maintenance, and maintaining compliance throughout an asset's lifecycle. This process is vital for businesses reliant on physical or intangible assets, such as machinery, equipment, and intellectual property. ERP systems integrate these elements into a seamless workflow, enabling organizations to achieve cost savings, better resource utilization, and operational efficiency.

### Criteria for Selecting the Best ERP for Asset Management

7. **Security and Compliance**: Verify adherence to industry regulations and robust data protection measures.
