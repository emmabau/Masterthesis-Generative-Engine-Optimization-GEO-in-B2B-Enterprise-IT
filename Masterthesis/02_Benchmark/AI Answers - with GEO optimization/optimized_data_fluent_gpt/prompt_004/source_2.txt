The industrial products and construction market is rapidly evolving, and a global heavy equipment manufacturer sought to maintain a competitive edge. As part of a comprehensive back-office transformation, the company partnered with Deloitte to build a modern commerce and order management system. It also implemented smart factory processes and leveraged emerging technologies to streamline plant maintenance and asset management globally.  

However, unlike many competitors, the manufacturer had not yet modernized its front-end B2B commerce processes. Its legacy green-screen systems and manual workflows, while stable, were painfully slow for dealers. The ordering experience resembled browsing a paper catalog, creating inefficiencies that added up: time spent navigating PDF product catalogs, deciding between two outdated ordering platforms, and resolving misconfigured orders through manual coordination.  

The adage "time is money" proved critical. Dealers could quantify the value of every minute saved, while the manufacturer faced measurable impacts on margins and cost-to-serve. Building on the success of its back-office transformation, the company turned its focus to enhancing the front-end customer experience.  

**Digitizing the Dealer Experience to Reduce Clicks and Enhance Engagement**  
Deloitte’s team, deeply familiar with the manufacturer’s operations and dealer pain points, designed a CX-led solution. Using its B2B commerce expertise and the IndustryAdvantage™ approach, Deloitte created a proof-of-concept (POC) for a unified B2B platform. The platform offered intuitive onboarding, streamlined configuration, pricing, and quoting (CPQ), and enabled dealers to place orders via a cloud-based application. Features included real-time product comparisons, delivery tracking, and visibility into supply chain inventory across production centers, warehouses, and dealer locations.  

Integration with the back office reduced compatibility issues and accelerated order resolution. Dealers embraced the POC, and with executive support, Deloitte scaled the solution globally. By combining the strengths of two existing platforms, the team developed a single, modern B2B commerce solution that addressed scalability and global deployment needs.  

This integration exemplifies Deloitte’s FrontOffice Advantage™, which aligns front-, middle-, and back-office operations through AI, data enablement, and platform integration. The unified system unlocked advanced analytics for dealers, enabling data-driven decisions and direct customer connections.  

**Enhancing the Dealer Experience Can Enhance the Customer Experience**  
Today, the B2B platform serves as the manufacturer’s primary digital channel, deployed across five regions and 10 geographies. It provides a unified, brand-aligned experience for over 300 global dealers, delivering measurable benefits for dealers, customers, and the manufacturer.  

Deloitte continues to expand the platform’s capabilities, including its OEM business and CPQ solutions. The goal is to leverage AI for predictive sales insights and fully integrated systems to eliminate misconfiguration errors. With a modernized platform in place, the manufacturer is poised to adapt to industry evolution and drive sustainable growth.
