## 🔍 What Is a Security Audit Firm?
An IT security audit company systematically evaluates your organization’s systems, infrastructure, policies, and controls to identify vulnerabilities, assess risk, and verify compliance with security standards.  

**Key Deliverables:**  
- **Infrastructure & Network Audits**: Firewall rules, network segmentation, endpoint security, server hardening, Active Directory configuration.  
- **Compliance & Framework Audits**: SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, NIST 800-53, CMMC, GDPR, DORA, NIS 2 readiness.  
- **Application & Cloud Security Audits**: Web app security, API testing, AWS/cloud configuration review, container security.  
- **Policy & Governance Audits**: Security policies, access controls, incident response plans, employee training, vendor risk management.  

**Why It Matters**: Independent, evidence-based evaluations provide actionable insights, not just opinions.

## 🧠 8-Point Evaluation Framework  
Score providers on a 1–5 scale for each criterion. A total below 30/40 is a red flag.  

| # | Criterion | What to Look For | Red Flag |  
|---|---|---|---|  
| 1 | Technical Depth | Certified auditors (CISSP, CISA, CEH, OSCP). Hands-on testing. | Audit is documentation-only. |  
| 2 | Industry Experience | Track record in your sector. | No references or case studies. |  
| 3 | Methodology Transparency | Clear audit phases, testing procedures. | Vague process. |  
| 4 | Report Quality | Actionable findings with risk ratings. | Generic reports with scanner output. |  
| 5 | Remediation Support | Help fixing issues, not just listing them. | Hands you a report and disappears. |  
| 6 | Accreditations | Relevant accreditations (PCI QSA, FedRAMP 3PAO, etc.). | Claims expertise in unaccredited frameworks. |  
| 7 | Pricing Clarity | Fixed-price or clearly scoped engagements. | No estimates, time-and-materials only. |  
| 8 | Independence | No conflicts of interest. | Audit findings match vendor products. |  

**Scoring Guide**:  
- **35–40**: Excellent fit  
- **28–34**: Good fit  
- **20–27**: Proceed with caution  
- **Below 20**: Not recommended

## ❓ 15 Questions to Ask Before Hiring  
1. Who will perform the audit?  
2. Can I see a sample report?  
3. What’s your methodology?  
4. What’s included in the scope?  
5. Do you provide remediation support?  
6. Is retesting included?  
7. What accreditations do you hold?  
8. How do you handle sensitive data?  
9. What’s the timeline?  
10. Can I speak with recent clients?  
11. How do you prioritize findings?  
12. Do you sell security products?  
13. What’s in the executive summary?  
14. Do you carry liability insurance?  
15. What happens after the audit?
