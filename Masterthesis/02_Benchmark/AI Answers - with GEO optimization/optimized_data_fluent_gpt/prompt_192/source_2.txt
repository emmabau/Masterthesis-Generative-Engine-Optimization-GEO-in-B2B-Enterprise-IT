1. **Cybersecurity is Now a Board-Level Responsibility**

   - Executives face personal liability for non-compliance  
   - Regulators demand operational oversight, not just policy rubber-stamping  
   - Cybersecurity is now a permanent strategic priority  
   - Leadership cybersecurity training is mandatory  

2. **Compliance is Measured by Capability, Not Documentation**  
   NIS2 replaces paper-based compliance with real-world readiness. Regulators will assess your ability to respond to incidents, not just the tools you own. This requires:  
   - Continuous risk assessments  
   - Active vulnerability management  
   - Real-time visibility into critical assets  
   - Immediate detection and response capabilities  

3. **NIS2’s Scope is Broader Than Expected**  
   The directive covers 18 critical sectors, applying to medium and large enterprises. This expands the number of organizations required to comply:  
   - Applies to companies with over 50 employees or 10M+ annual revenue  
   - Key industries: healthcare, transport, energy, finance, and digital sectors  
   - Includes vendors, supply chains, and third-party responsibilities

4. **Incident Disclosure is Mandatory**  
   NIS2 enforces strict reporting timelines:  
   - Initial disclosure within 24 hours  
   - Detailed reports within days  
   - Coordination across technical, legal, and executive teams
