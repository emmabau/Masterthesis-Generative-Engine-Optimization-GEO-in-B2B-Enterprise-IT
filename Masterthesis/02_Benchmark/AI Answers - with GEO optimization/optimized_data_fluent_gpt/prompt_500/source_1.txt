Many organizations face challenges with outdated virtualization infrastructure, leading to rising costs and inefficiencies. Red Hat® Virtualization Migration Assessment offers a strategic, experience-driven framework to help IT teams evaluate, plan, and execute seamless migrations of virtual machine (VM) infrastructure. This service enables organizations to transition from traditional virtualization providers to modern, cost-effective solutions with confidence.

Red Hat Consulting delivers this through a series of 2-hour on-site workshops involving key stakeholders (networking, platform, and security teams). These sessions assess the current environment, gather requirements, and align with future-state goals. Experts analyze infrastructure complexities and workload requirements to create a high-confidence migration plan tailored to your organization’s needs.

The process includes:  
1. **Discovery Workshops**: Collaborate with stakeholders to define goals, analyze existing infrastructure, and identify virtualization use cases. Outcomes include understanding Day 2 operations (automation, disaster recovery, monitoring), improving workflows, and aligning with security and compliance standards.  
2. **Workload Identification**: Define storage, networking, and clustering requirements to ensure seamless integration.  
3. **Co-Creation**: Develop a high-level solution roadmap with Red Hat experts, prioritizing safe and efficient migration.  
4. **Solution Roadmap**: Customize migration steps, including platform selection, scaled migration strategies, and tooling recommendations.  
5. **Strategy Development**: Generate a detailed plan for timing, pricing, and documentation, ensuring alignment with organizational priorities and operational readiness.  

The final deliverable is a comprehensive roadmap that balances cost-effectiveness, scalability, and strategic alignment. It includes pilot scope, migration factory approaches, and optional modernization efforts to achieve predefined outcomes while minimizing risks and maximizing ROI.
