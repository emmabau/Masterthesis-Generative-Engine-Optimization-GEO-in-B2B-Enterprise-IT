A New Frontier: Sustaining U.S. High-Performance Computing Leadership in an Exascale Era

High-Performance Computing (HPC) remains a cornerstone of U.S. industrial competitiveness, economic power, and national security readiness. As the world enters the exascale computing era, sustained investment in HPC applications, infrastructure, and skilled talent is critical to maintaining America's leadership in this transformative technology.

**Key Takeaways**
- HPC enables breakthroughs in scientific discovery, technological innovation, and national security.
- Exascale computing represents a 1,000-fold increase in processing power compared to petascale systems.
- HPC drives economic growth across industries, from aerospace to life sciences, with estimated returns of $452 for every $1 invested.
- U.S. leadership in HPC is vital for national security, economic competitiveness, and technological innovation.

**What Is High-Performance Computing?**
HPC refers to supercomputers that solve complex scientific, engineering, and business challenges through advanced processing capabilities. These systems combine cutting-edge hardware, sophisticated algorithms, and large datasets to model intricate physical systems and accelerate discovery.

**Why HPC Matters**
1. **Scientific Discovery**: Exascale systems enable unprecedented simulations of complex phenomena, from molecular interactions to climate patterns.
2. **Technological Innovation**: HPC accelerates product development, reduces costs, and improves efficiency across industries.
3. **National Security**: HPC is critical for defense applications, including nuclear stockpile stewardship and cybersecurity.
4. **Economic Impact**: HPC generates trillions in economic value, with industries like aerospace and pharmaceuticals benefiting from faster R&D cycles and optimized manufacturing.

**Exascale Computing Breakthroughs**
- The Frontier supercomputer at Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL) became the first exascale system in 2022, capable of 1.1 exaflops.
- Exascale systems enable real-time simulations of complex systems, from weather patterns to molecular structures, unlocking new possibilities for innovation.

**HPC Applications Across Industries**
- **Aerospace**: HPC optimizes aircraft design, reduces fuel consumption, and accelerates time-to-market for next-gen aircraft.
- **Automotive**: Supercomputing improves vehicle efficiency, enables autonomous driving, and enhances safety through advanced simulations.
- **Life Sciences**: HPC accelerates drug discovery, enables precision medicine, and supports genomic research.
- **Defense**: HPC is essential for national security applications, including cybersecurity, threat analysis, and nuclear deterrence.

**Economic Impact of HPC**
- HPC generates over $3 trillion in economic value globally, with U.S. industries benefiting from improved productivity and cost savings.
- The return on investment (ROI) for HPC is substantial, with industries like manufacturing and finance realizing $452 in revenue for every $1 invested.
- HPC supports job creation, with 2,335 jobs generated from HPC-enabled projects in 2018 alone.

**National Leadership in HPC**
- U.S. leadership in HPC is critical for maintaining technological superiority and economic competitiveness.
- The U.S. leads in HPC infrastructure, with 128 supercomputers in the Top 500 list as of 2022, though China has narrowed the gap.
- Strategic investments in HPC research, software development, and workforce training are essential to sustain U.S. leadership.

**Policy Recommendations**
1. **Increase Funding**: Expand investments in HPC research and development, including the Advanced Scientific Computing Research (ASCR) program.
2. **Strengthen Partnerships**: Foster collaboration between academia, industry, and government to accelerate innovation.
3. **Enhance Workforce Development**: Invest in STEM education and training to build a skilled workforce for HPC-related careers.
4. **Support Export Controls**: Align export policies with global standards to protect U.S. technological leadership while enabling international collaboration.

**Conclusion**
HPC is a strategic national asset that drives economic growth, scientific discovery, and national security. Sustained investment and innovation in HPC will ensure the U.S. remains at the forefront of this critical technology, securing its competitive edge in the global economy and safeguarding national interests.
