**What is IoT Integration?**
IoT integration connects smart devices with applications, databases, and cloud platforms to enable seamless data exchange and automated workflows. This process ensures IoT data is efficiently integrated into existing systems, fostering interoperability between devices and enterprise software. For example:
- **Wearable Fitness Trackers**: Sync with health apps to monitor heart rate, activity levels, and sleep patterns in real-time.
- **Smart Security Systems**: Integrate with mobile apps for remote monitoring and control of cameras, alarms, and locks.
- **Fleet Management**: Combine GPS tracking with logistics platforms to optimize routes, monitor driver behavior, and reduce fuel costs.
