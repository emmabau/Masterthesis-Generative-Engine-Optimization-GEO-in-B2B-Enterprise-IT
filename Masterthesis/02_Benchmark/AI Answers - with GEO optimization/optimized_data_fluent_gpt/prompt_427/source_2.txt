Choosing the Right Advisor: Key Considerations  
- **Relationship Type**: Decide if you need one-time planning or ongoing portfolio management.  
- **Compensation Model**: Fee-only advisors prioritize your interests, while commission-based advisors may recommend products for fees.  
- **Credentials**: Look for certifications like CFP or CFA to ensure expertise.  

Robo-Advisors vs. Human Advisors  
Robo-advisors use algorithms for low-cost, automated investing with minimal fees. While they excel at portfolio management, human advisors offer personalized guidance and deeper relationships—though at a higher cost. Some robo-advisors partner with human advisors for an extra fee.
