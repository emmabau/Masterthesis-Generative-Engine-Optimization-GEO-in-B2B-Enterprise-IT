**Types of Data Backup Solutions**  
There are three primary backup methods: full, differential, and incremental.  
- **Full Backup**: This comprehensive approach duplicates an entire dataset, ensuring complete recovery but requiring significant storage and time.  
- **Differential Backup**: It captures changes since the last full backup, offering faster restoration than full backups but growing storage demands over time.  
- **Incremental Backup**: This method only saves changes since the last backup, making it the fastest and most storage-efficient option. Most enterprises combine these methods for optimal balance between speed, cost, and reliability.
