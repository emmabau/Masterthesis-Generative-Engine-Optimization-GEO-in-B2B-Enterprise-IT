**What is a B2B Integration Platform?**  
A B2B integration platform connects applications, data sources, and services between organizations and their partners, customers, and vendors. The market is segmented into several types:  

1. **iPaaS (Integration Platform as a Service)**  
   - Cloud-based tools with visual workflow builders, pre-built connectors, and low-code environments.  
   - Examples: Boomi, MuleSoft, Workato, SnapLogic.  
   - Operational model: Customer manages integration operations.  

2. **Enterprise Integration Suites**  
   - Comprehensive platforms from vendors like IBM, Microsoft, Oracle, and SAP.  
   - Deep integration with vendor ecosystems, supporting hybrid cloud and on-premises systems.  

3. **API Management Platforms**  
   - Consolidate third-party APIs into unified solutions.  
   - Examples: Merge.dev, Kombo.  
   - Operational model: Customer integrates once; platform manages endpoints.  

4. **Managed Integration Service Providers**  
   - Operate integrations as a service, handling monitoring, maintenance, and optimization.  
   - Examples: ONEiO.  
   - Operational model: Provider manages the entire lifecycle.  

**Critical Distinction**  
Traditional platforms require customers to manage integrations (e.g., via EDI/API connections), while managed service providers handle operations entirely.
