**Understanding Asset Management**  
Asset management is the foundation of business success, encompassing the organization, management, and optimization of physical and digital assets. From IT hardware to manufacturing equipment, effective management ensures accountability, reduces downtime, and sustains long-term growth. Neglecting these practices can lead to financial strain and operational inefficiencies.  

**Common Challenges in Asset Management**  
1. **Tracking Assets**: Managing assets across multiple locations requires modern technologies like RFID, GPS, and IoT.  
2. **Maintenance Scheduling**: Poor maintenance leads to unscheduled downtime and increased costs.  
3. **Cost Management**: Balancing acquisition, maintenance, and replacement costs demands strategic planning.  
4. **Data Accuracy**: Inaccurate data hinders decision-making and operational efficiency.  
5. **Compliance**: Meeting regulatory requirements is critical for large asset portfolios.
