### Core Definitions
1. **Creation & Innovation**: To produce or bring into existence, such as "developing a new theory" or "a company developing cutting-edge materials."  
2. **Physical/Emotional Growth**: To undergo change or maturation, e.g., "a child developing emotionally" or "a patient developing symptoms."  
3. **Evolution & Progress**: To unfold or advance over time, like "a situation developing gradually" or "a country modernizing rapidly."  
4. **Technical/Artistic Refinement**: To elaborate or expand ideas, such as "developing a melody" or "refining a hypothesis."

- **Chess/Art**: Strategic positioning ("develop the rook") or musical composition ("develop a theme").
