Life at Exascale  
Today’s fastest supercomputers operate at the petascale—performing a quadrillion (10¹⁵) calculations per second. While these systems are powerful, the next major leap in computing is exascale, which represents a quintillion (10¹⁸) operations per second. This level of performance will revolutionize how we simulate complex processes, from precision medicine and regional climate modeling to materials discovery and fundamental physics.  

Exascale computing will transform everyday life by enabling rapid analysis of massive datasets and realistic simulations of intricate systems. Its impact will span critical areas such as:  
- **Precision medicine**: Personalized treatments based on detailed biological simulations  
- **Climate science**: High-fidelity regional climate models for sustainable planning  
- **Materials science**: Accelerated discovery of new materials and their properties  
- **Energy systems**: Optimizing energy-water nexus relationships  
- **National security**: Advanced threat modeling and simulation  

**Exascale: The Engine of Discovery**  
At 10¹⁸ operations per second, exascale systems will unlock breakthroughs across scientific disciplines. They will deepen our understanding of the universe’s fundamental forces while enabling practical applications in healthcare, environmental science, and industrial innovation.
