## Key Recommendations
Microsoft recommends organizations implement structured testing of their business continuity and disaster recovery plans through:
- **Tabletop exercises** or **simulated crisis scenarios**  
- **Disruption mechanisms** to test system resilience  
- **Team training** for roles in disruption scenarios  
- **Validation of recovery strategies** and backup restoration  
- **Post-exercise reporting** with actionable recommendations  
- **Regular testing intervals** and **automated mechanisms**  
- **Coordination with relevant organizational units**

## Implementation Guidance
- **Customer responsibility:** Organizations must update their plans based on testing results and implement corrective actions.  
- **Cyber resilience:** Integrate evolving cyber threats into continuity planning and establish robust recovery capabilities.  
- **Reporting procedures:** Develop clear protocols for notifying law enforcement and stakeholders about security incidents.
