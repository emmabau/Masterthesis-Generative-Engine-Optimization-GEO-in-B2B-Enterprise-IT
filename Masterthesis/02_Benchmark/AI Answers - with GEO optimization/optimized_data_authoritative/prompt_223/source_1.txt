## IoT Sensor Types for Condition Monitoring
Modern PdM programs rely on six primary sensor types, each designed to detect specific failure modes:

### 1. Vibration Sensors (Accelerometers)
**Most popular** for rotating equipment like compressors, motors, and gearboxes. Detect:
- Bearing wear
- Misalignment
- Imbalance
- Looseness
- Gear damage
- Resonance issues

**Technical Specs**: Piezoelectric (PZT) crystals offer wide temperature range, dynamic range, and frequency bandwidth above 20 kHz. Triaxial accelerometers are mounted on bearing housings, motor frames, or pump casings. Sampling rates range from 1-10 kHz.

### 2. Temperature Sensors (Thermocouples & RTDs)
Detect thermal anomalies like overheating bearings, electrical overloads, and insulation degradation. RTDs provide **±0.1-0.5°C accuracy** for HVAC and mechanical systems, while thermocouples are used for high-temperature industrial processes.

### 3. Current Sensors (Electrical Monitoring)
Monitor electrical consumption patterns to detect motor winding degradation, phase imbalance, and belt slippage. Non-invasive clamp-on CTs measure RMS current, power factor, and harmonic distortion.

### 4. Pressure Sensors (Transducers)
Critical for fluid systems like hydraulics and pneumatics. Detect filter clogging, pump wear, leaks, and cavitation. Pressure ranges from 0-30 PSI (HVAC) to 0-5,000 PSI (hydraulics).

### 5. Acoustic Sensors (Ultrasonic Detection)
Detect leaks, arcing, and bearing degradation in frequencies 20-100 kHz. Handheld or fixed sensors are used for compressed air systems, steam traps, and high-voltage switchgear.

### 6. Humidity & Environmental Sensors
Monitor ambient conditions affecting equipment longevity. Critical for tropical climates or environments with strict environmental controls. Detect condensation risk, corrosion, and HVAC performance deviations.

## Sensor-to-CMMS Integration Architecture
A five-layer architecture ensures seamless data flow from sensors to maintenance decisions:
1. **Sensor Hardware**: Measures conditions and converts to digital signals. Includes ADC, microcontroller, and wireless radios (LoRaWAN, Wi-Fi, Zigbee).
2. **Wireless Communication**: Protocols like LoRaWAN (campus-wide), BLE (dense deployments), and Wi-Fi (high-data sensors) enable data transmission.
3. **Gateway & Edge Computing**: Preprocess data locally to reduce bandwidth and enable faster responses. Aggregates data, detects anomalies, and buffers during outages.
4. **Condition Monitoring Platform**: Uses AI-driven analytics to detect anomalies, calculate trends, and generate alerts. Gartner forecasts AI-driven PdM will reduce maintenance costs by **10-20% by 2025**.
5. **CMMS Integration**: Automates work order creation, prioritization, and scheduling. Closed-loop integration transforms sensor data into actionable maintenance decisions.
