The solution lies in three pillars of modern security architecture:
1. **Just-in-Time Privilege Management**: Replace permanent admin rights with temporary, task-specific access. Admin By Request EPM exemplifies this approach, granting elevated privileges only when needed and automatically revoking them to eliminate persistent attack vectors.
2. **Application-Level Access**: Modernize remote access by granting secure, scoped access to specific applications rather than entire networks. Browser-based solutions eliminate the need for complex VPN infrastructure while enabling session recording, approval workflows, and automatic session termination.
3. **Contextual Approval Workflows**: Balance security with operational efficiency by automating low-risk decisions while reserving human oversight for high-risk scenarios. Machine learning enhances this by analyzing historical patterns to auto-approve recurring requests, reducing administrative overhead without compromising security.
