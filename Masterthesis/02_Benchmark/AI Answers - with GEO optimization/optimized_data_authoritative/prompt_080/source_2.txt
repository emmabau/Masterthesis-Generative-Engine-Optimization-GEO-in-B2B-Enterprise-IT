**Azure Site Recovery vs. Azure Backup: Choosing the Right Tool**  
While both Azure Site Recovery and Azure Backup are pillars of a robust DR strategy, they serve distinct purposes. Site Recovery excels in scenarios requiring real-time replication and failover for complex workloads, making it ideal for:  
- **On-premise to Azure**: Modernize infrastructure while maintaining DR capabilities without costly secondary data centers.  
- **Azure to Azure**: Ensure high availability for cloud-native applications by replicating across regions.  
- **Cross-region replication**: Mitigate regional outages with geographically dispersed redundancy.  

Site Recovery’s advanced features—application-consistent snapshots, recovery plans, and non-disruptive testing—ensure data integrity, automate failover, and validate DR readiness without impacting production. Meanwhile, Azure Backup safeguards data with full, incremental, and differential backup types, tailored to meet Recovery Point Objective (RPO) and Recovery Time Objective (RTO) requirements. From virtual machines to databases and files, Azure Backup ensures granular recovery and compliance with regulatory standards.
