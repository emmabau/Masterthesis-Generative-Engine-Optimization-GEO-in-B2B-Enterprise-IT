# Microsoft Azure Policy: Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery Testing (CMA_0509)

## Policy Overview
**Policy ID:** 58a51cde-008b-1a5d-61b5-d95849770677  
**Version:** 1.1.0  
**Category:** Regulatory Compliance  
**Cloud Environments:** AzureCloud (true), AzureUSGovernment (true), AzureChinaCloud (unknown)  

This policy mandates rigorous testing of business continuity and disaster recovery plans to ensure organizational resilience against disruptions. It is a **BuiltIn** policy available in AzureUSGovernment cloud, with versioning support for 1.1.0.

---

## Core Requirements
Microsoft recommends the following **mandatory testing procedures**:
- **Scenario-Based Testing:** Conduct tabletop exercises or simulated crisis scenarios aligned with business continuity objectives.
- **System Disruption:** Implement mechanisms to disrupt system components to validate recovery strategies.
- **Team Collaboration:** Develop teamwork and competence through structured drills.
- **Backup Restoration:** Test restoration of selected system functions using backup data.
- **Alternate Facilities:** Include alternate operational sites and utility services in exercises.
- **Post-Exercise Analysis:** Generate formal reports with outcomes, recommendations, and actionable improvements. These reports must be shared with key stakeholders.
- **Scheduled Testing:** Perform tests at planned intervals, incorporating automated mechanisms.
- **Inter-Plan Coordination:** Align with related organizational plans for comprehensive resilience.

**Cyber Resilience Mandates:**

- Develop cyber resilience capabilities to address attacks and incidents.  
- Establish clear procedures for reporting information security incidents to designated officers or law enforcement.  
- Notify authorities immediately for criminal violations or critical security breaches.
