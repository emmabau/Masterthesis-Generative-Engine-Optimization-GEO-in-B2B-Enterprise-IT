**The Path Forward**  
Healthcare leaders must adopt a holistic approach:  
- **Leverage Predictive Analytics**: Use localized data to model capacity needs and allocate resources strategically.  
- **Invest in Workforce Innovation**: Prioritize APPs, AI tools, and education pipelines to address staffing shortages.  
- **Rethink Care Delivery**: Shift low-acuity care to outpatient and home settings to reduce hospital strain.  
- **Tackle SDOH Barriers**: Target high-need communities with data-informed interventions to improve outcomes and reduce inpatient demand.
