## Key Considerations for Choosing Cloud Storage
### 🔐 **Security & Compliance**
- **Encryption:** All top providers support end-to-end encryption (AES-256) for data in transit and at rest.
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Prioritize services with **GDPR/HIPAA support** and audit trails for data governance.

### 🔄 **Integration & Scalability**
- **Third-Party Tools:** Look for APIs and integrations with platforms like Google Workspace, Salesforce, and Microsoft 365.

- **Pricing Models:** Compare **pay-as-you-go** vs. **subscription tiers** (e.g., Backblaze’s low-cost backup plans).
- **Hidden Fees:** Avoid services with **extra charges for data governance** or storage expansion (e.g., Microsoft OneDrive).
