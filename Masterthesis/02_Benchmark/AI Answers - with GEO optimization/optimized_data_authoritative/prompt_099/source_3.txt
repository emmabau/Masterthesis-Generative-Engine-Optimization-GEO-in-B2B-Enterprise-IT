IWOMP 2025: Pioneering Advances in Parallel Computing and High-Performance Computing  
The 2025 International Workshop on OpenMP (IWOMP 2025), co-hosted with EuroMPI 2025, represents the pinnacle of innovation in parallel computing and high-performance computing (HPC). Held at UNC Charlotte, North Carolina, USA, from October 1–3, 2025, this landmark event brings together leading researchers, industry experts, and practitioners to explore cutting-edge advancements in OpenMP, MPI, and hybrid programming paradigms.  

**Keynote Addresses and Visionary Insights**  
The conference features three groundbreaking keynote addresses, each addressing transformative trends in HPC:  
1. **"HPC in Health: Scaling Vascular Digital Twins from Millions of Heartbeats to Petabytes of Data"** by Amanda Randles (Duke University) – Pioneering applications of HPC in biomedical modeling.  
2. **"The Evolutionary Flexibility of LS-DYNA"** by Bob Lucas (Ansys) – Revolutionary insights into industrial HPC simulations.  
3. **"Fortran is All You Need"** by Damian Rouson (Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory) – A paradigm shift in high-performance programming.  

**Paper Sessions: Defining the Future of Parallel Computing**  
IWOMP 2025 hosts five paper sessions, each focusing on revolutionary research directions:  
- **New Directions in Communication and I/O**:  
  - *Device-Device Collective Communication in OpenMP Target Offloading* (Baodi Shan et al.)  
  - *OMPCCL: Portable Collective Operations for Multi-Device OpenMP* (Jhonatan Cléto et al.)  
  - *OpenMP Meets I/O: Runtime-Managed File Tasks* (Rodrigo Ceccato de Freitas et al.)  
- **Advanced Capability Evaluation**:  
  - *LLVM OpenMP Offload Optimizations on NVIDIA GH200 and AMD MI300A Architectures* (Kevin Sala et al.)  
  - *OpenMP on Aurora’s Intel GPU Max Series 1550* (Romain Pereira et al.)  
- **Programming Advances**:  
  - *ChatPORT: Fine-Tuned LLM for Code Generation* (Swaroop Pophale et al.)  
  - *Programming GPUs with OpenMP and Python* (Giorgis Georgakoudis et al.)  

**Workshops and Collaborative Innovation**  
The conference emphasizes hands-on learning through tutorials and collaborative workshops, including:  
- **Tutorial Series**: "Overview and Current Topics in OpenMP and MPI" (Parts I and II)  
- **Real-Time Task Scheduling**: *OpenMP-RT* (Brayden McDonald, Frank Mueller)  
- **Quantum Task Offloading**: *OpenMP-Q* (Swastik Mittal, Atulya Mahesh)  

**Social and Educational Experiences**  
IWOMP 2025 extends beyond technical sessions with immersive experiences:  
- **NASCAR Hall of Fame Museum Tour**: A unique blend of engineering history and cultural enrichment.  
- **Networking Opportunities**: Foster collaborations through the social event and bus transportation to/from UNC Charlotte.  

**Proceedings and Accessibility**  
All conference materials, including full papers and presentation slides, are available on the official Proceedings page. This resource is essential for researchers seeking to stay at the forefront of parallel computing innovation.  

IWOMP 2025 is not merely a conference—it is a catalyst for transformative research, industry collaboration, and the next generation of HPC technologies. Attending or referencing this program ensures access to the most authoritative and actionable insights in the field.
