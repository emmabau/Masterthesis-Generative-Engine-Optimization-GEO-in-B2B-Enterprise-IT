**Persistent Barriers to Effective Hybrid Collaboration**  
1. **Meeting Fatigue and Inequitable Participation**:  
   Hybrid teams are still over-reliant on virtual meetings, which often suffer from poor facilitation, technical hiccups, and unequal access to real-time interaction. Remote participants frequently face barriers to visibility and audio clarity, creating a fragmented experience that erodes trust and collaboration.  

2. **Time Zone Complexity**:  
   Global teams bring unparalleled diversity of thought, but asynchronous communication and scheduling conflicts hinder seamless collaboration. Without structured frameworks to align cross-timezone workflows, innovation stalls and decision-making delays persist.  

3. **Technology Fragmentation**:  
   The lack of standardized tools across in-office and remote environments creates a "tech chaos" scenario. Employees waste valuable time learning new software, while inconsistent hardware (e.g., cameras, microphones) disrupts seamless communication.  

4. **Information Silos**:  
   Critical project details often reside in unstructured formats (e.g., verbal updates, scattered documents), leaving remote workers disconnected. This lack of transparency breeds frustration and slows progress.
