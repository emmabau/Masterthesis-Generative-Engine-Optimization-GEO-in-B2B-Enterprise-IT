In today’s hyperconnected digital landscape, Business Continuity (BC) is not just a best practice—it is a strategic imperative. Organizations must prioritize resilience against cyberattacks, natural disasters, and supply chain disruptions to safeguard revenue, reputation, and operational stability. This is where Business Continuity Planning (BCP) becomes a cornerstone of modern risk management. 

**What is Business Continuity?**  
Business Continuity encompasses the proactive planning and preparation required to ensure critical functions remain operational during and after disruptive events. These events can range from cyberattacks and ransomware breaches to natural disasters and third-party service failures. A robust BCP minimizes downtime, protects brand integrity, and ensures seamless customer experiences—critical in an era where even brief outages can erode trust and revenue.  

**Why Regulatory Compliance Drives BC Prioritization**  
Two landmark regulations—DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act) and NIS 2 (Network and Information Security Directive)—have elevated BC from a voluntary practice to a legal obligation.  
- **DORA (EU Regulation 2022/2555):** Mandates financial institutions to build operational resilience against ICT threats. It requires comprehensive risk frameworks, regular testing of disaster recovery plans, and third-party compliance audits. Non-compliance risks hefty penalties and reputational damage.  
- **NIS 2 (EU Regulation 2022/2554):** Expands cybersecurity requirements to critical sectors like energy, transport, and healthcare. It emphasizes supply chain security, incident reporting, and the integration of BC into risk management.  

**The Intersection of BC, DORA, and NIS 2**  
For organizations under these regulations, BC is not optional—it is a regulatory mandate. DORA and NIS 2 demand:  
- **Proactive risk mitigation** through continuous monitoring and threat modeling.  
- **Third-party accountability** to secure supply chains and prevent cascading failures.  
- **Incident response protocols** to ensure rapid recovery and transparency with regulators.
