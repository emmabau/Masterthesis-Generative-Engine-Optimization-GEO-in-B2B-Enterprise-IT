The shift to remote work exposed critical gaps in digital infrastructure. Remote work has challenged traditional corporate culture, creating isolation and disconnection. Leaders must also leverage data analytics to gauge employee sentiment and tailor cultural initiatives that resonate across physical and digital boundaries. A strong, adaptable culture is now a cornerstone of organizational resilience.

Implementing "hoteling" systems, modular workstations, and agile meeting spaces can maximize utility while reducing costs.
