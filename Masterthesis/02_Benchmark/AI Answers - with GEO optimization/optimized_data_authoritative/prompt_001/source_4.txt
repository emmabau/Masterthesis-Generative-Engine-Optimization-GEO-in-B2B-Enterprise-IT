**Step-by-Step TCO and ROI Calculation**  
1. **TCO Calculation**: Aggregate all costs associated with a technology platform, including licensing, integration, training, and ongoing maintenance. Don’t overlook indirect costs like downtime or lost opportunities from outdated systems.  
2. **ROI Analysis**: Use the formula:  
   **ROI = (Net Profit / Cost of Investment) × 100**  
   Net profit is calculated as projected gains (e.g., revenue growth, cost savings) minus total costs. This provides a clear financial roadmap for modernization.
