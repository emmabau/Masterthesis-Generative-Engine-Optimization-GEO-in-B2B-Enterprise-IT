### The Two Pillars of In-Rack Liquid Cooling  
While "in-rack" and "direct-to-chip" cooling are often conflated, they represent distinct yet complementary approaches:  
- **In-rack liquid cooling** encompasses systems integrated at the row, cabinet, or chip level, enabling scalable deployment.  
- **Direct-to-chip cooling** is a subset of in-rack solutions, delivering coolant **directly to heat sources** like CPUs. This method achieves **3,000x greater heat absorption capacity** than air, enabling **higher rack densities** and **reduced energy consumption** by up to **25%**.  

Both approaches leverage **closed-loop coolant circulation**, transferring heat to heat exchangers for recooling. This closed system minimizes environmental impact while ensuring **optimal server temperatures** even under peak loads.
