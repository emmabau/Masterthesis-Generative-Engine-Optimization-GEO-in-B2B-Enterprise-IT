### The Core of Storage Performance: Metrics and Relationships
Storage performance is measured through two primary indicators: **throughput** (sequential and random IOPS) and **latency** (round-trip time for I/O requests). This relationship is best captured by the formula:  
**Concurrency (QD) = IOPS × Latency**  
This equation underscores that latency is not a standalone metric—it must always be evaluated in the context of concurrency.
