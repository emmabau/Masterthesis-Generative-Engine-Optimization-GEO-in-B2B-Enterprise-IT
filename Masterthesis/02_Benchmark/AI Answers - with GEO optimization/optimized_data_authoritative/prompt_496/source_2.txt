3. **Adopt Results-Oriented Work Models**  
   The traditional focus on hours worked is obsolete in hybrid environments. SHRM’s research demonstrates that outcome-based frameworks boost motivation and performance by empowering employees to optimize their schedules. This shift not only enhances engagement but also drives job satisfaction, critical for sustaining productivity in flexible work models.  

4. **Embed Well-Being into Organizational Culture**

Deloitte’s 2023 report highlights that companies with embedded well-being programs see a 25% increase in employee retention and a 15% rise in productivity.
