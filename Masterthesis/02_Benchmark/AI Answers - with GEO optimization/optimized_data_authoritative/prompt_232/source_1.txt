## Critical Considerations for Choosing Backup Software
### 1. **Backup Types: Local vs. Cloud**
- **Local backups** (external drives, NAS) offer faster access but risk physical damage.  
- **Cloud backups** provide offsite security but require ongoing internet connectivity and subscription fees.  

### 2. **Security & Encryption**
- Always prioritize **end-to-end encryption** with private keys.  
- Services like Acronis and IDrive support multi-factor authentication for added protection.  

### 3. **Disk Imaging: The Gold Standard**
- Disk image backups (e.g., ShadowProtect) replicate entire drives, enabling full system recovery.  
- Continuous imaging is ideal for critical systems but requires pre-boot restoration tools.  

### 4. **Versioning & Recovery**
- Services with versioning (e.g., Acronis, Backblaze) allow recovery of previous file states, crucial for ransomware mitigation.
