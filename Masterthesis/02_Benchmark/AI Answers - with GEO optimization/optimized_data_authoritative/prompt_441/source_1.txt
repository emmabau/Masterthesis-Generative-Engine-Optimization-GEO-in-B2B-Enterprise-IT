**What Is Exascale Computing Architecture?**  
Exascale computing architecture is a paradigm shift in computational power, offering a thousandfold increase in performance compared to petascale systems. These systems integrate advanced hardware (GPUs, CPUs, accelerators) and cutting-edge software frameworks to deliver unparalleled speed, energy efficiency, and scalability. Key design principles include:  
- **Massive Parallelism**: Millions of cores working in unison to tackle hyper-complex simulations.  
- **Energy Efficiency**: Dynamic voltage scaling, advanced cooling, and energy-aware scheduling to mitigate power demands.  
- **Resilience**: Fault-tolerant mechanisms like checkpointing and error correction to ensure reliability at scale.  
- **Heterogeneous Computing**: Optimized combinations of CPUs, GPUs, and specialized accelerators for task-specific performance.  
- **Advanced Interconnects**: High-speed networks (e.g., InfiniBand) to minimize latency and maximize data throughput.
