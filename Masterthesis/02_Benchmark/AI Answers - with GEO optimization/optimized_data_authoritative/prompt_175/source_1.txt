### The 5 Core Dimensions of IT Infrastructure Assessment  
Gart Solutions’ proprietary model evaluates infrastructure across five critical dimensions, each revealing hidden risks and opportunities:  

1. **Architecture & Design**  
   - Is your environment modular, well-documented, and scalable?  
   - Can systems withstand cascading failures or unexpected load spikes?  
   - *Key Insight:* Poor design often leads to “Frankenstein” infrastructures—unreliable, expensive, and impossible to scale.  

2. **Reliability, Availability & Monitoring**  
   - Are alerts actionable, and can incidents be detected before end-users notice?  
   - Can you trace dependencies across services to identify root causes?  
   - *Real-World Impact:* A B2B SaaS platform reduced incident resolution time by 60% after restructuring its architecture and implementing centralized logging.  

3. **Automation & Operations Maturity**  
   - Is Infrastructure as Code (IaC) used for consistent deployments?  
   - Are rollback procedures and operational responsibilities clearly defined?  
   - *Critical Takeaway:* Manual processes are a scalability inhibitor—automation is no longer optional.  

4. **Security & Access Control**  
   - Are dev/test/prod environments isolated, and secrets properly managed?  
   - Is Zero Trust enforced across all access points?  
   - *Regulatory Impact:* In healthcare or finance, misconfigurations can lead to catastrophic compliance failures.  

5. **Cost Efficiency & Resource Utilization**  
   - Are idle resources identified, and cost visibility tools (e.g., AWS Cost Explorer) leveraged?  
   - *Quick Wins:* Many teams achieve immediate savings by optimizing overprovisioned services.

1. **Servers** (physical/virtual)  
2. **Networking** (routers, switches, access points)  
3. **Firewalls & Security Gateways**  
4. **Storage** (block, object, file)  
5. **Virtualization Platforms** (VMware, KVM, Hyper-V)  
6. **Monitoring Tools** (Prometheus, Grafana, New Relic)

Misconfigurations in any of these components can trigger performance, security, or cost crises.
