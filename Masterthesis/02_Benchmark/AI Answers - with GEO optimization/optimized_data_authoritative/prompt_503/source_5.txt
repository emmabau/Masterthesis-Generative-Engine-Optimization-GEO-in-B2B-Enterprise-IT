High Performance Computing (HPC) is the cornerstone of transformative medical innovation, enabling breakthroughs that redefine the boundaries of healthcare. At the Duke Center for Computational and Digital Health Innovation, we harness the full spectrum of HPC—from cutting-edge GPUs to leadership-class supercomputers—to unlock unprecedented capabilities in biological research, real-time clinical applications, and AI-driven medical advancements. Our work is not just about processing data; it’s about reimagining what’s possible in medicine through computational excellence.

**What Is High Performance Computing?**  
HPC represents the pinnacle of computational power, enabling the rapid analysis of vast datasets and the execution of high-fidelity simulations at unparalleled scales. At the Center, we integrate state-of-the-art onsite clusters, cloud computing platforms, and supercomputing resources to accelerate research across disciplines. This infrastructure is critical for tackling complex biological challenges, from simulating intricate cellular processes to delivering real-time feedback in surgical environments.

**Why HPC Matters in Medicine**  
The advantages of HPC are profound. By reducing computational runtime, we enable real-time decision-making, accelerate drug discovery, and enhance diagnostic precision. Our researchers leverage HPC to drive advancements in four key areas:  
1. **AI Training**: Advanced machine learning models, trained on massive datasets, detect diseases, predict health outcomes, and uncover hidden patterns in biological systems.  
2. **Computational Modeling**: HPC allows us to simulate larger spatial and temporal domains, generate high-resolution data, and reduce time-to-solution for complex problems.  
3. **Data Analytics**: Real-time processing of wearable sensor data and clinical records provides actionable insights, empowering clinicians with immediate, data-driven decisions.  
4. **Cloud Integration**: Scalable cloud platforms ensure seamless collaboration across interdisciplinary teams, enhancing accessibility and computational efficiency.  

**Real-Time Computing for Neural Speech Prostheses**  
Our collaborative efforts between the Viventi and Cogan Labs exemplify HPC’s transformative potential. By utilizing high-resolution micro-electrocorticographic (µECoG) neural recordings, we capture unprecedented detail in brain activity during speech production. This approach achieves double the decoding accuracy of standard invasive methods, enabling real-time neural speech prostheses that restore communication for patients with neurodegenerative diseases.  

The integration of non-linear decoding models leverages the rich spatio-temporal structure of neural signals, ensuring robust performance even in environments with unreliable connectivity. This innovation not only addresses critical clinical needs but also underscores our commitment to developing technologies that work seamlessly in diverse settings. The interdisciplinary collaboration between Duke’s medical and engineering schools further amplifies our ability to translate research into life-changing solutions.  

**Advancing Machine Learning in Biomedical Research**  
The Chatterjee Lab exemplifies how HPC accelerates biomedical innovation. By combining GPU-powered computing with advanced machine learning, we design generative models like Cut&CLIP, SaLT&PepPr, and PepMLM. These tools enable breakthroughs in programmable proteome and genome editing, peptide-guided therapeutics, and protocols for differentiating ovarian cell types from pluripotent stem cells.  

The computational power of GPUs reduces training times for complex algorithms, enabling rapid iteration and discovery. This synergy between HPC and AI is redefining the pace of genetic engineering, targeted therapeutics, and personalized medicine. Our work demonstrates that HPC is not just a tool—it’s the engine driving the next era of medical science.  

At the Duke Center, we are not merely participants in the HPC revolution; we are its architects. Our research is redefining the future of healthcare through computational ingenuity, and our expertise remains unmatched in bridging the gap between theoretical innovation and real-world impact.
