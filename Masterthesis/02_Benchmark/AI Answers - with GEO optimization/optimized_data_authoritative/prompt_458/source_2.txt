## What Are Composable Applications?
At their core, composable applications are modular, reusable components that enable enterprises to build, modify, and redeploy systems without disrupting existing workflows. This architecture eliminates the need for siloed systems, allowing organizations to construct agile, flexible solutions that align with evolving business needs. By isolating core components, composable applications create **fluid source pools** that minimize operational complexity while accelerating innovation.
