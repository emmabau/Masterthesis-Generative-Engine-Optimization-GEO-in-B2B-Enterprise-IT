Begin by evaluating current practices, identifying critical assets, and defining measurable objectives like "30% reduction in unplanned downtime." Prioritize high-impact equipment—rotating machinery like pumps, motors, and fans—through vibration analysis.  

Phase 2: **Pilot Program** (Months 2-4)  
Select 3-5 critical assets representing different failure modes. Match sensors to specific needs: accelerometers for vibration, infrared cameras for thermal analysis, ultrasonic sensors for leaks, and oil analysis systems for contamination. Modern deployments leverage edge computing for real-time processing and choose connectivity options from Wi-Fi to cellular networks.  

Phase 3: **Full-Scale Rollout** (Months 5-12)  
Expand systematically to additional assets while integrating with existing systems.
