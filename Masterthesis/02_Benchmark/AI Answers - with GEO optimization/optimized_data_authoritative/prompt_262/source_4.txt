## What Is Hyper-Converged Infrastructure (HCI)?  
HCI integrates compute, storage, and networking into a single, software-defined system, eliminating the complexity of traditional three-tier architectures. Key components include:  
- Virtualized compute resources (servers)  
- Software-defined storage  
- Network virtualization  
- Centralized management tools  

This consolidation reduces hardware footprint, streamlines operations, and supports cloud-native workloads, virtual desktops, and data-intensive applications.  

## Key Factors Influencing HCI Pricing  
HCI costs are determined by a combination of hardware, software, and operational factors:  
1. **Hardware Specifications**: CPU cores, memory, storage type (SSD/HDD), and network interfaces  
2. **Software Licensing**: Per-node, per-CPU, or per-VM models  
3. **Support & Maintenance**: Annual fees for updates, patches, and technical assistance  
4. **Scalability**: Costs for adding nodes or expanding storage capacity  
5. **Included Features**: Backup, disaster recovery, security, and analytics tools

## Total Cost of Ownership (TCO): Beyond Upfront Costs

- **Support & Maintenance**: 15–25% of initial costs annually

- **Operational Savings**: Simplified management reduces IT labor expenses  
- **Energy & Space**: Compact HCI systems lower data center costs
