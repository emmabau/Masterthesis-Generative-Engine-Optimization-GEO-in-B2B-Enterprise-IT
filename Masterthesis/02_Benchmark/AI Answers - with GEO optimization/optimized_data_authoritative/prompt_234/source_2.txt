**The 7-Step Compliance Risk Assessment Framework**  
1. **Define Scope and Regulatory Universe**  
   Begin by clearly delineating product, geographic, and regulatory boundaries. This foundational step ensures no market or obligation is left unaddressed.  

2. **Map Obligations to Business Operations**  
   Connect each regulatory requirement to specific processes, systems, and teams. This mapping identifies critical contact points where compliance can be achieved or lost.  

3. **Identify and Document Risk Exposure**  
   Systematically uncover gaps in controls, documentation, knowledge, or change-lag. Each risk must be documented with specificity, including the regulation, affected product/process, and potential consequences.  

4. **Score and Prioritize Risks**  
   Use a simple 1-5 scale for likelihood and impact to categorize risks into high, medium, or low priority. This ensures resources are allocated to the most critical exposures.  

5. **Develop and Execute Remediation Plans**  
   Create actionable plans with clear owners, deadlines, and escalation paths. For enterprise-scale programs, leverage compliance platforms to track progress and maintain an auditable trail.  

6. **Test Controls and Validate Effectiveness**  
   Confirm remediation success through real-world testing of processes, documentation, and decisions. This step ensures controls are not just theoretical but operational.  

7. **Report and Monitor Continuously**  
   Translate risk assessments into business-relevant insights for leadership. Continuous monitoring of regulatory changes ensures the risk register remains accurate and actionable.
