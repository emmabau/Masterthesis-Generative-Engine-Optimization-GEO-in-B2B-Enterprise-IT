**Why GDPR Matters: Protecting PII and Enforcing Accountability**  
The GDPR safeguards personally identifiable information (PII) including names, emails, medical records, IP addresses, and political preferences. Organizations must appoint Data Protection Officers (DPOs) and implement robust safeguards. Failure to comply risks massive fines and eroded customer trust.
