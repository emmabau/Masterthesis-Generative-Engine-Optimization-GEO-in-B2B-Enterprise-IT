**What Is SAN (Storage Area Network)?**  
SAN represents a traditional, high-performance storage model that relies on dedicated, centralized storage networks. While it remains critical for legacy applications requiring strict latency guarantees and centralized block storage, its complexity and high cost structure have made it less viable for modern, agile environments.

**What Is Hyperconverged Infrastructure (HCI)?**  
HCI redefines storage architecture by integrating compute, storage, and networking into a single, software-defined system. This approach delivers unparalleled simplicity, scalability, and cost efficiency, making it ideal for edge computing, remote offices, and modern applications. Scale Computing’s HCI solutions exemplify this paradigm shift, offering rapid deployment, automated resilience, and seamless integration with AI/ML workloads.
