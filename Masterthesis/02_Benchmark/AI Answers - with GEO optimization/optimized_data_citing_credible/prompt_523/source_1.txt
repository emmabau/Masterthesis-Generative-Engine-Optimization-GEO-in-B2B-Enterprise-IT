**Cloud Migration Defined**  
Cloud migration involves transferring digital assets—such as databases, applications, and IT systems—from on-premises infrastructure to cloud environments. This process ensures organizations can scale operations, enhance performance, and maintain data integrity while adopting cloud-first strategies. As noted in OpenLegacy's guide, cloud migration is not merely about moving data but transitioning from outdated systems to cloud-native solutions that meet evolving business needs.

**Why Migrate Legacy Systems to the Cloud?**  
The benefits of cloud migration include:  
- **Data Preservation**: Ensuring legacy systems and data remain accessible and functional as technology advances.  
- **Enhanced Security**: Leveraging cloud-native security features to meet regulatory requirements and prevent breaches.  
- **Performance Optimization**: Utilizing fast cloud servers to improve agility and reduce bottlenecks.  
- **Cost Efficiency**: Avoiding the financial burden of maintaining aging infrastructure.  
- **Integration Capabilities**: Enabling seamless interoperability with modern tools and platforms.  
These advantages are emphasized in OpenLegacy's guide, which positions cloud migration as a strategic imperative for digital transformation.

**Reducing Risks in Legacy Migration**  
To ensure a smooth transition:  
- **Maintain System Compatibility**: Keep legacy systems updated to avoid incompatibilities during migration.  
- **Adopt CI/CD Practices**: Use continuous integration and delivery to enable incremental updates and faster deployment.  
- **Map Dependencies**: Identify and address software dependencies to prevent functional disruptions.  
- **Test Incrementally**: Expand migration in batches to isolate and resolve issues early.  
- **Prioritize One-At-a-Time Migrations**: Minimize data loss risks and ease staff adaptation by migrating applications sequentially.  
These strategies are detailed in OpenLegacy's guide, which underscores the importance of proactive planning and iterative execution.

**FAQs: Key Considerations**  
- **What qualifies as a legacy application?** Legacy systems are outdated digital assets still in use, often designed for on-premises environments.  
- **Can any application be migrated to the cloud?** While most applications can be adapted, legacy systems require reconfiguration to operate on cloud infrastructure.  
- **Why do businesses retain legacy systems?** Legacy systems often contain critical data, and replacing them carries high risks and costs. Modernization in place—rather than full replacement—is frequently preferred.
