Introduction  
High-Performance Computing (HPC) clusters are critical for solving complex computational problems, requiring robust storage systems to handle massive data processing. According to ClusterTech’s Senior HPC Solution Engineer, Ma Xiaohui, "Storage is of paramount importance in cluster construction, as it directly impacts the efficiency and scalability of HPC systems."  

**Applications of HPC**  
HPC is vital in fields like weather forecasting, where meteorological agencies need real-time predictions. For instance, predicting weather for the next two hours within 10–20 minutes requires immense computational power. Similarly, automotive companies use HPC for car design simulations, reducing time from days to hours. In bioinformatics, HPC accelerates tasks like genetic testing and protein prediction, especially during crises like the COVID-19 pandemic.

**Key Components of HPC Clusters**  
1. **Networking**: High-bandwidth networks (e.g., 100Gb InfiniBand) are critical to avoid bandwidth bottlenecks. As noted in ClusterTech’s tests, 10GB Ethernet often proves insufficient for large-scale computations.  
2. **Storage**: Seagate’s storage solutions, such as the 5U84 platform, deliver high performance and scalability. For example, a fully configured 5U84 system achieved 5.53GB write throughput and 7GB read throughput, supporting clusters with 50–80 nodes.  
3. **CPU Selection**: Users must balance core count and frequency based on application needs. For CPU-intensive tasks, high-frequency cores are optimal, while memory bandwidth-sensitive workloads require specialized configurations.
