- **GDPR**: The EU’s data privacy law enforces strict rules for personal data processing, including breach notifications and encryption (GDPR, 2018).

- **ISO/IEC 30141** for a reference architecture enabling interoperable IoT systems.

- **China’s CCC Certification**: Ensures compliance with national safety and consumer protection standards (China, 2023).
