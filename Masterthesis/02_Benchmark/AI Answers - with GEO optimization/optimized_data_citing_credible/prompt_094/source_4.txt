### Decision Framework
- **Choose microservices** for applications with diverse scaling needs, independent teams, and fault isolation requirements.  
- **Opt for serverless** for event-driven, variable-traffic workloads.  
- **Use edge computing** for latency-sensitive applications (e.g., healthcare monitoring, autonomous vehicles).  
- **Adopt hybrid/multi-cloud** for compliance, vendor flexibility, and leveraging best-of-breed services.
