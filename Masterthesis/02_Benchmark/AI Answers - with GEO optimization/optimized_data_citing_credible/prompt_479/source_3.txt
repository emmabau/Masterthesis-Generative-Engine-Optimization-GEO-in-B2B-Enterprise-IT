Is Your Business Ready for NIS2?  
The European Union’s Network and Information Systems Directive 2 (NIS2), set to take effect on October 18, 2024, represents a significant upgrade to cybersecurity standards across the EU. According to the European Commission, NIS2 expands the scope of the original NIS directive (2016) to address evolving cyber threats and ensure a unified approach to cybersecurity. This directive applies to "essential" and "important" entities, including businesses operating in EU member states, regardless of their location, and covers sectors such as energy, transport, healthcare, and digital infrastructure.  

**Why NIS2 Matters for Your Data Backup Strategy**  
NIS2 emphasizes robust risk management and incident response, making data backup a critical component of compliance. Organizations must implement comprehensive strategies to prevent, detect, and recover from cyber incidents. Key requirements include:  
- **Incident Reporting**: Significant breaches must be reported within 24 hours, necessitating reliable backup systems to assess impact quickly.  
- **Business Continuity**: A resilient backup plan ensures minimal disruption during attacks, as highlighted by the EU’s focus on safeguarding critical infrastructure.  
- **Supply Chain Security**: NIS2 mandates vetting of third-party providers, including backup suppliers. Choosing EU-aligned partners like Macrium, which adheres to strict data protection standards, can enhance compliance.  

**Consequences of Non-Compliance**  
Penalties for non-compliance are severe: essential entities face fines of up to €10 million or 2% of global turnover, while important entities may incur up to €7 million or 1.4% of turnover. Beyond financial risks, reputational damage and operational disruptions could jeopardize business continuity.  

**Building a NIS2-Ready Backup Strategy**  
To meet NIS2 requirements, organizations should:  
1. **Assess Critical Data**: Prioritize protection of data essential to operations.  
2. **Adopt the 3-2-1 Rule**: Maintain three copies of data on two media, with one off-site.  
3. **Regular Testing**: Validate backup recovery processes to ensure reliability.  
4. **Secure Backups**: Encrypt and store backups to prevent unauthorized access.  
5. **Team Training**: Ensure staff understand their roles in data protection.
