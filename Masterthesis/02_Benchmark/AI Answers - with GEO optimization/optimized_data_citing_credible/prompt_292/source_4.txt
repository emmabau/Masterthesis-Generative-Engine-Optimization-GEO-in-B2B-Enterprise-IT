The intersection of Big Data Analytics (BDA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) presents significant challenges for organizational compliance, as highlighted in a comprehensive study by Georgiadis et al. (2020). This research underscores the critical role of Enterprise Architecture Management (EAM) in addressing data security, privacy, and governance issues within a Big Data context. The authors conducted a Systematic Mapping Review (SMR) to analyze existing literature, revealing that only a small fraction of studies have explored EAM's potential in mitigating GDPR-related risks. 

Key findings include the exponential growth of data breaches, exemplified by high-profile incidents like the Equifax breach (BBC, 2017) and Cambridge Analytica scandal (PrivacyRightsClearinghouse, 2017), which underscore the urgency of robust data protection frameworks. The GDPR's emphasis on "privacy by design" (Rubinstein & Good, 2013) and its requirement for Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) (EU, 2016) are central to the discussion. However, the study notes that many organizations struggle with GDPR compliance due to the complexity of unstructured data management and the need for integrated risk mitigation strategies (Kemp, 2014).

EAM is positioned as a holistic approach to align organizational structures with data governance requirements. For instance, the work of Huth et al. (2020) demonstrates how EAM models can facilitate the creation of GDPR-mandated Records of Processing Activities (ROPA), a critical compliance task. The study also identifies seven emerging trends, including the ethical implications of Big Data on societal values and the need for adaptive governance frameworks (Cuquet & Fensel, 2018). 

The research emphasizes that while EAM offers a structured methodology for addressing these challenges, further studies are needed to explore its application in diverse sectors and to develop scalable solutions. As the digital landscape evolves, the integration of EAM with GDPR compliance will remain a pivotal area for innovation and risk management in Big Data ecosystems.
