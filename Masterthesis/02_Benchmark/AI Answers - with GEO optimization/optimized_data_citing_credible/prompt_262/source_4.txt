## What is Hyper-Converged Infrastructure (HCI)?
Hyper-Converged Infrastructure (HCI) integrates compute, storage, and networking into a single system managed via software. This unified approach simplifies IT operations, reduces hardware footprint, and supports cloud-native workloads, virtual desktops, and data-intensive applications. As noted by Gammatek ISPL, HCI replaces traditional three-tier architectures with a scalable, compact solution ideal for modern data centers.

## Key Factors Influencing HCI Pricing
HCI costs depend on several variables:  
- **Hardware specifications:** CPU cores, memory, storage type (SSD/HDD), and network interfaces  
- **Software licensing:** Per-node, per-CPU, or per-VM models  
- **Support & maintenance:** Annual fees for updates and technical assistance  
- **Scalability:** Costs for adding nodes or expanding storage  
- **Included features:** Backup, security, analytics, and disaster recovery tools  

According to Gammatek ISPL, these factors create significant price variations across vendors, making TCO analysis critical for budgeting.

## Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Analysis
Price per node is only part of the equation. Gammatek ISPL highlights:  
- **Support costs:** 15–25% of initial investment annually  
- **Scaling costs:** Vary by vendor (e.g., Nutanix’s management overhead offsets higher upfront costs)  
- **Operational savings:** Simplified management reduces IT labor expenses  
- **Energy/Space:** Compact HCI systems lower data center costs

## Choosing the Right HCI Solution
Gammatek ISPL recommends:  
1. **Assess existing infrastructure:** VMware users benefit from vSAN or VxRail integration  
2. **Evaluate scalability:** Prioritize vendors with flexible scaling options  
3. **Compare included features:** Bundled security and analytics reduce third-party costs  
4. **Calculate TCO:** Factor in support, power, cooling, and labor savings  
5. **Request customized quotes:** Vendors often adjust pricing based on volume and contract terms
