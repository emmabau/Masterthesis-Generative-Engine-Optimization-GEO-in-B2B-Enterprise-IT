**Regulatory Compliance and Key Standards**  
Financial firms must comply with a complex web of regulations, including:  
- **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation)  
- **DORA** (Digital Operational Resiliency Act)  
- **EU AI Act**  
- **SEC’s Amendments to Regulation S-P**  
- **FCA’s Operational Resilience Framework**  
- **FSRA** (Financial Services Regulatory Authority)  
- **DFSA** (Dubai Financial Services Authority)  
- **NFA** (National Futures Association)  

These frameworks emphasize proactive risk management, with regulators like the SEC prioritizing cybersecurity in examinations. As noted by ACA, "Cybersecurity is now a regulatory examination priority, requiring firms to demonstrate robust risk management despite the absence of prescriptive frameworks" (ACA Vantage, 2023).

**Why Cybersecurity Matters for Financial Firms**  
Financial institutions handle sensitive data, making them prime targets for cyberattacks. As highlighted in ACA’s FAQs, "Robust cybersecurity measures are essential to protect data, maintain customer trust, and comply with regulations" (ACA Vantage, 2023).  

**Proactive Steps for Cybersecurity Resilience**  
- Conduct annual risk assessments and update them post-major system changes.  
- Implement encryption, access controls, and secure authentication.  
- Train employees to recognize threats and foster a security-aware culture.  
- Develop and test incident response plans to minimize disruption.
