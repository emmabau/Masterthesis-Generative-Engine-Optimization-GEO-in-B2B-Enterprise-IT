A structured TCO breakdown includes implementation and running costs. To ensure accurate ROI calculations, organizations must consider both direct and indirect costs, including power consumption for on-premises systems and carbon footprint metrics. As the ERP lifecycle shortens due to technological advancements, TCO transparency becomes even more vital for aligning investments with business goals.
