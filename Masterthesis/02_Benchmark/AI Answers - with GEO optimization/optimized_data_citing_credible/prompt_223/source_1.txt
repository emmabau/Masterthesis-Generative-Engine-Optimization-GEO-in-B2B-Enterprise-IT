## IoT Sensor Types for Condition Monitoring
Modern predictive maintenance programs use six primary sensor types:  

### 1. **Vibration Sensors (Accelerometers)**  
- **Detect**: Bearing wear, misalignment, imbalance, looseness, gear wear, resonance.  
- **Technical Specs**: Piezoelectric (PZT) crystals for wide temperature range and dynamic bandwidth (20 kHz+).  
- **Equipment**: Motors, compressors, gearboxes, fans, conveyors.  

### 2. **Temperature Sensors (Thermocouples/RTDs)**  
- **Detect**: Overheating bearings, electrical overloads, thermal expansion, insulation degradation.  
- **Technical Specs**:  
  - Thermocouples: -200°C to +1,800°C, ±1-2°C accuracy.  
  - RTDs: -200°C to +850°C, ±0.1-0.5°C accuracy.  
- **Equipment**: HVAC systems, motors, hydraulic systems, data center cooling.  

### 3. **Current Sensors (Electrical Monitoring)**  
- **Detect**: Motor winding degradation, phase imbalance, overloading, belt slippage, cavitation.  
- **Technical Specs**: Clamp-on CTs for RMS current, power factor, harmonic distortion.  
- **Equipment**: Three-phase motors, UPS systems, refrigeration compressors.  

### 4. **Pressure Sensors (Transducers)**  
- **Detect**: Filter clogging, pump wear, leaks, valve malfunctions, cavitation.  
- **Technical Specs**: 0-30 PSI to 0-5,000 PSI range, ±0.25-1% accuracy.  
- **Equipment**: Hydraulic systems, steam systems, compressed air distribution.  

### 5. **Acoustic Sensors (Ultrasonic Detection)**  
- **Detect**: Compressed air leaks, steam trap failures, electrical arcing, bearing lubrication issues.  
- **Technical Specs**: Piezoelectric elements for 20-100 kHz frequencies.  
- **Equipment**: Compressed air systems, steam traps, high-voltage switchgear.  

### 6. **Humidity and Environmental Sensors**  
- **Detect**: Condensation risk, corrosion, air quality issues, HVAC deviations.  
- **Technical Specs**: ±2-3% humidity accuracy, ±0.3-0.5°C temperature accuracy.  
- **Equipment**: Data centers, electrical rooms, clean rooms, battery storage.  

## Sensor-to-CMMS Integration Architecture
The integration process involves five layers:  
1. **Sensor Hardware**: Measures conditions and converts to digital signals (e.g., accelerometers, thermocouples).  
2. **Wireless Communication**: Protocols like LoRaWAN, BLE, Wi-Fi, and Zigbee for data transmission.  
3. **Gateway Devices**: Perform edge computing for data aggregation, anomaly detection, and local alarming.  
4. **Condition Monitoring Platform**: Uses AI-driven analytics to detect anomalies and generate alerts.  
5. **CMMS Integration**: Automates work order creation, prioritization, and scheduling via API.  

## Implementation Roadmap: From Pilot to Enterprise Scale
### Phase 1: Strategy and Asset Selection  
Focus on high-criticality assets (e.g., primary chillers, fire pumps) with high failure rates and clear failure modes.  

### Phase 2: Sensor Selection and Procurement  
Match sensor types to failure modes (e.g., vibration for bearings, temperature for overheating).  

### Phase 3: Installation and Baseline Establishment  
Ensure proper sensor placement, baseline data collection (2-4 weeks), and network verification.  

### Phase 4: Alert Tuning and Threshold Optimization  
Refine thresholds to reduce false alarms (target <5%) using multi-parameter logic and rate-of-change alerts.  

### Phase 5: ROI Measurement and Business Case

- **Downtime reduction**: 30-50% with predictive maintenance.  
- **Cost savings**: 18-25% maintenance cost reduction.  
- **ROI**: 95% of adopters report positive ROI, with 27% achieving full amortization within one year (Gartner, 2024).
