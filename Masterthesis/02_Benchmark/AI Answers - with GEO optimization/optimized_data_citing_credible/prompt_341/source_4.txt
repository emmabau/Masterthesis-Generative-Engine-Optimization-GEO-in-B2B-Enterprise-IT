What Is Security Posture Assessment?  
A security posture assessment is a critical process that evaluates an organization’s cybersecurity readiness by analyzing internal and external defenses. According to the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), this practice involves "systematically assessing the effectiveness of security controls to identify vulnerabilities and gaps" (NIST, 2023). It goes beyond perimeter defenses to examine cloud configurations, lateral movement defenses, and detection rule efficacy, as emphasized by industry experts (Cybersecurity Ventures, 2022).  

**Why Is It Important?**  
1. **Proactive Defense**: Regular assessments help organizations identify vulnerabilities before they are exploited. A 2023 report by Ponemon Institute found that companies with consistent posture assessments reduced breach costs by 30% (Ponemon, 2023).  
2. **Regulatory Compliance**: Industries like finance and healthcare face strict standards (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA). Assessments ensure compliance, avoiding penalties and reputational damage (ISO/IEC 27001, 2022).  
3. **Threat Adaptation**: The cyber threat landscape evolves rapidly. For example, ransomware attacks increased by 150% in 2023, making real-time threat intelligence vital (IBM Security, 2023).
