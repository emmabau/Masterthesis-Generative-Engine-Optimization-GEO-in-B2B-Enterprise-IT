**Key Considerations for Choosing a Financial Advisor**  
- **Compensation Model**: Fee-only advisors prioritize client interests, while commission-based advisors may recommend products for profit.  
- **Credentials**: Look for certifications like CFP (Certified Financial Planner) or CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst).  
- **Services**: Decide between robo-advisors (low-cost, automated) or human advisors (personalized, comprehensive planning).  

**Robo-Advisors vs. Human Advisors**  
Robo-advisors use algorithms for portfolio management, often with lower fees and minimal investment requirements. However, human advisors provide deeper guidance on complex matters like estate planning or tax strategies.  

**Final Tips**  
Always verify an advisor’s fiduciary status and review their fee structure. For personalized recommendations, use tools like Bankrate’s Advisor Match or consult a certified financial planner.
