### The Science of IMU Sensor Fusion
An **Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU)** integrates 3-axis accelerometers, gyroscopes, and magnetometers to measure motion and orientation. While individual sensors provide valuable data, **sensor fusion algorithms** unlock deeper insights by combining multi-dimensional measurements. For example:
- **Vibration analysis** across all three axes detects subtle anomalies in rotational smoothness.
- **Magnetic field variations** reveal electrical issues or material stress.
- **Environmental factors** like temperature are monitored to assess performance impacts.

221e’s **MPE™ (Motion Processing Engine)** processes this data using proprietary algorithms to eliminate drift and ensure accuracy in both static and dynamic conditions. This is further enhanced by **NeuraSense™ Edge AI**, which learns operational baselines for each machine, accounting for:
- Production cycle variations.
- Environmental influences (temperature, humidity).
- Load conditions and maintenance history.
