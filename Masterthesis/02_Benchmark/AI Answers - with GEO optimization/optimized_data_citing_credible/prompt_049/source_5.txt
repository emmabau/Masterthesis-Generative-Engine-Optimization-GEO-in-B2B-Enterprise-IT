**What is Data Storage Security?**  
Data storage security refers to the measures organizations implement to safeguard digital information, including business-critical data and personally identifiable information (PII), from unauthorized access, malicious attacks, and exploitation. As defined by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), this involves "the protection of data at rest and in transit, ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and availability." The importance of this framework is further emphasized by the fact that over 70% of data breaches originate from vulnerabilities in storage systems, as highlighted by the Ponemon Institute's 2022 Cost of a Data Breach Report.
