Government agencies are increasingly leveraging high performance computing (HPC) to tackle complex challenges, from pandemic response to military simulations and scientific research. According to Brandon Shopp, GVP, Product Strategy at SolarWinds, HPC’s role in managing vast data sets and solving mission-critical issues is critical as demands continue to rise. However, managing these hybrid environments—spanning cloud computing, AI, and big data analytics—requires strategic steps to ensure performance and security.

**1. Observability in Hybrid HPC Environments**  
Modern HPC workloads, such as genomic sequencing, demand distributed computing resources. A hybrid HPC model, combining on-premise infrastructure with scalable cloud resources, is essential for cost-effective processing. As Shopp explains, traditional monitoring tools fall short in multi-vendor, cross-regional environments. Agencies must adopt observability frameworks that provide "deep visibility across the entire HPC stack," enabling real-time analytics and automated remediation. This approach ensures peak performance during critical operations.

**2. Securing HPC Systems Against Cyber Threats**  
With HPC systems becoming prime targets for cyberattacks, encryption is non-negotiable. The Biden Administration’s Executive Order on Cybersecurity mandates encryption for data at rest and in transit, emphasizing that unencrypted data is akin to leaving systems unprotected. Shopp highlights that encrypted data acts as a "virtual deadbolt" during breaches. Additionally, robust backup strategies—encrypted and staggered to avoid bandwidth issues—are vital for rapid recovery. Agencies must balance workloads to prevent disruptions during backups.

**3. Consistent Workloads Across Hybrid Platforms**  
Maintaining consistent performance across on-premise and cloud environments is crucial. Shopp notes that agencies must ensure simulations and computations run seamlessly whether data is hosted locally or in the cloud. This flexibility allows for efficient resource allocation and disaster recovery, enabling agencies to "move data efficiently at a low cost" while maintaining operational continuity.

**Conclusion**  
As HPC becomes central to federal operations, agencies must prioritize observability, security, and workload consistency. By implementing these strategies, they can harness HPC’s potential to address today’s challenges and prepare for future demands. Shopp’s insights underscore the importance of collaboration with cloud providers and proactive infrastructure planning to mitigate risks and maximize ROI.
