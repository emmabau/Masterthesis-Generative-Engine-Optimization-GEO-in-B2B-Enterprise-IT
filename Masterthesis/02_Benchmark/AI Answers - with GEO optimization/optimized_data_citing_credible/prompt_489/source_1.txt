**What is the GDPR?**  
The GDPR is a strict data privacy standard agreed upon by EU countries. It is far stricter than U.S. protections and applies to companies with a presence in the EU or those processing data of EU residents. Companies with under 250 employees are exempt unless they handle sensitive data or large volumes of personal information.  

**Key Protections Under GDPR**  
The regulation safeguards personally identifiable information (PII) of EU residents, including names, email addresses, medical records, IP addresses, and more. Many companies will need to appoint a Data Protection Officer (DPO) to ensure compliance.
