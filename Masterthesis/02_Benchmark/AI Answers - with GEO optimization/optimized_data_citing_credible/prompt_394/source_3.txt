Nutanix hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI) offers a transformative approach to securing complex IT environments, addressing critical gaps in traditional architectures. According to Barmonde, "Nutanix HCI creates a unified operating model with centralized security policy enforcement, eliminating misconfigurations and reducing attack surfaces."

Similarly, its integrated backup and disaster recovery solutions provide real-time protection, unlike siloed traditional systems that require complex configurations.
