The ECP, initiated in 2016 and extending through 2022, focuses on designing software infrastructure for next-generation supercomputers capable of exceeding 10¹⁸ operations per second. According to the DOE, this initiative aims to address intractable scientific challenges by developing a vertically integrated software stack across exascale and pre-exascale systems. The project, a collaboration between the Office of Science and the National Nuclear Security Administration, involves over 100 teams from national labs, universities, and industry, as noted in the ECP's RD&D activities.
