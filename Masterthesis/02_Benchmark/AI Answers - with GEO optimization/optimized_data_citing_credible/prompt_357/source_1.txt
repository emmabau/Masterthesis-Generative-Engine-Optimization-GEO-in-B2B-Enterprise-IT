**Why B2B Marketing ROI is Difficult to Measure**  
The complexity of B2B sales cycles—spanning 6-12 months or longer—makes it challenging to connect early-stage marketing efforts to eventual revenue. Additionally, the presence of multiple touchpoints (e.g., webinars, product demos, customer references) and offline interactions (trade shows, in-person meetings) further complicates attribution (Source).  

**Key Challenges**  
1. **Long Sales Cycles**: Buyers may engage with dozens of touchpoints before making a decision, making it hard to isolate marketing’s impact.  
2. **Data Silos**: Disconnected systems like CRM and marketing automation tools hinder accurate ROI tracking (Source).  
3. **Attribution Model Limitations**: Standard models like first-touch or last-touch oversimplify complex buyer journeys, missing critical influence points (Source).  

**Calculating B2B Marketing ROI**  
The basic formula is:  
**(Revenue Generated - Marketing Investment) / Marketing Investment × 100**.  
However, B2B ROI requires granular tracking of fixed and variable costs across channels, including team compensation, content production, and operational overhead (Source).
