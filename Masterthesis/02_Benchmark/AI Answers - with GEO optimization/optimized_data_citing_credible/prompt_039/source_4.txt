**Why Businesses Might Consider Alternatives to Datto**  
While Datto’s solutions are praised for their reliability, several limitations have prompted organizations to explore alternatives. For instance, Datto’s pricing model—primarily subscription-based with hardware dependencies—can be costly for small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs). As noted in a G2 review, "Datto’s pricing may be unaffordable for organizations optimizing IT budgets." Additionally, its limited customization options and scalability challenges for growing enterprises further drive the search for alternatives.

**Top Datto Alternatives in 2025**  
1. **Axcient**  
   Axcient’s chain-free technology eliminates traditional backup dependencies, enabling faster recovery and reduced storage costs. Its cloud-based model and 14-day free trial (no credit card required) make it appealing for MSPs and SMBs. A user on Capterra praised its "Linux-based appliance, no Windows license required," while noting the need for careful hardware sizing.  

2. **Bacula Enterprise**  
   Bacula stands out for its enterprise-grade security, including AES-256 encryption and immutable backups. Its flat-rate licensing model allows scaling without storage cost increases, making it ideal for large organizations. A Gartner review highlighted its "platform and storage independence," with users praising its "world-class support" and "exceptional deduplication method."  

3. **Druva Data Resiliency Cloud**  
   Druva’s cloud-native approach offers pay-as-you-go pricing and AI-driven ransomware detection. Its immutability feature ensures data protection against unauthorized modifications. However, its on-premises support is limited, making it less suitable for businesses reliant on local storage.  

4. **Unitrends**  
   Unitrends combines hardware, software, and cloud infrastructures for hybrid environments, with AI-powered ransomware protection. While praised for its "all-in-one backup appliance," some users noted outdated dashboards and limited third-party integrations.  

5. **CrashPlan for Endpoints**  
   CrashPlan’s real-time backup and unlimited versioning make it ideal for remote teams. Its 256-bit AES encryption and cross-platform support (Windows, macOS, Linux) ensure robust security. However, its lack of full-system imaging and slow initial backups may deter enterprises requiring comprehensive disaster recovery.  

6. **Acronis Cyber Protect**  
   Acronis’ AI-driven threat detection and unified backup-security platform cater to SMBs and MSPs. Its "one-click recovery" and cloud-based flexibility are highlighted in G2 reviews, though higher-tier plans are needed for advanced features like forensic analysis.
