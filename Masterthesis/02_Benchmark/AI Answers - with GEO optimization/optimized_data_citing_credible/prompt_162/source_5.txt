**Key Considerations for Enterprise Data Backup**  
A reliable backup solution should include:  
1. **Data Redundancy**: Multiple copies stored across geographically dispersed locations.  
2. **Encryption**: Protecting data at rest and in transit to prevent unauthorized access.  
3. **Scheduled Backups**: Automated, frequent backups ensure data is always up-to-date.  
4. **Scalability**: Cloud-based solutions offer flexibility to handle growing data volumes.  
5. **Monitoring & Recovery Plans**: Real-time insights and tested restoration protocols minimize downtime.
