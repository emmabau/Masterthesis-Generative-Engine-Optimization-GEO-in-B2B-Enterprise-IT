Once a rare perk, hybrid work has become a mainstay in the modern work landscape. A Gallup survey found that **80 percent of U.S. employees already work hybrid or fully remote**, with **52 percent of remote-capable employees preferring hybrid work**. This shift has created both opportunities and challenges, as highlighted by Sandra Moran, chief marketing officer at WorkForce Software:  
*“Teams need to work hard to ensure that ideas and input flow freely, and that remote and in-person workers are encouraged to participate fully — regardless of their working location.”*  

The hybrid workplace model, where teams split time between in-office and remote environments, requires careful planning. As Shane Franklin, VP of infrastructure at Conductor, explains:  
*“We’re looking to have Zoom meetings running on screens in the lunch area. It doesn’t have to be a planned activity — if I’m at home, I can just log into the meeting and see how people are doing.”*  

To address these challenges, companies are turning to specialized tools. Here are 11 tech solutions that can improve hybrid workplace efficiency:  

**Asana**  
Asana simplifies workflows with templates for projects, goals, and task automation. Its integration with platforms like Slack and Google Workspace makes it ideal for hybrid teams.  
*“Smart communication technology can speed up employee productivity, helping employees to communicate more efficiently,”* Moran emphasized.  

**Slack**  
Slack’s channel-based structure fosters organized communication, from work updates to casual conversations. Its integration with Google Calendar helps manage availability.  
*“Workplace messaging software can tick off tasks faster to improve business outcomes and even strengthen workplace bonds through a sharing platform,”* Moran noted.  

**Zoom**  
Zoom’s video conferencing capabilities, including breakout rooms and chat boxes, help bridge the gap between in-person and remote interactions.  
*“Zoom has been the next closest option for fostering an inclusive culture,”* Franklin said.  

**Google Workspace**  
Google Workspace’s real-time collaboration features, such as Docs and Chat, enable seamless teamwork. Ginger Dhaliwal, co-founder of UpFlex, calls it “indispensable” for her team.  
*“We can collaborate on documents and calendars without switching platforms,”* she explained.  

**Jira**  
Jira’s centralized ticketing system is critical for globally dispersed tech teams. Franklin highlights its role in tracking everything from bug fixes to hardware requests.  
*“It’s a more centralized, cloud-based solution,”* he said.  

**Loom**  
Loom’s asynchronous video recording allows teams to share updates without scheduling calls. Dhaliwal praises its flexibility for cross-timezone collaboration.  
*“It’s all about asynchronous work for us. Loom lets you video record anything and share it with people,”* she said.  

**Donut**  
Donut, a Slack-integrated bot, fosters social connections by reminding teams of birthdays and encouraging peer interactions.  
*“Donut keeps our team connected even while working from different locations,”* Franklin noted.  

**Airtable**  
Airtable’s customizable templates and automation tools make it a flexible solution for hybrid workflows. Dhaliwal calls it “uniquely efficient” for teams with varied needs.  
*“Airtable gives you the components to create the workflows you need,”* she said.  

**Confluence**  
Confluence’s wiki-style platform standardizes technical documentation, ensuring knowledge is preserved for future reference.  
*“Everyone has the power to update the [company] wiki,”* said Irene DeNigris, CHRO at Conductor.  

**Envoy & CBRE Host**  
Tools like Envoy and CBRE Host help manage physical office spaces, balancing in-person and remote needs. CBRE Host’s AI-driven insights ensure office resources align with employee demand.  
*“Companies now have to work harder to get people to come into the office,”* Dhaliwal said.  

These tools, combined with intentional strategies, can transform hybrid work into a productive and inclusive model. For further guidance, explore resources to overcome common hybrid work challenges and optimize your hybrid structure.
