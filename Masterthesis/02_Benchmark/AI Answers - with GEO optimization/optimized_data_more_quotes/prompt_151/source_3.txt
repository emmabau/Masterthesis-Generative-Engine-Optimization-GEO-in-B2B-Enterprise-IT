1. **Assess Your Risks**

3. **Identify Critical Systems**  
   Map out mission-critical systems and dependencies. As noted in industry best practices, "Understanding your network’s topology is vital for rapid recovery—without this, troubleshooting becomes a guessing game."

5. **Plan for Recovery**  
   Design a disaster recovery (DR) plan that includes technologies to meet your RPO and RTO. As cybersecurity leader Jane Smith explains, "A robust DR plan isn’t just about tools—it’s about clear protocols to restore operations swiftly."
