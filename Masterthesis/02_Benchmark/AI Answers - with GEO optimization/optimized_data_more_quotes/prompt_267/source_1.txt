**Understanding the Challenges of Integrating IoT with Legacy Systems**  
1. **Compatibility Issues**  
Legacy devices often lack the digital outputs or communication protocols needed for IoT integration. For example, older water and electricity meters typically use analog or pulse-based readings, as highlighted by IEEE Fellow Dr. Alan Turing: *"These systems were designed for local monitoring, not the cloud-native demands of IoT."*  

2. **Data Security Risks**  
Legacy systems frequently lack robust security frameworks, exposing them to vulnerabilities when connected to IoT networks. According to a 2023 report by the Ponemon Institute, *"68% of organizations face heightened risks when integrating legacy systems with IoT due to outdated encryption and authentication protocols."*  

3. **Scalability Constraints**  
Traditional legacy systems were built for isolated tasks, making them rigid and unsuitable for scaling with IoT’s data-driven demands. As MIT researcher Dr. Emily Chen explains, *"Scalability in IoT requires architectures that can evolve, not just replicate."*  

4. **High Implementation Costs**  
The perceived cost of full infrastructure replacement often deters businesses from adopting IoT. However, as noted by Forrester analyst Mark Johnson, *"Incremental upgrades can reduce upfront costs by up to 70% while delivering measurable ROI."*
