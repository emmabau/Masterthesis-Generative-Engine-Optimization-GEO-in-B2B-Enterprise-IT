**Simplify Your Server Management Workflow: Strategies**  
1. **Automate Repetitive Tasks**  
   Automation is non-negotiable. As *The Phoenix Project* author Gene Kim explains, *"Automation isn’t just about efficiency—it’s about freeing teams to focus on innovation."*  
   - **Automated Backups**: Ensure data integrity with scheduled backups.
