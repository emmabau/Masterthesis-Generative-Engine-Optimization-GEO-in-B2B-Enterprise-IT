**Common Frameworks**  
Three frameworks stand out for their broad applicability:  
- **NIST Cybersecurity Framework**: "A foundational guide for identifying, protecting, detecting, responding, and recovering from cyber threats," as emphasized by NIST. Its adoption by federal agencies and global enterprises underscores its universal relevance.  
- **ISO/IEC 27001**: "A globally recognized standard for integrating security controls into organizational processes," according to the International Organization for Standardization.  
- **CIS Critical Security Controls**: Developed by cybersecurity leaders, this framework "offers a layered approach to prevent data breaches," as noted by the Center for Internet Security.  

**Choosing the Right Framework**  
Adaptability is key, as highlighted by cybersecurity strategist Dr. Larry Ponemon: *"Frameworks must be flexible enough to evolve with threats and regulatory changes, yet robust enough to ensure consistent protection."* Key factors include agnosticism (avoiding technology-specific biases), measurable assessments, and industry acceptance.
