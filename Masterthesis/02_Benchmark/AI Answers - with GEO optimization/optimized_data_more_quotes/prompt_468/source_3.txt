### Key Regulatory Frameworks in Cybersecurity
Understanding and adhering to key frameworks like **HIPAA**, **PCI-DSS**, and **GDPR** is foundational to cybersecurity compliance. These regulations set the gold standard for data protection and operational security:
- **HIPAA** (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) enforces stringent controls over healthcare data, as emphasized by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services: **"HIPAA ensures that sensitive health information is protected through administrative, physical, and technical safeguards."**
- **PCI-DSS** (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard) mandates robust protections for payment card data, with the PCI Security Standards Council noting: **"PCI-DSS is the global standard for securing cardholder data, reducing fraud, and building consumer trust."**
- **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation) imposes strict requirements for data privacy in the EU, with the European Data Protection Board stating: **"GDPR empowers individuals with control over their personal data, fostering trust in digital services."**
