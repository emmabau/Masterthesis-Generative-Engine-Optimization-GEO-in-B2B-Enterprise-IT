**The Evolution of IT Infrastructure**  
Before HCI, traditional IT relied on the 3-2-1 architecture—virtual machines (VMs) running on clustered servers, connected via two network switches and backed by shared storage. This led to the rise of hyperconverged infrastructure, which integrates compute, storage, and networking into a single, unified system.

| Traditional IT Infrastructure | Hyperconverged Infrastructure |
