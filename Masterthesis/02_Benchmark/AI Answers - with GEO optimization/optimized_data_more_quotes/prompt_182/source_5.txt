**Backup & Disaster Recovery (3-2-1 Rule):**  
- **Three copies** of data  
- **Two storage media types**  
- **One offsite copy**  
- **Test full restoration quarterly** (IBM Cost of a Data Breach Report 2025: Organizations with tested plans saved $2.66M per breach).  

**Server Hardening Controls (NIST SP 800-123, CIS Benchmarks):**  
1. **Disable unused services/ports** (Reduces attack surface)  
2. **Enforce least privilege** (Separate admin accounts; no shared logins)  
3. **Host firewall with default-deny posture** (Allow-list only required traffic)  
4. **Disk encryption at rest** (BitLocker, LUKS)  
5. **Centralized audit logging** (90-day retention floor)  
6. **Secure remote administration** (MFA, bastion hosts, no direct RDP/SSH from internet)  

**Monitoring Essentials (2026):**  
- Track **CPU, memory, disk I/O, network throughput, service uptime, and event logs** continuously.  
- Set thresholds: **Warn at 70%, alert at 85%, critical at 95%**.  
- **Log all authentication attempts** and failed logins.  
- **Audit permission changes** and unauthorized access.
