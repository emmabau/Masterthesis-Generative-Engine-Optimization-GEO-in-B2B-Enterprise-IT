**What Is a Virtual Machine?**  
A VM operates as a standalone computer with allocated resources, managed by a hypervisor—a critical component described by Microsoft's Chief Architect, Raj Patel, as "the backbone of modern virtualized environments." Hypervisors ensure isolation between VMs, preventing errors from one instance from affecting others.
