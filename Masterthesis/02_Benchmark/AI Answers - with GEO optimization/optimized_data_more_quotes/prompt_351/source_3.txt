This vision underpins the ECP’s mission to deliver a capable exascale ecosystem, ensuring the U.S. maintains its leadership in science, security, and economic competitiveness.

Exascale computing is critical to national security, enabling next-generation simulation tools for nuclear stockpile stewardship. These systems also enhance readiness against hostile threats, ensuring the U.S. remains a leader in defense innovation.
