As co-host of the Auto Supply Chain Prophets podcast, I’ve had the privilege of interviewing industry leaders who consistently emphasize the urgent need to transform automotive supplier relationships. A recurring theme is the imperative to move beyond the “us versus them” mindset of the past century, as highlighted by **Kate Vitasek** in Episode 92: “We can’t run the automotive industry like we have done for the past 100 years.” This shift is driven by five critical factors: **consumer demand for innovation**, **technological disruption**, **supply chain fragility**, **labor shortages**, and **emerging competitors**.  

The Top 5 Rules for Enhanced Collaboration, as outlined by our guests, provide a roadmap for this transformation:  

1. **Establish Win-Win Opportunities**  
   As **Kate Vitasek** explains, “You don’t change culture by simply declaring it; you change it one relationship at a time.” Shifting from transactional interactions to collaborative, total cost of ownership models requires transparency, shared success, and co-created solutions. This mindset ensures that both OEMs and suppliers benefit from innovation and efficiency.  

2. **Prioritize Communication**  
   **Jenni Smith** (Episode 90) warns that “silence is costly” in supply chains. Delays or vague updates lead to scrambling teams, making constant, rapid communication essential. OEMs that grant tier-two suppliers visibility into manufacturing calls and changes enable faster adjustments and deeper data understanding, as she emphasizes: “You can never over-communicate.”  

3. **Cultivate Strong Relationships**  
   **Dr. Angela Johnson** (Episode 86) underscores that strong relationships “reduce costs, boost efficiency, and drive better financial outcomes.” Historical Working Relationship Index (WRI) data shows a strong positive correlation between relationship scores and year-end financial results, proving that trust and collaboration are non-negotiable for profitability.  

4. **Implement Solutions for Timely Decisions**  
   **Achim Gatternig** (Episode 80) stresses that “guessing won’t suffice” in a rapidly changing world. Investing in Supplier Relationship Management (SRM) tools provides transparency, enabling timely decisions that save money and improve product quality. As he states, “Delaying decisions means lost opportunities.”  

5. **Build Trust**  
   **Guillermo Cano** (Episode 76) shares a powerful anecdote: “Early engagement and collaboration, even through aggressive timelines and challenges like COVID, allowed our team to produce parts on time, with the right quality, and meet cost and weight targets.” Trust, transparency, and a win-win mindset are the foundation for overcoming obstacles and achieving exceptional results.
