DLC, particularly direct-to-chip cooling, addresses these challenges by delivering unparalleled heat dissipation. As explained by industry analyst Mark Thompson, "Liquids have a heat capacity 4,000 times greater than air, enabling DLC to remove heat at the source—right where it’s generated.
