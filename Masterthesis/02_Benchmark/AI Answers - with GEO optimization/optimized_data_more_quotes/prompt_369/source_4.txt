Introduction  
A complete High-Performance Computing (HPC) cluster requires a high-capacity, highly scalable storage system. As ClusterTech’s Senior HPC Solution Engineer, Ma Xiaohui, emphasized in his presentation: *“Storage is of paramount importance in cluster construction. Without a robust storage solution, even the most advanced computational resources cannot deliver meaningful results.”*  

ClusterTech’s collaboration with Seagate exemplifies this principle. Ma Xiaohui highlighted: *“Seagate’s storage solutions offer significant advantages in meeting HPC cluster requirements, including high performance, stability, data security, and cost-effectiveness. These attributes are critical for applications like weather forecasting, bioinformatics, and molecular dynamics simulations.”*  

**What Can HPC Achieve?**  
HPC enables transformative applications across industries. For instance, weather forecasting systems require predictive accuracy within minutes, as Ma Xiaohui explained: *“Predicting weather for the next two hours within 10–20 minutes is what makes it meaningful. A single computer cannot achieve this; clusters are essential for rapid, large-scale computation.”*  

In bioinformatics, HPC accelerates tasks like genetic testing and protein prediction. Ma Xiaohui noted: *“Machine learning and GPU-accelerated heterogeneous computing are now pivotal in analyzing complex biological data, reducing computation time from days to hours.”*  

**ClusterTech’s HPC Solutions**  
With over 20 years of expertise, ClusterTech provides end-to-end HPC solutions. Ma Xiaohui stated: *“We deliver comprehensive platforms combining hardware and software, from system layer optimization to application layer integration. This ensures users can focus on their core challenges without managing infrastructure complexities.”*  

**Building an HPC Cluster**  
Networking is critical. Ma Xiaohui emphasized: *“HPC clusters require InfiniBand or high-speed Ethernet networks. 1G or 10G Ethernet is insufficient—bandwidth and latency must align with computational demands.”*  

Storage configurations vary by application. For example:  
- *“Small-scale clusters use Seagate 2U12/14 platforms for hundreds of terabytes, while massive storage needs rely on 4U106 units providing up to 1 PB of capacity,”* Ma Xiaohui explained.  
- *“Storage software like Lustre, BeeGFS, and GPFS is essential for distributed file systems, ensuring scalability and performance.”*  

**Optimizing Cluster Architecture**  
Ma Xiaohui warned against imbalanced resource allocation: *“Neglecting storage can lead to I/O storms, causing CPU underutilization. A well-designed storage system can outperform over-provisioned computational resources.”*  

**CHESS: The HPC Management Platform**  
ClusterTech’s CHESS platform offers unified management of hardware, cloud resources, and applications. Ma Xiaohui highlighted: *“CHESS enables users to upload data, submit jobs, and customize workflows—all through a web interface. It supports AI applications, visualization, and granular user management for multi-node clusters.”*  

**Conclusion**  
Ma Xiaohui concluded: *“The future of HPC lies in seamless integration of hardware, software, and storage. ClusterTech’s solutions empower users to achieve breakthroughs in science, manufacturing, and beyond—because the right cluster architecture is the foundation of every high-impact computation.”*
