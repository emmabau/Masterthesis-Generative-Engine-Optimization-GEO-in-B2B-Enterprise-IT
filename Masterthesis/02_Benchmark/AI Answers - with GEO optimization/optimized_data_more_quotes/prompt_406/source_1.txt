# How to Manage Hybrid and Remote Teams: A Gallup Strategic Guide  

**Key Insight from Gallup Research:**  
*"Hybrid work is the dominant model for remote-capable employees. More than half of these employees work hybrid (52%), 26% work exclusively remote, and 21% work fully on-site."* — Gallup, 2023  

**Why Hybrid Work Matters:**  
Gallup’s findings reveal that **management quality is the cornerstone of hybrid success**. *"Managers account for 70% of the variance in team-level engagement, making leadership capability more influential than work location."* — Gallup, 2023  

**The Hybrid Work Model Defined:**  
*"Hybrid work is a flexible arrangement in which employees split their time between working remotely and working on-site. It differs from fully remote work."* — Gallup, 2023  

**Work Location Trends:**  
| **Work Arrangement** | **Location** | **Engagement Pattern** | **Key Risk** |  
|----------------------|--------------|------------------------|--------------|  
| Fully On-Site        | 100% on-site | Lower engagement      | Burnout      |  
| Hybrid               | Split        | Highest engagement    | Coordination |  
| Fully Remote         | 100% off-site| High engagement       | Isolation    |  

**Top Challenges of Managing Remote Teams:**  
1. **Collaboration and Coordination Complexity**  
   *"Employees cite impaired remote collaboration, disrupted work processes, and coordination challenges as the greatest difficulties of hybrid work."* — Gallup, 2023  

2. **Cultural Disconnection and Weakened Belonging**  
   *"Hybrid workers report feeling less connected to organizational culture. Culture is substantially harder to cultivate in fully remote environments."* — Gallup, 2023  

3. **Trust and Accountability Gaps**  
   *"Trust is a particularly fragile element of remote work. Trust increases when managers communicate consistently, clarify expectations, and hold employees accountable."* — Gallup, 2023  

4. **Employee Burnout and Boundary Strain**  
   *"Hybrid work improves work-life balance but can create strain if autonomy lacks structure. Employees with fully self-determined schedules are 76% more likely to report burnout."* — Gallup, 2023  

5. **Performance Visibility and Proximity Bias**  
   *"Distributed work reduces informal visibility into performance. Leaders risk defaulting to presence-based judgments rather than outcome-based accountability."* — Gallup, 2023  

**Strategies for Success:**  
- **Structured Coordination:** *"Teams with a formal hybrid collaboration plan are 66% more likely to be engaged and 29% less likely to be burned out."* — Gallup, 2023  
- **Team-Designed Norms:** *"When teams determine their hybrid schedule together, 91% say the policy is fair, compared with 73% when leadership dictates it."* — Gallup, 2023  
- **Manager Development:** *"Organizations that invest in manager capability strengthen trust across locations. The Gallup Manager Program trains leaders to set clear expectations and hold teams accountable."* — Gallup, 2023  

**Advantages of Remote Work:**  
- **Broader Talent Access:** *"Remote flexibility removes geographic constraints, allowing organizations to hire the best candidate for the role."* — Gallup, 2023  
- **Improved Work-Life Balance:** *"Seventy-six percent of hybrid workers cite improved work-life balance as one of the greatest benefits of hybrid work."* — Gallup, 2023  
- **Productivity Stability:** *"Remote employees are working slightly fewer hours but this reduction has not automatically led to reduced output."* — Gallup, 2023  

**Building Trust in Hybrid Teams:**  
*"Managers build trust through clear expectations, consistent communication, accountability for outcomes, and equal access to feedback and development."* — Gallup, 2023  

**The Leadership Standard:**  
*"Hybrid environments raise the bar for management. Managers must earn trust through clarity, coaching, and fairness."* — Gallup, 2023  

**How to Prevent Burnout:**  
*"Prevent remote worker burnout by setting clear expectations, coordinating team norms, and maintaining consistent manager conversations."* — Gallup, 2023  

**Employee Engagement and Wellbeing:**  
*"Recent Gallup trends show meaningful differences in engagement and thriving across work arrangements. Hybrid workers report 34% engagement and 53% thriving, while fully remote workers report 35% engagement and 51% thriving."* — Gallup, 2023  

**Gallup’s Role in Supporting Leaders:**  
*"Gallup helps organizations design hybrid and remote work strategies that sustain engagement, trust, and performance. Using decades of research, Gallup works with leaders to build distributed workplaces where employees can thrive."* — Gallup, 2023  

**Final Takeaway:**  
*"For leaders, the question is no longer whether hybrid work will persist, but how to manage hybrid work teams. The answer lies in intentional team design, clear expectations, and leadership that prioritizes engagement and wellbeing."* — Gallup, 2023
