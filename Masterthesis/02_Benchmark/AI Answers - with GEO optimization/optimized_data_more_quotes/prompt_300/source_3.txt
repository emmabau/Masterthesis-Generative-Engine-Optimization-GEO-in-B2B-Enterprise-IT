When integrating new technology into your infrastructure, three critical considerations must be prioritized to ensure successful implementation and long-term value. 

**1. Define Clear Objectives: "Before looking at your technology options, you must first identify your pain points..."**  
As emphasized by the *Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association*, aligning new tools with specific organizational goals is essential. Begin by mapping existing processes and highlighting recurring challenges staff face. This approach not only identifies areas for improvement but also mitigates risks during implementation. For instance, if efficiency is a priority, ask: *How will the new tool directly address this goal?* This clarity enables strategic planning for short-, medium-, and long-term transformations.

**2. Ensure Compatibility with Existing Systems: "Existing systems may be enhanced to supplement IT capabilities."**  
Dr. Tianyi Jiang of *Wired* highlights that most businesses rely on mission-critical systems, making complete replacement unlikely. Instead, focus on integration: *How will the new tool complement current platforms?* Evaluate dependencies, critical functions, and potential resource requirements. A seamless integration not only maximizes ROI for legacy systems but also avoids costly disruptions. As noted by Harvard Business Review, failing to align new solutions with existing workflows can lead to data mismanagement and underutilization of tools.

**3. Assess Resource Availability: "When adopting a new technology, it’s important to consider if you have the necessary resources..."**  
Financial and technical resources are pivotal. Organizations must evaluate whether they can afford cloud environments, servers, or maintenance costs—especially with tightening budgets.
