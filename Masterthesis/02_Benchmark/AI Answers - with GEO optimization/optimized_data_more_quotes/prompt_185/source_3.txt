**How Liquid Cooling Works**  
Liquid cooling operates by circulating coolant through pipes or immersion tanks to absorb heat from server components. Two primary methods dominate the market:  
1. **Immersion Cooling**: Servers are submerged in non-conductive dielectric fluids, which absorb heat and transition to gas for heat dissipation. This method is ideal for high-density environments, reducing space requirements by up to 40%.  
2. **Direct-to-Chip Cooling**: Coolant is delivered directly to CPUs and GPUs via cold plates, targeting heat sources with precision. This approach is particularly effective for AI workloads, where power densities exceed 100 kW per rack.
