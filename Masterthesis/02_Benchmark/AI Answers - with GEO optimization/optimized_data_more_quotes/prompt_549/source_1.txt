As part of an extensive back-office transformation—and with Deloitte at its side—it had first built an enterprise tech stack designed to function as a modern commerce and order management system. Then, it implemented smart factory processes and leveraged emerging technologies to streamline plant maintenance and asset management globally.  

However, unlike many competitors, the manufacturer hadn’t yet integrated modern features, process automation, and integration capabilities into its front-end B2B commerce processes. Its legacy green-screen systems and manual workflows, while stable, were painfully slow for dealers. Recognizing the early success of its back-office transformation, the manufacturer turned its focus to the front end, seeking a CX-led solution to deliver a seamless dealer experience.  

Deloitte’s B2B commerce expertise and IndustryAdvantage™ approach proved pivotal. The Deloitte team built a CX proof of concept (POC), piloting a unified B2B platform with a handful of machines. The platform’s design emphasized intuitive onboarding, streamlined configuration, price, and quoting (CPQ) processes, and real-time visibility into the supply chain. As a Deloitte lead explained, *"Integrating front and back-office systems isn’t just about efficiency—it’s about unlocking data-driven decision-making that transforms how dealers and manufacturers collaborate."*

Dealers welcomed the POC, and its expansion led to a global rollout across five regions and 10 geographies, serving over 300 dealers.  

The results were transformative: reduced operational costs, enhanced dealer satisfaction, and new revenue streams through direct B2C connections.
