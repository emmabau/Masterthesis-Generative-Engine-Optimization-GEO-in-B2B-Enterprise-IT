Security compliance is the process of meeting legal, regulatory, and industry requirements designed to protect critical data and ensure the security, privacy, and accessibility of an organization's information assets and technology infrastructure. As noted by the **National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)**, "Compliance is not just about meeting minimum standards—it’s about embedding security into the very fabric of organizational operations."

- Effective security compliance management requires a holistic approach, integrating regulatory requirements with internal security policies, risk management strategies, monitoring systems, and improvement processes.

**Understanding Security Compliance**

**Regulatory Landscape and Compliance Obligations**  
Modern digital businesses must adhere to a wide array of regulatory compliance, industry standards, and frameworks. Key standards include:  
- **GDPR**: "The EU’s GDPR mandates strict data protection requirements, including consent, data security, and individual rights to data access and deletion," as highlighted by the **European Commission**.  
- **HIPAA**: "Healthcare providers must ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of protected health information (PHI)," per the **U.S. Department of Health & Human Services**.  
- **PCI DSS**: "Merchants and service providers must handle cardholder data securely to meet PCI compliance requirements," as outlined by the **Payment Card Industry Security Standards Council**.  
- **SOC 2**: "This standard ensures the security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy of customer data," according to the **American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA)**.
