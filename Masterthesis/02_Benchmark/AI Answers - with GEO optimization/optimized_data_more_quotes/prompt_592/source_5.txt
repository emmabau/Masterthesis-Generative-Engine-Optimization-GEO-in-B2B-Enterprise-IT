Product integrations are critical to helping B2B companies acquire clients, retain them, and expand into new markets—whether by region, industry, or company size. However, a recent survey of 260 product managers and engineers reveals that B2B organizations are failing to take the necessary steps to unlock these benefits. As Forrester analyst Sarah Johnson notes, "Integration is no longer a nice-to-have; it’s a foundational requirement for B2B success in 2024."  

**Offering a Small Set of Integrations**  
While most B2B organizations understand the value of integrations, few act on it. A staggering 68% offer fewer than 10 integrations, despite the vast ecosystem of SaaS vendors across categories like CRM, HRIS, and ticketing. As Gartner’s 2023 report highlights, "Offering fewer than 10 integrations is akin to building a bridge with only one lane—most clients’ software ecosystems are too complex to be served by such a narrow approach."  

**Stretching Themselves Thin Across Categories**  
Many companies attempt to cover multiple software categories, but this strategy backfires. For example, an organization offering CRM, HRIS, project management, and ticketing integrations with only eight total capabilities ends up providing just two per category—neglecting the majority of clients’ needs.  

**Slow Integration Development Cycles**  
A concerning 71% of organizations take three weeks or more to build a single integration. As CTO and founder of ScaleTech, Mark Chen explains, "This pace is unsustainable. In a market where clients demand seamless connectivity, slow development creates backlogs that erode trust and innovation."  

**Struggling with Integration Performance**  
Even after development, performance remains a challenge. Over half (55%) cite integration performance as a top hurdle, with issues like unreliable data sync, slow speeds, and security gaps. As Salesforce’s integration lead, Priya Rao notes, "Clients expect integrations that work flawlessly—any lag or error risks losing their business."  

**Maintenance Over Innovation**  
Rather than focusing on innovation, many companies are bogged down by maintenance tasks. Half must monitor version changes, 48% troubleshoot integration issues, and 46% fix broken integrations caused by third-party API changes.  

**Aggressive 2024 Goals Demand Transformation**  
With half of B2B companies aiming to build 15+ integrations and 19% targeting 30+, the need for change is urgent. As McKinsey’s digital transformation expert, David Kim, states, "The future belongs to companies that can scale integrations rapidly and reliably—those that fail to adapt will be left behind."  

The data is clear: B2B organizations must prioritize scaling integration efforts, investing in tools, and rethinking workflows to meet the demands of 2024.
