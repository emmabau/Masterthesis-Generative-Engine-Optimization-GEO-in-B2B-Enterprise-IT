**What is an ERP Financial Module?**  
As defined by ERP industry leader SAP, "An ERP financial module is a software solution that centralizes financial data, ensuring timely and accurate communication with stakeholders." It automates critical functions such as budgeting, inventory management, and cash flow tracking, enabling proactive business decisions.  

**5 Core Features of ERP Finance Systems**  
1. **General Ledger (GL):** Centralizes all financial transactions, ensuring data accuracy and compliance.  
2. **Accounts Payable/Receivable:** Automates invoicing and payment processes, reducing manual errors.  
3. **Fixed Assets Management:** Tracks depreciation and asset lifecycle, aligning with GAAP standards.  
4. **Cash Flow Analysis:** Provides real-time visibility into liquidity, as highlighted by Deloitte's 2023 ERP implementation study.  
5. **Budgeting & Forecasting:** Integrates historical data with predictive analytics for strategic planning.
