**8 Best Practices for Data Backup**

According to the IDC, *"93% of organizations that experienced a ransomware attack recovered faster with offsite backups."* Cloud storage or remote services are ideal, but must align with security and compliance standards.  

3. **Use Cloud Backup with Intelligence**

4. **Choosing the Right Backup Solutions**
