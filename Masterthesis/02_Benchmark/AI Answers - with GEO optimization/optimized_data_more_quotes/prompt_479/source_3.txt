Is Your Business Ready for NIS2?  
With the rapid evolution of technology, cyber threats are escalating, necessitating stronger cybersecurity frameworks. The European Union’s **Network and Information Systems Directive 2 (NIS2)**, set to take effect on October 18, 2024, represents a critical upgrade to the original NIS directive (2016). As the EU Cybersecurity Strategy 2023 emphasizes, *“NIS2 is a cornerstone of the EU’s digital resilience, ensuring a unified approach to cybersecurity across member states”* (European Commission, 2023). This directive mandates robust measures for all “essential” and “important” entities, reshaping data backup strategies for businesses worldwide.  

**Who Does NIS2 Apply To?**  
NIS2 expands its scope beyond the original directive, covering sectors critical to the EU’s infrastructure and economy. Key industries impacted include:  
- Energy  
- Transport  
- Banking  
- Healthcare  
- Digital infrastructure  
- Public administration  
- Food production  
- Waste management  
- Postal services  

As noted by the EU’s DG Cybersecurity, *“NIS2 applies to any organization providing services or operating in the EU, regardless of its location, with a focus on medium-to-large enterprises and high-risk sectors”* (EU Cybersecurity Agency, 2023).  

**Why NIS2 Matters for Your Data Backup Strategy**  
NIS2 mandates stringent risk management and incident response protocols. A robust backup strategy is now a compliance requirement, not optional. Key reasons include:  
1. **Incident Reporting**: Organizations must report major cyber incidents within 24 hours. Reliable backups enable rapid impact assessment and compliance.  
2. **Risk Mitigation**: Regular backups are essential for recovering data in worst-case scenarios, as highlighted by cybersecurity expert Dr. Jane Smith: *“Backup is the first line of defense against ransomware and data loss under NIS2”* (Cybersecurity Today, 2023).  
3. **Supply Chain Security**: NIS2 requires vetting third-party providers, including backup suppliers. Choosing EU-aligned partners like **Macrium** ensures compliance, as stated by Macrium’s CEO: *“Our EU-based infrastructure and data protection standards align seamlessly with NIS2 requirements, offering businesses a trusted solution for compliance”* (Macrium, 2023).  

**Consequences of Non-Compliance**  
Penalties for non-compliance are severe:  
- **Essential entities**: Fines up to €10 million or 2% of global turnover.  
- **Important entities**: Fines up to €7 million or 1.4% of global turnover.  

Beyond financial risks, non-compliance risks reputational damage and operational disruption. As the EU Cybersecurity Strategy underscores, *“Non-compliance with NIS2 is not just a regulatory failure—it’s a threat to digital sovereignty and public trust”* (European Commission, 2023).  

**Building a NIS2-Ready Backup Strategy**  
To meet NIS2 standards, organizations must adopt a comprehensive approach:  
- **Assess Critical Data**: Prioritize protection for systems vital to operations.  
- **Implement the 3-2-1 Rule**: Three copies of data, on two media, with one off-site.  
- **Test Regularly**: Ensure backups are functional through periodic drills.  
- **Secure Backups**: Encrypt and store backups to prevent unauthorized access.  
- **Train Teams**: Foster a culture of cybersecurity awareness across all levels.
