Hyperconvergence (HCI) addresses critical challenges in modern IT infrastructure, as emphasized by Gartner: "HCI is a cornerstone of digital transformation, enabling organizations to meet the demands of big data, AI, and hybrid cloud environments while simplifying complex legacy systems."
