**What Is an Asset Management Plan (AMP)?**  
An AMP is a strategic blueprint designed to manage a company’s assets, involving monitoring, maintaining, upgrading, and replacing them cost-effectively. As emphasized by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO), “An effective AMP aligns with business growth and technological advancements, ensuring optimal asset utilization and informed decision-making.”  

**What Should an AMP Include?**  
An effective plan should cover:  
- *Asset Inventory and Classification*: Listing and categorizing all assets forms the foundation for management.  
- *Levels of Service (LoS)*: Defining service quality standards enables performance assessment.  
- *Risk Assessment and Criticality*: Evaluating risks from asset failure helps prioritize critical assets.  
- *Decision Criteria and Life Cycle Costs*: Establishing criteria for acquisition, maintenance, and disposal ensures lifecycle planning.  
- *Current Performance and Condition*: Knowing equipment status aids in repair and replacement decisions.  

**Advantages of AMPs**

- *Improves Decision-Making*: AI and IoT provide data-driven insights for proactive adjustments.

- *Extends Asset Lifespan*: Predictive maintenance and regular upkeep prolong asset longevity.  
- *Facilitates Risk Management*: Proactive strategies mitigate disruptions and ensure safety compliance.  
- *Streamlines Processes*: Automation and standardization reduce redundancies.  
- *Supports Financial Planning*: Holistic asset views enable cost-effective budgeting and forecasting.
