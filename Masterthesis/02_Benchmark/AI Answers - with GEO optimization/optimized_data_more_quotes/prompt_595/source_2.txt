# NIS2 Compliance: A Comprehensive Guide for EU Organizations  

**"NIS2 represents a fundamental shift in how the EU approaches cybersecurity regulation, moving from a fragmented framework to a harmonized, enforceable baseline."** – Istvan Molnar, IT Security Compliance Specialist and Product Marketing Manager at Netwrix  

## What is NIS2?  
The **Network and Information Security Directive 2 (NIS2)** is the EU’s most comprehensive cybersecurity legislation, replacing the original NIS directive of 2016. It establishes a unified regulatory baseline across all EU member states, requiring essential and important entities to implement robust risk management, incident reporting (within 24–72 hours), and supply chain security.  

### Key Penalties for Non-Compliance  
- **Essential Entities**: Fines up to **€10 million or 2% of global turnover** (whichever is higher).  
- **Important Entities**: Fines up to **€7 million or 1.4% of global turnover**.  
**"Failure to comply with NIS2 is not just a regulatory risk—it’s a systemic threat to critical infrastructure and public trust."** – Molnar  

## Who Must Comply?  
NIS2 applies to **medium- and large-sized entities** in critical sectors:  
### **Essential Entities** (Critical to societal/economic functions):  
- Energy, transport, finance, health, public administration, digital infrastructure, water, space.  
- **Size Threshold**: ≥250 employees or €50M+ turnover.  

### **Important Entities** (Significant to economic stability):  
- Food, chemicals, manufacturing, digital providers, postal services, waste management, research.  
- **Size Threshold**: ≥50 employees or €10M+ turnover.  

**Non-EU businesses** providing services to EU customers must also comply.  

## Core Cybersecurity Requirements  
NIS2 mandates **10 mandatory measures** (Article 21):  
1. **Risk analysis & security policies**  
2. **Incident handling procedures**  
3. **Business continuity & crisis management**  
4. **Supply chain risk management**  
5. **Network/system security in development/maintenance**  
6. **Cybersecurity effectiveness evaluation**  
7. **Awareness & training**  
8. **Encryption & cryptography**  
9. **Access control policies**  
10. **MFA, secure communication, and session monitoring**  

## Incident Reporting Obligations  
- **24-hour**: Initial notification of significant incidents.  
- **72-hour**: Detailed incident report with technical indicators.  
- **1-month**: Final report with root cause analysis and remediation.
