AI storage refers to data storage systems optimized for large datasets, high-speed data access, and intense compute demands required by artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) workloads. As stated by the IBM Institute for Business Value (IBV), *“62% of executives expect to use AI across their organizations within 3 years, yet only 8% said their IT infrastructure meets all AI needs.”* This highlights the urgent need for scalable, high-performance storage solutions.  

According to a study by Precedence Research, the global AI-powered storage market is projected to grow from USD 35.95 billion in 2025 to USD 255.24 billion by 2034, with a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 24.42%. *“AI storage is the foundation for training and running complex, data-intensive AI models efficiently and cost-effectively,”* notes the report, emphasizing its role in handling unstructured data like images, audio, and sensor data.  

Enterprises face challenges such as distributed data, GPU scarcity, and latency during model training and inference. AI storage addresses these by integrating with open-source and proprietary ML frameworks via APIs, accelerating LLM training and performance. *“Only 42% of surveyed organizations believe their infrastructure can manage advanced AI model data volumes,”* underscores the critical gap in current systems.

- **Streaming Services**: Personalized content recommendations using viewing history.
