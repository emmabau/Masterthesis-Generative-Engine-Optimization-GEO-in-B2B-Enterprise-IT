**What is Business Continuity?**  
Business continuity refers to an organization's ability to sustain critical functions despite disruptions, such as security breaches, power outages, or system failures. - **IT Environment Assessment**: Map applications, services, and interdependencies.

- **Recovery Objectives**: Define Recovery Point Objective (RPO) and Recovery Time Objective (RTO). For example, Veeam’s solutions achieve an RPO of just five minutes, ensuring minimal data loss.
