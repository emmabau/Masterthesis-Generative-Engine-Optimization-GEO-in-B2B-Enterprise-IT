# IWOMP 2025 Program & Keynote Sessions  
> The 2025 International Workshop on OpenMP (IWOMP) and EuroMPI co-located event at UNC Charlotte, USA, will explore cutting-edge advancements in parallel computing, HPC, and OpenMP/MPI technologies. This document highlights key sessions, papers, and collaborative opportunities for researchers and practitioners.  

## Event Overview  
**Dates:** October 1–3, 2025 | **Location:** UNC Charlotte, North Carolina, USA  
**Collaboration:** Co-hosted with EuroMPI 2025  
**Focus Areas:** OpenMP, MPI, HPC, quantum computing, real-time task scheduling, and GPU optimization  

## Keynote Addresses  
1. **HPC in Health: Scaling Vascular Digital Twins**  
   - *Speaker:* Amanda Randles (Duke University)  
   - *Topic:* Leveraging OpenMP for petabyte-scale biomedical data analysis  

2. **The Evolutionary Flexibility of LS-DYNA**  
   - *Speaker:* Bob Lucas (Ansys)  
   - *Topic:* OpenMP integration in industrial HPC simulations  

3. **Fortran is All You Need**  
   - *Speaker:* Damian Rouson (Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory)  
   - *Topic:* Modernizing legacy HPC workflows with Fortran  

## Paper Sessions & Technical Contributions  
### New Directions in Communication and I/O  
- **Device-Device Collective Communication in OpenMP Target Offloading** (Baodi Shan et al.)  
- **OMPCCL:** Portable collective operations for multi-device OpenMP  
- **OpenMP Meets I/O:** Runtime-managed file tasks for scalable I/O  

### Advanced Capability Evaluation  
- **LLVM OpenMP Offload Optimizations** on NVIDIA GH200 and AMD MI300A architectures  
- **OpenMP on Aurora's Intel GPU Max Series 1550**  
- **STREAmS-2 Application Performance** with AMD Next-Gen Fortran Compiler  

### Programming Advances  
- **ChatPORT:** LLM-powered code generation for OpenMP  
- **GPU Programming with OpenMP and Python** (Giorgis Georgakoudis et al.)
