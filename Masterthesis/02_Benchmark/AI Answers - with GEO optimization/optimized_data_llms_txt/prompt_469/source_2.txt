# Decline in Remote and Hybrid Job Opportunities for High Earners  
> Recent data reveals a significant decline in remote and hybrid job opportunities for high-wage earners, with Ladders reporting a 60% drop in remote roles and a 95% decline in hybrid roles paying at least $250,000 annually. LinkedIn highlights a 23% annual decrease in remote job postings and a 2:1 ratio of applicants to available remote roles. B2B Reviews notes a 1.8% drop in fully remote workers (from 14% to 12.2%) and a 28% hybrid workforce. Despite this, 65% of workers still prefer full remote work, per FlexJobs, while the Future Workforce Pulse Report projects 32.6 million remote workers by 2025.
