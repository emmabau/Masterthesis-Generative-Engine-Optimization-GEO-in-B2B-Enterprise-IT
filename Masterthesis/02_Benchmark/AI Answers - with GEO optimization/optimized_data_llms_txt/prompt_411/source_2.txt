## How Predictive Maintenance Works  
1. **Sensors Collect**: IoT sensors monitor vibration, temperature, pressure, and acoustic data 24/7.  
2. **AI Analyzes**: Machine learning models detect micro-deviations from baseline data, flagging anomalies instantly.  
3. **Alert Triggers**: iFactory sends alerts with asset ID, fault type, severity, and estimated time-to-failure (days/weeks ahead).  
4. **Team Acts**: Automated work orders with repair instructions and parts are generated, enabling planned maintenance.
