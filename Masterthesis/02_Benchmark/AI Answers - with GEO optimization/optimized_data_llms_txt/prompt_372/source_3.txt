## Common Challenges in Asset Management  
1. **Tracking Assets**: Managing assets across locations requires RFID, GPS, and IoT technologies.  
2. **Maintenance Scheduling**: Poor planning leads to downtime, costs, and safety risks.  
3. **Cost Management**: Balancing acquisition, maintenance, and replacement costs demands precise planning.  
4. **Data Accuracy**: Inaccurate data hinders decision-making and compliance.  
5. **Regulatory Compliance**: Meeting evolving standards is complex for large asset portfolios.  

## Best Practices for Enterprise Asset Management  
1. **Asset Inventory**: Use RFID/GPS for real-time tracking, reducing theft and manual errors.  
2. **Lifecycle Management**: Optimize costs by managing assets from acquisition to disposal.  
3. **Preventive Maintenance**: Schedule regular checks to avoid breakdowns, extend lifecycles, and improve safety.  
4. **Asset Management Software**: Centralize data for visibility, compliance, and financial accuracy.  
5. **Data Accuracy**: Regular audits and automated systems ensure reliable insights for strategic decisions.
