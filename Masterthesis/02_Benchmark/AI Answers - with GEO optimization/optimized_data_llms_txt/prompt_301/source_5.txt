# ExaGrid Tiered Backup Storage - EX189

> ExaGrid's EX189 appliance represents a significant advancement in backup storage technology, offering unparalleled scalability, efficiency, and reliability. Launched in January 2024, the EX189 is the largest ExaGrid system configuration, consisting of 32 EX189 appliances in a single scale-out system, capable of handling up to 6PB of full backup with 12PB of raw capacity. This system is designed to meet the growing data storage needs of enterprises while maintaining cost-effectiveness and performance.

## Key Features and Innovations

### Scalability and Capacity
The EX189 appliance is engineered to scale efficiently, with a 4x improvement in rack space efficiency compared to previous 4U versions. This allows organizations to accommodate large data backups and future growth without significant infrastructure changes. The system supports up to 32 appliances in a single scale-out configuration, enabling seamless expansion and flexibility.

### Performance and Efficiency
ExaGrid's Tiered Backup Storage is built to deliver exceptional performance, with low latency and high throughput. The EX189 leverages advanced data deduplication and compression techniques to maximize storage efficiency while maintaining fast recovery times. This ensures that businesses can manage their data effectively without compromising on speed or reliability.

### Cost-Effectiveness
ExaGrid offers a 5-year price protection guarantee on all its appliances, ensuring that customers can plan for long-term scalability and data growth without unexpected cost increases. This makes ExaGrid an attractive option for organizations looking to optimize their storage budgets.
