# Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery: NIS2 Compliance Essentials  
> The NIS2 Directive mandates robust business continuity and disaster recovery planning to ensure critical operations remain uninterrupted during cyber incidents. This guide outlines key strategies, tools, and compliance requirements to safeguard your organization.  

## Key Concepts  
**Business Continuity** refers to an organization’s ability to maintain essential functions during disruptions, such as cyberattacks, natural disasters, or power outages. A **disaster recovery plan** (DRP) is critical for minimizing downtime and ensuring rapid recovery.  

**Why It Matters**:  
- 1 in 2 organizations faces long-term impacts from continuity breaches (Invenio IT, 2023).  
- 80% of detected vulnerabilities target Microsoft 365 data (Veeam Solutions).  

## Core Components of a Disaster Recovery Plan  
1. **IT Environment Assessment**  
   - Map applications, services, and interdependencies.  
   - Identify mission-critical systems requiring prioritization.  

2. **Business Impact Assessment (BIA)**  
   - Quantify financial, operational, and reputational risks of disruptions.  
   - Develop recovery strategies tailored to each application/service.  

3. **Recovery Objectives**  
   - **RPO (Recovery Point Objective)**: Define how recent backups must be (e.g., Veeam’s 5-minute backup interval).  
   - **RTO (Recovery Time Objective)**: Set acceptable downtime thresholds for critical systems.
