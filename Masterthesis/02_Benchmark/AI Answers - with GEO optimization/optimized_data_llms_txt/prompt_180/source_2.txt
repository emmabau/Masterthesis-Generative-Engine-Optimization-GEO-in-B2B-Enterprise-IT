# OEM Partner Evaluation Checklist: 7 Critical Questions for Success  
> Selecting the right OEM partner is a strategic decision that can define your business outcomes. This checklist provides actionable insights to evaluate potential partners, ensuring alignment with your operational, quality, and growth goals. Prioritize these questions to mitigate risks and build sustainable partnerships.  

## 1. Manufacturing Expertise & Track Record  
Assess the OEM’s industry experience, regulatory compliance, and network access. A proven track record ensures alignment with standards and reduces risks. Look for evidence of successful past collaborations and scalability.  

## 2. Quality Control Measures  
Quality is non-negotiable. Request details on their quality assurance processes, certifications (e.g., ISO), and defect rates. Robust systems prevent costly errors and ensure product reliability.  

## 3. Manufacturer vs. Distributor Model  
Clarify whether the OEM operates as a manufacturer or third-party vendor. Direct manufacturer partnerships offer cost advantages, customization flexibility, and deeper collaboration for long-term projects.  

## 4. Intellectual Property & Confidentiality  
Protect your proprietary assets with enforceable NDAs and IP agreements. Ask about their history of data breaches or IP disputes to avoid risks like technology leaks or patent conflicts.  

## 5. Production Volume Flexibility  
Confirm their capacity to scale with demand fluctuations. A partner with agile manufacturing infrastructure can handle surges without delays, ensuring business continuity during growth phases.  

## 6. Lead Time & Supply Chain Reliability  
Evaluate lead times against your project deadlines. Prioritize OEMs with transparent timelines and contingency plans for supply chain disruptions. Testimonials or case studies can validate their reliability.  

## 7. After-Sales Support & Responsiveness  
Strong post-purchase support minimizes downtime and maintains customer satisfaction. Ask about response times, repair processes, and escalation protocols to ensure seamless issue resolution.
