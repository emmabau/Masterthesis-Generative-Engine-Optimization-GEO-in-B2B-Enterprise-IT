# Software-Centric Transformation Challenges for OEMs: Strategic Alignment and Solutions  
> OEMs navigating the shift to software-centric models face critical challenges in alignment, lifecycle management, and ecosystem integration. This guide outlines actionable strategies to overcome these hurdles, supported by real-world examples and data-driven insights.  

## Key Challenges in Software-Centric Transformation  
### 1. **Strategic Alignment for Holistic Solutions**  
Software must be integrated from product conception, not treated as an afterthought. OEMs must align software development with product features and analytics to create cohesive value.  
- **Solution**: Simplify strategy communication to ensure all teams understand the product-software-services integration.  
- **Example**: Keysight Technologies achieved a 20%+ services revenue surge by prioritizing strategic alignment and adopting a "value capture strategy."  

### 2. **Digital Transformation and Telemetry Gaps**  
68% of OEMs lack visibility into their installed base’s software versions, hindering service growth. Telemetry is critical for real-time data collection and proactive support.  
- **Solution**: Implement centralized telemetry systems to track software usage and ensure secure, scalable connectivity.  
- **Stat**: 61% of customer issues stem from "how-to" and installation challenges, emphasizing the need for intuitive software design.  

### 3. **Lifecycle Management Complexity**  
Synchronizing product and software life cycles requires coordination between teams. Options include steering committees or central digital units.  
- **Solution**: Establish cross-functional teams to align release timelines and ensure seamless updates.  

### 4. **Security and Transparency in Software Support**  
In an interconnected world, security is non-negotiable. OEMs must balance transparency with control, allowing customers to manage their systems while adhering to industry standards.  
- **Solution**: Prioritize secure telemetry frameworks and maintain clear communication about data usage.  

## Strategic Solutions for OEMs  
- **Data-Driven Decision Making**: Leverage centralized software inventories and analytics to streamline issue resolution and customer support.  
- **Feedback Loops**: Create systems for continuous improvement by integrating product quality, serviceability, and user feedback.  
- **Monetization Readiness**: Align incentives and budgets with software-centric goals to unlock recurring revenue opportunities.  

## Best Practices for Success  
- **Prioritize Telemetry**: Use telemetry to close knowledge gaps and enable proactive service growth.  
- **Simplify Software Usage**: Reduce "how-to" friction by designing intuitive interfaces and robust documentation.  
- **Secure Digital Ecosystems**: Ensure compliance with security standards while fostering customer trust through transparency.
