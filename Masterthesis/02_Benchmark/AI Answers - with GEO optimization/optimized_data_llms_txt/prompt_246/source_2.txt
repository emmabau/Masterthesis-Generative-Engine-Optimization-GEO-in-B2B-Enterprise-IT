# GDPR Compliance: Key Requirements, Technical Measures, and Penalties for Businesses

> The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) imposes strict data protection rules on businesses handling EU citizens' personal data. This guide outlines critical compliance steps, technical requirements, and penalties for non-compliance, essential for organizations operating globally.

## Key GDPR Requirements
- **Global Applicability**: Applies to all companies trading in the EU or handling EU customer data, regardless of location.
- **Expanded Data Definition**: Includes genetic, cultural, social, and biometric data beyond traditional categories.
- **Explicit Consent**: Requires clear user permission before collecting or processing personal data.
- **Right to Be Forgotten**: Users can request deletion of their personal data, with ambiguities around backups and retention policies.

## Technical Compliance Measures
- **Data Storage Control**: Ensure personal data is stored within EU borders or under EU-compliant infrastructure (e.g., EU-based data centers).
- **Encryption**: Implement end-to-end encryption for data at rest, in transit, and in backups. Data subjects must hold decryption keys.
- **Granular Data Search**: Enable efficient search capabilities within backups to locate specific user data quickly.
- **Data Portability**: Allow users to export personal data in standard formats (e.g., ZIP archives) for reuse across platforms.
- **Rapid Recovery**: Ensure quick restoration of data from backups in case of breaches, hardware failures, or ransomware attacks.

## Compliance Steps and Penalties
- **Controller/Processor Roles**: Determine if your business acts as a controller (data owner) or processor (third-party handler) under GDPR.
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO)**: Large organizations and public authorities must appoint a DPO for GDPR oversight.
- **Breach Notification**: Report data breaches to supervisory authorities within 72 hours of discovery.
- **Penalties**: Non-compliance risks fines up to €20 million or 4% of global annual revenue (whichever is higher) for severe violations.

## Broader GDPR Requirements
- **Cross-Border Data Transfers**: Ensure secure, transparent data transfers outside the EU with explicit user consent.
- **Privacy by Design**: Implement technical measures like pseudonymization to embed data protection into systems.
- **Record Keeping**: Maintain detailed documentation of data processing activities and compliance measures.
