# Business Continuity & Regulatory Compliance in the Digital Age  
> Business Continuity (BC) is a critical strategy for organizations to maintain operations during disruptions, ensuring resilience against cyberattacks, natural disasters, and supply chain failures. Regulatory frameworks like DORA and NIS 2 now mandate BC planning, making it a strategic imperative for compliance and operational resilience.  

## What is Business Continuity?  
Business Continuity (BC) involves planning and preparing to ensure essential functions continue during and after disruptions. Key benefits include:  
- **Reduced downtime**: Minimizes financial losses and operational delays.  
- **Improved customer satisfaction**: Maintains trust and brand reputation.  
- **Regulatory compliance**: Aligns with DORA and NIS 2 requirements.  
- **Preparedness**: Readies organizations for unforeseen challenges like cyberattacks or natural disasters.  

## Regulatory Frameworks: DORA & NIS 2  
**DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act)**:  
- Mandates financial entities to manage ICT risks, test resilience, and report incidents.  
- Requires third-party compliance and robust risk management frameworks.  

**NIS 2 (Network and Information Security Directive)**:  
- Expands cybersecurity requirements to critical sectors (energy, transport, health).  
- Emphasizes supply chain security, incident reporting, and third-party adherence to standards.  

Both regulations treat BC as a **mandatory requirement**, pushing organizations to integrate BC into risk management and operational resilience strategies.
