# Cloud vs On-Prem: Security Compliance and the Rise of Hybrid Solutions  
> In today's digital landscape, industries face a critical dilemma: balancing cloud innovation with strict security and compliance requirements. This article explores the trade-offs between cloud and on-prem solutions, with a focus on sectors like healthcare, finance, and defense, and highlights how Cybereason On-Prem bridges this gap.  

## Key Challenges in Cloud Adoption  
- **Healthcare**: HIPAA compliance and hybrid IT/OT environments require secure, air-gapped solutions for patient data.  
- **Finance**: PCI DSS and GDPR regulations demand robust security for financial data, complicating cloud migration.  
- **Defense/Space**: Classified data and restricted access protocols make traditional cloud infrastructures unsuitable.  
- **Architectural Limits**: Legacy systems and specialized hardware create barriers to seamless cloud transitions.  

## Regulatory Pressures: The NIS2 Directive  
- **NIS2 Framework**: Upcoming EU regulations will expand cybersecurity mandates to smaller companies, emphasizing the need for compliance-ready solutions.  
- **Impact**: Organizations must now prioritize cybersecurity maturity, with management held accountable for security posture.
