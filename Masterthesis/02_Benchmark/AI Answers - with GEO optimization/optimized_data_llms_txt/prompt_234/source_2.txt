# Compliance Risk Assessment: A Step-by-Step Framework for Regulatory Teams  
> This guide provides a structured approach to compliance risk assessment, emphasizing proactive risk management, regulatory alignment, and technology integration. Key concepts include defining scope, mapping obligations, scoring risks, and leveraging AI tools like C2P for real-time regulatory monitoring.  

## Key Concepts  
**Compliance Risk Assessment** is a systematic process to identify, score, and prioritize regulatory risks, transforming compliance from a reactive task into an early-warning system. It involves:  
- **Mapping obligations** to business operations (e.g., product safety, import/export rules).  
- **Scoring risks** by likelihood and impact (financial, reputational, operational).  
- **Developing remediation plans** with ownership, deadlines, and evidence trails.  
- **Continuous monitoring** to adapt to evolving regulations.  

## Step-by-Step Framework  
1. **Define Scope & Regulatory Universe**  
   - Identify product, geographic, and regulatory scopes.  
   - Example: A global electronics manufacturer must account for RoHS, REACH, and energy efficiency mandates across 50 markets.  

2. **Map Obligations to Operations**  
   - Link regulations to business processes (e.g., supply chain, documentation).  
   - Use a compliance risk matrix to assign ownership and track controls.  

3. **Identify Risk Exposure**  
   - Document gaps like control deficiencies, documentation gaps, or outdated processes.  
   - Example: No formal process for reviewing component changes against substance restriction lists.  

4. **Score & Prioritize Risks**  
   - Use a 1-5 scale for likelihood (probability of violation) and impact (financial, reputational, safety).  
   - Prioritize high-impact risks for immediate remediation.  

5. **Develop Remediation Plans**  
   - Specify actions, owners, and deadlines.  
   - Example: "Implement substance restriction review checkpoint in component approval workflow by Q2 end."  

6. **Test Controls & Validate**  
   - Sample transactions or documents to confirm controls are operational.  
   - Example: Review product launches against safety standards to verify compliance.  

7. **Report & Monitor Continuously**  
   - Translate risk data into business terms (e.g., market access, revenue exposure).  
   - Use AI tools like C2P to track regulatory changes and update risk registers in real time.  

## Common Gaps  
- **Narrow scope**: Overlooking emerging markets or new regulatory categories.  
- **Static obligation libraries**: Outdated regulatory inventories.  
- **Weak remediation follow-through**: Failing to maintain accountability for fixes.  
- **No product integration**: Isolating compliance from design and engineering processes.

## FAQ Highlights  
- **Compliance audit vs. risk assessment**: Audits check current compliance; assessments predict future risks.  
- **Frequency**: Annual minimum; quarterly for high-risk areas.  
- **Risk scoring**: Combines likelihood (probability) and impact (financial, reputational, safety).  
- **Ownership**: Led by compliance leaders but requires cross-functional input (legal, engineering, supply chain).
