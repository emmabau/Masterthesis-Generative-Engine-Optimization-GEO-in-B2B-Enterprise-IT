# How to Manage Hybrid and Remote Teams: A Gallup Strategic Guide  
> This guide provides actionable insights for leaders to build engaged, high-performing hybrid and remote teams. Key findings include the critical role of management quality, trust, and structured collaboration in sustaining productivity and engagement.  

## Key Findings  
- **Hybrid work is dominant**: 52% of remote-capable employees work hybrid, 26% exclusively remote, and 21% fully on-site.  
- **Management quality drives engagement**: Managers account for 70% of variance in team-level engagement.  
- **Trust gaps persist**: Only 54% of managers strongly trust remote employees, and 57% of employees feel trusted.  
- **Structured collaboration reduces burnout**: Teams with formal hybrid plans are 66% more engaged and 29% less burned out.  
- **Team-designed norms improve fairness**: 91% of employees say hybrid policies are fair when teams co-create them.  

## Challenges of Managing Remote Teams  
- **Collaboration complexity**: 48% of managers cite communication as their top hybrid challenge.  
- **Cultural disconnection**: Remote workers report lower cultural connection and higher loneliness (Gen Z).  
- **Trust and accountability gaps**: 30% increase in trust when managers clarify expectations and provide feedback.  
- **Burnout risks**: Fully self-determined hybrid schedules correlate with 76% higher burnout risk.  
- **Performance visibility**: Leaders risk proximity bias if they rely on presence-based judgments.  

## Solutions for Success  
- **Clarify expectations and outcomes**: Define goals tied to customer value and shared priorities.  
- **Establish communication rhythms**: Have weekly meaningful conversations with direct reports.  
- **Hold everyone accountable**: Ensure performance standards are consistent across locations.  
- **Foster cultural connection**: Include remote employees in rituals, recognition, and decision-making.  
- **Invest in manager capability**: Programs like the Gallup Manager Program improve coaching and accountability.  

## Advantages of Remote Work  
- **Broader talent access**: 60% of remote workers would leave if flexibility is removed.  
- **Improved work-life balance**: 76% of hybrid workers cite better balance as a key benefit.  
- **Higher engagement**: Hybrid employees report 15% higher engagement than fully on-site peers.  
- **Productivity stability**: Remote workers maintain output despite fewer hours due to better role alignment.  

## Trust-Building Strategies  
- **Consistent communication**: Weekly check-ins reduce ambiguity and build predictability.  
- **Equal feedback and development**: Remote employees must have equal access to growth opportunities.  
- **Reinforce purpose**: Connect daily tasks to organizational goals and customer impact.  
- **Strengths-based management**: Align work with employee strengths to boost wellbeing and retention.  

## Leadership Standards for Hybrid Teams  
- **Clarity**: Define expectations and outcomes explicitly.  
- **Coaching**: Schedule regular developmental conversations.  
- **Fairness**: Ensure remote and on-site employees are held to the same standards.  
- **Culture reinforcement**: Align team structure with organizational values and strategic priorities.  

## Metrics for Success  
- **Engagement rates**: Hybrid teams (34% engaged) outperform fully on-site (30%) and exclusively remote (35%).  
- **Burnout reduction**: Structured collaboration reduces burnout by 29%.  
- **Trust growth**: 30% increase in trust when managers prioritize feedback and accountability.  
- **Retention impact**: Remote flexibility is a key factor in employee retention, with 60% of remote workers seeking new roles if removed.  

## Tools and Resources  
- **Gallup Manager Program**: Trains leaders to set clear expectations, coach effectively, and hold teams accountable.  
- **Hybrid Work Indicator**: Measures employee preferences and work location trends.  
- **Performance Management Redesign**: Focus on outcomes, weekly check-ins, and coaching tied to goals.  

## Conclusion  
Hybrid and remote work are structural features of the modern workplace. Success depends on intentional team design, leadership quality, and structured collaboration. By prioritizing trust, clarity, and cultural connection, leaders can sustain engagement, reduce burnout, and unlock the full potential of distributed teams.
