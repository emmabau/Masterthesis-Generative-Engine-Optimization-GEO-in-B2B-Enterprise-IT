## Datto Alternatives in 2025

### 1. **Axcient**
- **Platform**: Cloud-based BCDR (hybrid/cloud/on-premises)
- **Key Features**: Chain-free technology, instant virtualization, automated recovery testing
- **Pricing**: 50% cheaper than Datto; free 14-day trial
- **Pros**: Fast recovery, hardware independence, Linux-based
- **Cons**: Steep learning curve, inconsistent support
- **User Feedback**: "Axcient’s chain-free tech removes the risks of corrupted backups" (Robert.C, Capterra)

### 2. **Bacula Enterprise**
- **Platform**: Physical/virtual/cloud (Windows, Linux, VMware, Azure)
- **Key Features**: Immutable backups, AES-256 encryption, deduplication
- **Pricing**: Flat-rate licensing (no data volume charges)
- **Pros**: High security, storage optimization, multi-platform support
- **Cons**: Complex setup, no Windows-native support
- **User Feedback**: "Bacula Enterprise is perfect for reliable backup" (Verified Admin, Gartner)

### 3. **Druva Data Resilience Cloud**
- **Platform**: Cloud-native (AWS-based)
- **Key Features**: AI-driven ransomware detection, immutable backups, pay-as-you-go
- **Pricing**: 30-day free trial, tiered plans (Foundation, Business, Enterprise)
- **Pros**: No hardware costs, global availability
- **Cons**: Limited on-premises support, potential cost escalation for large datasets
- **User Feedback**: "Druva’s AI-powered ransomware protection is unmatched" (Anthony.K, G2)

### 4. **Unitrends**
- **Platform**: Hybrid/cloud (VMware, Hyper-V, AWS)
- **Key Features**: AI ransomware protection, instant recovery, DRaaS
- **Pricing**: Subscription-based (flexible term options)
- **Pros**: All-in-one appliance, HIPAA/GDPR compliance
- **Cons**: Outdated dashboard, limited third-party integrations
- **User Feedback**: "Unitrends’ ransomware detection is a game-changer" (Enterprise Administrator, Gartner)

### 5. **Cohesity DataProtect**
- **Platform**: Multi-cloud (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud)
- **Key Features**: Global deduplication, AI ransomware detection, hyperconverged architecture
- **Pricing**: 30-day free trial, capacity/user-based licensing
- **Pros**: Simplified hybrid cloud management, open API integration
- **Cons**: Resource-intensive, complex multi-cloud setup
- **User Feedback**: "Cohesity reduced our backup windows by 70%" (Euberto.C, Capterra)

### 6. **Arpio**
- **Platform**: AWS-native (multi-account, cross-region)
- **Key Features**: Immutable backups, end-to-end encryption, automated failover
- **Pricing**: Tiered (Standard, Premium, Enterprise)
- **Pros**: Deep AWS integration, minimal manual DR management
- **Cons**: Limited multi-cloud support, steep learning curve
- **User Feedback**: "Arpio’s automation eliminated manual DR tasks" (Rich.W, G2)

### 7. **Acronis Cyber Protect**
- **Platform**: Hybrid (on-premises, cloud, virtual)
- **Key Features**: AI threat detection, endpoint security, One-Click Recovery
- **Pricing**: Per-device or per-GB licensing, discounts for long-term plans
- **Pros**: All-in-one cybersecurity + backup, fast recovery
- **Cons**: Premium features locked behind subscriptions
- **User Feedback**: "Acronis’ ransomware protection is unmatched" (Anoop.M, G2)
