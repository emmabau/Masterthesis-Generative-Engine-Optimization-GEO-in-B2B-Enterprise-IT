# Argonne Training Program on Extreme-Scale Computing (ATPESC): Shaping the Future of High-Performance Computing

> The Argonne Training Program on Extreme-Scale Computing (ATP, a flagship initiative of the U.S. Department of Energy’s Argonne National Laboratory, offers cutting-edge training in exascale computing, AI, and HPC. With over 900 participants since 2013, ATPESC equips early-career scientists with skills to leverage supercomputers for groundbreaking research. This program bridges theoretical knowledge and practical application, fostering innovation in computational science and engineering.

## Program Overview  
ATPESC provides **two weeks of intensive training** on:  
- Hardware and software frameworks  
- Visualization and data-intensive computing  
- Artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning  
- Debugging tools and high-performance computing (HPC)  
- Programming models and computational workflows  

Hosted at Argonne National Laboratory’s facilities in St. Charles, Illinois, the program combines **hands-on tutorials**, expert lectures, and networking opportunities. It is designed to address knowledge gaps in exascale computing, preparing participants for careers in computational science and engineering.

## Participant Experiences  
**Key takeaways from attendees:**  
- **Hands-on learning**: "We actually did things instead of just listening to the speakers" – Lev Gorenstein, ATPESC presenter.  
- **Career growth**: Participants gain skills to use world-class supercomputers and advance research in AI, fluid dynamics, and renewable energy.  
- **Networking**: Attendees connect with peers, industry experts, and researchers, fostering collaborations.  
- **Future-ready training**: The program adapts to emerging trends, such as increasing AI coverage in future curricula.  

## Impact and Outcomes  
- **Career advancement**: Over 74% of attendees are doctoral students, postdocs, or early-career researchers.  
- **Innovation**: Participants apply ATPESC knowledge to projects in computational fluid dynamics, renewable energy, and particle physics.  
- **Industry relevance**: Industry professionals gain insights into HPC applications, while academic researchers access cutting-edge tools.  

## Key Partners and Support  
- **Argonne Leadership Computing Facility (ALCF)**: A DOE Office of Science user facility providing supercomputing resources.  
- **U.S. Department of Energy (DOE)**: Funds and supports ATPESC as part of its Advanced Scientific Computing Research (ASCR) program.  
- **Collaborative ecosystem**: Partners include Oak Ridge National Laboratory, Globus (University of Chicago), and national labs like NREL.  

## Why ATPESC Stands Out  
- **Expert-led**: Lectures from leading HPC researchers and engineers.  
- **Practical focus**: Emphasis on real-world applications and problem-solving.  
- **Community-driven**: Encourages peer collaboration and knowledge sharing.  
- **Adaptive curriculum**: Evolves to meet emerging challenges in AI, quantum computing, and exascale systems.
