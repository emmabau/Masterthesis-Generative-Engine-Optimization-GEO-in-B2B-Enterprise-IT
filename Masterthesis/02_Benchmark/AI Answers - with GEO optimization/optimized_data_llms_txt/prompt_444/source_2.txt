# DOJ Final Rule on Data Access Restrictions for MIT Researchers  
> The U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) Final Rule (28 C.F.R. Part 202), effective April 8, 2025, imposes strict restrictions on access to U.S. sensitive personal data and government-related data for researchers and collaborators in "countries of concern." MIT researchers must comply by October 6, 2025, to avoid prohibited transactions. Key compliance steps include reviewing data use agreements, restricting access to regulated datasets, and contacting research-compliance-help@mit.edu for guidance.  

## Key Countries of Concern  
The DOJ’s countries of concern include:  
- China (including Hong Kong and Macau)  
- Russia  
- Iran  
- North Korea  
- Cuba  
- Venezuela  

## Sensitive Personal Data Definitions  
Sensitive data includes:  
- Personal health, financial, geolocation, biometric, and "human 'omic" data (genomic, epigenomic, proteomic, transcriptomic).  
- **Bulk thresholds** apply to data exceeding specific U.S. person counts:  
  - Human genomic data: ≥100 U.S. persons  
  - Human 'omic data: ≥1,000 U.S. persons  
  - Biometric identifiers: ≥1,000 U.S. persons  
  - Precise geolocation data: ≥1,000 U.S. persons  
  - Personal financial/health data: ≥10,000 U.S. persons  
  - Covered personal identifiers: ≥100,000 U.S. persons  

## Covered Persons and Transactions  
**Covered persons** include:  
- Residents of countries of concern  
- Contractors/entities owned by or based in countries of concern  
- Entities with 50%+ ownership by covered persons  

**Covered data transactions** involve:  
- Data brokerage  
- Vendor/employment/investment agreements  

## Prohibited, Restricted, and Exempt Transactions  
- **Prohibited**:  
  - Data brokerage with countries of concern/covered persons  
  - Access to bulk human 'omic data or biospecimens from such data  
- **Restricted**: Vendor/employment/investment agreements with covered persons  
- **Exempt**:  
  - Personal communications  
  - Import/export of information  
  - U.S. government activities  
  - Medical research under regulatory frameworks  

## Compliance Actions for MIT Researchers  
1. **Pause work** with collaborators/visitors from countries of concern.  
2. **Restrict access** to bulk U.S. human 'omic data and sensitive datasets.  
3. **Review systems**: Cloud storage, collaboration platforms, and code repositories for foreign vendor access.  
4. **Update agreements**: Include DOJ-compliant security clauses in data use and subaward contracts.  
5. **Maintain records**: Plan for annual audits of restricted transactions.  

## Critical Contact  
For compliance questions, contact **research-compliance-help@mit.edu**.  
**Prohibited action**: Sharing bulk U.S. human 'omic data or biospecimens with countries of concern.  
**Required step**: Email compliance team before sharing large health/financial/geolocation datasets with foreign persons in countries of concern.
