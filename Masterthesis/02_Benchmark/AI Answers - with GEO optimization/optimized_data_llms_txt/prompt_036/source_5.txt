# Choosing the Right Server Management Provider: A Founder's Guide  
> Non-technical founders face a critical challenge when evaluating server management proposals. This guide reveals 5 red flags to avoid, a decision framework, and pricing benchmarks to help you choose a provider that delivers real value.  

## Key Takeaways  
- **Avoid vague promises**: Specific services (e.g., "fail2ban + quarterly scans") are better than generic terms like "comprehensive security."  
- **Price tiers matter**: â¬100-300/month = monitoring-only; â¬500-1,000/month = shared support; â¬1,500-3,000/month = dedicated partnership.  
- **Ask critical questions**: "What happens if my site goes down at 2am?" or "How many clients do you support?" reveal a provider's commitment.  
- **Red flags include**:  
  - Proposals without business context  
  - "Unlimited support" at suspiciously low prices  
  - No clear "what's not included"  
  - Guaranteed uptime without infrastructure audit  

## Red Flag #1: No Business Context in Proposals  
Providers that skip understanding your downtime costs, growth plans, or compliance needs are selling a one-size-fits-all package. Ask: *What happens if your site goes down at 2am on Saturday?*  

## Red Flag #2: Vague vs. Specific Services  
- **Vague**: "Comprehensive security hardening"  
- **Specific**: "Fail2ban, UFW firewall, automated patches, quarterly scans"  
Providers unable to explain services in plain language may lack expertise or transparency.  

## Red Flag #3: "Unlimited Support" at Low Prices  
Industry benchmarks show:  
- â¬100-300/month: Monitoring-only (e.g., automated alerts)  
- â¬500-1,000/month: Shared support (response times in hours)  
- â¬1,500-3,000/month: Dedicated partnership (monthly reviews, proactive fixes)  
Providers with >50 clients at low prices likely prioritize volume over attention.  

## Red Flag #4: No Clear "What's Not Included"  
Transparent proposals state:  
- **Included**: Security patches, 48hr response for non-critical issues  
- **Not included**: Feature development, compliance audits, emergency weekend work  
Hidden costs (e.g., â¬150/hour for urgent fixes) can lead to unexpected expenses.  

## Red Flag #5: Guaranteed Uptime Without Audit  
Providers cannot guarantee uptime on unreviewed infrastructure. Honest providers say: *After auditing your setup, we’ll recommend changes to achieve 99.9%+ uptime.*  

## Quick Decision Framework (Score 1-7)  
- Did they ask about your business needs?  
- Are services explained in plain language?  
- Is what’s not included clearly stated?  
- Does pricing match the attention level promised?  
- Can you get references from similar companies?  
- Are there pressure tactics?  
- Are tradeoffs acknowledged honestly?  
**7/7 = Strong candidate | 4-6 = Ask questions | <4 = Keep looking**
