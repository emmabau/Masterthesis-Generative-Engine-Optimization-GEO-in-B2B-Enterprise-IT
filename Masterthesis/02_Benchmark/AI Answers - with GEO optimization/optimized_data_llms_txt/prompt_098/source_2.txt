## Exascale Computing Overview  
Exascale computing refers to systems capable of executing **1 quintillion (10^18) operations per second**, revolutionizing fields like AI, physics, and medicine. Altair is leading this shift by developing tools that scale with next-gen hardware, ensuring efficient workload management for exascale systems.  

## Altair’s Role in Exascale  
Altair’s **PBS Professional** orchestrates exascale workflows, enabling:  
- **Aurora Supercomputer**: Argonne’s exascale system (2 quintillion calculations/sec) powered by PBS Professional, supporting transformative science.  
- **Scalability**: Handles 50,000 nodes, 10 million jobs/hour, and 1,000 concurrent users with sub-10-second job execution.

## Case Studies: Real-World Impact  
### 1. **Gordon Bell Prize for Virus Variant Tracking**  
Argonne researchers used Polaris (GPU-accelerated) and PBS Professional to track SARS-CoV-2 variants, winning the 2022 Gordon Bell Special Prize. This AI-driven approach accelerates vaccine development by analyzing viral mutations at scale.  

### 2. **3D Supernova Simulations**  
Princeton and UC Berkeley researchers leveraged Polaris and PBS Professional to create the largest 3D supernova simulations, advancing astrophysics by modeling stellar explosions and element distribution.  

### 3. **NIAR’s Radioss Scalability Study**  
Altair’s Radioss structural analysis solver demonstrated twice the speed of competitors on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, showcasing exascale-ready performance for crash analysis.
