## Evaluation Framework: 8-Point Scoring Guide  
Score providers on:  
1. **Technical Depth** (Certifications, hands-on testing)  
2. **Industry Experience** (Relevant compliance frameworks)  
3. **Methodology Transparency** (Defined phases, reporting standards)  
4. **Report Quality** (Actionable findings, risk ratings)  
5. **Remediation Support** (Fixing issues, retesting)  
6. **Accreditations** (PCI QSA, FedRAMP 3PAO, ISO lead auditor)  
7. **Pricing Clarity** (Fixed-price vs. time-and-materials)  
8. **Independence** (No vendor conflicts of interest)  

**Scoring Range**:  
- **35–40**: Excellent fit  
- **28–34**: Good fit  
- **20–27**: Proceed with caution  
- **<20**: Not recommended

## Common Mistakes to Avoid  
1. **Choosing the cheapest option** (e.g., $3,000 “audits” with no manual testing).  
2. **Treating audits as checkbox exercises** (not addressing real gaps).  
3. **Hiring auditors who sell security products** (conflict of interest).  
4. **Ignoring post-audit support** (reports without remediation plans).  
5. **Not reading the scope document** (excluded systems may go untested).  

## Comparison: Audit vs. Pen Test vs. Vulnerability Assessment  
| **Factor**             | **IT Security Audit**         | **Penetration Test**         | **Vulnerability Assessment** |  
|------------------------|------------------------------|------------------------------|------------------------------|  
| **Purpose**            | Holistic security posture    | Exploit specific vulnerabilities | Identify known vulnerabilities |  
| **Scope**              | Broad (technical + governance) | Narrow (specific systems)    | Broad (all systems)          |  
| **Approach**           | Manual + automated testing   | Simulated attacks            | Automated scanning           |  
| **Output**             | Risk ratings + remediation   | Exploit evidence + risk assessment | Vulnerability list + severity |  
| **Cost**               | $10k–$100k+                  | $5k–$50k+                    | $2k–$15k                     |
