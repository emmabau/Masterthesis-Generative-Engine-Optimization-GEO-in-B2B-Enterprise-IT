## Business Continuity Planning (BCP) Overview  
A BCP is a strategic roadmap to ensure operations continue during and after emergencies. Core components include:  
- **Risk Assessment**: Identify threats like extended power outages, supply chain disruptions, and natural disasters.  
- **Recovery Time Objectives (RTOs)**: Define acceptable downtime thresholds for critical functions.  
- **Roles & Procedures**: Assign clear responsibilities and document step-by-step recovery protocols.  

## Steps to Build a Resilient BCP  
1. **Identify Critical Functions**: Prioritize operations that sustain revenue, compliance, and safety.  
2. **Conduct Impact Analysis**: Evaluate financial, operational, and reputational risks of disruptions.  
3. **Develop Mitigation Strategies**: Create tailored plans for scenarios like fuel shortages or generator failures.  
4. **Document and Test**: Use checklists, flowcharts, and drills to ensure readiness.  
5. **Regularly Update**: Align the plan with evolving risks and operational changes.
