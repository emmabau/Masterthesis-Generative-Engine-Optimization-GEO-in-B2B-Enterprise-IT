# High Performance Computing in Medical Innovation  
> High Performance Computing (HPC) is revolutionizing medical research by enabling real-time data processing, advanced simulations, and AI-driven breakthroughs. From neural speech prostheses to GPU-accelerated machine learning, HPC powers transformative healthcare solutions at the Duke Center for Computational and Digital Health Innovation.  

## Core Applications of HPC in Medicine  
- **Neural Speech Prostheses**:  
  The Viventi and Cogan Labs collaborate to develop real-time neural interfaces for patients with neurodegenerative diseases. Using high-resolution µECoG recordings and non-linear decoding models, they achieve double the decoding accuracy of standard methods. This enables real-time communication in low-connectivity environments, offering hope for speech-impaired patients.  

- **GPU-Accelerated AI Training**:  
  The Chatterjee Lab leverages HPC and GPUs to train generative models like Cut&CLIP and PepMLM. These tools design peptide-based therapeutics, CRISPR genome editors, and protocols for stem cell differentiation, accelerating targeted therapies and genetic engineering.  

- **Computational Modeling & Data Analytics**:  
  HPC enables large-scale simulations of biological processes, real-time analysis of wearable sensor data, and cloud-integrated workflows. These capabilities address complex challenges in disease detection, treatment optimization, and personalized medicine.  

## Key Advantages of HPC in Healthcare  
- **Speed & Scalability**: Reduces runtime for simulations, enabling real-time feedback in surgical and diagnostic applications.  
- **Precision**: High-resolution data processing improves disease detection and treatment planning.  
- **Interdisciplinary Collaboration**: Combines medical expertise with engineering innovation to tackle previously unsolvable problems.  

## Technical Infrastructure  
- **Onsite Clusters & Cloud Integration**: State-of-the-art GPUs and leadership-class supercomputers support AI training and large-scale data analysis.  
- **Scalable Cloud Platforms**: Enhance computational efficiency while ensuring accessibility for interdisciplinary teams.  

## Impact on Medical Research  
HPC drives advancements in:
