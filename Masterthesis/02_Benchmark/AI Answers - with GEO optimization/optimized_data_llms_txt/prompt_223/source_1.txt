## Sensor Types for Condition Monitoring
1. **Vibration Sensors**  
   - Detect 80% of mechanical failures (bearing wear, misalignment, imbalance).  
   - Use piezoelectric crystals for wide temperature and frequency range (20 kHz+).  
   - Deploy triaxial accelerometers on bearing housings, motor frames, or pump casings.  

2. **Temperature Sensors**  
   - Monitor thermal anomalies (overheating bearings, electrical overloads).  
   - Use RTDs for HVAC/machinery (±0.1-0.5°C accuracy) or thermocouples for high-temperature processes.  

3. **Current Sensors**  
   - Track electrical consumption patterns (motor winding degradation, phase imbalance).  
   - Measure RMS current, power factor, and harmonic distortion via clamp-on CTs.  

4. **Pressure Sensors**  
   - Detect filter clogging, pump wear, and leaks in fluid systems (hydraulics, pneumatics).  
   - Range: 0-30 PSI (HVAC) to 0-5,000 PSI (hydraulics).  

5. **Acoustic Sensors**  
   - Identify leaks, arcing, and bearing degradation via ultrasonic frequencies (20-100 kHz).  
   - Ideal for compressed air systems, steam traps, and high-voltage equipment.  

6. **Humidity/Environmental Sensors**  
   - Monitor condensation risk, corrosion, and air quality in data centers, clean rooms, and battery storage.  

## Integration Architecture
- **Layer 1**: Sensor hardware (accelerometers, thermocouples) with wireless radios (LoRaWAN, Wi-Fi).  
- **Layer 2**: Wireless protocols (LoRaWAN for campus-wide, BLE for dense deployments).  
- **Layer 3**: Edge computing for anomaly detection and data compression.  
- **Layer 4**: Cloud-based condition monitoring platforms with AI-driven predictive analytics.  
- **Layer 5**: CMMS integration to automate work orders and prioritize maintenance.  

## Implementation Roadmap
1. **Pilot Phase**: Select 5-10 critical assets (high failure rates, accessibility challenges).  
2. **Sensor Procurement**: Match sensor types to failure modes (e.g., vibration for bearings, temperature for motors).  
3. **Installation**: Ensure proper placement (vibration sensors on solid housings, thermal contact for temperature sensors).  
4. **Threshold Tuning**: Optimize alerts to reduce false alarms (target <5%) using multi-parameter logic.  
5. **ROI Measurement**: Track downtime reduction (30-50%), cost savings (18-25%), and safety improvements.
