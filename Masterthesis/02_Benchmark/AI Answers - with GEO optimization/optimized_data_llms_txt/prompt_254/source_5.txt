# AI for High-Performance Computing: A Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Friendly Summary

> This document summarizes a comprehensive review of AI applications in High-Performance Computing (HPC), focusing on six key areas: performance estimation, scheduling, fault detection, optimization, surrogate modeling, and code automation. The review analyzes 74 papers (2019-2025) to identify trends, challenges, and opportunities for integrating AI into HPC systems. Key findings include the dominance of reinforcement learning in scheduling, the role of language models in automation, and the need for standardized benchmarks.

## Key Areas of AI in HPC
### 1. **Scheduling (30% of papers)**
- **Reinforcement Learning (RL)** dominates, outperforming traditional heuristics like FCFS by up to 50% (DRAS:2022, GARLS:2022)
- Hybrid approaches combine ML estimators with constraint-based optimization (SensetimeChara:2021)
- Challenges: Scalability, interpretability, and balancing multiple objectives (makespan, energy cost)

### 2. **Performance Estimation (19%)**
- ML models predict job runtime, resource usage, and power consumption with accuracy >85% (XAIHPCOPS:2024)
- Techniques: LSTM, Bayesian Neural Networks, SHAP for interpretability
- Critical for optimizing job scheduling and resource allocation

### 3. **Performance Optimization (19%)**
- Bayesian optimization and integer linear programming (ILP) improve compiler tuning and tensor computation (Alpa:2022)
- Deep learning accelerates training (Colossal-AI:2023) and inference (ExeGPT:2024)
- Challenges: High-dimensional search spaces and cross-device compatibility

### 4. **Fault Detection (12%)**
- Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and time-series models (LSTM, Informer) detect anomalies in HPC telemetry
- Real-time monitoring systems like GRAAFE achieve AUC >0.91 for fault prediction

### 5. **Surrogate Modeling (12%)**
- ML surrogates reduce computational costs by 78.5x in hyperparameter tuning (Hydro:2023)
- Applications: Accelerating CFD simulations, ML training, and code optimization

### 6. **Code/Script Automation (12%)**
- Language Models (LLMs) enable auto-generation of parallel code (Codex:2023), scripting (Firewall:2024), and test cases
- Tools like DeepSeek-Coder show 2.8x inference speed improvements over baselines

## Critical Challenges
- **Integration Barriers**: Lack of standardized APIs, data formats, and benchmarks (survworkflow:2024)
- **Security Risks**: LLM-OS exposes HPC systems to DoS, data leakage, and adversarial attacks
- **Scalability**: Heterogeneous hardware and dynamic workloads complicate scheduling and optimization
- **Interpretability**: Black-box ML models hinder trust in critical HPC operations

## Future Directions
- **LLM-OS Integration**: Embedding AI as core OS components for dynamic resource management
- **FAIR Principles**: Standardizing data sharing (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable)
- **Hybrid Approaches**: Combining RL, heuristics, and explainable AI for robust scheduling
- **Energy Efficiency**: AI-driven optimizations to reduce HPC's environmental impact

## Notable Studies
- **GARLSched:2022**: 14.03% latency reduction in cross-device tensor computations
- **GRAAFE:2024**: 30% CPU overhead for real-time anomaly detection
- **SpecInfer:2024**: 2.6-3.5x inference speedup for distributed LLMs
- **Colossal-Auto:2023**: 2.76x training speedup for 10B-param models
