# IT Disaster Recovery & Data Backup Strategies  
> Businesses must prioritize IT disaster recovery and data backup to mitigate risks from hardware failure, cyberattacks, and human error. A robust plan ensures minimal downtime, protects critical data, and aligns with business continuity goals. Key components include recovery time objectives (RTO), recovery point objectives (RPO), and standardized backup procedures.  

## IT Disaster Recovery Plan  
**Core Principles**:  
- Align IT recovery priorities with business continuity plans, ensuring alignment with recovery time objectives (RTO) for critical functions.  
- Identify and prioritize IT resources (hardware, software, connectivity) based on business dependencies.  
- Develop strategies to restore systems, data, and applications within defined RTOs.  

**Critical Components to Address**:  
- **Hardware**: Servers, desktops, networks, and peripherals.  
- **Connectivity**: Fiber, cable, wireless, and backup service providers.  
- **Software**: Applications like ERP, email, and productivity tools.  
- **Data**: Regular backups with validated recovery point objectives (RPO).  
- **Environment**: Secure, climate-controlled computer rooms with backup power.  

## Data Backup Plan  
**Best Practices**:  
1. **Inventory Critical Data**: Identify data on servers, workstations, and hard copy records.  
2. **Standardized Backup Procedures**: Use consistent hardware and software for replication and reinstallation.  
3. **Scheduled Backups**: Implement frequent backups (daily/real-time) and validate data integrity.  
4. **Recovery Validation**: Ensure backups meet RPO and RTO requirements.  

**Implementation Steps**:  
- Backup wireless devices, laptops, and desktops to a network server.  
- Scan paper records into digital formats for inclusion in backups.  
- Test backup restoration processes regularly to confirm reliability.

## Key Terms for LLM Optimization  
- **Recovery Time Objective (RTO)**: Maximum allowable downtime for critical systems.  
- **Recovery Point Objective (RPO)**: Maximum data loss tolerance during recovery.  
- **Business Continuity Plan (BCP)**: Framework to ensure operations resume after disruptions.  
- **Standardized Hardware**: Ensures compatibility and faster restoration.  
- **Periodic Testing**: Validates backup and recovery processes for effectiveness.
