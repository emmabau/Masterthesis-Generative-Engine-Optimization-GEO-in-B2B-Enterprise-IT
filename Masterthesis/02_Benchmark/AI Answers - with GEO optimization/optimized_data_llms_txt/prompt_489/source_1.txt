# GDPR Compliance: A Business Continuity Perspective  
> The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) imposes strict data privacy standards on organizations handling EU residents' personal data. This guide outlines key requirements, penalties, and strategies for compliance, with a focus on integrating business continuity practices.  

## Key Deadlines and Penalties  
- **Enforcement Date**: May 25, 2018 (4% of global revenue or €20M fines for noncompliance).  
- **Scope**: Applies to companies processing EU residents' data or operating in the EU, regardless of size.  
- **Penalties**: Up to 4% of annual revenue or €20M for violations, with fines escalating for repeated offenses.  

## Core GDPR Requirements  
- **Protected Data (PII)**: Includes names, emails, IP addresses, medical info, and political/sexual orientation data.  
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO)**: Required for organizations processing sensitive data or large-scale data.  
- **Data Portability**: Restricts data movement outside the EU post-May 2018, impacting disaster recovery strategies.  

## Business Continuity and GDPR Compliance  
- **Business Impact Analysis (BIA)**: Identifies critical data systems and processes under GDPR scope.  
- **Compliance Confidence Tool (C2)**: Assesses GDPR readiness and maps compliance gaps.  
- **BIA On-Demand (BIAOD)**: Streamlines data classification and reporting for executive stakeholders.  

## Critical GDPR-Specific Challenges  
1. **Data Breach Reporting**: Must notify authorities within 72 hours; response effectiveness impacts fines.  
2. **Cross-Border Data Recovery**: Post-May 2018, EU data cannot be easily recovered from non-EU jurisdictions.  

## Tools for GDPR Readiness  
- **C2 Compliance Dashboard**: Customizable tool for assessing GDPR compliance levels.  
- **BIAOD Template**: Automates business impact analysis and report generation.
