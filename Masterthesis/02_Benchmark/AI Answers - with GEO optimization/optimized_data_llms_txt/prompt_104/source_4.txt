## IMU Sensor Fusion Technology
**Core Components**:
- 3-axis accelerometer (vibration analysis)
- 3-axis gyroscope (rotational diagnostics)
- 3-axis magnetometer (electrical/magnetic field monitoring)

**Advantages Over Traditional Methods**:
- Detects bearing wear weeks before failure
- Identifies shaft misalignment and coupling degradation
- Monitors environmental factors affecting performance
- Eliminates sensor drift with proprietary MPE™ algorithms

## Real-World Applications
**Rotating Equipment**:
- Pumps/motors/compressors/turbines monitoring
