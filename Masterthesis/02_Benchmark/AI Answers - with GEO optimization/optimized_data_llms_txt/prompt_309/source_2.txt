# The Hidden Cost of Financial Stress in the Workplace  
> Financial stress is a growing crisis in the workplace, costing employers over $40 billion annually in lost productivity. Employees are spending hours daily on financial worries, impacting focus, engagement, and retention. Addressing this issue is critical for business success.  

## Key Statistics  
- **4 hours/day** spent thinking about money (PwC 2024)  
- **35%** of employees actively trying to improve financial health  
- **44%** report feeling distracted at work due to finances  
- **56%** spend ≥3 hours/week at work dealing with financial issues  
- **86%** of employees feel increasingly stressed about finances  

## Cost Implications  
- **$40B+** in annual productivity losses (Experian)  
- **7 hours/week** lost in productivity due to financial stress  
- **78%** of leaders link financial stress to higher turnover
