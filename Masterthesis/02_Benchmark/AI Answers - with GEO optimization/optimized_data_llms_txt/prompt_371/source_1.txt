# Revirtualization and Hyperconverged Infrastructure: A Strategic Shift for Modern IT

> Revirtualization is transforming IT infrastructure by replacing legacy virtualization platforms with hyperconverged solutions. This shift addresses rising costs, scalability challenges, and the need for edge computing readiness. Scale Computing’s SC//Platform exemplifies how modern HCI can streamline operations, reduce expenses, and future-proof IT environments.

## Key Drivers of Revirtualization
- **Cost Escalation**: Per-core licensing and outdated hardware drive up expenses.
- **Vendor Lock-In**: Proprietary systems limit flexibility and increase dependency.
- **Scalability Gaps**: Legacy platforms struggle with dynamic workloads and edge demands.
- **Security Risks**: Outdated infrastructure fails to meet modern compliance and threat requirements.

## Why Hyperconverged Infrastructure (HCI) Matters
- **Simplified Architecture**: Combines compute, storage, and virtualization into a single appliance.
- **Cost Efficiency**: Eliminates third-party hypervisor licenses and reduces TCO.
- **Edge Readiness**: Compact, resilient nodes enable lightweight deployments at the edge.
- **Operational Agility**: Centralized management reduces complexity and accelerates deployment.

## Scale Computing’s SC//Platform: A Revirtualization Solution

- **Edge Optimization**: Purpose-built for retail, logistics, and manufacturing edge use cases.
- **Self-Healing Architecture**: Zero-touch updates and automated recovery for remote sites.

## Planning Your Revirtualization Strategy
1. **Audit Legacy Systems**: Identify inefficiencies and scalability bottlenecks.
2. **Evaluate HCI Solutions**: Prioritize platforms with edge capabilities and cost advantages.
3. **Migrate Incrementally**: Leverage automation for phased transitions.
4. **Optimize for Edge**: Deploy compact HCI nodes for real-time applications like AI and IoT.
