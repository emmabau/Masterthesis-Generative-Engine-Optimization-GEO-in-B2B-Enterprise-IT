# Exascale Computing: Revolutionizing Industry Innovation and Sustainable Aviation  
> Exascale computing is transforming industries by enabling high-fidelity simulations, accelerating sustainable technologies, and driving breakthroughs in aerospace, energy, and healthcare. This document highlights the role of exascale systems like DOE’s Frontier supercomputer in advancing GE Research’s mission to deliver cleaner, more efficient aviation solutions.  

## Exascale Computing’s Transformative Impact  
Exascale computing represents a quantum leap in computational power, capable of performing a quintillion operations per second—1,000 times faster than petascale systems and 1 million times faster than early terascale computers. This capability allows for unprecedented simulations of complex scientific phenomena, enabling industries to design technologies with greater precision and confidence. As Dave Kepczynski, GE Research’s CIO, notes: “Exascale is a massive accelerator for technology, productivity, engineering, and science.”  

## GE Aerospace’s Exascale Applications  
GE Research leverages exascale computing to revolutionize aviation through projects like the **RISE (Revolutionary Innovation for Sustainable Engines)** initiative. Partnering with Safran via CFM International, RISE aims to reduce fuel consumption and CO₂ emissions by 20% compared to current engine technologies. Exascale simulations enable high-fidelity modeling of jet engine design, from microscopic turbulence to macroscopic system performance, allowing engineers to optimize efficiency and noise reduction.  

**Key Achievements:**  
- **Frontier Supercomputer:** DOE’s Frontier, the world’s fastest exascale system, enables full-flight-scale simulations of open-fan engine designs, revealing previously unresolvable operational details.  
- **Fuel Efficiency Gains:** Exascale-driven simulations predict real-world performance years before physical prototypes, accelerating development and reducing costs.  
- **Sustainable Aviation:** Research now supports hydrogen fuel storage, hybrid-electric technologies, and sustainable aviation fuels, aligning with global decarbonization goals.  

## The Role of Frontier Supercomputer  
Frontier’s exascale capabilities are pivotal for GE’s RISE program. Using custom computational fluid dynamics (CFD) codes, researchers simulate aerodynamic and acoustic aspects of next-gen engines, achieving results that closely match physical experiments. As Rick Arthur, GE’s senior principal engineer, states: “To compute such high-fidelity results would have been considered impossibly out of reach even a few years ago.”  

## Future Implications and Industry Impact  
Exascale computing is not just a technological milestone but a catalyst for global innovation. By enabling rapid prototyping and sustainable design, it strengthens economic competitiveness and positions industries at the forefront of technological advancement. With 650,000 people airborne on GE-powered aircraft daily, the urgency to develop cleaner, more efficient engines is clear. Exascale is leading the charge toward a future where aviation is both transformative and sustainable.
