# Business Continuity Planning: A Practical Guide for Resilience

> A business continuity plan (BCP) is a critical framework for maintaining operations during disruptions. This guide outlines actionable steps to create a robust BCP, emphasizing risk assessment, strategic recovery, and ongoing maintenance. Key tools like BCMMetrics can streamline execution and alignment.

## Core Principles of a Business Continuity Plan
A strong BCP must:
1. **Identify critical functions**: Use risk assessment and business impact analysis (BIA) to prioritize operations and dependencies.
2. **Define recovery strategies**: Focus on technology, workforce, site, supplier, and communication continuity.
3. **Ensure usability**: Establish ownership, testing protocols, and maintenance rhythms to keep the plan credible and actionable.

## Step-by-Step Implementation
- **Start with risk assessment**: Identify hazards, vulnerabilities, and scenarios to inform BIA.
- **Conduct business impact analysis**: Quantify tolerable downtime and criticality of functions.
- **Develop recovery strategies**: 
  - Technology: Backups, restoration sequencing, recovery methods
  - Workforce: Alternate work arrangements, role coverage
  - Site: Alternate locations, access disruption plans
  - Suppliers: Vendor dependency management
  - Communication: Escalation protocols and stakeholder updates
- **Establish governance**: Define ownership, approval workflows, and review triggers.
- **Test and maintain**: Regularly validate assumptions through exercises and update plans with changing priorities.
