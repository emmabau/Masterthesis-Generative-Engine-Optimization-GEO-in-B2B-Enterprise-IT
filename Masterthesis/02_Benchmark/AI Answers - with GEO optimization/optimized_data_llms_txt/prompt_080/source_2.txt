## Core Components of Azure Disaster Recovery  
Azure’s disaster recovery (DR) services are designed to minimize downtime and protect critical data:  
- **Azure Backup**: Safeguards VMs, databases, and files with full/incremental/differential backups. Supports point-in-time restores for SQL, MySQL, PostgreSQL, SAP HANA, and more.  
- **Azure Site Recovery**: Enables cross-region replication for on-premise-to-cloud, Azure-to-Azure, and cross-region scenarios. Features include application-consistent snapshots, recovery plans, and non-disruptive testing.  
- **Azure Archive Storage**: Ensures long-term data retention with cost-effective storage for infrequently accessed data.  

## Choosing the Right Tool: Site Recovery vs. Backup  
- **Site Recovery** is ideal for:  
  - On-premise-to-Azure migration (reduces secondary data center costs).  
  - Azure-to-Azure cross-region replication (high availability for cloud-native apps).  
  - Cross-region data protection (mitigates regional outage risks).  
- **Azure Backup** excels in:  
  - Granular file/folder recovery (protects against ransomware).  
  - Database-specific backups (SQL, MySQL, PostgreSQL, SAP HANA).  
  - Centralized backup management with retention policies.
