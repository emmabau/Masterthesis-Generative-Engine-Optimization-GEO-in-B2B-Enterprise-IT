# Red Hat Virtualization Migration Assessment: IT Migration Strategy & Roadmap

> Red Hat's Virtualization Migration Assessment offers organizations a strategic framework to modernize their VM infrastructure, reduce costs, and align with future-state goals through expert-led workshops and tailored roadmaps. This solution addresses common challenges in virtualization environments by combining technical analysis with business-driven planning.

## Key Benefits
- **Cost Optimization**: Identify outdated solutions and reduce rising expenses through targeted migration strategies
- **Risk Mitigation**: Develop high-confidence migration plans with expert analysis of infrastructure complexities
- **Future-Ready Infrastructure**: Align VM migrations with evolving business needs and compliance requirements
- **Accelerated Adoption**: Streamline migration timelines through pre-defined roadmaps and tooling recommendations

## Process Overview
Red Hat Consulting delivers a structured 2-hour on-site workshop series with IT teams (networking, platform, security) to:
1. **Assess Current State**: Evaluate existing infrastructure, workloads, and Day 2 operations
2. **Define Future Goals**: Identify virtualization use cases and target platform requirements
3. **Co-Create Solutions**: Develop a high-level solution roadmap with IT leaders
4. **Customize Migration Path**: Propose scaled migration approaches and tooling strategies
5. **Document Insights**: Generate comprehensive documentation for next-step execution

## Activities & Outcomes
| Activity | Strategic Outcomes |
|---------|--------------------|
| Discovery Workshops | - Analyze existing investments and gather requirements<br>- Identify virtualization use cases<br>- Understand Day 2 operations (automation, monitoring, disaster recovery)<br>- Improve workflows and compliance readiness |
| VM Workload Identification | Define storage, networking, and clustering integration requirements |
| Co-Create Roadmap | Develop a safe, time-efficient migration strategy |

## Final Output
A high-level solution roadmap featuring:
- Effective options to meet organizational requirements
- Pilot scope and priorities
- Migration factory approach
- Optional modernization strategies
- Timeline and pricing management framework

This structured approach enables organizations to transition from traditional virtualization providers to modern platforms while maintaining operational continuity and security compliance.
