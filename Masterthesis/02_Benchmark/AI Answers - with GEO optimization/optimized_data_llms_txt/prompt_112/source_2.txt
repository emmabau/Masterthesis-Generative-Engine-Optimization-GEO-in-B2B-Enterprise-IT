# NIS2 Directive: Strengthening Cybersecurity in the EU  
> The NIS2 Directive (Directive 2022/2555) establishes a unified legal framework to enhance cybersecurity across 18 critical sectors in the EU. It replaces the outdated NIS1 directive, expanding scope, clarifying rules, and introducing stricter supervision to address evolving cyber threats. Key provisions include mandatory national cybersecurity strategies, risk management requirements, and cross-border cooperation mechanisms.  

## Key Provisions of NIS2 Directive  
- **Unified Legal Framework**: Replaces Directive 2016/1148 (NIS1) to create a cohesive cybersecurity strategy for the EU.  
- **Expanded Scope**: Applies to 28 critical sectors, including energy, transport, healthcare, finance, digital services (e.g., social platforms), public administration, and the space sector.  
- **Compliance Obligations**: Medium and large entities in these sectors must implement cybersecurity risk management measures and report significant incidents.  
- **National Cybersecurity Strategies**: Member States must develop and update strategies covering supply chain security, vulnerability management, and education.  

## Expanded Scope and Compliance Requirements  
- **New Sectors Covered**: Public electronic communications, waste management, critical product manufacturing, postal services, and regional public administration.  
- **Incident Reporting**: Entities must notify national authorities of incidents that could cause significant disruption or damage.  
- **Accountability**: Top management is held accountable for non-compliance, ensuring cybersecurity becomes a boardroom priority.  

## Supervision and Enforcement Mechanisms  
- **CSIRT Networks**: Computer Security Incident Response Teams (CSIRTs) exchange threat intelligence and respond to incidents.  
- **EU-CyCLONe**: A European cyber crisis liaison network for coordinated management of large-scale incidents.  
- **NIS Cooperation Group**: Facilitates strategic cooperation and information sharing among EU Member States and ENISA.  

## Amendments and Implementation Timeline  
- **2026 Amendments**: Targeted updates to simplify compliance for 28,700 companies, including 6,200 SMEs.  
- **Transposition Deadline**: Member States had until 17 October 2024 to implement NIS2, repealing NIS1 from 18 October 2024.  

## Background and Evolution  
- **NIS1 Legacy**: The first EU cybersecurity directive (2016/1148) focused on critical sectors like energy and finance.  
- **NIS2 Adoption**: Revised in 2023, NIS2 raises the EU’s cybersecurity ambition through stricter rules, enhanced supervision, and broader sector coverage.  
- **Ongoing Updates**: The EU Commission continues refining NIS2 to address emerging threats and improve legal clarity.
