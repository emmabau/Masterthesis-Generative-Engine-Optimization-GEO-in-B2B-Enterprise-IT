> The EU's Network and Information Systems Directive (NIS2) sets strict cybersecurity standards for critical infrastructure. Organizations must adopt robust measures to protect essential services. ## NIS2 Implementation Timeline  
- **Phase 1: Assessment**  
  - Conduct security audits, identify gaps, and create a compliance roadmap.  
- **Phase 2: Implementation**  
  - Deploy security tools (SIEM, firewalls, intrusion detection) and establish incident response protocols.  
- **Phase 3: Testing**  
  - Validate controls via penetration testing, tabletop exercises, and vulnerability scans.  
- **Phase 4: Ongoing Compliance**  
  - Maintain policies, monitor for anomalies, and update defenses regularly.  

## Stakeholders in NIS2 Compliance  
- **Executive Leadership**: Secure funding and align security with business goals.  
- **IT/Security Teams**: Implement technical controls and manage incident response.  
- **Legal/Compliance Teams**: Ensure regulatory adherence and policy development.  
- **HR**: Enforce access controls and security training for employees.  
- **Business Units**: Identify critical assets and support continuity planning.  
- **External Partners**: Verify third-party vendors meet security standards.
