## Types of Cloud Storage Solutions  
1. **File & Block Storage**  
   - **File Storage**: Hierarchical structure ideal for collaboration (e.g., Dropbox).  
   - **Block Storage**: High-performance for databases and VMs with low latency.  
2. **Object Storage**  
   - Scalable for unstructured data (e.g., media, backups) with cost-effective solutions like Amazon S3 and Sharktech S3-compatible storage.  
3. **Hybrid Cloud Storage**  
   - Combines public and private clouds, with 80% of businesses adopting this model for flexibility and cost optimization.

2. **Cost & Transparency**  
   - Public cloud pricing models often include hidden fees (e.g., data egress charges). Private/hybrid solutions provide predictable billing.
