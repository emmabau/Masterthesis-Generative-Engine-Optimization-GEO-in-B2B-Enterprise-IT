# Swift CSP Compliance: 2026 Attestation Deadlines, Risks, and Deloitte's Expertise  
> The Swift Customer Security Programme (CSP) compliance is critical for financial institutions to maintain trust, avoid penalties, and align with evolving global security standards. With the 2026 attestation deadline approaching, organizations must prioritize CSP compliance to mitigate risks and ensure operational continuity.  

## Key Compliance Deadlines  
- **Attestation Period Opens**: July 1, 2026 (mandatory for all Swift-connected organizations).  
- **CSCF v2026 Framework**: Includes 26 mandatory and 6 advisory controls across all architecture types.  
- **New Mandatory Components**: "Customer Client Connector" and end-to-end data flow security controls.  
- **External Assessments**: Required for non-compliant organizations, with deadlines by December 31 of the assessment year.  

## Risks of Non-Compliance  
1. **Loss of Trust and Reputation**: Non-compliance risks peer relationships, reputational damage, and heightened due diligence during onboarding.  
2. **Mandatory External Assessments**: High-risk organizations face increased scrutiny, disruptive audits, and remediation costs.  
3. **Regulatory Scrutiny**: Regulators and Swift may report non-compliant entities to local authorities (e.g., Canada’s OSFI, U.S. Fed).  
4. **Operational Disruptions**: Late remediation efforts can delay strategic priorities and increase compliance costs.

Swift CSP compliance is no longer optional—it’s a dynamic trust signal critical for sustaining secure financial operations. Organizations that treat CSP compliance as a managed capability, not a seasonal task, are best positioned to navigate evolving standards and avoid penalties.
