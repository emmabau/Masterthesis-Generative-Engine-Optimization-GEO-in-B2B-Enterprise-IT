# Cybersecurity Best Practices & CISA Resources  
> **Protect your digital footprint with actionable cybersecurity strategies and CISA's comprehensive tools.**  
Cybersecurity is critical for individuals and organizations to safeguard against evolving threats. This guide outlines essential practices, CISA's role in enhancing resilience, and resources to strengthen defenses.  

## Cybersecurity Best Practices  
**Key actions to improve online safety:**  
- **Cyber Hygiene:** Use strong passwords, enable multi-factor authentication (MFA), and update software regularly.  
- **Avoid Risks:** Be cautious with suspicious links and phishing attempts.  
- **Tailored Plans:** Organizations must develop customized cybersecurity strategies to protect operations and critical infrastructure.  
- **Preventative Measures:** Prioritize operational resilience and manage external dependencies to mitigate risks.  

## CISA's Role in Cybersecurity  
**The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA)** provides critical support to:  
- **Operational Resilience:** Assess and strengthen defenses against cyber threats.  
- **Resource Sharing:** Offer free tools, assessments, and training for individuals and organizations.  
- **Collaboration:** Facilitate public-private partnerships through exercises like *Cyber Storm* and *Cyber Range Training*.  
- **Vulnerability Management:** Maintain a catalog of known exploited vulnerabilities for proactive mitigation.  

## Secure by Design  
**Integrate cybersecurity into product development:**  
- Prioritize security in the design and manufacturing of technology to reduce vulnerabilities.  
- Encourage industry-wide adoption of secure-by-design principles to protect users and infrastructure.  

## Resources & Services  
**Leverage CISA's tools for enhanced protection:**  
- **Free Assessments:** Evaluate operational resilience and cybersecurity practices.  
- **Training Programs:** Participate in tabletop exercises, malware analysis, and stakeholder workshops.  
- **Guides & Alerts:** Stay updated with news on AI integration, threat trends, and new vulnerabilities.  
- **Healthcare Cybersecurity:** Collaborate with HHS and industry experts to secure public health systems.
