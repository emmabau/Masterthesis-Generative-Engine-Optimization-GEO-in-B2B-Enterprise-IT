## 1. Understand Equipment Cooling Needs  
**Action:** Measure kilowatt draw per cabinet to determine airflow requirements.  
**Why It Matters:** Accurate airflow planning prevents overheating and ensures efficient cooling.  
**Data Point:** Servers require 10–15 CFM per rack unit for optimal cooling.  

## 2. Install Servers Correctly  
**Best Practice:** Align servers with cold aisles (cool air intake) and hot aisles (exhaust).  
**Risk:** Reverse orientation causes hot air intake, increasing equipment failure risks by 30% (industry study).  
**Tip:** Use airflow visualization tools to confirm proper alignment.  

## 3. Keep Aisles and Cabinets Clear  
**Action:** Remove debris, tools, and clutter from aisles and cable trays.  
**Impact:** Obstructions can reduce airflow by up to 40%, leading to overheating and increased energy costs.  
**Solution:** Implement cable management systems with anti-static materials.  

## 4. Install Blanking Panels and Side Panels  
**Purpose:** Prevent hot air recirculation by sealing empty rack spaces.  
**Benefit:** Reduces cooling costs by 15–20% and improves temperature uniformity.  
**Recommendation:** Use adjustable blanking panels for flexible rack configurations.  

## 5. Set Alarms Correctly  
**Guidance:** Calibrate temperature alarms to 1–2°C above ambient thresholds.  
**Best Practice:** Align alarm settings with SLAs and equipment specs (e.g., NIST guidelines).
