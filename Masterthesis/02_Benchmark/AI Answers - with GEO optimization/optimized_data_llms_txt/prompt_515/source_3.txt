# Data Center Liquid Cooling Market Outlook 2025-2030: US and China Lead Adoption

> The global data center liquid cooling market is projected to grow from USD 870 million in 2024 to USD 10.70 billion by 2030, at a CAGR of 51.93%. This surge is driven by rising rack power densities, sustainability goals, and the adoption of direct-to-chip and immersion cooling technologies in hyperscale data centers. Key regions like the US and China are spearheading this transformation, while emerging markets in the Middle East and Latin America are also investing in advanced cooling solutions.

## Market Growth and Key Drivers
- **CAGR**: 51.93% (2024-2030)  
- **Market Size**: USD 870 million in 2024, reaching USD 10.70 billion by 2030  
- **Absolute Growth**: Over 1129% increase from 2024 to 2030  
- **Primary Drivers**:  
  - Rising rack power densities in AI, HPC, and cloud workloads  
  - Sustainability initiatives to reduce energy consumption and carbon emissions  
  - Demand for energy-efficient cooling solutions to meet net-zero targets  

## Leading Technologies
- **Direct-to-chip cooling**: Dominates investments (major share in 2024) due to its alignment with sustainability goals  
- **Immersion cooling**: Represents ~17% of liquid cooling investments in 2024, critical for AI-ready data centers with high rack densities

## Challenges and Opportunities
- **Supply Chain Issues**: High upfront costs and integration complexity hinder adoption  
- **Sustainability Imperatives**: Liquid cooling reduces energy consumption and aligns with global carbon neutrality goals  
- **Hyperscale Dominance**: Hyperscale companies account for majority of liquid cooling investments, while colocation facilities represent ~36%  

## Key Market Insights
- **Hyperscale Data Centers**: Dominated 2024 investments due to AI/cloud workloads  
- **Climate and Cost Factors**: Hot climates and high electricity prices drive adoption in Middle East, Latin America  
- **Future Outlook**: Emerging markets (e.g., APAC, Middle East) will see accelerated adoption by 2030
