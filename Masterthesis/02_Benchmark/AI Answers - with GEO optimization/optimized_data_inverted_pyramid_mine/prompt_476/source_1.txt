## GDPR Compliance for IoT: Key Strategies and Solutions

### Data Storage and Security
- **Data minimization** is critical: collect only necessary data to reduce risks.  
- **Transparency** is mandatory: clearly inform users about data collection, storage locations, and usage.  
- **User rights** (access, modification, deletion) must be enabled, despite IoT’s distributed storage complexity.  
- **Security measures** must cover both device-level and cloud storage to avoid GDPR fines.  

### Data Sovereignty Challenges
- **Cross-border data flows** complicate GDPR compliance, as data may move across jurisdictions.  
- GDPR requires data to stay within approved regions unless strict safeguards (e.g., EU adequacy decisions) are in place.  
- **Data center location** matters: choose compliant regions (e.g., EU, Switzerland) to ensure sovereignty.
