## What is High Performance Computing (HPC)?  
HPC leverages supercomputers and clusters to perform complex calculations at unprecedented speeds, enabling tasks like weather modeling and AI training.  

### Key Applications of HPC  
- **Weather Forecasting:** Simulates atmospheric data for accurate, real-time predictions.  
- **Drug Discovery:** Accelerates compound screening to expedite vaccine development (e.g., COVID-19).  
- **Financial Modeling:** Enables high-frequency trading and risk analysis.  
- **Aerospace Engineering:** Optimizes aircraft design through aerodynamic simulations.  

## Why is HPC Architecture Important?  
HPC architecture relies on cluster-based designs with high-speed interconnects (e.g., InfiniBand) to ensure efficient data transfer and scalability.  

### Core Components of HPC Systems  
- **Compute Nodes:** High-performance servers with multiple processors and memory.  
- **Network Interconnects:** Technologies like InfiniBand or Ethernet enable low-latency communication.  
- **Storage:** Parallel file systems or cloud storage handle vast datasets.  
- **Software Tools:** Job schedulers (Slurm, PBS Pro) and frameworks for parallel programming (MPI, OpenMP).

## Challenges of HPC Deployments  
1. **Cost:** High capital expenditure for hardware and maintenance.  
2. **Complexity:** Requires specialized expertise for design and management.  
3. **Sustainability:** Energy-intensive operations demand efficient cooling (e.g., liquid cooling).  
4. **Data Security:** Protects sensitive datasets with encryption and access controls.
