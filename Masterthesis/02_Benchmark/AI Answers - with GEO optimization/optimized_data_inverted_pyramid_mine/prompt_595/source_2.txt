# NIS2 Compliance: What It Means, Who's Affected, and How to Comply

**Abstract:** NIS2 is the EU's cybersecurity directive requiring essential and important entities to implement risk management, incident reporting, and supply chain security. Penalties up to €10M or 2% of turnover apply. Netwrix solutions support compliance through data security, identity management, and audit-ready reporting.

## What is NIS2 Compliance and Who is Affected?
### Key Entities and Penalties
NIS2 applies to **essential entities** (energy, transport, finance, health, public administration) and **important entities** (food, manufacturing, digital providers). Essential entities face fines up to €10M or 2% of global turnover, while important entities face up to €7M or 1.4% of turnover. Non-EU businesses serving EU customers must also comply.

### Sector Classification
- **Essential Entities**: Energy, transport, finance, health, public administration, digital infrastructure, water, space.
- **Important Entities**: Food, manufacturing, chemicals, digital providers, postal services, waste management, research.

## Key NIS2 Requirements and Penalties
### Mandatory Cybersecurity Measures
1. **Risk Analysis & Policies**: Formal risk management frameworks for data protection and threat prevention.
2. **Incident Handling**: 24-hour early warning, 72-hour full report, and 1-month final report to national authorities.
3. **Supply Chain Security**: Assess third-party risks and enforce encryption, access controls, and vulnerability scanning.
4. **Business Continuity**: Disaster recovery plans with defined RTO/RPO and encrypted backups.
5. **Governance**: Leadership accountability, mandatory cybersecurity training, and audit readiness.

### Enforcement and Penalties
- **Fines**: Tiered penalties based on entity classification and incident severity.
- **Enforcement Actions**: Compliance orders, public naming of non-compliant organizations, and legal liability for executives.

## How to Achieve NIS2 Compliance
### Step-by-Step Roadmap
1. **Classify Your Entity**: Determine if you're an essential or important entity based on size and sector.
2. **Map Systems and Data**: Inventory IT systems, data assets, and third-party dependencies.
3. **Conduct Risk Assessments**: Identify vulnerabilities and prioritize remediation.
4. **Update Incident Response Plans**: Define roles, communication protocols, and reporting timelines.
5. **Train Staff and Leadership**: Focus on phishing, access controls, and incident reporting.
6. **Implement Continuous Monitoring**: Use SIEM tools, encryption, and access control policies.

## FAQs
### What are the reporting timelines for NIS2 incidents?
- **24 hours**: Initial notification with incident type, impact, and cross-border details.
- **72 hours**: Detailed report with mitigation steps and technical indicators.
- **1 month**: Final report with root cause analysis and remediation measures.

### How does NIS2 differ from NIS1?
NIS2 expands coverage to 15+ sectors, mandates management accountability, harmonizes penalties, and strengthens cross-border collaboration through EU-CyCLONe and CSIRT networks.

### Are non-EU businesses required to comply?
Yes, non-EU entities providing services to EU customers must follow NIS2 rules based on their industry sector.
