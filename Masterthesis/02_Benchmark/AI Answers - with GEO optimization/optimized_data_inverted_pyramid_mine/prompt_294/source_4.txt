# NIS2 Compliance: Requirements, Challenges, and Strategic Support

**Abstract:** NIS2 compliance mandates enhanced cybersecurity for EU essential and important entities, requiring risk management, incident reporting, and third-party oversight. Challenges include unclear guidelines, audit gaps, and alignment with frameworks like ISO 27001. ## What are the NIS2 compliance requirements?  
The NIS2 Directive imposes proportionate cybersecurity obligations on EU essential and important entities, including:  
- Risk management frameworks  
- Incident reporting timelines  
- Supply chain security controls  
- Governance oversight and documented evidence  
- Alignment with standards like ISO 27001 and DORA  

## What are common NIS2 compliance challenges?  
Organizations face hurdles such as:  
- Ambiguous interpretation of risk management measures  
- Limited third-party security oversight  
- Inadequate incident response processes  
- Lack of audit trails and regulatory evidence  
- Difficulty demonstrating operational resilience

## Key NIS2 compliance considerations  
### Applicability  
- Applies to EU-based entities in energy, transport, healthcare, finance, and digital infrastructure  
- UK organizations with EU operations or supply chains may also fall under scope  

### Costs & Penalties  
- Costs vary by size, sector, and maturity; include risk assessments, training, and audits  
- Non-compliance risks: fines, regulatory sanctions, and increased oversight  

### Documentation Requirements  
- Regulators expect: risk assessments, security policies, audit trails, and incident management records  
- Independent assessments strengthen compliance evidence  

### Relationship with ISO 27001  
- ISO 27001 provides a framework for NIS2 compliance but does not replace regulatory obligations  
- Alignment with ISO 27001 reduces implementation effort for mature organizations
