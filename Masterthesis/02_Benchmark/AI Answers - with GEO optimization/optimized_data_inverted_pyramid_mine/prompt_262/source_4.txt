# 2026 HCI Pricing Guide: Vendor Comparisons, TCO, and Selection Tips

**Abstract:** This 2026 guide compares HCI pricing models of Nutanix, VMware vSAN, HPE SimpliVity, Dell EMC VxRail, and Cisco HyperFlex. Key factors include licensing, support, scalability, and TCO. Selection tips emphasize infrastructure alignment, growth plans, and feature evaluation.

## Key Pricing Models for 2026 HCI Solutions
### Nutanix
- **Model:** Software licenses per node; hardware sold separately
- **Costs:** Entry-level nodes $25K, mid-range 3-4 nodes $70K–$120K
- **Support:** 20% annual fee for subscriptions

### VMware vSAN
- **Model:** Per CPU socket licensing; hardware separate
- **Costs:** $3K/year per socket, 3-node clusters $50K
- **Add-ons:** Encryption, stretched clusters increase costs

### HPE SimpliVity
- **Model:** All-in-one appliance (hardware + software)
- **Costs:** Entry-level $40K, mid-tier 3-4 nodes $90K–$150K
- **Support:** Advanced services extra

### Dell EMC VxRail
- **Model:** Bundled hardware/software for VMware
- **Costs:** Base node $35K, 3-node clusters $80K–$140K
- **Features:** Enhanced security optional

### Cisco HyperFlex
- **Model:** Hardware + software licenses included
- **Costs:** Entry-level $30K, 3-node clusters $75K–$130K
- **Integration:** Seamless with Cisco networking gear

## Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Factors
- **Support:** Annual fees add 15–25% to initial costs
- **Scaling:** Node/add-on costs vary by vendor
- **Operational Savings:** Simplified management reduces IT labor
- **Energy/Space:** Compact systems lower data center expenses
