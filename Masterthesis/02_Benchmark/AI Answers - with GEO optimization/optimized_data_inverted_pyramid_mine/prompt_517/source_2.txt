## What is Business Process Optimization?
Business process optimization enhances operational efficiency, reduces costs by up to 25%, and boosts customer satisfaction by 15–30%. It involves refining workflows to eliminate waste, streamline operations, and align with strategic goals. Key benefits include faster delivery times, improved resource allocation, and sustainable growth.

### Key Benefits
- **Efficiency Gains**: Optimized processes reduce cycle times by 20% and improve productivity.  
- **Cost Savings**: Eliminating redundancies cuts operational expenses, with manufacturing firms reporting 25% cost reductions.  
- **Customer Satisfaction**: Faster response times and higher-quality outputs increase retention rates by 20–30%.  

### Core Steps
1. **Set Clear Goals**: Define objectives like reducing bottlenecks or improving profitability.  
2. **Map Current Processes**: Visualize workflows to identify inefficiencies.  
3. **Identify Improvements**: Engage stakeholders to pinpoint pain points.  
4. **Redesign Processes**: Eliminate redundancies and automate repetitive tasks.  
5. **Implement Changes**: Train teams and monitor progress to ensure adoption.
