# Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery: NIS2 Compliance Essentials

**Abstract:** The NIS2 Directive mandates robust business continuity and disaster recovery plans to ensure critical operations during cyber incidents. Key steps include conducting a Business Impact Assessment (BIA), defining Recovery Point Objective (RPO) and Recovery Time Objective (RTO), and leveraging tools like Veeam’s Microsoft 365 backup solutions to minimize downtime and meet compliance requirements.

## What is Business Continuity?
Business continuity ensures organizations can maintain essential operations during disruptions like cyberattacks, natural disasters, or power outages. Over half of organizations face long-term impacts from such incidents, underscoring the need for proactive planning.

## What is a Disaster Recovery Plan?
A disaster recovery plan outlines steps to restore operations quickly after an incident, minimizing downtime. It must cover all critical applications and services, with tailored strategies based on a Business Impact Assessment (BIA).

### Key Steps to Create a Disaster Recovery Plan
- **Assess IT Environment:** Map applications, services, and interdependencies.  
- **Conduct BIA:** Evaluate financial and operational impacts of disruptions.  
- **Define RPO/RTO:** Set recovery targets (e.g., Veeam’s 5-minute RPO for Microsoft 365 data).  
- **Test & Document:** Validate recovery processes and create detailed documentation.
