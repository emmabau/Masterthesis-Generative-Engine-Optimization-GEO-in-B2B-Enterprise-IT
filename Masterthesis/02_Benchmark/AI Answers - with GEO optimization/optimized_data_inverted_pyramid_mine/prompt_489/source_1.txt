## What is the GDPR?
The GDPR (effective May 25, 2018) is a strict data privacy law for EU residents, requiring companies to protect personal data (names, emails, IP addresses, etc.) and face fines up to €20M or 4% of global revenue. It applies to any organization processing EU residents' data, including those with under 250 employees if handling sensitive data.
