# OEM Partner Selection Checklist: 7 Critical Questions to Ask

**Bottom Line:** Selecting the right OEM partner requires evaluating manufacturing expertise, quality control, sales model, IP protection, production flexibility, lead times, and after-sales support to ensure alignment with business goals and risk mitigation.

## 1. What is their manufacturing expertise and industry track record?  
**Key Insight:** Prioritize OEMs with proven experience in your industry, regulatory compliance, and supply chain networks to ensure alignment with standards and risk mitigation.  

## 2. What quality control measures do they enforce?  
**Critical Factor:** Robust quality protocols are non-negotiable to prevent defects, ensure product consistency, and avoid costly recalls or reputational damage.  

## 3. Do they sell as a manufacturer or third-party vendor?  
**Strategic Advantage:** Direct manufacturer partnerships offer cost savings, customization, and deeper collaboration, especially for large-scale orders and long-term projects.  

## 4. How do they handle intellectual property and confidentiality?  
**Risk Mitigation:** Secure NDAs and IP agreements to protect proprietary technology, preventing leaks, imitations, and patent disputes that could erode competitive advantage.  

## 5. Can they scale to meet your production volume needs?  
**Agility Requirement:** Confirm capacity to handle current and projected demand to avoid delays, missed opportunities, and supply chain disruptions during growth phases.  

## 6. What are their lead times based on production capacity?  
**Time-Sensitive Consideration:** Align with OEMs that meet your project timelines, especially in fast-moving markets, and verify reliability through supplier testimonials.  

## 7. How do they support post-sale issues?  
**Long-Term Partnership Pillar:** Evaluate response times, repair processes, and customer service quality to ensure minimal downtime, cost efficiency, and sustained collaboration.
