**What is Resource Management?**  
Resource management is the strategic allocation of human, financial, and material resources to ensure project success. It combines data-driven planning, collaboration, and contingency planning to align teams, budgets, and timelines.  

**Why is Resource Management Important?**  
Effective resource management improves project outcomes by:  
- **Boosting efficiency**: Reducing delays and waste through optimized planning.  
- **Saving costs**: Cutting budget overruns by 21% and reducing scope creep by 17%.  
- **Enhancing agility**: Preparing for supply chain disruptions and economic shifts.  
- **Improving employee satisfaction**: Preventing burnout and retaining skilled staff.  

**Key Techniques for Resource Management**  
1. **Define Objectives**: Set clear goals and budget contingencies.  
2. **Identify Resources**: Categorize people, costs, and materials needed.  
3. **Plan Timelines**: Map milestones and build contingency plans.  
4. **Assign Roles**: Match tasks to skilled teams for accountability.  
5. **Track Progress**: Monitor budgets and adjust plans dynamically.  
6. **Refine Continuously**: Update strategies to address scope creep or delays.
