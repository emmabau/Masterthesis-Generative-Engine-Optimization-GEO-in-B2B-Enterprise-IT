# Security Awareness Training: Mitigating Human-Driven Cyber Risks

**Structured Abstract:** Human error drives 95% of breaches. Security awareness training (SAT) mitigates risks by educating employees on phishing, AI threats, and safe practices. Modern SAT uses AI simulations and personalized paths to reduce vulnerabilities and build a proactive security culture.

## What is Security Awareness Training?
### Key Components
- Phishing and social engineering: Recognize malicious emails, smishing, QR code phishing, and voice cloning.
- Generative AI deepfakes: Identify fake video/voice communications mimicking executives.
- Password security: Create strong passwords and use password managers.
- Malware awareness: Understand ransomware, viruses, and prevention tactics.
- Data security: Comply with GDPR, HIPAA, and protect sensitive information.
- Physical security: Secure devices and report lost equipment.
- Safe internet usage: Avoid public Wi-Fi risks and secure networks.
- Incident reporting: Know how to flag suspicious activity promptly.

### Benefits
- **Reduces human error:** Training lowers phishing click rates and data mishandling.
- **Protects against AI threats:** Employees detect deepfakes, voice scams, and multi-channel attacks.
- **Meets compliance:** Aligns with GDPR, HIPAA, and ISO 27001 standards.
- **Reduces financial losses:** Cuts costs from BEC scams (e.g., $25M UK fraud) and regulatory fines.
- **Builds security culture:** Empowers employees to act as human firewalls.
