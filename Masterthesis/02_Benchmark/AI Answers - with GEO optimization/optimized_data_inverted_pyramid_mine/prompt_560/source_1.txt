**What Is Composable Infrastructure?**  
Composable infrastructure virtualizes physical IT resources (compute, storage, network) into software-defined pools, managed via a single application. It eliminates hardware-specific configurations, allowing real-time resource composition to meet workload demands. As explained in *What Is Composable Infrastructure*, it’s a critical evolution beyond hyperconverged systems, enabling "infrastructure as code" for seamless automation.  

**Top 10 Business Advantages of Composable Infrastructure**  
1. **Cost Efficiency**: Reduces CapEx by eliminating overprovisioning and OpEx through streamlined tooling.  
2. **Simplicity**: Unifies infrastructure management, minimizing silos and training costs.  
3. **Operational Efficiency**: Automates IT workflows, accelerating service delivery and reducing manual effort.  
4. **Software-Driven Agility**: Leverages RESTful APIs for programmable resource orchestration, enabling rapid provisioning.  
5. **Flexibility**: Supports hybrid models, integrating legacy systems with modern containerized and bare-metal workloads.  
6. **Business Acceleration**: Templates cut provisioning times, aligning IT with agile business needs.  
7. **Modernization**: Aligns with DevOps and Agile, enabling dynamic, on-demand infrastructure for next-gen applications.  
8. **Automatic Integration**: New hardware is instantly recognized and added to resource pools, eliminating manual provisioning.  
9. **Frictionless Operations**: Enables automated updates, diagnostics, and firmware management without disrupting workflows.  
10. **Scalability**: Supports rapid scaling, closing the gap between development and operations teams.  

**Why Composable Infrastructure Matters**  
IDC highlights that composable infrastructure aggregates compute, storage, and fabric into shared pools, delivering on-demand resources for mission-critical workloads. This shift to "infrastructure as code" is pivotal for enterprises seeking to balance innovation with security and governance.
