# Is Your Business Ready for NIS2?  
**Bottom Line:** NIS2 (Network and Information Systems Directive 2), effective October 2024, mandates robust data backup strategies for EU-based and global organizations. Non-compliance risks fines up to €10M and reputational damage. Prioritize risk management, incident response, and supply chain security to align with NIS2 requirements.  

## Why NIS2 Matters for Your Data Backup Strategy  
### Key Requirements Under NIS2  
- **Incident Reporting:** Report major cyber incidents within 24 hours.  
- **Risk Mitigation:** Regular backups ensure data recovery during breaches.  
- **Business Continuity:** Rapid restoration prevents operational downtime.  
- **Supply Chain Security:** Vet third-party backup providers to meet NIS2’s "aggressive actor" safeguards.  

### Who Is Affected by NIS2?  
NIS2 applies to **essential** and **important** entities, including:  
- Energy, transport, banking, healthcare, and digital infrastructure sectors.  
- Organizations with >50 employees or €10M+ annual revenue, regardless of EU location.  

### Consequences of Non-Compliance  
- **Fines:** Up to €10M or 2% of global turnover for essential entities; €7M or 1.4% for important entities.  
- **Reputational Risk:** Breaches and non-compliance erode customer trust and operational resilience.  

## How to Build a NIS2-Ready Backup Strategy  
### Core Elements for Compliance  
1. **Assess Critical Data:** Identify and prioritize data essential for operations.  
2. **Adopt the 3-2-1 Rule:** Maintain three copies of data on two media, with one off-site.  
3. **Test Regularly:** Validate backup recovery processes to ensure reliability.  
4. **Secure Backups:** Encrypt and store backups to prevent unauthorized access.  
5. **Train Teams:** Ensure staff understand roles in data protection and incident response.
