**Inverted Pyramid Summary:**  
Node4's managed data centre solution enabled West Midlands Trains to enhance data availability, redundancy, and disaster recovery, while reducing downtime and carbon footprint. The project was completed within a tight window, ensuring minimal disruption to train services.

**What was the driving force behind the project?**  
West Midlands Trains (WMT) needed to improve backup, disaster recovery, and redundancy after a power outage at its legacy data centre. The company sought a managed data centre with advanced security, backup, and redundancy capabilities to ensure continuous service for its 1,300 train services.

**How did the solution address the challenges?**  
WMT migrated all 100 servers to Node4’s main data centre, which is located within an hour’s drive of Birmingham. The solution included redundant internet links, auto-failover, and 100% renewable energy, significantly improving reliability and reducing downtime from 99.97% to 99.99%.

**What were the key innovations?**  
- **Local Managed Services Provider:** Node4’s proximity to Birmingham ensured fast emergency access and minimal impact on train services during the migration.  
- **Hybrid Cloud Integration:** Node4 created a hybrid cloud environment, allowing WMT to seamlessly connect cloud and non-cloud storage and network components.  
- **Automated Failover:** The use of two independent internet links and automated failover processes improved resiliency and reduced recovery time by at least 50%.  
- **Sustainable Energy:** Node4’s data centres run on 100% renewable energy, aligning with WMT’s ESG goals and reducing its carbon footprint.

**What challenges were faced during the project?**  
The migration had to be completed within a tight window, as train services operated around the clock. The team had to shut down non-critical servers at 11:00 PM, complete the migration by 4:00 AM, and ensure all critical systems were back online before the first train departed at 4:00 AM.

**What tangible benefits has the organization seen?**  
- **Improved Reliability:** WMT’s reliability increased from 99.97% to 99.99%, with a reduced risk of downtime due to power outages.  
- **Cost Efficiency:** Node4’s project management kept storage transformation costs under control and on budget.  
- **Enhanced Support:** Node4’s onsite support and proactive approach improved operational efficiency and reduced the time required for data management tasks.  
- **Sustainability:** The use of renewable energy supports WMT’s ESG goals and helps offset its carbon emissions from train operations.
