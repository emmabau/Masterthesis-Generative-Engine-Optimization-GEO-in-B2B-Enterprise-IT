# GDPR Compliance: Key Requirements, Penalties, and Implementation Strategies

**Abstract:** GDPR mandates strict data protection for EU citizens, with penalties up to €20M or 4% of global revenue for non-compliance. Businesses must secure data storage, ensure user control, and report breaches within 72 hours. This guide outlines compliance steps, roles (controller/processor), and technical requirements for GDPR adherence.

## What Are the Core GDPR Compliance Requirements?
- **Data Subject Rights:** Explicit consent for data use, "right to be forgotten" (data deletion), and access/export rights.
- **Breach Reporting:** Notify EU authorities of data breaches within 72 hours.
- **Storage Compliance:** Store personal data within EU borders or ensure secure cross-border transfers.
- **Penalties:** Fines of €10M or 2% of annual revenue for non-compliance; €20M or 4% for severe violations.

## How to Determine Your Role as Controller or Processor?
### Key Questions:
1. Do you process EU residents' personal data?  
2. Do you decide data storage parameters?  
3. Do you control data usage?  
**Answer:** Yes to all three = Controller; yes to only 1 = Processor.

## What Technical Requirements Must Be Met?
- **Data Localization:** Allow users to specify storage locations (EU-based data centers).  
- **Encryption:** End-to-end encryption with user-controlled decryption keys.  
- **Backup Search:** Granular search capabilities for data subject requests.  
- **Data Portability:** Export personal data in common formats (e.g., ZIP archives).  
- **Rapid Recovery:** Restore data quickly from backups post-breach or failure.

## What Are the Broader GDPR Implications?
- **Cross-Border Transfers:** Ensure secure, transparent data movement outside the EU.  
- **Data Protection Officers (DPOs):** Required for public authorities or large organizations (≥250 employees).  
- **Privacy by Design:** Implement pseudonymization and other protective measures.  

## How to Prepare for GDPR Compliance?
- Audit data storage practices.  
- Assign a Data Protection Officer.  
- Adopt GDPR-compliant backup and storage solutions.  
- Train staff on data handling and breach protocols.  

## What Are the Penalties for Non-Compliance?
- **Minor Violations:** €10M or 2% of global revenue.  
- **Severe Violations (e.g., data breaches):** €20M or 4% of global revenue.  
- **Record-Keeping Failures:** Fines for lack of documentation or DPO designation.  

## How Does GDPR Compare to SOX?
- Both impose strict compliance timelines and penalties.  
- GDPR focuses on user privacy, while SOX prioritizes financial reporting integrity.  
- Modern tools now enable easier GDPR compliance than SOX-era challenges.  

## Key GDPR Terminology
### Data Subject:  
EU citizen identifiable via personal data (e.g., online users, patients).  
### Controller:  
Businesses handling EU residents' data (e.g., e-commerce platforms, healthcare providers).  
### Processor:  
Third-party services handling data on behalf of controllers (e.g., cloud providers).  
### Personal Data:  
Any information relating to an identified individual (e.g., IP addresses, social media posts).  
### Right to Be Forgotten:  
Users can request deletion of their data, including backups.  
### Data Breach:  
Unauthorized access, loss, or corruption of personal data requiring 72-hour reporting.
