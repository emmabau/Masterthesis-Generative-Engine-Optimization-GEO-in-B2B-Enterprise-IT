# HPC as a Strategic National Asset: Sustaining U.S. Leadership in the Exascale Era

## Core Takeaway
High-Performance Computing (HPC) is a cornerstone of U.S. economic competitiveness, national security, and scientific innovation. Exascale computing unlocks breakthroughs in aerospace, healthcare, energy, and defense, but sustained investment in HPC infrastructure, software, and workforce development is critical to maintain U.S. leadership.

## Why HPC Matters
### Strategic National Capability
HPC drives U.S. competitiveness in advanced computing, enabling breakthroughs in AI/ML/DL, climate modeling, and national security. Exascale systems like Frontier (1.1 exaflops) and El Capitan (1.5 exaflops) represent a 1,000x leap in computational power over petascale systems, enabling unprecedented scientific discovery.

### Economic Impact
HPC delivers $452 in revenue and $37.60 in cost savings for every $1 invested, with applications in aerospace (Boeing's 787 Dreamliner design), automotive (BMI Corp's fuel savings), and life sciences (Procter & Gamble's material optimization). The U.S. HPC industry generated $3 trillion in economic value over 25 years.

### National Security
HPC underpins U.S. nuclear stockpile stewardship, defense innovation, and secure computing. Reliance on foreign HPC systems poses risks to national security, underscoring the need for domestic leadership in both hardware and software ecosystems.

## Key Applications
### Aerospace Innovation
HPC accelerates aircraft design (e.g., Boeing's 787 Dreamliner) and enables real-time simulation of complex aerodynamic phenomena. Exascale systems reduce wind tunnel testing by 70% and enable predictive modeling of turbulence and material behavior.

### Healthcare Breakthroughs
HPC powers pandemic response (e.g., Summit's role in COVID-19 drug discovery), cancer research (RAS gene modeling), and personalized medicine (Nicholas Volker's genome sequencing). It enables rapid simulation of molecular interactions and drug candidate screening.

### Energy Optimization
Exascale computing advances wind energy (ExaWind initiative) and smart grid management, reducing turbine inefficiencies by up to 40% and enabling real-time power grid optimization. HPC also accelerates clean energy innovation in solar and nuclear technologies.

## Policy Recommendations
### Fully Appropriating HPC Investments
The CHIPS and Science Act should fully fund the $280B authorized for HPC, including a 40% increase in Advanced Scientific Computing Research (ASCR) funding to $1.42B by 2027. The National Science Foundation (NSF) must prioritize regional supercomputing access and secure computing enclaves.

### Strengthening STEM Education
Expand STEM education to address the 400,000 annual shortfall in computer science graduates. Implement green card pathways for foreign STEM graduates and incentivize universities to expand computer science programs.

### Export Control Reforms
Align HPC export controls with allied nations to avoid stifling U.S. innovation while protecting national security. Remove controls on technologies no longer exclusive to the U.S. market.

### Industry-Academia Partnerships
Foster public-private collaboration through Regional Innovation Engines (RIE) and national lab partnerships, ensuring industry access to HPC resources for R&D and IP protection.
