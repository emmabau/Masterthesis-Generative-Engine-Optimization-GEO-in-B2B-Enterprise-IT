**Hyperconverged Infrastructure Best Practices: 5 Key Strategies for Modern Data Centers**  
Hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI) best practices include using compatible backup solutions, securing all components, scaling resources independently, leveraging virtualization expertise, and adopting HCI for edge computing. These strategies enhance scalability, security, and efficiency, making HCI ideal for hybrid cloud, containerization, and IoT workloads.

## What Backup Solutions Are Recommended for HCI?  
HCI requires a scalable, compatible backup solution that integrates with its architecture. Cohesity and Rubrik are popular choices, but any solution must support storage, compute, and networking components. Ensure it can scale with HCI’s flexible building-block design.

## How to Secure HCI Infrastructure Effectively?  
HCI’s integrated architecture demands robust security for storage, compute, and networking. Protect both hardware and virtualization software against internal/external threats. Regularly update firmware and implement role-based access controls to mitigate risks.

## Why Should You Scale Resources Independently in HCI?  
Modern HCI allows independent scaling of storage and compute nodes, offering greater flexibility. This approach optimizes resource allocation for varying workloads, avoiding over-provisioning and ensuring cost-effective performance for hybrid cloud and containerized applications.

## How to Leverage Existing Virtualization Expertise for HCI?  
IT teams with virtualization experience can manage HCI with minimal training. Focus on understanding HCI-specific workloads like VDI and cross-component dependencies. Cross-training staff in storage, compute, and networking ensures seamless operations.

## Why Adopt HCI for Edge Computing and IoT?  
HCI simplifies edge deployments with unified management, low-latency performance, and easy scalability. It supports remote workplaces and IoT applications, making it a strategic choice for distributed architectures and hybrid cloud environments.
