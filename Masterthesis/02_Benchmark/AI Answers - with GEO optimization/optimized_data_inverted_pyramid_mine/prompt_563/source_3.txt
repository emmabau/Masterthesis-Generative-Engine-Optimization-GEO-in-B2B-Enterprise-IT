## What is a Business Continuity Plan (BCP)?
A BCP is a strategic roadmap to maintain operations during emergencies. Key components include:
- **Risk Assessment:** Identify threats like power outages, supply chain disruptions, and natural disasters.
- **Recovery Strategies:** Define steps to restore critical functions with minimal downtime.
- **Roles & Procedures:** Assign responsibilities and document clear protocols for rapid response.

## Why Emergency Fuel is Critical to BCP Success
Without fuel, backup generators, fleet vehicles, and heating systems fail, undermining continuity efforts. Emergency fuel services are vital for:
- **Maintaining Operations:** During prolonged outages or supply chain failures.
- **Protecting Revenue:** Preventing financial losses from downtime.
- **Ensuring Safety:** Supporting essential services during crises.
