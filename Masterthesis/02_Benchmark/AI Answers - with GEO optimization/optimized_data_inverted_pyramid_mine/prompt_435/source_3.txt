# How to Reduce Cybersecurity Risks: A Proactive Guide

**Abstract:** To reduce cybersecurity risks, implement encryption, regular backups, phishing training, multi-factor authentication, and third-party vendor management. Proactive measures like attack surface analysis and physical security are also critical. Download our guide on scaling third-party risk management.

## 1. Encrypt Sensitive Data and Implement Strong Access Controls
- **Encrypt all sensitive data** to limit access to authorized parties. Use encryption software that alerts you to tampering attempts.
- **Store encrypted data securely**, ensuring only those with decryption keys can access it.

## 2. Prioritize Regular Backups with the 3-2-1 Rule
- **Backup critical data frequently** using the 3-2-1 strategy: 3 copies, 2 storage media types, 1 offsite location.
- Avoid operational disruptions by ensuring backups are reliable and secure.

## 3. Combat Phishing Threats with Employee Training
- **Train employees to recognize phishing emails**, which account for 3.4 billion global attempts annually.
- **Verify email addresses and links** before responding or clicking. Establish clear policies for sharing sensitive info.

## 4. Strengthen Password Security and Authentication
- **Use complex passwords** and enforce multi-factor authentication (MFA) to counter 80% of breaches linked to weak passwords.
- **Avoid password sharing** and store credentials in encrypted formats. Enable iOS Security Recommendations for password strength monitoring.

## 5. Manage Third-Party Vendor Risks
- **Audit third-party vendors** to mitigate risks, as 80% of breaches involve external actors.
- **Integrate vendor risk management** into your cybersecurity strategy, not just incident response.

## 6. Analyze Attack Surfaces and Secure Physical Premises
- **Conduct attack surface analysis** to identify vulnerabilities in IoT, software, and human factors like social engineering.
- **Secure physical infrastructure** with biometrics, keycards, and restricted access to prevent breaches from physical intrusions.

## 7. Deploy Killswitches and Monitor Network Activity
- **Implement killswitches** to shut down systems during suspicious activity and prevent large-scale attacks.
- **Regularly audit server logs** and use network forensic tools to detect and analyze threats.

## 8. Address Insider Threats and Human Error
- **Screen new hires** for potential risks and enforce policies to reduce employee negligence, which contributes to 94% of insider breaches.
- **Invest in firewalls** with full visibility and protection capabilities to block brute-force attacks and spyware.

## 9. Strengthen Policies and Legal Frameworks
- **Update cybersecurity policies** to include data breach consequences and legal actions for mishandling.
- **Conduct regular risk assessments** and penetration testing to identify policy gaps.

## 10. Stay Proactive with Continuous Improvement
- **Adopt a proactive mindset** to counter evolving threats, as 118 million people were impacted by breaches in 2021 alone.
- **Integrate cybersecurity into organizational culture** to ensure long-term resilience.
