# Structured Abstract (30-70 words)  
OEMs face critical challenges in transitioning to software-centric models, including alignment, digital transformation, life cycle management, and telemetry. Strategic alignment, centralized governance, and data-driven decisions are key to overcoming these hurdles and unlocking recurring revenue opportunities.  

## Challenge 1: Aligning for Holistic Software-Centric Solutions  
### Problem  
Software must be integrated from product conception, not treated as an afterthought.  
### Solution  
Prioritize strategic alignment at the top, ensuring all teams understand the value of software, analytics, and services. Keysight’s success highlights the importance of a “value capture strategy” and cross-functional collaboration.  

## Challenge 2: Aligning Software Management with Digital Transformation  
### Problem  
Customers demand transformative productivity gains, requiring OEMs to adapt to digitized, connected ecosystems.  
### Solution  
Establish centralized digital units and steering committees to align teams. Focus on telemetry for real-time insights and secure, scalable connectivity.  

## Challenge 3: Software and Asset Life Cycle Management  
### Problem  
Syncing software and product life cycles is complex, with fragmented release timelines.  
### Solution  
Implement centralized repositories for software inventory and product status. Coordinate updates with customers to ensure seamless integration and profitability.  

## Challenge 4: Continuous Connectivity with Installed Base  
### Problem  
68% of OEMs lack visibility into software versions in use, hindering service growth.  
### Solution  
Invest in telemetry to gather actionable data without security compromises. Communicate value to stakeholders and enforce compliance with security standards.  

## Challenge 5: Building an Integrated Ecosystem  
### Problem  
Fragmented tools and systems create “how-to” and configuration challenges.  
### Solution  
Simplify software intuitiveness and establish feedback loops for continuous improvement. Align incentives and prioritize user-centric design to reduce friction.  

## Key Takeaways for OEMs  
- **Alignment and management** are foundational for software strategies.  
- **Centralized software inventory** streamlines issue resolution and proactive support.  
- **Telemetry and security** are non-negotiable for scalable, customer-centric growth.  
- **Feedback loops** ensure product quality and serviceability.  
- **Data-driven decisions** are critical for navigating digital transformation.
