# ATPESC: Shaping the Future of High-Performance Computing

## Core Insight  
The Argonne Training Program on Extreme-Scale Computing (ATPESC) is a pivotal initiative for early-career scientists, offering hands-on training in supercomputing, AI, and HPC tools. Since 2013, over 900 participants have gained critical skills to advance computational science, with 2024’s program emphasizing AI integration and interdisciplinary collaboration.

## Program Overview  
**What is ATPESC?**  
ATPESC is an annual two-week training program at Argonne National Laboratory, designed to equip researchers with expertise in extreme-scale computing. It covers hardware, software, AI, and data-intensive computing, bridging knowledge gaps for attendees.

## Participant Experiences  
**Why do scientists attend ATPESC?**  
Participants like Lucas Sanches and Rui Fang highlight its value:  
- Hands-on tutorials replace passive learning  
- Networking connects researchers across disciplines  
- Exposure to cutting-edge tools (e.g., debugging software, high-performance toolkits)  
- Insights from industry leaders and national lab experts  

## Curriculum & Impact  
**What topics does ATPESC cover?**  
The program’s curriculum includes:  
- Supercomputing frameworks and visualization  
- AI/machine learning integration  
- Hardware-software co-design  
- Debugging and optimization techniques  
- Future trends like AI-driven computational science  

**How does ATPESC adapt?**  
Director Raymond Loy notes:  
- Increased AI focus in future curricula  
- Dynamic content tailored to attendees’ backgrounds and research interests  
- Collaborative learning through daily topic-specific sessions  

## Industry & Academic Collaboration  
**How does ATPESC foster innovation?**  
- Industry professionals like Ilker Topcuoglu share real-world applications  
- Academics like Jyotsna Rajaraman gain efficiency in large-scale computing  
- Partnerships with DOE facilities (e.g., ALCF, OLCF) provide access to world-class resources  

## Long-Term Benefits  
**What skills do attendees gain?**  
- Mastery of next-gen supercomputing tools  
- Career advancement in computational science and engineering  
- Cross-disciplinary collaboration networks  
- Practical problem-solving through hands-on demonstrations and expert mentorship  

## Conclusion  
ATPESC remains a cornerstone for training the HPC workforce, combining rigorous technical training with collaborative innovation. Its evolving focus on AI and interdisciplinary approaches ensures it remains a vital resource for researchers worldwide.
