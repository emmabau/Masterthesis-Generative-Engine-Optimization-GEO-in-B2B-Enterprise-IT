**What Are Data Center Security Best Practices?**  
Best practices include:  
- **Physical Security:** Multi-factor access (biometrics + badges), mantraps, and surveillance with 90-day retention.  
- **Network Security:** Segmentation, microsegmentation, encryption (TLS 1.3+, AES-256), and zero trust architecture.  
- **Identity & Access:** Role-based IAM, privileged access workstations (PAWs), and just-in-time provisioning.

- **Compliance:** ISO 27001, SOC 2, and regional regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS).

**How Does Zero Trust Work in Data Centers?**  
Zero trust replaces implicit trust with continuous verification. All workloads authenticate before communication, default policies deny traffic, and privileged sessions are recorded. NIST SP 800-207 provides the framework for implementation.

**What Is Data Center Compliance and Why Does It Matter?**  
Compliance frameworks like ISO 27001 and SOC 2 mandate baseline controls to reduce breach risks and legal exposure. Regional regulations (GDPR, HIPAA) add jurisdiction-specific requirements. Enterprises must maintain a compliance matrix mapping regulations to controls.

**How to Secure Hybrid Cloud Environments?**
