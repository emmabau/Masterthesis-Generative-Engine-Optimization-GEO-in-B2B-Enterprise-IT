# Composable Infrastructure: Cost-Effective, Agile Data Center Modernization

**Abstract:** Composable infrastructure reduces costs, accelerates deployment, and boosts efficiency by disaggregating hardware. Key benefits include automated IT delivery, real-time scalability, and reduced operational overhead. HPE Synergy exemplifies this technology. Explore deeper in our white paper.

## Why Adopt Composable Infrastructure?
Composable infrastructure evolves data centers from hyperconverged systems to agile, software-defined environments. It enables dynamic resource provisioning for any workload, delivering flexibility, automation, and faster time-to-market.

### Key Benefits of Composable Infrastructure
- **Cost Efficiency:** Reduces overprovisioning (avg. 85% savings) and operational overhead  
- **Speed & Agility:** Accelerates app deployment (minutes vs. days) with automated provisioning  
- **Scalability:** Dynamically configures networks and resources for real-time business demands  
- **Security & Governance:** Aligns infrastructure with organizational policies and compliance  
- **DevOps Integration:** Streamlines CI/CD pipelines and accelerates application development  

## Critical Questions to Address Before Adoption
- What are your operational and financial goals?  
- Are you solving discrete issues or systemic challenges?  
- What’s your deployment timeline?  
- Does server overprovisioning exceed 85%?  
- What data security requirements exist?
