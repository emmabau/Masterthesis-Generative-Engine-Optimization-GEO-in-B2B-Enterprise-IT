**Key Regulatory Frameworks**  
**HIPAA**  
HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) enforces strict controls over healthcare data, ensuring patient privacy and secure handling of health information.  

**PCI-DSS**  
PCI-DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard) mandates protections for payment card data, requiring organizations to safeguard credit card information from breaches.  

**GDPR**  
GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) governs data protection for EU citizens, imposing stringent requirements for data handling, transparency, and breach notifications.
