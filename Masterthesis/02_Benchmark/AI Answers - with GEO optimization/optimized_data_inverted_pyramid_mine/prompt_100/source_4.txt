# How Microsegmentation Enhances NIST Cybersecurity Framework Compliance

**Bottom Line:** Microsegmentation aligns with NIST CSF’s six functions (Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover, Govern) by automating access controls, improving visibility, and enabling rapid incident response, reducing risk and strengthening compliance.

## What Is the NIST Cybersecurity Framework?
The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) is a trusted roadmap for managing cybersecurity risk, comprising six core functions: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover, and Govern. It supports compliance with major regulations and is increasingly critical for organizational resilience.

## How Does Microsegmentation Support NIST CSF Compliance?
### 1. **Govern (Operationalize Cybersecurity Strategies)**
Microsegmentation automates enforcement of third-party access controls, provides real-time dashboards for audit readiness, and streamlines oversight, aligning with NIST governance requirements.

### 2. **Identify (Enhance Visibility to Understand Risks)**
By enabling real-time device and user inventory, learning network behavior, and preventing lateral movement, microsegmentation supports NIST’s Identify function for risk assessment and asset management.

### 3. **Protect (Secure Assets with Granular Access Controls)**
Microsegmentation enforces least privilege, applies just-in-time MFA, and isolates assets in secure zones, directly addressing NIST Protect requirements to prevent privilege escalation and lateral movement.

### 4. **Detect (Spot Anomalies Faster and More Accurately)**
Real-time traffic insights, integration with security tools, and robust audit trails enable rapid threat detection, meeting NIST Detect standards for proactive monitoring.

### 5. **Respond (Minimize Attackers’ Blast Radius)**
Automated segmentation and instant isolation of compromised assets reduce breach impact, aligning with NIST Respond requirements for targeted, rapid containment.

### 6. **Recover (Improve from Cyber Incidents)**
Detailed telemetry and immediate threat quarantine streamline recovery, ensuring compliance with NIST Recover goals for adaptive resilience and learning from incidents.

## NIST CSF 2.0 and Tier 4 Maturity
NIST CSF 2.0 emphasizes adaptive maturity levels. Microsegmentation supports Tier 4 implementation through dynamic policy enforcement, context-aware access decisions, and real-time monitoring of network and identity activity.
