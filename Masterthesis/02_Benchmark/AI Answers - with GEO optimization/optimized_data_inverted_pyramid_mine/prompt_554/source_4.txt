## What is Exascale Computing?
Exascale computing represents the next generation of supercomputing, delivering at least one exaFLOP (10^18 floating-point operations per second). This is 1,000x faster than petascale systems, enabling complex simulations, massive data analysis, and AI training at unprecedented scales. It combines advanced hardware, optimized software, and energy-efficient cooling to tackle scientific, industrial, and societal challenges.
