## What is High-Performance Computing (HPC)?
HPC clusters enable rapid, large-scale computational tasks critical for scientific research, industrial design, and pandemic response. They require specialized hardware-software integration to handle complex simulations and data analysis.

### Key Components of HPC Clusters
- **Networking**: InfiniBand or 25GB+ Ethernet for low-latency, high-bandwidth communication
- **Storage**: Seagate solutions provide high-density, cost-effective storage for PB-scale data
- **Compute Nodes**: CPU/GPU hybrid configurations tailored to application workloads
- **Software Stack**: Integrated management platforms for resource optimization

## Why HPC Matters
- Weather forecasting: 10-minute predictions for 2-hour forecasts
- Bioinformatics: Accelerated genetic analysis and protein prediction
- Materials science: Accurate molecular dynamics simulations
- Industrial design: Reduced computational time from days to hours
