## What Is Predictive Maintenance?  
Predictive Maintenance (PdM) uses IoT sensors, AI, and machine learning to monitor equipment health and forecast failures before they occur. Unlike reactive or preventive approaches, it leverages real-time data from machines to enable precise, data-driven decisions.  

### How Does It Work?  
Modern platforms like iFactory combine AI anomaly detection, cloud-connected IoT sensors, and automated work orders into one system. This enables real-time insights into asset degradation, fault severity, and failure timelines—days or weeks in advance.
