## Key Benefits of Predictive Maintenance
**Q: What are the financial impacts of unplanned downtime?**  
**A:** Unplanned downtime costs manufacturers $50,000/hour, with annual losses exceeding $1M for 20 hours of downtime. Direct costs include emergency repairs, overtime labor, and lost revenue, while indirect costs involve supply chain disruptions and customer relationship damage.

**Q: How does IMU sensor fusion improve predictive maintenance?**  
**A:** IMU sensor fusion combines 3-axis accelerometers, gyroscopes, and magnetometers to detect subtle anomalies in vibration, rotation, and magnetic fields. This multi-dimensional data analysis enables early detection of bearing failure, shaft misalignment, and coupling degradation, reducing downtime by 45-60%.

## Traditional vs. Predictive Maintenance
**Q: What are the limitations of traditional maintenance approaches?**  
**A:**  
- **Reactive maintenance:** High downtime costs and safety risks.  
- **Preventive maintenance:** Unnecessary part replacements and wasted labor.  
- **Condition-based maintenance:** Single-sensor limitations miss gradual anomalies.  
IMU fusion overcomes these by providing real-time, multi-parameter insights.
