## What is a B2B Procurement Platform?  
A B2B procurement platform is an online solution designed to manage all aspects of the procurement lifecycle between businesses. It automates tasks like purchase order creation, invoice processing, and supplier communication, reducing manual effort and errors.  

### Key Features  
- **Automation:** Streamlines purchasing, approvals, and invoice reconciliation, cutting delays and costs.  
- **Real-Time Analytics:** Tracks spending trends, supplier performance, and inventory levels for data-driven decisions.  
- **Supplier Management:** Monitors delivery histories, diversifies supplier bases, and strengthens relationships.  
- **ERP Integration:** Syncs procurement data with finance, inventory, and accounting systems for seamless operations.
