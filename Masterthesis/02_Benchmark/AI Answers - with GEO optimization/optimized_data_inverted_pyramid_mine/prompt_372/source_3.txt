**What Are the Core Benefits of Effective Asset Management?**  
- **Operational Efficiency:** Automation reduces errors, maximizes asset utilization, and optimizes resource allocation.  
- **Cost Savings:** Preventive maintenance and lifecycle management minimize downtime and replacement costs.  
- **Sustainability:** Proactive strategies extend asset lifecycles and reduce environmental impact.  
- **Compliance:** Real-time tracking ensures adherence to industry standards and regulatory requirements.  

**What Are the Key Challenges in Asset Management?**  
- **Asset Tracking:** Managing assets across multiple locations requires advanced technologies like RFID, GPS, and IoT.  
- **Maintenance Scheduling:** Poor planning leads to unplanned downtime and increased repair costs.  
- **Data Accuracy:** Inaccurate data hampers decision-making and operational efficiency.  
- **Compliance Risks:** Navigating evolving regulations demands robust systems and continuous monitoring.

**What Is the Difference Between EAM and General Asset Management Software?**  
- **Scope:** EAM covers the full lifecycle of high-value assets (e.g., manufacturing, utilities), while general software focuses on tracking and financial management across sectors.  
- **Features:** EAM includes maintenance scheduling and work order management; general software emphasizes compliance, depreciation tracking, and asset visibility.
