## How to Choose a Financial Advisor  
### 1. **What Type of Relationship Do You Need?**  
- **One-time planning:** Use robo-advisors or single-session advisors.  
- **Ongoing management:** Opt for dedicated advisors (e.g., Schwab Wealth Advisory, Fidelity Wealth Management).  

### 2. **How Is the Advisor Compensated?**  
- **Fee-only:** Prioritize fiduciaries (e.g., Vanguard, Edward Jones) for unbiased advice.  
- **Commission-based:** Avoid advisors pushing products for fees.  

### 3. **What Credentials Do They Have?**  
- Look for **CFP (Certified Financial Planner)** or **CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst)** designations.  

## Robo-Advisors vs. Human Advisors  
### 1. **Robo-Advisors**  
- **Best for:** Low-cost, automated investing with minimal fees.  
- **Pros:** Low minimums, tax-loss harvesting, and algorithm-driven portfolios.  
- **Cons:** Limited personalization and fewer services compared to human advisors.  

### 2. **Human Advisors**  
- **Best for:** Complex needs like estate planning, tax strategies, and long-term wealth management.  
- **Pros:** Personalized guidance, fiduciary duty, and multi-expert collaboration.  
- **Cons:** Higher fees (0.2%–1.5%) and less automation.
