# Structured Abstract  
Digital transformation (DT) enhances business efficiency but introduces cybersecurity risks. This study identifies vulnerabilities across sectors and recommends a staged cybersecurity readiness framework to mitigate risks during DT implementation.  

## Key Findings and Recommendations  
### Cybersecurity Challenges in Digital Transformation  
Digital transformation (DT) drives efficiency but exposes organizations to cyber threats like data breaches and ransomware. Emerging technologies (AI, blockchain, IoT) accelerate DT but also increase attack surfaces. Cybersecurity is critical to protect digital assets and ensure business continuity.  

### Sector-Specific Vulnerabilities  
- **Finance**: Cyberattacks on banks and fintech platforms threaten operational stability. Blockchain and AI offer solutions but require rigorous implementation.  
- **Healthcare**: Patient data privacy and medical device security are paramount. Digital tools like telemedicine and AI diagnostics face risks of data breaches and ransomware.  
- **Government**: Digital transformation in public services requires robust cybersecurity frameworks to prevent data leaks and ensure transparency.  
- **Industry**: Industrial IoT and automation systems are vulnerable to cyber-attacks, necessitating zero-trust architectures and real-time threat detection.  

### Cybersecurity Readiness Framework  
A four-tier framework is proposed to guide organizations:  
1. **Ad Hoc**: Reactive measures with no strategic planning.  
2. **Basic**: Minimal cybersecurity protocols without organizational policies.  
3. **Planned**: Structured strategies with regular vulnerability assessments.  
4. **Optimized**: Proactive risk management using AI and predictive analytics.  

### Recommendations for Businesses  
- Prioritize cybersecurity in DT planning with dedicated budgets and skilled teams.  
- Adopt encryption, multi-factor authentication, and continuous employee training.  
- Leverage AI-driven threat detection and cloud-based security solutions.  
- Align with global standards like NIST and GDPR to ensure compliance and resilience.  

## Methodology and Scope  
This systematic review analyzed 42 peer-reviewed papers (2019–2023) using PRISMA guidelines. Keywords included "digital transformation," "cybersecurity," and "cyber risk." The study highlights the need for integrated cybersecurity strategies across sectors to address evolving threats.
