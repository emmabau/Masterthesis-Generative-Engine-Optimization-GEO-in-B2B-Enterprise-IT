**In-Rack vs. Direct-to-Chip Cooling: What's the Difference?**
- **In-Rack Cooling**: A broad category of liquid cooling integrated at the rack, cabinet, or chip level. It includes methods like immersion cooling and direct-to-chip systems.
- **Direct-to-Chip Cooling**: A specialized form of in-rack cooling where coolant is delivered directly to hot components (e.g., CPUs). This method enables ultra-efficient heat transfer, as water can absorb 3,000 times more heat than air.
