High Performance Computing (HPC) is a powerful tool that enables researchers to process massive data sets and run complex simulations at incredible speeds. Its applications span from medical research to real-time surgical feedback and advanced machine learning. At the Duke Center for Computational and Digital Health Innovation, HPC drives breakthroughs in medicine by solving complex biological challenges and accelerating healthcare advancements.

**What is HPC?**  
HPC uses advanced computational systems to handle large-scale data and high-fidelity simulations. Researchers at the Center rely on cutting-edge technologies like GPUs, cloud platforms, and supercomputers to push the boundaries of medical research.

**Key Advantages of HPC**  
- **Speed and Efficiency**: Reduces runtime, enabling real-time feedback and faster medical research.  
- **Scalability**: Supports large spatial and temporal domains, producing high-resolution data.  
- **Versatility**: Powers AI training, computational modeling, data analytics, and cloud integration.  

**Applications in Medicine**  
1. **AI Training**: Advanced AI models analyze vast datasets to detect diseases and predict health outcomes.  
2. **Computational Modeling**: Simulates complex biological processes with high accuracy and speed.  
3. **Real-Time Systems**: Enables instant feedback in critical applications like neural speech prostheses.  
4. **Data Analytics**: Processes real-time data from wearables and clinical records for actionable insights.  

**Real-World Examples**  
- **Neural Speech Prostheses**: Researchers use high-resolution µECoG recordings to decode brain signals with double the accuracy of standard methods. Non-linear decoding models and real-time processing help patients with neurodegenerative diseases regain communication.  
- **Machine Learning in Biomedicine**: The Chatterjee Lab uses HPC and GPUs to train models like Cut&CLIP and PepMLM, accelerating protein design, CRISPR tools, and stem cell research.  

By combining HPC with interdisciplinary collaboration, the Center is transforming healthcare through innovative solutions that were once considered impossible.
