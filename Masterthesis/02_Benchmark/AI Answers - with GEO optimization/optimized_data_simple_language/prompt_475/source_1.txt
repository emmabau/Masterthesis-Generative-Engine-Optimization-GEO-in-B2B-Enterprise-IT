**Key NIS2 Requirements Overview**  
NIS2 (Network and Information Security Directive 2) imposes stricter cybersecurity rules on EU organizations, with enforcement expected by 2025. Non-compliance risks heavy fines (up to €10M for essential entities) and personal liability for executives.  

**Who Must Comply?**  
NIS2 applies to:  
- **Essential entities**: Energy, healthcare, finance, transport, water, digital infrastructure, public administration, and space industry.  
- **Important entities**: Food production, postal services, chemical industry, manufacturing, digital services, research, and waste management.  
- **Suppliers**: Vendors supporting essential or important entities must also comply.  

**Critical Deadlines**  
- **Implementation**: EU countries must transpose NIS2 into law by October 2024.  
- **Reporting**:  
  - 24-hour early warning for major incidents  
  - 72-hour full report  
  - 1-month final report  

**Core Compliance Measures**  
1. **Risk Management**:  
   - Multi-factor authentication (MFA)  
   - Data encryption (at rest and in transit)  
   - Network security (firewalls, IDS/IPS)  
   - Incident response plans and tabletop exercises  
2. **Corporate Accountability**:  
   - Leadership must oversee cybersecurity strategies and training  
   - Penalties for non-compliance include executive bans  
3. **Reporting Obligations**:  
   - Mandatory incident notifications to national authorities  
4. **Business Continuity**:  
   - Backup systems, crisis teams, and recovery plans
