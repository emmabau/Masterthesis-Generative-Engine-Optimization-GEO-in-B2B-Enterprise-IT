Government agencies are increasingly using high-performance computing (HPC) to handle massive data volumes and solve critical challenges. HPC has already helped with COVID-19 research, military simulations, and scientific breakthroughs. As challenges grow more complex, the need for HPC will only rise.  

Managing HPC environments—spanning cloud computing, AI, big data, and storage—requires careful planning. Agencies must act now to avoid complications. Here are three key steps to build a strong HPC strategy:  

1. **Prioritize Observability**  
   HPC workloads like genomic sequencing demand powerful systems. A hybrid HPC model combines on-premise infrastructure with scalable cloud resources, offering flexibility and cost-efficiency. However, traditional monitoring tools can’t track performance across hybrid environments. Agencies need advanced observability tools that provide real-time insights across all systems—on-premises and in the cloud. These tools enable proactive problem-solving, automation, and seamless management of complex workloads.  

2. **Secure HPC Systems with Encryption**  
   HPC systems are prime targets for cyberattacks. Encryption is essential to protect data both in transit and at rest. The Biden Administration’s cybersecurity order mandates encryption, and agencies must adopt it to prevent data breaches. Backing up data and encrypting backups ensures rapid recovery after attacks. IT teams should also balance workloads to avoid disrupting operations during backups.  

3. **Ensure Consistent Workloads Across Environments**  
   HPC workloads must run reliably whether on-premise or in the cloud. Agencies should design systems that allow seamless data and computation movement between environments. This flexibility ensures uninterrupted operations during outages and enables efficient use of big data at lower costs.  

By focusing on these steps, agencies can simplify HPC management, protect critical systems, and leverage data to address today’s and tomorrow’s challenges.  

The author, Brandon Shopp, is GVP, Product Strategy, SolarWinds
