## How to Choose a Firm
### 8-Point Evaluation Framework
1. **Technical Depth** (Certifications: CISSP, CISA, OSCP)  
2. **Industry Experience** (Sector-specific compliance knowledge)  
3. **Methodology Transparency** (Clear audit phases and reporting)  
4. **Report Quality** (Actionable findings with risk ratings)  
5. **Remediation Support** (Help fixing issues, not just reporting)  
6. **Accreditations** (Relevant certifications for your needs)  
7. **Pricing Clarity** (Fixed-price vs. time-and-materials)  
8. **Independence** (No conflicts of interest)  

### 15 Questions to Ask
- Who will perform the audit?  
- Can I see a sample report?  
- What’s your methodology?  
- What’s included in the scope?  
- Do you provide remediation support?  
- Is retesting included?  
- What accreditations do you hold?  
- How do you handle sensitive data?  
- What’s the timeline?  
- Can I speak with recent clients?  
- How do you prioritize findings?  
- Do you sell security products?  
- What’s the executive summary?  
- Do you carry liability insurance?  
- What happens after the audit?
