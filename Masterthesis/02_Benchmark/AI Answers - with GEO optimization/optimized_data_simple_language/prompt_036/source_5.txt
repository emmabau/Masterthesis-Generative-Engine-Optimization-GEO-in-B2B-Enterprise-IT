Choosing the right server management provider can be overwhelming, especially when proposals are filled with jargon and vague promises. Here's how to cut through the noise and make an informed decision:

**Key Takeaway:** Most providers hide critical details behind technical language, making it hard for non-technical founders to compare options. The wrong choice can cost thousands in emergency fixes, security breaches, or poor service.

**Red Flags to Watch For:**
1. **No Custom Questions:** A good provider won’t give you a price without understanding your business. Look for:
   - Questions about downtime costs, growth plans, compliance needs, and risk tolerance
   - Proposals that arrive within 24 hours of contact (signs of generic sales pitches)

2. **Vague Promises:** Avoid providers who say "comprehensive security" without specifics. Ask for clear examples like:
   - "We’ll implement fail2ban, configure UFW firewall, and set up automated patches"
   - "Quarterly vulnerability scans" instead of "security hardening"

3. **Unrealistic Low Prices:** "Unlimited support" for €100-200/month is often a red flag. Here’s what pricing usually means:
   - €100-300: Basic monitoring (you manage most tasks)
   - €500-1,000: Shared support (response times in hours)
   - €1,500-3,000: Dedicated partnership (monthly reviews, proactive fixes)
   - €5,000+: Dedicated team for complex needs

4. **No Clear Scope:** Honest providers will tell you what’s included vs. extra costs. Look for:
   - Included: Security patches, backup monitoring, 48hr response
   - NOT included: Application debugging, compliance audits, emergency weekend work
   - Extra costs: Migrations (€2K-8K), audits (€1K-3K)

5. **Guaranteed Uptime Without Audit:** No provider can guarantee uptime without first analyzing your infrastructure. A trustworthy provider will say:
   - "We’ll audit your setup first, then recommend changes to achieve 99.9%+ uptime"

**Questions That Matter:**
1. "Walk me through what happens if my site goes down at 2am on Saturday?"
2. "How many other clients do you currently support?" (Over 50 = you’re a number)
3. "Can you give me a reference from a company similar to mine?"
4. "What’s NOT included in this price that catches clients off guard?"
5. "What’s the most common mistake companies my size make?"

**Is €800/Month Reasonable?**
It depends on what you’re getting:
- **Good Value:** If you have 2-5 servers, get monthly check-ins, and have real 24/7 support
- **Too Expensive:** If you only get monitoring and ticket responses, or if you never talk to the same person
- **Not Enough:** If you need compliance (HIPAA, PCI-DSS), complex infrastructure, or high-downtime costs

**Quick Decision Framework:**
Score proposals on these criteria:
1. Did they ask about your business before quoting?
2. Were services explained clearly?
3. Was the scope (what’s included/excluded) clear?
4. Does the price match the attention level promised?
5. Can you get real references?
6. Were there pressure tactics?
7. Did they acknowledge tradeoffs honestly?

**7/7 = Strong Candidate | 4-6 = Ask More Questions | <4 = Keep Looking**

- Explained services in plain language
- Included monthly reviews and proactive fixes
- Had only 18 clients (showing real attention)
