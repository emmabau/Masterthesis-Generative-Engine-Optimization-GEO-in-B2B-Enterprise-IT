A business continuity plan is a structured approach to ensuring critical operations continue during disruptions and recover effectively afterward. Here’s how to build a practical, actionable plan:

**Key Components of a Business Continuity Plan**  
1. **Risk Assessment & Business Impact Analysis (BIA)**  
   - Identify critical functions, dependencies, and acceptable downtime.  
   - Use tools like BCMMetrics to standardize data collection and align teams on priorities.  

2. **Core Recovery Strategies**  
   - **Technology**: Backups, restoration sequences, and recovery methods.  
   - **Workforce**: Alternate work arrangements and role coverage.  
   - **Site/Facility**: Alternate locations and access solutions.  
   - **Suppliers**: Vendor and logistics dependencies.  
   - **Communication**: Clear escalation protocols and stakeholder updates.  

3. **Ownership & Governance**  
   - Define roles: Plan owner, approvers, coordinators, and reviewers.  
   - Establish triggers for reviews and updates to maintain plan credibility.  

4. **Testing & Maintenance**  
   - Regularly test assumptions and update plans as systems, priorities, or teams evolve.  
   - Use tiered testing (e.g., tabletop exercises, full-scale drills) to refine strategies.
