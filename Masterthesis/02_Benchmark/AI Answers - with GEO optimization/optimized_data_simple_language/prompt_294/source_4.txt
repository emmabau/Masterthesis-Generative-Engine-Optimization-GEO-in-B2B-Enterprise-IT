NIS2 Compliance: A Guide to Meeting EU Cybersecurity Standards  

What is NIS2?  
The NIS2 Directive is the EU’s updated cybersecurity regulation for essential and important entities, including sectors like energy, transport, healthcare, finance, and digital infrastructure. It requires organizations to implement appropriate technical, operational, and organizational measures to ensure robust cybersecurity and resilience.  

Key NIS2 Compliance Requirements  
Organizations must:  
- Conduct risk assessments and maintain documented evidence of risk management  
- Establish incident reporting processes that meet strict timelines  
- Implement supply chain security controls  
- Ensure governance oversight and management accountability  
- Maintain audit trails to demonstrate compliance  

Common Challenges in NIS2 Compliance  
1. Unclear interpretation of risk management requirements  
2. Limited oversight of third-party and supply-chain security  
3. Inadequate incident response preparedness  
4. Lack of documented governance and accountability  
5. Insufficient audit trails for regulatory scrutiny

Costs and Penalties  
Compliance costs vary by organization size, sector, and maturity. Typical expenses include risk assessments, governance improvements, and training. Non-compliance risks include:  
- Administrative fines (EU-level maximums)  
- Regulatory sanctions and increased oversight  
- Mandatory corrective actions  

NIS2 vs. ISO 27001  
While ISO 27001 provides a framework for information security, NIS2 is a legally binding directive. Many organizations use ISO 27001 as a foundation for NIS2 compliance.  

What Regulators Expect  
Documented evidence is critical:  
- Risk assessments and security policies  
- Incident management procedures  
- Staff training records  
- Audit trails showing control effectiveness
