When sourcing home appliances like dust mite controllers on Alibaba.com, choosing between OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) and ODM (Original Design Manufacturer) is critical. Here's a clear breakdown:

**OEM (You Design, They Build):**  
- You own the product design and specifications.  
- Manufacturer builds exactly per your requirements (appearance, materials, branding).  
- **Pros:** Full control, unique branding.  
- **Cons:** Higher costs, longer timelines (5 stages: design finalization → mold creation → sample approval → mass production → quality inspection).  

**ODM (Pre-Designed, You Brand):**  
- Manufacturer provides an existing design; you customize branding, packaging, and minor tweaks.  
- **Pros:** Faster time-to-market, lower upfront costs.  
- **Cons:** Less differentiation, potential competition from others using the same base design.  

For Southeast Asian sellers, the choice depends on your business stage, budget, brand goals, and speed needs. Neither model is universally better—each suits different strategies and risk tolerances. Prioritize what aligns with your long-term vision.
