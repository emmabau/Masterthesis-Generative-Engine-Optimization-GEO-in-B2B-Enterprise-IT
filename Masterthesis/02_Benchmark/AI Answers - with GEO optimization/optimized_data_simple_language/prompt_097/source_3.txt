What is IoT predictive maintenance?  
The Internet of Things (IoT) is changing how manufacturers operate. Predictive maintenance uses IoT to anticipate equipment failures before they happen, helping businesses save costs and improve efficiency.  

How does IoT help in predictive maintenance?  
IoT sensors collect real-time data (like temperature, vibration, and voltage) from machines. This data is sent to cloud storage and analyzed using AI and machine learning to predict potential issues. Maintenance teams can then schedule repairs proactively, reducing unplanned downtime.  

Key components of IoT predictive maintenance:  
1. **Sensors**: Gather data from machines and environments.  
2. **Data Communication**: Transmits data via Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or Ethernet to a central system.  
3. **Cloud Storage**: Stores data securely for easy access and analysis.  
4. **Predictive Analytics**: Uses AI to identify risks and optimize maintenance schedules.  

Industry applications:  
- **Manufacturing**: Monitors equipment to prevent breakdowns and boost production.  
- **Service**: Reduces reactive maintenance by predicting failures.  
- **Life Sciences**: Ensures lab equipment (like freezers and incubators) operates reliably.  

Benefits of IoT predictive maintenance:  
- **Lower costs**: Avoid expensive repairs by addressing issues early.  
- **Maximize asset use**: Extend equipment lifespan and improve productivity.  
- **Improve safety**: Prevent accidents by monitoring equipment in real time.  
- **Reduce downtime**: Schedule maintenance during low-impact times.  
- **Compliance**: Ensure equipment meets safety and regulatory standards.  

Getting started:  
1. Identify critical equipment with high downtime costs.  
2. Use a Computerized Maintenance Management System (CMMS) to track data.  
3. Monitor performance continuously and refine strategies as needed.  

By combining IoT, AI, and data analytics, predictive maintenance transforms how industries manage equipment, ensuring efficiency, safety, and long-term savings.
