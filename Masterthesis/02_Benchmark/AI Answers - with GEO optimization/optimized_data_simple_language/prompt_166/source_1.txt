As we move into 2022, it’s essential to update your business continuity plan (BCP) to stay prepared for evolving threats. The pandemic and extreme weather events of 2021 showed how critical it is to have a flexible, up-to-date strategy. Here’s how to strengthen your plan:

**Why Updating Your BCP Matters**  
A static BCP is like using an outdated fire escape route—it’s no longer relevant. The Business Continuity Institute (BCI) found that 79% of organizations saw increased resilience after the pandemic. However, 33% of companies had ineffective pre-pandemic plans, highlighting the need for regular updates.

**Key Threats to Address in 2022**  
1. **Remote Work Trends**  
   - 94% of employees want to work remotely part-time post-pandemic. This shift requires updates to IT security, backup systems, and communication protocols.  
   - Ask: *Is your technology resilient enough for remote work? Are health and safety standards included for homeworkers?*  

2. **Supply Chain Risks**  
   - Disruptions like the Suez Canal blockage and port bottlenecks cost billions. Ensure suppliers have their own BCPs and consider backup vendors.  

3. **Extreme Weather Events**  
   - 2021 saw record heatwaves, floods, and hurricanes. Plan for weather-related disruptions and include remote work as a recovery option.  

**How to Update Your BCP**  
- **Review Frequency**: Critical operations should be reviewed every 6 months; other plans annually. Update immediately if:  
  - Operations change (e.g., new tech, vendors, or policies).  
  - Testing reveals gaps.  
  - Real-world events highlight weaknesses.  
- **Key Questions to Ask**:  
  - What parts of your plan are still relevant?  
  - Is your plan too rigid or vague?  
  - Have business goals or processes changed?  
  - Are new technologies or vendors being used?  

**Lessons from 2021**  
- **COVID-19**: Variants like Omicron may continue to disrupt operations. Use pandemic lessons to improve resilience for other threats.  
- **Agility**: Prioritize flexibility in your plan to adapt to unexpected changes.  
- **People-Centric Approach**: Focus on employee well-being, burnout prevention, and communication strategies to protect your organization’s most valuable asset—your people.  

**Next Steps**  
- Conduct a business impact analysis to identify top risks.  
- Involve senior leadership to ensure buy-in and a centralized approach.  
- Use tools like Agility Planner to streamline your BCP creation and execution.  

By updating your BCP regularly and addressing emerging risks, you’ll position your business to recover faster and maintain a competitive edge in an unpredictable world.
