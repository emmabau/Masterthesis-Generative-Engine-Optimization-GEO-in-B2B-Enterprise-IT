The Argonne Training Program on Extreme-Scale Computing (ATPESC) is a premier initiative for early-career scientists seeking to master high-performance computing (HPC). This two-week program, hosted by the U.S. Department of Energy’s Argonne National Laboratory, equips participants with critical skills to leverage the world’s most powerful supercomputers. Over 900 attendees have joined since its 2013 launch, with this year’s cohort of 74 including doctoral students, postdocs, and industry professionals.

**Key Highlights:**
- **Hands-On Learning:** Participants engage in practical tutorials, avoiding passive lectures. As one attendee noted, “We actually did things instead of just listening to the speakers.”
- **Comprehensive Curriculum:** The program covers hardware, software, AI, machine learning, data-intensive computing, and visualization—addressing gaps in attendees’ knowledge.
- **Expert-Led Insights:** Industry leaders and researchers share real-world applications, teaching how to navigate challenges and push computational boundaries.
- **Networking Opportunities:** Attendees connect with peers and mentors, fostering collaborations that extend beyond the program.

**Participant Testimonials:**
- *Lev Gorenstein* (Globus Solutions Architect): “The atmosphere was an intellectual paradise—talented students, great speakers, and a well-oiled machine of organization.”
- *Rui Fang* (University of Pittsburgh): “This was one of the best summer programs I’ve ever attended. The networking led to unexpected research collaborations.”
- *Ilker Topcuoglu* (National Renewable Energy Lab): “The program revealed hidden layers of scientific computing I hadn’t considered.”

**Program Vision:**
ATPESC director Raymond Loy emphasizes adapting to emerging trends, such as increasing AI coverage. The Argonne Leadership Computing Facility (ALCF), a DOE Office of Science user facility, ensures participants gain tools like debugging software and high-performance toolkits to accelerate their research.

By blending theoretical knowledge with practical experience, ATPESC remains a cornerstone for training the next generation of HPC experts, bridging academia, industry, and national laboratories.
