Data centers are facing a critical cooling challenge as next-gen chips exceed 400W in power consumption. Air cooling, which has been the standard, is no longer sufficient. This article explains why liquid cooling is becoming essential and compares two key methods: single-phase immersion cooling and direct-to-chip cooling.

**What is Single-Phase Immersion Cooling?**  
This method submerges servers in a non-conductive dielectric fluid (like vegetable oil or synthetic hydrocarbons) that absorbs heat directly from components. The fluid is circulated through a heat exchanger to cool it down. Key benefits include:  
- No need for sealed containers (open baths work at atmospheric pressure)  
- Full heat capture from all components  
- Lower energy use and carbon footprint compared to air cooling  

**What is Direct-to-Chip Cooling?**  
This approach uses cold plates (metallic channels) to circulate coolant (usually water) directly over hot components like CPUs. While efficient for high-heat areas, it requires:  
- Additional air cooling for non-heat-generating parts (like DIMMs)  
- Complex retrofitting for existing hardware  
- Higher risk of catastrophic damage if leaks occur  

**Key Differences**  
| Feature               | Single-Phase Immersion       | Direct-to-Chip               |  
|----------------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------------|  
| Heat Capture         | 100% of all heat            | Limited to high-heat areas  |  
| Maintenance          | Easier (fluid can be drained)| Requires tube disassembly   |  
| Cost (TCO)           | Higher upfront but cheaper long-term | Expensive retrofitting |  
| Safety               | Safer (no water contact)    | Risk of electrical damage   |  
| Sustainability       | Lower PUE (energy efficiency)| Higher PUE due to air cooling |  

**Why Choose Immersion Cooling?**  
At Submer, we prefer single-phase immersion because:  
✅ Works with any existing hardware (no server modifications needed)  
✅ Captures 100% of heat for potential reuse (e.g., district heating)  
✅ Reduces energy costs and carbon emissions  
✅ Minimizes maintenance risks and downtime  

Whether you're upgrading for energy efficiency or future-proofing your data center, liquid cooling is the smart choice. But the best option depends on your specific needs—will immersion cooling be the right fit for your operations?
