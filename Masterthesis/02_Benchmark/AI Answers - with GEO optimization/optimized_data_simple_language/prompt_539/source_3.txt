What is a hybrid workplace strategy?  
A hybrid workplace strategy blends remote and in-office work to create a flexible, employee-centric model. This approach balances the benefits of remote flexibility with the value of in-person collaboration, helping companies adapt to modern work demands.  

Why adopt a hybrid strategy?  
Hybrid work boosts employee satisfaction, productivity, and work-life balance. It also allows organizations to reduce overhead costs while maintaining team cohesion. Studies show 72% of U.S. companies plan to adopt hybrid models by 2023 (Grant Thornton).  

Key steps to build a hybrid strategy:  
1. **Define clear guidelines**: Set rules for work hours, communication, performance metrics, and in-office attendance.  
2. **Clarify office purpose**: Focus the physical workspace on collaboration, team meetings, and creative tasks.  
3. **Choose the right tools**: Invest in platforms that enable seamless communication, task management, and workspace booking (e.g., Joan’s Room/Desk/Parking booking tools).  
4. **Foster consistent communication**: Use regular check-ins, clear channels, and shared goals to keep remote and in-office teams aligned.  
5. **Monitor and adapt**: Track feedback and metrics to refine your hybrid model over time.  

The 5Cs of hybrid work:  
- **Collaboration**: Encourage teamwork through shared goals and tools.  
- **Communication**: Prioritize transparency and accessible channels.  
- **Culture**: Maintain company values and inclusivity across all work modes.  
- **Connectivity**: Use technology to bridge physical distances.  
- **Care**: Support employee well-being and flexibility.
