6 Essential Tips for Optimizing Data Center Cooling  
Cooling is critical for data centers, but it’s not just about cranking up the AC. Proper airflow management is key to keeping equipment cool, efficient, and sustainable. Even if you colocate servers, you’re still responsible for airflow unless your provider handles it for you.  

Here are six actionable steps to improve cooling efficiency:  

1. **Know Your Cooling Needs**  
   Start by understanding how much power your equipment uses. This helps determine the airflow required to keep cabinets at optimal temperatures.  

2. **Install Servers Correctly**  
   Servers must face the cold aisle (cool air) and have the hot aisle (warm air) on the opposite side. Fans pull cool air through the equipment and exhaust warm air into the hot aisle. Installing servers backward causes fans to suck in hot air, risking overheating and equipment damage.  

3. **Keep Aisles and Cabinets Clean**  
   Clutter like boxes or tangled cables blocks airflow. Ensure aisles are clear and use organized cable management to prevent overheating.  

4. **Use Blanking Panels and Side Panels**  
   Install panels in empty rack spaces and on cabinets to block hot air from entering servers. This improves airflow and reduces energy waste.  

5. **Set Alarms Wisely**  
   Temperature alarms should balance between avoiding false alerts and catching real issues. Check your equipment specs and align with your provider’s SLA for accurate settings.  

6. **Partner with Your Colocation Provider**  
   Optimal cooling requires teamwork between you and your data center provider. Use tools like CoreSite’s CoreINSITE® platform for real-time monitoring of temperature, humidity, and power usage.  

For visual guides, check out CoreSite’s 60-second YouTube videos on airflow management. These resources offer practical tips to boost efficiency and sustainability.
