5 Ways IoT Drives Cost Efficiency

1. Prevent Downtime with Predictive Maintenance  
IoT sensors monitor machinery, energy use, and inventory in real time. Downtime costs up to $300,000 per hour (ITIC) and $2 million annually (median). Predictive maintenance cuts costs by 18-25% (IBM) by avoiding reactive repairs and reducing unplanned stops.

2. Optimize Resource Use  
Real-time data identifies bottlenecks and automates processes. For example, adjusting HVAC systems in large facilities saves costs while providing instant feedback on performance. This reduces waste and improves productivity.

3. Enhance Safety and Compliance  
IoT-enabled smart tokens track worker movements and alert on safety risks. Safety programs save $4-6 for every dollar invested (US Department of Labor), reducing accidents and ensuring compliance.

4. Reduce Unnecessary Maintenance  
30% of time-based maintenance is unnecessary. IoT enables data-driven decisions, prioritizing high-impact actions and avoiding costly overhauls.

5. Long-Term Strategic Value  
Continuous data collection informs future decisions, avoiding costly mistakes. IoT adoption is an ongoing journey requiring data-driven strategies to sustain cost efficiency.
