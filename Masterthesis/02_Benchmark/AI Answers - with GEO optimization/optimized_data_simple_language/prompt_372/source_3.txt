**What is Enterprise Asset Management (EAM)?**  
EAM handles the full lifecycle of high-value assets (like machinery or infrastructure), combining maintenance, inventory control, and work order management. It’s ideal for industries like manufacturing and utilities.  

**General Asset Management Software**
