Datto Competitors and Alternatives in 2025  
Updated April 17, 2025 by Rob Morrison  

**Why Backup Solutions Matter**  
In today’s digital world, data protection is critical for businesses of all sizes. Backup and disaster recovery (BCDR) systems help safeguard data from cyberattacks, human errors, and system failures. Datto is a leading provider of BCDR solutions, but no single system fits every business. This guide explores Datto’s strengths, limitations, and top alternatives for 2025.  

**What Makes Datto Stand Out?**  
Datto offers multi-layered backup solutions for businesses, including:  
- **Datto SIRIS**: Business continuity and disaster recovery with fast data recovery.  
- **Datto ALTO**: Enterprise-grade protection for small businesses and MSPs.  
- **Ransomware Protection**: Immutable backups and proactive detection to block ransomware.  
- **Cross-Platform Support**: Works across Windows, macOS, Linux, and cloud apps like Microsoft 365.  
- **Instant Virtualization**: Restore systems quickly using virtual machines.  

**Why Businesses Might Choose Alternatives**  
While Datto is strong, some businesses seek alternatives due to:  
1. **Cost**: Datto’s subscription model and hardware costs may exceed budgets.  
2. **Customization**: Limited flexibility for tailored workflows or hybrid storage.  
3. **Scalability**: Challenges handling large data volumes or expanding infrastructures.  
4. **Performance**: Slower recovery times for massive datasets.  

**Top Datto Alternatives in 2025**  

1. **Axcient**  
   - **Key Features**: Chain-free backup, instant virtualization, automated recovery testing.  
   - **Pros**: 50% cheaper than competitors, cloud-based, Linux-compatible.  
   - **Cons**: Learning curve, inconsistent support.  
   - **Pricing**: Free 14-day trial, subscription-based.  

2. **Bacula Enterprise**  
   - **Key Features**: AES-256 encryption, immutable backups, multi-cloud support.  
   - **Pros**: Flat-rate pricing, high security, deduplication for storage efficiency.  
   - **Cons**: Complex setup, no Windows support.  
   - **Pricing**: Tiered plans (Standard, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum).  

3. **Barracuda Backup**  
   - **Key Features**: Hybrid cloud protection, immutable storage, granular file recovery.  
   - **Pros**: Centralized management, ransomware protection.  
   - **Cons**: Slow large-scale backups, limited customization.  
   - **Pricing**: Subscription-based, contact for details.  

4. **Druva Data Resiliency Cloud**  
   - **Key Features**: AI-driven ransomware detection, pay-as-you-go pricing, cloud-native.  
   - **Pros**: No on-premises hardware, global availability.  
   - **Cons**: Limited on-premises support, potential cost growth.  
   - **Pricing**: Free 30-day trial, tiered plans (Foundation, Business, Enterprise).  

5. **Unitrends**  
   - **Key Features**: AI ransomware protection, instant recovery, hybrid cloud support.  
   - **Pros**: All-in-one appliance, compliance with HIPAA/GDPR.  
   - **Cons**: Outdated dashboard, limited third-party integrations.  
   - **Pricing**: Subscription-based, contact for quotes.  

6. **CrashPlan**  
   - **Key Features**: Unlimited file versioning, real-time backup, cross-platform.  
   - **Pros**: Easy to use, encrypted storage.  
   - **Cons**: No full-system imaging, slow initial backups.  
   - **Pricing**: Essential ($2.99/month), Professional ($8/user/month), Enterprise ($120/user/year).  

7. **Arcserve Backup**  
   - **Key Features**: Deduplication, hybrid cloud support, ransomware protection.  
   - **Pros**: Scalable for enterprises, strong security.  
   - **Cons**: High cost, complex setup.  
   - **Pricing**: Component-based licensing, contact for details.  

8. **Arpio**  
   - **Key Features**: AWS-native disaster recovery, automated replication, role-based access.  
   - **Pros**: Simplified multi-region recovery, pay-as-you-go.  
   - **Cons**: Limited to AWS, lacks customization.  
   - **Pricing**: Standard ($12k/year), Premium ($36k/year), Enterprise (custom).  

9. **Cohesity DataProtect**  
   - **Key Features**: Global deduplication, AI ransomware detection, multi-cloud support.  
   - **Pros**: Unified backup and recovery, flexible pricing.  
   - **Cons**: Resource-intensive, complex multi-cloud setup.  
   - **Pricing**: Free 30-day trial, subscription-based.  

10. **Acronis Cyber Protect**  
    - **Key Features**: AI threat detection, endpoint protection, hybrid cloud backup.  
    - **Pros**: All-in-one cybersecurity solution, fast recovery.  
    - **Cons**: Premium features require higher-tier plans.  
    - **Pricing**: Standard ($68/year), 3-5 year discounts.  

**Choosing the Right Solution**  
Select a BCDR solution that aligns with your business needs:  
- **Cloud-First**: Axcient, Druva, or CrashPlan.  
- **Enterprise Security**: Bacula or Cohesity.  
- **AWS-Only**: Arpio.  
- **Cost-Effective**: Axcient or Unitrends.
