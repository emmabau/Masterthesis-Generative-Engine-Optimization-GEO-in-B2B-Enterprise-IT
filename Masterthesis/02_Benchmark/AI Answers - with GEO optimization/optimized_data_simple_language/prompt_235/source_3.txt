1. **Compute Resources**: High-speed CPUs, GPUs, and FPGAs handle parallel processing for tasks like AI training.  
2. **Networking**: High-bandwidth, low-latency connections (e.g., InfiniBand) ensure seamless communication between servers.  
3. **Storage**: Advanced storage systems manage massive datasets for scientific simulations and machine learning.  

**HPC for AI and Enterprises**  
- **AI Model Training**: HPC accelerates training of deep learning models by processing petabytes of data quickly.

- **Competitive Edge**: Companies with HPC infrastructure can analyze data faster, leading to better decision-making and innovation.

1. **Heterogeneous Accelerators**: Combining GPUs, TPUs, FPGAs, and ASICs optimizes performance for specific tasks.  
2. **Energy Efficiency**: New hardware focuses on low-power chips, smart cooling, and dynamic power management to reduce costs.  
3. **Quantum-Classical Hybrids**: Integrating quantum computing with HPC solves problems like complex optimization and AI research.
