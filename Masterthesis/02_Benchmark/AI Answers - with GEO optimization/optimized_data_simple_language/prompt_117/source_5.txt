Abstract  
Modern buildings face growing demands to improve energy efficiency, a critical step toward global sustainability. This study identifies key factors influencing building energy use and proposes a smart platform to optimize energy management. By analyzing three real-world smart buildings, we demonstrate how integrating occupancy patterns, environmental data, and automation can reduce energy consumption by up to 23%. Our solution, City Explorer, uses IoT and AI to monitor and control systems like HVAC and lighting, offering a scalable model for energy-efficient buildings.  

1. Introduction  
Buildings consume a significant portion of global energy, especially in developed nations. Reducing their carbon footprint is a priority for researchers and policymakers. While many solutions exist, they often focus on isolated aspects of energy use. This work takes a holistic approach, combining real-time data, occupant behavior, and automation to create a comprehensive energy management system.  

2. Key Findings  
- **Critical Parameters**: Energy use in buildings depends on occupancy patterns, environmental conditions (temperature, humidity), and device usage.  
- **Smart Solutions**: Integrating IoT sensors and AI enables real-time adjustments to lighting, HVAC, and other systems, reducing waste.  
- **Case Studies**:  
  - A university building saw 40.5% of energy used for heating/cooling, highlighting the need for smarter thermal management.  
  - A test lab reduced energy use by 14–30% through automated adjustments based on occupancy and environmental data.  
  - An office building achieved 23% energy savings using a hybrid automation system.  

3. City Explorer Platform  
Our platform uses a modular architecture to monitor and control building systems:  
- **Sensors**: Track occupancy, temperature, and energy use.  
- **AI Analysis**: Predict energy needs and adjust systems dynamically.  
- **User Interaction**: Allow occupants to customize comfort settings while maintaining efficiency.  
- **Scalability**: Works in residential, commercial, and industrial settings.  

4. Practical Applications  
- **Occupancy-Based Controls**: Adjust lighting and HVAC based on real-time presence.  
- **Energy Forecasting**: Use historical data to predict and optimize energy use.  
- **User Feedback**: Provide real-time energy usage insights to encourage sustainable habits.  

5. Conclusion  
By combining data-driven insights with automation, our approach offers a practical path to energy-efficient buildings. The 23% savings in real-world tests prove its effectiveness. Future work will expand this model to include semantic technologies and broader IoT integration.  

Keywords: Energy efficiency, smart buildings, IoT, automation, City Explorer
