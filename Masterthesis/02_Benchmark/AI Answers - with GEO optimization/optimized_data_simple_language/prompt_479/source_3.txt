Is Your Business Ready for NIS2?  
The European Union’s new cybersecurity regulation, NIS2, is set to take effect in October 2024. This directive aims to strengthen cybersecurity across the EU by requiring businesses and organizations—both inside and outside the EU—to adopt robust measures to protect critical systems and data. Here’s what you need to know about NIS2 and how it impacts your data backup strategy.  

**What is NIS2?**  
NIS2 replaces the original NIS directive (2016) and expands its scope to cover more sectors and organizations. It focuses on creating a unified cybersecurity framework across the EU, ensuring networks and systems vital to society and the economy are protected.  

**Who is Affected by NIS2?**  
NIS2 applies to "essential" and "important" entities, including:  
- **Location**: Any organization operating in the EU, even if based elsewhere.  
- **Size**: Medium and large businesses (over 50 employees or €10M annual revenue).  
- **Industries**: Energy, transport, healthcare, digital infrastructure, food production, waste management, banking, and more.  

**Why NIS2 Matters for Your Data Backup Strategy**  
NIS2 emphasizes risk management, incident response, and business continuity. A strong backup strategy is critical for compliance, as it helps:  
- **Report incidents quickly**: Backups enable rapid assessment of cyberattacks.  
- **Mitigate risks**: Regular backups ensure data recovery in worst-case scenarios.  
- **Maintain operations**: Quick recovery prevents business disruption during attacks.  
- **Secure supply chains**: Backups must protect data shared with third-party providers.  

**Penalties for Non-Compliance**  
Failure to comply with NIS2 can result in severe fines:  
- **Essential entities**: Up to €10M or 2% of global turnover.  
- **Important entities**: Up to €7M or 1.4% of global turnover.  
Non-compliance also risks reputational damage and loss of customer trust.  

**How to Prepare for NIS2**  
Build a resilient backup strategy with these steps:  
1. **Identify critical data**: Prioritize protection for essential systems.  
2. **Adopt the 3-2-1 rule**: Keep three copies of data on two different media, with one off-site.  
3. **Test regularly**: Ensure backups are functional and recoverable.  
4. **Secure backups**: Encrypt and store backups to prevent unauthorized access.  
5. **Train your team**: Ensure staff understand their roles in data protection.  

**Choosing a Backup Provider**  
Select a provider with strong EU ties, like Macrium, to align with NIS2 requirements. EU-based companies are better positioned to meet data protection standards and ensure compliance across your supply chain.  

**Next Steps**  
NIS2 comes into effect on October 18, 2024. Start evaluating your backup strategy and cybersecurity measures now to avoid penalties and ensure business continuity.
