**What is Predictive Maintenance?**  
Predictive maintenance uses sensors, IoT, and AI to predict equipment failures before they happen. Unlike fixed schedules (preventive maintenance) or reactive fixes, it optimizes repair timing based on real-time equipment conditions.  

**Key Benefits**  
- **Downtime reduction**: 35-45% less unplanned downtime

- **ROI**: 5-10x returns in 2-3 years, with some achieving 40% cost reductions  
- **Equipment lifespan**: 20-30% longer  
- **Safety**: Improved workplace safety and compliance  

**Real-World Examples**  
- Ford saved 122,000 downtime hours and $7 million by predicting 22% of failures.  
- A medical device company cut costs by 25% and boosted customer satisfaction.  
- Tetra Pak reduced downtime by 140 hours through accurate failure prediction.  

**Calculating ROI**  
ROI = (Financial Benefits - Investment Costs) / Investment Costs × 100.  
A Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) approach includes all costs:  
- Initial purchase  
- Maintenance and operating expenses  
- Downtime costs  
- Training and risk management  

**Implementation Steps**  
1. **Assessment**: Identify critical assets and set goals (e.g., "30% fewer unplanned outages").  
2. **Pilot**: Test 3-5 assets using sensors like accelerometers (vibration), infrared cameras (thermal analysis), or ultrasonic sensors (leak detection).  
3. **Scale**: Expand to full systems, integrating with existing software and training teams.  

**Challenges & Solutions**  
- **Data quality**: Use IoT infrastructure and edge computing to improve data accuracy.  
- **Skills gap**: Invest in training ($2,000-$5,000 per person) and partnerships.
