**Key Challenges in IoT Troubleshooting**  
Remote device issues are often complex, costly, and time-consuming to resolve. Traditional methods like trial-and-error updates, technician visits, or third-party help tickets are slow and unreliable. The ideal solution? Fix problems instantly from your laptop using real-time insights.

**Why IoT Connectivity is Hard to Debug**  
IoT networks are often treated as "black boxes," limiting self-service data. The quality of troubleshooting depends on your tech stack, including:  
- **Network Logs**: Track device network connections and changes.  
- **Signaling Logs**: Analyze protocols like Diameter, GTP, and GSM MAP.  
- **Traffic Monitor**: Live packet analysis without device modifications.  

With Onomondo, you get all three tools in one platform, enabling fast, accurate troubleshooting.

**Problem 1: No Data Received from a Device**  
When data fails to arrive, check these steps:  
1. **Network Connection**:  
   - Use Network Logs to see if the device is connected (e.g., LTE “attached 4G” or +CEREG: 5).  
   - Look for SIM deactivation events. If the SIM is inactive, reactivate it.  
2. **Data Connection**:  
   - Check for “Attached data connection” logs in Network Logs.  
   - If data limits are reached, reset the limit or wait for the next cycle.  
3. **Data Transmission**:  
   - Use Signaling Logs to detect authentication loops or PurgeUE requests.  
   - If data is lost mid-session, analyze Traffic Monitor for packet traces.  

**Problem 2: Unexpected Data Usage**  
Devices often have consistent data patterns. Unusual spikes may indicate:  
- **Server Issues**: Firewalls blocking traffic or incorrect server IPs.  
- **Network Problems**: TLS handshake failures, TCP retransmissions, or MTU mismatches.  
- **Unauthorized Access**: SIM misuse or device tampering.  

**How Onomondo Helps**  
- **Traffic Monitor**: Capture packet traces without device changes.  
- **Usage Alerts**: Set thresholds to detect anomalies early.  
- **Real-Time Insights**: Diagnose issues instantly with Network Logs, Signaling Logs, and Traffic Monitor.  

By leveraging these tools, you can reduce downtime, improve reliability, and ensure your IoT fleet operates smoothly.
