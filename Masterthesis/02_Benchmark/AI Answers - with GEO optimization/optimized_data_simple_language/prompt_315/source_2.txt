Virtualization is the core of modern IT infrastructure, focusing on modernization, efficiency, and value. When combined with hybrid cloud strategies—where workloads exist on-premise, in the cloud, and at the edge—it transforms how enterprises manage IT resources. This approach enables faster innovation and adaptability in complex IT environments.

However, the virtualization landscape is changing rapidly due to new trends and vendor acquisitions. Enterprises now face rising costs and challenges balancing flexibility with smooth operations. Many organizations are reevaluating their strategies, with open-source solutions emerging as a key option for both cost and strategic reasons.

While maintaining the status quo may seem practical in the short term, the growing uncertainty in IT leadership suggests it’s time to explore alternative platforms. These platforms should offer flexibility, agility, and the ability to focus IT investments on innovation and growth.

Open-source virtualization platforms, like Red Hat OpenShift Virtualization, are gaining traction. They integrate cloud-native technologies (such as Kubernetes and containers) with traditional virtual machines (VMs), enabling scalable and efficient innovation. This approach aligns IT infrastructure with evolving business goals while simplifying transitions from legacy systems.

Open-source solutions provide modular flexibility and community-driven innovation. Enterprise-grade open-source options, like Red Hat OpenShift, combine certified technology with vendor support, giving organizations control and reliability. This makes them a strategic choice for modernizing infrastructure without sacrificing performance.

To address immediate challenges and prepare for future migrations, businesses can shift new workloads to open-source platforms. This reduces reliance on single vendors and accelerates adoption of flexible, future-ready solutions.
