# Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery: Key Steps for NIS2 Compliance  

**Why Business Continuity Matters**  
The NIS2 Directive emphasizes the need for organizations to maintain critical operations during incidents like cyberattacks, natural disasters, or power outages. A robust disaster recovery plan ensures minimal downtime and compliance with strict cybersecurity standards.  

**What is Business Continuity?**  
It means your organization can keep functioning at an acceptable level despite disruptions. This includes protecting data, systems, and services from threats like security breaches, hardware failures, or natural disasters.  

**Key Statistics**  
- 50% of organizations face long-term impacts from continuity disruptions (Invenio IT, 2023).  
- 80% of detected vulnerabilities involve Microsoft 365 data (Veeam Solutions).  

**Creating a Disaster Recovery Plan**  
Follow these steps to build a comprehensive strategy:  
1. **Assess Your IT Environment**  
   - Map all applications, services, and dependencies.  
   - Identify critical systems and their interdependencies.  
2. **Conduct a Business Impact Assessment (BIA)**  
   - Evaluate financial, operational, and reputational risks of downtime.  
   - Develop recovery strategies tailored to each application.  
3. **Define Recovery Objectives**  
   - **Recovery Point Objective (RPO):** How recent your data must be (e.g., Veeam’s 5-minute backups).  
   - **Recovery Time Objective (RTO):** How quickly systems must be restored.  
4. **Implement Backup Solutions**  
   - Use tools like Veeam’s Microsoft 365 backup to protect data from accidental deletion, ransomware, and compliance risks.  
   - Ensure immutable backups and cloud-native storage for multi-cloud environments.  

**Critical Steps During an Incident**  
1. **Identify & Report**  
   - Use monitoring tools to detect risks and report incidents promptly.  
2. **Restore Applications**  
   - Recover data to predefined locations, with granular options for users, files, or entire organizations.  
3. **Test Restored Systems**  
   - Automate testing and documentation with recovery orchestration tools.  
4. **Notify Stakeholders**  
   - Keep application owners and stakeholders updated, and conduct post-incident analysis to prevent future issues.
