Introduction  
High-Performance Computing (HPC) clusters require powerful, scalable storage systems to handle complex tasks. ClusterTech, in partnership with Seagate, offers solutions to build efficient HPC systems. Here's a simplified overview of their approach:  

**What is HPC Used For?**  
HPC powers critical applications like weather forecasting, automotive design, and bioinformatics. For example:  
- Weather agencies need real-time predictions, requiring massive computational power.  
- Automotive companies use HPC for car design simulations, reducing time from days to hours.  
- Bioinformatics relies on HPC for tasks like genetic analysis and protein prediction, especially during global health crises like the pandemic.  

**ClusterTech’s HPC Solutions**  
ClusterTech has 20+ years of experience in HPC, offering end-to-end solutions:  
- **Hardware**: CPUs, memory, storage, and high-speed networks (like InfiniBand or 25GB Ethernet).  
- **Software**: Cluster management platforms, optimization tools, and compatibility with operating systems.  
- **Services**: Deployment, maintenance, and optimization of HPC systems.  

**Key Components of an HPC Cluster**  
1. **Networking**: High-speed networks (e.g., 25GB Ethernet) are critical to avoid bottlenecks.  
2. **Storage**: Seagate’s storage solutions are tailored for HPC:  
   - Small clusters: 2U12/2U14 models (hundreds of TB).  
   - Medium clusters: 5U84 units (up to 1 PB capacity).  
   - Large clusters: 4U106 units for ultra-high-density storage.  
3. **Software**: Distributed file systems like Lustre, BeeGFS, and GPFS are used for large-scale data processing.

- **Features**: Job scheduling, resource monitoring, user management, and application integration.  
- **Use Cases**: Supports TensorFlow, Fluent, and other HPC tools.  
- **Benefits**: Simplifies cluster management, enables workflow automation, and provides billing/reporting tools.
