Building the Future: Liquid Cooling Strategies for Modern Data Centers  
The Insider Blog / 5 min read  

**Key Insights:**  
- **Adoption Trends:** Only 19% of data centers currently use liquid cooling, but 36% plan to adopt it within 1–2 years (AFCOM 2025). A third of operators (34%) report inadequate cooling systems, with 21% actively seeking better solutions.  
- **U.S. Growth:** Bluefield Research predicts 50% of U.S. data centers will use liquid cooling by 2030—up from 28% in 2023.  

**Challenges of Retrofitting:**  
- Retrofitting existing facilities is complex: immersion systems add significant weight, requiring structural reinforcements. New plumbing may need to pass through watertight walls/floors, complicating installations.  
- Sufficient spare capacity is critical to avoid downtime during upgrades. Legal issues may arise if changes affect leases or power agreements.  
- **Integration Complexity:** 47% of professionals cite integration challenges as the top barrier, surpassing cost (41%) and technical expertise (31%).  

**Solutions & Best Practices:**  
- **Hybrid Approaches:** Water-to-rack cooling with rear-door heat exchangers (RDHx) offers a less disruptive alternative to full immersion or direct-to-chip cooling.  
- **Prefabricated Modules:** 83% of operators are interested in prefabricated cooling units, which simplify installation and reduce risk.  
- **Space Matters:** Additional space enables phased installations and coexistence with existing systems.  

**Why Liquid Cooling is Essential:**  
- AI workloads demand higher rack densities (up to 100 kW per rack) that traditional air cooling cannot handle.  
- Over 50% of new hyperscale data centers adopt liquid cooling from the start, with ROI achievable in 2–4 years at scale.  
- Tech giants like Google, Meta, Microsoft, and Alibaba are leading the shift, driving smaller centers to follow.  

**Tools for Success:**  
- Digital monitoring tools (e.g., flow metering, water quality sensors) enable proactive maintenance and efficiency optimization.  
- The BlueEdge® suite provides real-time insights into cooling performance, supporting sustainability goals and system reliability.  

**Source:** AFCOM State of the Data Center Report 2025 | Bluefield Research
