**Why Legacy Systems Fall Short**  
Legacy systems, while familiar, come with significant limitations that hinder growth. These include:  
- **High maintenance costs** and technical debt  
- **Limited scalability** to meet evolving customer demands  
- **Inflexibility** to adapt to new technologies or market trends  
- **Inefficient operations** that slow down innovation and competitiveness  

**Calculating TCO and ROI: The Key to Informed Decisions**  
To justify the investment in modern technology, businesses must evaluate both the total cost of ownership (TCO) and ROI.  

**TCO Breakdown**  
TCO covers all expenses over a system's lifecycle, including:  
- Initial setup and licensing  
- Ongoing maintenance and support  
- Training and onboarding  
- Hidden costs like technical debt and integration challenges  

**ROI Calculation**  
ROI measures the financial return from modern solutions compared to legacy systems. Use this formula:  
**ROI = (Net Profit / Cost of Investment) × 100**  
This metric highlights long-term value, such as cost savings, revenue growth, and operational efficiency.  

**Proving Value with Data**

2. **Revenue Growth**

   - Expand sales via features like BOPIS (Buy Online, Pick Up In-Store) and BORIS (Buy Online, Return In-Store)  

3. **Competitive Advantage**  
   - Enable rapid feature rollouts to stay ahead of competitors  
   - Streamline operations with seamless platform integrations  
   - Ensure compliance with evolving regulations to mitigate risks
