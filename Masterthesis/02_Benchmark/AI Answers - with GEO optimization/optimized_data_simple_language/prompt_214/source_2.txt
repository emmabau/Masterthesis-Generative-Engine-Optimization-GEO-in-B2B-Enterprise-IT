Is Your IoT Security Strategy Scalable?  
The Internet of Things (IoT) is growing rapidly, with 16.6 billion active connections by the end of 2023. However, securing these devices is a major challenge. Cyberattacks against IoT systems are rising—over 112 million attacks occurred in 2022 alone, a 87% increase from the previous year. Traditional security methods are no longer enough.  

A scalable IoT security strategy is essential because:  
1. **IoT devices are constantly expanding**. Cybercriminals are also evolving faster than businesses can adapt.  
2. **Compatibility is key**. New devices must work with existing systems, using secure protocols like encryption and multi-factor authentication.  
3. **Manual processes are unsustainable**. 63% of security professionals face burnout due to high workloads.  

To build a scalable strategy:  
- **Use cloud-based solutions** for automatic asset tracking and inventory management.  
- **Adopt zero-trust architecture**. This approach treats every connection as a potential threat, verifying access at every step.  
- **Automate critical tasks** like software updates, malware scans, and network monitoring. Automated tools can save up to $2.22 million by stopping attacks early.  
- **Combine defense and offense**. "Purple team" strategies—where security teams simulate attacks and test defenses—help identify vulnerabilities before they’re exploited.  

Without a scalable plan, businesses risk outdated protections, unpatched vulnerabilities, and costly breaches. Prioritizing adaptability and automation is no longer optional—it’s a necessity for staying secure in the IoT era.
