IT infrastructure optimization is essential for modern businesses. This refers to the process of improving an organization's physical and virtual components—such as hardware, software, networks, and data centers—to enhance performance, reduce costs, and support growth. 

Why is it important?  
- **Competitive Advantage**: Optimized infrastructure enables faster adaptation to market changes through technologies like cloud computing and AI.
