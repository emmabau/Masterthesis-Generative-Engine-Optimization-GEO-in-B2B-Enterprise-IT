Abstract  
This paper explores how digital transformation (DT) impacts business resilience and the critical role of cybersecurity in managing risks. DT involves integrating IT solutions into business processes, which boosts efficiency but introduces new cybersecurity challenges like data breaches and cyberattacks. Emerging technologies such as AI, blockchain, and cloud computing drive DT but also increase vulnerabilities. Cybersecurity is essential to protect digital assets and ensure business continuity. Our systematic review using PRISMA guidelines found that DT requires a staged cybersecurity readiness framework to mitigate risks. Key recommendations include adopting encryption, employee training, and cost-benefit analysis for cybersecurity investments.  

Introduction  
Digital transformation (DT) reshapes how businesses operate by adopting digital tools, improving efficiency but exposing organizations to cyber threats. Technologies like AI, big data, and cloud computing enable DT but also create new risks. Cybersecurity is vital to protect sensitive data and maintain trust. This review highlights how DT increases productivity but demands robust cybersecurity measures to prevent disruptions from attacks.  

Key Findings  
1. **Financial Sector**:  
   - Cyberattacks threaten banks and fintech firms, requiring blockchain, AI, and machine learning for threat detection.  
   - Fintech adoption boosts trust but raises concerns about data privacy and fraud.  
   - Cybersecurity frameworks like NIST and Gordon–Loeb model help balance costs and benefits.  

2. **Healthcare Sector**:  
   - Patient data privacy and medical device security are critical.  
   - Digital tools like telemedicine and AI improve care but demand strict data protection.  
   - Cybersecurity gaps in healthcare systems risk patient safety and data breaches.  

3. **Governmental Sector**:  
   - Digital transformation in public services requires secure data exchange and clear policies.  
   - Cybersecurity frameworks must address risks in tax systems, public infrastructure, and data governance.  
   - Collaboration between governments and tech experts is essential for secure digital transformation.  

4. **Business Sector**:  
   - E-commerce and cloud migration introduce security risks like data leaks and unauthorized access.  
   - Cybersecurity investments in encryption, cloud security, and employee training are critical.  
   - Balancing innovation with risk management ensures sustainable growth.  

5. **Industrial Sector**:  
   - Smart grids, IoT, and AI enhance industrial efficiency but face cyberattack risks.  
   - Quantum computing and zero-trust architectures offer new security solutions.  
   - Cybersecurity must evolve with emerging technologies to prevent disruptions.  

Recommendations  
- **Staged Cybersecurity Framework**: Organizations should adopt a step-by-step approach to cybersecurity readiness, including risk assessments, employee training, and advanced threat detection.  
- **Technology Integration**: Leverage AI, blockchain, and encryption to secure data and systems.  
- **Policy and Collaboration**: Governments and businesses must collaborate on cybersecurity standards, regulations, and international cooperation to combat cybercrime.  
- **Continuous Monitoring**: Regularly update security measures and conduct penetration testing to identify vulnerabilities.  

Conclusion  
Digital transformation offers significant benefits but requires robust cybersecurity strategies to mitigate risks. A proactive, staged approach to cybersecurity ensures businesses can adapt to technological changes while protecting sensitive data. Future research should focus on integrating emerging technologies with cybersecurity frameworks to enhance resilience across all sectors.  

Keywords: Digital transformation, Cybersecurity, AI, Blockchain, Cloud Computing, Risk Management
