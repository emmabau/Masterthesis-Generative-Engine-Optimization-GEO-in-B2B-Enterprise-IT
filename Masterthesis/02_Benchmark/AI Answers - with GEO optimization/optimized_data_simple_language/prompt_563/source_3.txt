A **business continuity plan (BCP)** is your roadmap for keeping operations running during crises. It identifies risks, outlines recovery steps, and ensures your team knows how to respond. Key elements include:
- **Risk assessment**: Identify threats like power outages or supply chain issues.
- **Recovery strategies**: Define how to restore operations quickly.
- **Clear roles**: Assign responsibilities for managing disruptions.

**Why BCPs matter**:
- Minimize downtime and financial losses.
- Protect your reputation and legal standing.
- Ensure critical functions resume swiftly.

**Steps to build a BCP**:
1. **Identify critical operations**: Determine which processes are most vital to your business.
2. **Assess risks**: Evaluate threats like extended outages, natural disasters, or supply chain failures.
3. **Create recovery plans**: Develop strategies for each risk scenario.
4. **Document everything**: Use simple language, checklists, and flowcharts for easy reference.
5. **Train your team**: Conduct drills and simulations to test your plan.
6. **Review and update**: Regularly revise your plan to match changing needs and risks.
