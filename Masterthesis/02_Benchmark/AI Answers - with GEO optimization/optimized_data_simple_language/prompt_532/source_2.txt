IT procurement, financial planning, and asset lifecycle management are often treated as separate processes, leading to inefficiencies. Procurement focuses on low unit costs, finance manages capital spending, and IT handles device lifecycle, but this fragmentation causes delays, budgeting challenges, and operational risks. As work becomes more dynamic with hybrid and remote models, these issues worsen.  

Device as a Service (DaaS) offers a unified solution by treating devices as a managed service, not owned assets. This model provides flexibility, transparency, and control across the entire device lifecycle. Key benefits include:  
- **Predictable Costs**: Replace upfront capital expenses with fixed monthly fees, aligning spending with actual usage.  
- **Scalability**: Adjust device fleets based on real demand, reducing waste and supporting agile workforce planning.  
- **Simplified Procurement**: Streamline processes by working with a single provider, avoiding fragmented vendor contracts.  
- **Risk Reduction**: Standardized lifecycle management ensures secure data removal, compliance, and proactive asset tracking.  

Traditional models face challenges like vendor fragmentation, delayed device delivery, and unclear financial forecasting. DaaS addresses these by:  
- **Centralizing Management**: Tracking devices from deployment to retirement under one framework.  
- **Improving Collaboration**: Aligning IT, finance, and procurement teams under a shared cost and service model.  
- **Enhancing Visibility**: Providing clear insights into IT spending and asset usage, reducing operational stress.  

For remote teams, DaaS ensures consistent device delivery and support, improving employee experience and reducing downtime. By shifting to a subscription-based model, organizations gain agility, cost control, and the ability to adapt to changing work environments.
