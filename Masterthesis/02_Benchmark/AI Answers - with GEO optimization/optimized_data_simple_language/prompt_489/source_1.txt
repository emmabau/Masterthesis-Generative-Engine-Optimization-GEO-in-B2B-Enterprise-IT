Prepare for GDPR Compliance: A Guide for Businesses

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), effective May 25, 2018, is a critical data privacy law for businesses handling EU residents' personal information. Non-compliance risks fines up to 4% of global revenue or €20 million. Here's how to prepare:

**Key GDPR Requirements**
- Applies to companies with EU presence or processing EU residents' data
- Protects personal data like names, emails, medical records, IP addresses
- Requires appointing a Data Protection Officer (DPO) for high-risk data

**Action Steps for Compliance**
✅ Understand GDPR requirements

✅ Identify systems storing personal data  
✅ Develop protection strategies
