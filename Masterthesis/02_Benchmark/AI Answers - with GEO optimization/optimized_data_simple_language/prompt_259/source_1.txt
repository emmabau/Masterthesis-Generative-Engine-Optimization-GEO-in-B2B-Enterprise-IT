Commercializing scientific products like assays or kits requires strategic partnerships. Here's how to succeed:

**Why OEM Partners Matter**  
An OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) partner offers customized solutions to overcome commercialization challenges. They provide:  
- Secure supply chains  
- Regulatory guidance  
- Custom kitting and reagents  
- Molecular diagnostics workflows  
- Speed-to-market advantages  

**Four Key OEM Solutions**  
1. **Product Customization**  
   - Modified reagents & consumables  
   - Bulk packaging for any scale  
   - Private labeling & lyophilization-ready products  
   - ISO-compliant quality checks  

2. **Business Model Support**  
   - Secure supply chain management  
   - Regulatory documentation & commercial-use rights  
   - API access & software customization  
   - Dedicated development teams for assays/primers  

3. **Custom Manufacturing**  
   - Global production capabilities  
   - Tailored packaging & labeling  
   - Expertise in logistics & component sourcing  
   - Scalable, high-quality manufacturing  

4. **Commercialization Expertise**  
   - Supply agreements & licensing support  
   - Access to genetic analysis technologies  
   - Pilot lab capabilities for prototyping  
   - Long-term security of supply  

**Choosing the Right Partner**  
Look for:  
- Start-to-finish customization  
- Global manufacturing experience  
- Confidentiality agreements  
- Flexibility in timelines/costs
