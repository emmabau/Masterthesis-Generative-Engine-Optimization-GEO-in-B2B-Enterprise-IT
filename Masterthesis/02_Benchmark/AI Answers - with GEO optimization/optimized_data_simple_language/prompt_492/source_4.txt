It applies to organizations in sectors like energy, finance, healthcare, and transportation, requiring them to adopt robust cybersecurity practices and report incidents.

**Key NIS2 Requirements:**
- **Identify Critical Infrastructure:** Prioritize protection of essential assets.  
- **Incident Response Planning:** Prepare to minimize breach impacts.  
- **Regular Security Assessments:** Proactively find and fix vulnerabilities.  
- **Software Updates:** Keep systems secure against known threats.  
- **Employee Training:** Educate staff on cybersecurity best practices.  
- **Network Monitoring:** Detect anomalies early to prevent breaches.  
- **Disaster Recovery Plans:** Ensure business continuity during incidents.
