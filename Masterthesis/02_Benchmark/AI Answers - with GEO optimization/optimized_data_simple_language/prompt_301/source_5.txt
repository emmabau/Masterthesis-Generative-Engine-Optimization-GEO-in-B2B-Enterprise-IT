**Storage Company of the Year - ExaGrid**  
ExaGrid’s Tiered Backup Storage is a specialized solution designed specifically for backup needs. It improves backup and restore performance, scalability, security, and ransomware recovery while keeping costs low. Key features include:  
- **Scalable Capacity**: The EX189 appliance supports up to 6PB of full backups with 12PB raw capacity, making it the largest single system in the industry with data deduplication.  
- **Efficient Design**: 2U models save rack space and cooling costs, while 5-year price protection ensures long-term budget predictability.  
- **Reliability**: Auto-failover between primary and secondary data centers reduces recovery time by 50%, boosting uptime to 99.99%.  
- **Sustainability**: 100% renewable energy-powered data centers align with ESG goals.

- **Cost Efficiency**: Reduces cybersecurity spend by up to 30% compared to other solutions.

- **Expert Support**: Dedicated senior engineers ensure quick issue resolution.  

**Innovation Winner - StorPool**  
StorPool’s block storage software transforms standard x86 servers into high-performance, scalable virtual storage systems. Highlights:  
- **Hyperconvergence**: Combines compute, storage, and networking in a single system, reducing TCO by up to 30%.  
- **Advanced Data Protection**: Erasure Coding (v21) provides 4x capacity with minimal performance impact.  
- **Flexibility**: Supports hybrid cloud environments and integrates with CloudStack, OpenStack, and Proxmox.  
- **Resilience**: Dual internet links and N+1 redundancy ensure uninterrupted operations.
