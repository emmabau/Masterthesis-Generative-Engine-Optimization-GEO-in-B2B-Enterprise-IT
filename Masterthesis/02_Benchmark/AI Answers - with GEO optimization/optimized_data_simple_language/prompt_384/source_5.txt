Business Continuity (BC) is the practice of preparing for and managing disruptions to ensure essential operations continue without interruption. In today’s digital world, disruptions like cyberattacks, natural disasters, or supply chain failures can severely impact businesses. BC planning helps organizations minimize downtime, protect revenue, and maintain customer trust during crises.  

Key benefits of a strong BC strategy include:  
- **Reduced Downtime**: Quick recovery from disruptions prevents financial losses.  
- **Improved Customer Satisfaction**: Consistent service delivery strengthens brand loyalty.  
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Laws like DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act) and NIS 2 (Network and Information Security Directive) now require businesses to have robust BC plans.  

**DORA** focuses on financial institutions, mandating risk management frameworks to handle ICT threats and third-party compliance. **NIS 2** expands this to critical sectors like energy, transport, and healthcare, emphasizing supply chain security and incident reporting. Both regulations highlight BC as a legal requirement, not just a best practice.
