# The Cost of Equipment Failure and the Power of Predictive Maintenance  

Unplanned equipment failure is a major challenge for manufacturers, costing an average of **$50,000 per hour** in downtime. This makes shifting from reactive to **predictive maintenance** a business necessity, not just a competitive edge.  

## Why Predictive Maintenance Matters  
Traditional methods like reactive or preventive maintenance have clear limitations:  
- **Reactive maintenance** waits for equipment to fail, risking safety and costly downtime.  
- **Preventive maintenance** schedules repairs based on time or usage, often leading to unnecessary work.  
- **Condition-based maintenance** relies on single-sensor data, missing subtle issues.  

Predictive maintenance, powered by **IMU sensor fusion**, changes this. By combining data from accelerometers, gyroscopes, and magnetometers, it provides a full picture of equipment health.

IMUs measure motion, orientation, and environmental factors. When combined through **sensor fusion algorithms**, they detect issues like:
