**Key Considerations When Choosing an Advisor**  
1. **Relationship Type**: Decide if you need occasional advice (e.g., a one-time plan) or ongoing support (e.g., portfolio management).  
2. **Compensation Model**:  
   - *Fee-only advisors* charge directly for their services and avoid conflicts of interest.  
   - *Commission-based advisors* may recommend products they earn money from.

3. **Credentials**: Look for certifications like *Certified Financial Planner (CFP)* or *Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA)*.

- *Robo-advisors* use algorithms for low-cost, automated investing (min. $1k–$5k).  
- *Human advisors* offer personalized advice, tax strategies, and deeper relationships but at higher fees.  
- Some robo-advisors partner with human advisors for an extra fee.  

**How to Choose**
