2. **Quality Control**: Machine vision systems integrated with IoT detect defects during production, ensuring consistent product quality. These systems provide instant feedback to adjust production parameters on the fly.

**Calculating ROI for IoT Projects**  
- **Impact Chains**: Link IoT actions (e.g., real-time data collection) to financial outcomes like cost savings or revenue growth.  
- **Transition Costs**: Include expenses for training, software licenses, and process overhauls when calculating ROI. For example, retraining staff to use IoT data is critical for successful implementation.  
- **Statistical Simulations**: Use probabilistic models to account for uncertainties in cost savings or productivity gains.  

**Future Trends**  
- **Digital Twins**: Virtual models of physical systems allow testing of process improvements without disrupting operations.  
- **AI Integration**: AI analyzes IoT data to predict failures, optimize supply chains, and enable self-adjusting production lines.  
- **5G Connectivity**: Enables ultra-low latency and high-speed data transfer, critical for robotics, AR/VR training, and real-time automation.
