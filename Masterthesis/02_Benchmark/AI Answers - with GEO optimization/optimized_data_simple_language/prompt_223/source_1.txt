**Sensor Types & Use Cases**  
1. **Vibration Sensors**  
   - Detect bearing wear, misalignment, imbalance, and gear damage  
   - Ideal for motors, pumps, and gearboxes  
   - Use triaxial accelerometers with sampling rates 1-10 kHz  

2. **Temperature Sensors**  
   - Monitor overheating bearings, electrical overloads, and insulation issues  
   - RTDs (high accuracy) for HVAC; thermocouples (wide range) for industrial heat  

3. **Current Sensors**  
   - Track motor winding degradation, phase imbalance, and belt slippage  
   - Measure RMS current, power factor, and harmonic distortion  

4. **Pressure Sensors**  
   - Detect leaks, pump wear, and cavitation in fluid systems  
   - Range from 0-30 PSI (HVAC) to 0-5,000 PSI (hydraulics)  

5. **Acoustic Sensors**  
   - Identify leaks, electrical arcing, and bearing lubrication issues  
   - Detect ultrasonic frequencies (20-100 kHz)  

6. **Environmental Sensors**  
   - Monitor humidity, temperature, and dew point for corrosion and condensation risks  
   - Critical for data centers, clean rooms, and battery storage
