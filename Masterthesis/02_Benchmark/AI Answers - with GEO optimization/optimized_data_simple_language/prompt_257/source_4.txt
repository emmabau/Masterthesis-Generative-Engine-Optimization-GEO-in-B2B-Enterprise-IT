What is IoT?
The Internet of Things (IoT) connects smart devices that collect and share data in real-time. These devices range from smart home gadgets to industrial sensors, enabling automation and improved efficiency across industries.

4. **Efficient Workflows**: Automate processes like inventory restocking to reduce manual work.
