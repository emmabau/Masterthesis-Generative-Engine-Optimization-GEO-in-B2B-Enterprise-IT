Hybrid work is now the standard for many businesses. While some companies had already experimented with remote work before the pandemic, the global shift accelerated its adoption. According to Steelcase’s Global Report, 72% of corporate leaders plan to offer hybrid work models. Now is the time to build a sustainable strategy for your hybrid workplace. Here are five key strategies to consider:

1. **Create Smart Workspaces**  
   Modern hybrid work requires seamless integration of physical and virtual environments. Invest in tools that connect remote and in-office teams, like user-friendly software and secure platforms. These solutions should enhance collaboration, provide insights, and adapt to evolving work patterns.

2. **Reconfigure Office Spaces**  
   Traditional open offices are evolving. Focus work (like deep tasks) should move to enclosed areas like pods, while open spaces will host meetings and collaboration. Design flexible layouts that can adapt to new work habits, such as quick stand-up meetings without dedicated conference rooms.

3. **Build a Hybrid Culture**  
   Hybrid work thrives on balance. Encourage a 2/3 or 3/2 split between in-office and remote days. Ensure clarity on eligible roles (e.g., not all jobs can be hybrid, like pilots or electricians). Foster collaboration with tools teams agree to use, and eliminate outdated practices that hinder productivity.

4. **Combat Isolation with Team Bonding**  
   Remote workers often feel disconnected. Create opportunities for in-person interactions, like scheduled meetings between remote and office teams. Use communication tools (e.g., Slack) to build social connections and help employees feel part of the company’s mission.

5. **Involve Key Leaders**  
   Hybrid success depends on leadership. Include leaders like the CEO and HR manager in planning to ensure alignment with company goals. Their involvement helps guide teams and maintain productivity during the transition.
