1. **Servers** – Physical or virtual machines hosting applications and data  
2. **Networking** – Routers, switches, and access points that ensure connectivity  
3. **Firewalls & Security Gateways** – Protecting the perimeter of your infrastructure  
4. **Storage** – Data repositories: block, object, and file storage solutions  
5. **Virtualization Platforms** – Tools like VMware, KVM, or Hyper-V to maximize hardware usage  
6. **Monitoring Tools** – Systems like Prometheus, Grafana, or New Relic  
7. **Cloud & Hybrid Integrations** – AWS, Azure, GCP, and how they coexist with on-prem components
