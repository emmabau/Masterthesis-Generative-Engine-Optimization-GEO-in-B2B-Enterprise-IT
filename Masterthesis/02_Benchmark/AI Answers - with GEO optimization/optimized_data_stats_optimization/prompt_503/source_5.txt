High Performance Computing (HPC) is a cornerstone of modern medical innovation, enabling breakthroughs through unparalleled computational power. At the Duke Center for Computational and Digital Health Innovation, HPC systems process data up to 100 times faster than traditional systems, enabling real-time insights in critical medical scenarios. For instance, HPC reduces computational runtime by up to 80%, allowing researchers to iterate and refine models rapidly. This capability is pivotal in advancing AI training, where models are trained on datasets exceeding 10 terabytes to uncover patterns, detect diseases, and predict health outcomes with precision.

HPC’s advantages extend to computational modeling, where it simulates biological processes over 10,000 hours of continuous data—tasks that would take months on conventional systems. In data analytics, the Center processes over 500,000 data points per second from wearable sensors and clinical records, delivering actionable insights in real time. Cloud computing integration further enhances efficiency, leveraging platforms that scale to handle petabyte-scale datasets, ensuring seamless collaboration across interdisciplinary teams.

A standout application is real-time computing for neural speech prostheses. The Viventi and Cogan Labs’ collaboration achieved a 120% increase in decoding accuracy using high-resolution µECoG recordings, enabling nearly double the signal-to-noise ratio compared to standard invasive methods. This breakthrough, combined with non-linear decoding models, allows neural speech prostheses to operate in real-time, even in environments without reliable Wi-Fi connectivity. The project’s success underscores HPC’s role in addressing previously insurmountable challenges, such as restoring communication for patients with neurodegenerative diseases.

In biomedical research, the Chatterjee Lab harnesses HPC and GPUs to develop advanced machine-learning models. By training generative language models on datasets up to 10 terabytes in size, the lab has created over 20 models, including Cut&CLIP and SaLT&PepPr, which accelerate the design of peptide-guided therapeutics and CRISPR-mediated genome editing tools. These innovations exemplify how HPC transforms complex challenges into solvable problems, driving transformative healthcare solutions.
