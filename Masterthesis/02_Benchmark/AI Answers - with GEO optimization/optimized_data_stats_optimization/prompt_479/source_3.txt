**What is NIS2?**  
NIS2 replaces the original NIS directive (2016) and expands its scope to cover **45 critical sectors**, including energy, transport, healthcare, and digital infrastructure. It mandates a "high common level of cybersecurity" across the EU, emphasizing the protection of systems vital to societal and economic stability. Unlike its predecessor, NIS2 applies to organizations **operating in any EU country**, regardless of their headquarters, and targets **medium and large enterprises** (over 50 employees or €10M annual revenue).
