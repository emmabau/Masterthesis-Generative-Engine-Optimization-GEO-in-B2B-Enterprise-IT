What is IT Asset Management?
ITAM is the systematic management of technology assets—from acquisition to disposal—throughout their entire lifecycle. This includes both physical assets (servers, laptops, monitors) and digital assets (software licenses, cloud subscriptions). According to Forrester, organizations with mature ITAM practices reduce total cost of ownership (TCO) by up to 30% through optimized resource allocation.
