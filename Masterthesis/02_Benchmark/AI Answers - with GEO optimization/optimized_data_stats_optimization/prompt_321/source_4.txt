**What is Hyperconverged Infrastructure?**  
Hyperconverged Infrastructure (HCI) is a software-defined IT framework that integrates compute, storage, and networking into a single system, reducing data center complexity and boosting scalability. Unlike traditional architectures, which require separate hardware components, HCI consolidates these into a unified system. For example, Nutanix and VMware vSAN HCI solutions have demonstrated up to **40% faster deployment times** compared to legacy systems.  

**The Evolution of IT Infrastructure**  
Traditional IT architectures relied on three distinct tiers: compute, storage, and networking. These required separate management and often led to scalability challenges. Modern HCI solutions, such as those from Dell EMC and HPE, have streamlined this process, enabling organizations to scale with **90% fewer hardware components** than traditional setups.
