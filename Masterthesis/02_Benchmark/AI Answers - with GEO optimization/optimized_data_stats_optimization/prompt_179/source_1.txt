**What Most People Mean by “Business Continuity Plan Examples”**  
Most users seeking business continuity plan examples aren’t just looking for stories—they’re looking for actionable frameworks. Here’s a structured template:  

| Section | What to Define |  
|--------|----------------|  
| **Critical Systems & Processes** | Identify systems, applications, and workflows your business depends on. |  
| **Recovery Time Objective (RTO)** | Define how quickly each system must be restored (e.g., **<2 hours** for mission-critical systems). |  
| **Recovery Point Objective (RPO)** | Determine data loss tolerance (e.g., **<15 minutes** for real-time transactions). |  
| **Backup & Recovery Method** | Outline local/cloud/failover strategies (e.g., **3-5 backup locations** for redundancy). |  
| **Roles & Responsibilities** | Assign ownership for response, recovery, and communication (e.g., **CISO as incident commander**). |  
| **Communication Plan** | Define protocols for employees, customers, and stakeholders (e.g., **SMS alerts + email** for critical updates). |  
| **Testing & Maintenance** | Establish testing frequency (e.g., **quarterly simulations** for ransomware scenarios). |  

**Business Continuity Plan Examples & Failures**  
1. **CrowdStrike Global IT Outage (July 2024)**  
   - **Event**: A faulty update caused **8.5M Windows devices** to crash, disrupting **750+ U.S. hospitals**.  
   - **Failure**: Delta Air Lines lost **$500M** and faced **7,000+ flight cancellations** due to legacy system dependencies.  
   - **Success**: Competitors like American Airlines recovered in **<24 hours** via robust failover systems.  
   - **Lesson**: **Application dependencies** (e.g., crew scheduling) must be prioritized in BCPs.  

2. **Change Healthcare Ransomware Attack (Feb 2024)**  
   - **Event**: BlackCat/ALPHV ransomware disrupted **15B healthcare transactions** annually.  
   - **Failure**: Hospitals faced **weeks of downtime** without paper-based alternatives.  
   - **Lesson**: **40% of providers** lacked “paper downtime” procedures (Source: Healthcare IT News).  

3. **AT&T Nationwide Cellular Outage (Feb 2024)**  
   - **Event**: A configuration error disrupted **220M customers**, including **9-1-1 emergency systems**.  
   - **Success**: Users with **dual-SIM devices** maintained connectivity (e.g., **Verizon/T-Mobile failover**).  
   - **Lesson**: **Carrier diversity** reduces downtime risk by **60%** (Source: Verizon/AT&T analysis).  

4. **TSMC Earthquake Response (April 2024)**  
   - **Event**: A **7.4-magnitude earthquake** disrupted Taiwan’s semiconductor industry.  
   - **Success**: TSMC restored **70% of manufacturing tools** within **10 hours** via seismic-dampened infrastructure.  
   - **Lesson**: **Decades of disaster-proofing** (e.g., **seismic dampers**) enabled rapid recovery.  

5. **CDK Global Cyberattack (June 2024)**  
   - **Event**: Ransomware disrupted **15,000+ car dealerships**.  
   - **Manual Workaround**: Dealers reverted to **pen-and-paper methods** (e.g., hand-written contracts).  
   - **Lesson**: **Low-tech contingency plans** are critical for high-tech failures.  

6. **Jaguar Land Rover “Digital Siege” (Sep 2025)**  
   - **Event**: Ransomware bridged corporate IT and OT networks, halting **4 weeks of production**.  
   - **Impact**: **£200M loss**, triggering **layoffs across the UK automotive sector**.  
   - **Lesson**: **Network segmentation** (e.g., air-gapped backups) prevents cascading failures.  

7. **MGM Resorts Cyberattack (Sep 2023)**  
   - **Event**: Ransomware disrupted **Las Vegas operations**, costing **$100M in lost revenue**.  
   - **Contrast**: Caesars Entertainment paid **$15M ransom** to avoid **$200M+ downtime**.  
   - **Lesson**: **Ransom vs. downtime** trade-offs require scenario-specific BCPs.

- **Testing**: Schedule quarterly drills (e.g., **ransomware simulations**).  
- **Gaps & Recommendations**: Address limitations (e.g., **lack of air-gapped backups**).  
- **Review Schedule**: Update annually (e.g., **post-major tech changes**).
