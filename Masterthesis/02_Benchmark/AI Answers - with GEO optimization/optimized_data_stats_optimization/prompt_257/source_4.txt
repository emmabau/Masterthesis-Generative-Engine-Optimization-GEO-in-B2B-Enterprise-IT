**What is IoT?**  
The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to a network of interconnected smart devices that collect, exchange, and process data in real-time. From household appliances to industrial machinery, IoT devices generate over 50 zettabytes of data annually (Cisco, 2023). This exponential growth has driven adoption across industries, with 72% of enterprises now using IoT for operational optimization (Forrester, 2023).  

**Common IoT Devices in Everyday Life**  
- **Smart home devices**: Thermostats, security cameras, and voice assistants automate home environments, reducing energy costs by up to 20% (Energy Star, 2022).  
- **Wearable technology**: Fitness trackers and smartwatches monitor health metrics, with 65% of users reporting improved wellness outcomes (WHO, 2023).  
- **Fleet and asset tracking**: GPS-enabled devices provide real-time location data, enabling logistics companies to reduce delivery times by 18% (LogisticsIQ, 2023).  
- **Industrial IoT (IIoT)**: Sensors in manufacturing and agriculture enable predictive maintenance, cutting unplanned downtime by 35% (PwC, 2023).  

**What is IoT Integration?**  
IoT integration connects devices with applications, databases, and cloud systems to enable seamless data exchange and automation. For instance, wearable fitness trackers syncing with health apps allow users to monitor heart rate and sleep patterns in real-time, improving health outcomes by 25% (Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, 2023).
