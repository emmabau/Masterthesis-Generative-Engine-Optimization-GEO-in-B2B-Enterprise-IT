This initiative, a joint effort between the Office of Science Advanced Scientific Computing Research Program and the National Nuclear Security Administration Advanced Simulation and Computing Program, has deployed a vertically integrated software stack on DOE HPC exascale and pre-exascale systems, establishing the foundation for the US exascale ecosystem.
