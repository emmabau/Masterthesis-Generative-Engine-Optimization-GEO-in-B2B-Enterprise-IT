**What is a Disaster Recovery Plan?**  
The NIS2 Directive mandates that businesses have robust disaster recovery plans to minimize downtime. A well-structured plan includes:  
- **Business Impact Assessment (BIA)**: Identifies financial and operational risks, such as revenue loss or irrecoverable data.  
- **Recovery Point Objective (RPO)**: Measures how recent your backups must be. For example, **Veeam’s RPO of 5 minutes** ensures minimal data loss.  
- **Recovery Time Objective (RTO)**: Defines how quickly systems must be restored.
