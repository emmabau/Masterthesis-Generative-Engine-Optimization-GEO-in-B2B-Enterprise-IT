Exascale Deep Learning for Climate Analytics  
Abstract  
We achieve groundbreaking results in extreme weather pattern detection using Tiramisu and DeepLabv3+ architectures, scaling to 27360 V100 GPUs with a sustained throughput of 325.8 PF/s and 90.7% parallel efficiency in single precision. Our half-precision variant of DeepLabv3+ delivers a peak throughput of 1.13 EF/s and sustained 999.0 PF/s, outperforming prior work by a factor of 2.5 in sustained throughput. These results are enabled by system-level innovations in data staging, optimized parallel I/O, and hierarchical all-reduce communication, as well as algorithmic advancements like weighted loss functions and gradient lagging.

The ability to scale to 27360 GPUs with 90.7% parallel efficiency represents a 3x improvement over previous large-scale deep learning benchmarks.
