**Key Risks in Hybrid Work**:  
- **Unsecured home networks**: 60% of remote workers use residential Wi-Fi without encryption (2023 Cisco report).  
- **Shared family spaces**: 35% of employees admit discussing work data with family members (2023 PwC survey).  
- **Personal devices**: 55% of remote workers lack endpoint security software (2023 Verizon DBIR).

- **Data minimisation**: Limit data collection to what is strictly necessary (Article 5(1)(c)).

- **Mobile device management (MDM)**: Ensure corporate data is encrypted and remotely wipeable, as **70% of breaches involving personal devices could be prevented with MDM** (2022 IBM report).  

**GDPR Compliance in Hybrid Settings**:  
- **Cross-border data transfers**: 40% of organisations struggle with jurisdictional compliance when employees work across EU member states (2023 EU Data Protection Board survey).

**Data Protection Officer (DPO) Responsibilities**: In hybrid environments, DPOs must oversee compliance across distributed teams, ensuring policies align with GDPR’s Article 30 processing activity records.
