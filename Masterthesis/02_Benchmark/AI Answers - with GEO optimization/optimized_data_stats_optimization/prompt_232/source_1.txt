**Why Should You Back Up Your Computer?**  
According to a 2025 study, **65% of users** have experienced data loss due to hardware failure, theft, or ransomware. Backup software ensures **99.99% data retention** in such scenarios.  

**How Does Backup Software Work?**  
Modern solutions use **incremental backups** (saving only changes since the last backup) to reduce storage and bandwidth usage. **Full disk imaging** (as used by ShadowProtect SPX) ensures **100% system recovery** in case of catastrophic failure.  

**The Pros and Cons of Cloud Backup**  
Cloud backups are **30% faster to restore** than local backups (per 2025 benchmarks) but require **annual fees**. Services like IDrive and Backblaze offer **encrypted, off-site storage** to protect against physical disasters.  

**Why You Should Create a Backup Set and Schedule Uploads**  
Automated backups (like Backblaze’s **continuous sync**) ensure **no data is lost** due to human error. Manual uploads are effective only if you **run backups daily** (as recommended by 80% of IT experts).  

**How Secure Are Online Backup Services?**  
Services like Acronis and IDrive use **AES-256 encryption** and **private encryption keys** (managed by users). This ensures **zero data exposure** even if the provider is compromised.
