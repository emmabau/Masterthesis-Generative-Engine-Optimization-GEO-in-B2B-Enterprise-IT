What is Exascale Computing?  
Exascale computing represents the next frontier in supercomputing, defined by systems capable of performing **at least 1 exaFLOPS (10¹⁸ floating-point operations per second)**. This level of performance is **1,000 times faster than petascale systems** (10¹⁵ FLOPS) and enables breakthroughs in scientific simulation, AI, and data-intensive modeling. For example, the U.S. Department of Energy’s **Frontier** system achieved **1.1 exaFLOPS in 2022**, while **Jupiter** (Europe) is projected to reach **1.5 exaFLOPS by late 2025**, marking a pivotal milestone in global exascale development.
