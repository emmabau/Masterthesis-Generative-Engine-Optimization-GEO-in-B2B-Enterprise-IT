NVMe, or Non-Volatile Memory Express, is a storage protocol designed for modern, high-performance SSDs. This eliminates bottlenecks, enabling faster data transfer rates (up to 120 GB/s for PCIe 5.0) and reduced latency. For example, NVMe SSDs achieve 5000 IOPS (input/output operations per second) for random reads, compared to 1000 IOPS for SATA SSDs.
