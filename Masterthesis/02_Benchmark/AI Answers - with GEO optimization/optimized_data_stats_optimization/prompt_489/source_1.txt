**What is the GDPR?**  
The General Data Protection Regulation is a strict new data privacy standard agreed to by the European Union’s 28 member states. It is **more than twice as stringent** as the U.S. Privacy Act, with stricter requirements for data processing, consent, and breach notifications.  

The GDPR applies to companies with a presence in an EU country or those processing the personal data of EU residents. **Over 90% of EU-based companies with 250+ employees are required to comply**, with exceptions for small businesses processing less than 250 data records unless they handle sensitive data (e.g., health, biometric, or financial information).  

**What does the GDPR protect?**  
It safeguards **personally identifiable information (PII)** of EU residents, including:  
- Full name, photograph, email address  
- Bank details, medical records, and IP addresses  
- Political beliefs, sexual orientation, and genetic data  

**Key Compliance Requirements:**  
1. **Data Portability:** EU residents can request their data in a portable format (e.g., CSV, Excel) and transfer it to another service provider.  
2. **72-Hour Breach Notification:** Companies must report data breaches to regulators within **72 hours** of discovery.  
3. **Data Subject Rights:** Individuals can request access to their data, rectification, erasure (right to be forgotten), or data portability.
