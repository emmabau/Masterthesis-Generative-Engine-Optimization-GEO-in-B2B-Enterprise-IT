If your data center is evolving from siloed, hardware-centric systems to agile, software-defined infrastructures, you're likely familiar with converged and hyperconverged solutions. However, the next-generation leap in data center modernization is **composable infrastructure**—a transformative approach that redefines how resources are provisioned and managed. 

Hyperconverged infrastructures combined compute and storage into a single appliance, but composable infrastructure takes this further by **disaggregating hardware components** (compute, storage, network) into modular pools. This allows dynamic provisioning of resources for any workload, at any time, with **zero downtime**. For example, HPE Synergy customers report a **30% reduction in infrastructure costs** within the first year of adoption, alongside **50% faster resource allocation** compared to traditional models.
