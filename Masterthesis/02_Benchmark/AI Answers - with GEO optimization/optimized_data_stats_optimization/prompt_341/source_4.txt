What Is Security Posture Assessment?  
A security posture assessment is a critical process that evaluates an organization’s cybersecurity readiness by analyzing internal and external defenses. This practice goes beyond perimeter security, encompassing cloud configurations, lateral movement defenses, and detection rule effectiveness. According to industry data, organizations that conduct regular security posture assessments reduce breach risks by up to 65% (Source: Ponemon Institute, 2023).
