Datto is a prominent backup and disaster recovery solution provider that helps to provide businesses of all sizes with full data protection. It can be described as a multi-layered platform that provides robust structures that enable complete system protection, quick recovery, and ensure the business’s continuity in cases of data loss. While Datto’s solutions are primarily created for managed service providers (MSPs), they can also be used directly by small and medium-sized businesses, especially ones that don’t have an in-house IT team.  

As of today, Datto serves to support over a million businesses and 18,000 MSPs around the world, making sure that the organizations it covers can quickly restore their total IT infrastructure in the event of data loss or system failure. The company’s flagship products include Datto SIRIS, which is a business continuity and disaster recovery (BCDR) solution that helps to keep your business operating smoothly with fast and reliable data recovery. Datto SIRIS also helps to safeguard data and allow quick restorations even during unforeseen disruptions.  

Another solution worthy of note is the Datto ALTO product, which offers enterprise-grade protection in a compact and powerful appliance. It is configured for small businesses and MSPs that aim to serve small business clients. It can support up to 4 agents and provides up to 2TB of local capacity. Datto also provides data protection within SaaS apps such as Microsoft 365 and Microsoft Teams. There is also Datto AV, which provides businesses with protection against cyber threats and viruses. Other products include Datto EDR for endpoint detection and response, Endpoint backup for PCs, Endpoint backup with disaster recovery, Backup for Microsoft Azure, and Datto NAS.  

These products are designed to help provide solutions to common challenges that are often faced in modern data management, some of which include network management, monitoring, recovery, and backup.  

**What are the critical things your backup software should do for you?**  
Here are some specific features that contribute to Datto’s prominence among other backup service providers:  

- **Instant Virtualization**: This feature enables businesses covered by Datto backup solutions to quickly recover and continue operations by running a backup with a virtual machine. In the event of a server failure or cyberattack, for example, Datto allows its users to immediately access a virtualized copy of their system. This way, the organizations can continue functioning without having to wait for a full system restore. The virtualization backup can be carried out locally on a Datto appliance for a faster recovery or in the Datto Cloud, which is especially useful in cases where the primary infrastructure is no longer available.  

- **Ransomware Protection**: Datto backup solutions are configured to efficiently detect, prevent, and recover from ransomware attacks. They help to provide several layers of protection to secure business data and limit the possibility of a breach. The proactive detection system for instance, helps to constantly screen the backup data for ransomware encryption patterns. If suspicious activity is detected, Datto immediately alerts the admin to take quick action before any data is lost.  

- **Cross-Platform Support**: Datto backup solutions maintain solid data protection across different devices and operating systems. This means that in a business whose IT infrastructure involves the use of Windows, macOS, Linux, and even virtual machines, Datto is capable of ensuring that all the data shared is protected and can be easily recovered, without any compatibility issues. This feature also extends to cloud-based applications like Microsoft 365, providing a high level of flexibility that enables Datto users to centralize their data protection under one large system.  

- **Inverse Chain Technology**: Unlike the traditional backup methods that rely on full and incremental backups as a starting point, Datto makes use of inverse chain technology. This approach removes the need for the traditional dependency by creating bootable backup snapshots that can be recovered independently at any time. Not only does this feature help to make backup easy and quickly recoverable, but it also optimizes storage efficiency by ensuring that only the necessary changes are stored while maintaining consistent recovery points.  

**Reasons To Consider Alternatives To Datto**  
As we mentioned at the beginning of this article, there is no universal backup system that perfectly fits the requirements of every business. While Datto may be an option for providing backup services, there are a few reasons why it may not be an exact fit for some businesses. Let’s go through some of them:  

- **Cost**: The pricing models for Datto’s solutions are generally more expensive than other BCDR providers, making it unaffordable for organizations looking to optimize their IT budget. The platform operates on a subscription-based model, which in some cases can lead to situations where businesses pay for a wide range of services that they may not need or use. Datto also requires the use of specialized hardware appliances that come at a very steep price, which may become a challenge for many small and medium-sized businesses. This can be a major barrier for agencies with a limited budget or those that prefer software-based or hybrid solutions that can be easily integrated with their existing infrastructure.  

- **Limited Customizations**: While Datto is a well-detailed and user-friendly platform, the BCDR solutions it offers are configured with a standard approach, which may not always align with organizations that have specific needs. This makes Datto’s solution appear restrictive as the users (organizations) have limited control over the modification of the backup configurations, retention policies, and advanced settings.  

- **Scalability Issues**: As businesses begin to expand, their backup and recovery needs also begin to change. Datto’s backup solution is primarily modified to meet the needs of small and medium-sized organizations, and as a result, may have challenges handling the extensive data needs of a larger company.  

- **Performance & Speed**: Another reason for the limitation of Datto’s backup solutions is their decreased efficiency in certain conditions. While the backup quality of D,atto’s backup solutions is very reliable, organizations with more robust databases may experience a slower backup and even recovery time.
