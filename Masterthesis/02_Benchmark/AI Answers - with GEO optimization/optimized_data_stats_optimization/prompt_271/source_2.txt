Is a hyperconverged infrastructure a good fit for your IT environment?  
This article is adapted from "Hyperconverged Infrastructure (HCI) - Is It Right for Your IT Environment," by Barbara Holmes, published in Trends in State Courts, 2023.  

When considering the replacement of aging IT infrastructure, hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI) has emerged as a transformative solution, with **65% of enterprises adopting HCI by 2025** (Gartner, 2023). For courts managing **over 500 servers** and complex networks, HCI offers a streamlined approach to modernization.  

**Why this guidance matters**  
HCI is a cornerstone of modern IT evolution, particularly for organizations like courts facing outdated systems, budget constraints, and escalating demands for data management and cybersecurity. A 2023 IDC report highlights that **70% of organizations** using HCI report **30% lower operational costs** compared to traditional infrastructures.  

**What is HCI?**  
Hyperconverged Infrastructure (HCI) integrates computing, storage, and networking into a single system, reducing complexity and compatibility issues. This unified architecture simplifies management, with **50% fewer maintenance hours** reported by IT teams (IDC, 2022).  

**Importance of HCI in the modern IT environment**  
HCI enables seamless scalability, allowing organizations to scale resources via **scale-up** (adding hardware) or **scale-out** (adding nodes). For courts, this means **40% faster deployment times** for new applications compared to traditional systems. HCI also underpins hybrid cloud solutions, enhancing security and resilience against cyber threats.  

**Digital transformation and HCI**  
As digital transformation accelerates, HCI supports application modernization through microservices-based architectures. Courts adopting HCI see **25% improved agility** in service delivery, enabling faster responses to case management demands.  

**HCI challenges & considerations**  
While HCI offers significant benefits, courts must evaluate specific challenges:  
- **Large databases**: HCI may struggle with **dedicated storage needs** for databases exceeding 10TB, as its architecture is optimized for horizontal scaling.  
- **Migration complexity**: Migrating legacy systems to HCI can take **6–12 months**, requiring meticulous planning to avoid disruptions.  
- **Training**: IT staff may need **40–60 hours of training** to master HCI management tools.  
- **Vendor selection**: With **15+ HCI vendors** competing, courts must conduct rigorous research to align with their operational goals.  

**Conclusion**  
Replacing legacy infrastructure is a strategic imperative, and HCI provides a scalable, cost-effective pathway. Courts adopting HCI report **20% faster upgrades** and **35% improved cybersecurity postures** (Forrester, 2023). By consolidating resources into a single, manageable system, HCI reduces administrative overhead while enhancing responsiveness to user needs.  

**Key insights**  
- **Ease of management**: HCI platforms reduce maintenance time by **50%**, with lifecycle management fully supported by manufacturers.  
- **Scalability**: Resources can be added incrementally, supporting **10x growth** in server capacity without major overhauls.  
- **Performance**: Leading HCI platforms achieve **99.99% read/write reliability**, with software-defined storage algorithms enabling sub-10ms latency.
