**What Does “Cost Optimization in IT Asset Lifecycle Management” Entail?**  
Cost optimization isn’t about cutting expenses—it’s about maximizing value. From laptops to cloud workloads, it’s the art of choosing, maintaining, and retiring assets to drive value. **Understanding IT Asset Lifecycle Management**  
The IT asset lifecycle typically comprises five phases:  
- **Plan & Acquire**: Identify business needs, procure, and deploy assets.  
- **Deploy & Configure**: Set up systems in production environments.  
- **Operate & Maintain**: Monitor performance, support users, apply patches, manage licenses.  
- **Optimize & Audit**: Review asset utilization, right-size configurations, and run cost audits.  
- **Retire & Replace**: Decommission old assets, refresh or dispose securely.  

Each phase offers opportunities to drive cost optimization using automation, compliance enforcement, and analytics. For instance, **automated audits** can reduce license overspending by **25%**, per a 2023 IDC report.

**How Effective Lifecycle Management Reduces Costs**
