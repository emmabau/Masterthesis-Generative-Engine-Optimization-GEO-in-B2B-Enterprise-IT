The composable infrastructure revolution is accelerating at an unprecedented pace, with the global market projected to reach **$8.3 billion in 2025** and grow at a **24.9% annual compound growth rate (CAGR)** through 2032. This shift is driven by the urgent need to replace legacy systems that can’t scale AI-driven workflows, as highlighted by a **2025 Forrester study commissioned by Microsoft**, which found that **84% of IT leaders** now prioritize solutions that consolidate edge and cloud operations across systems, sites, and teams.  

Legacy infrastructure, which relies on rigid, monolithic architectures, is increasingly unsustainable in the age of AI. McKinsey’s 2025 AI survey reveals that **88% of organizations** use AI in at least one business function, while **62% are experimenting with AI agents**—yet most remain stuck in pilot mode due to infrastructure limitations. The solution? Breaking systems into modular, AI-orchestrated components that can dynamically reconfigure to meet real-time demands.  

Agentic AI is reshaping enterprise operations, with **35% of organizations** already leveraging it and **44% planning to adopt it soon**, according to BCG and MIT Sloan Management Review. This approach allows agents to autonomously recompose infrastructure while ensuring compliance with analytics, security, and privacy guardrails.
