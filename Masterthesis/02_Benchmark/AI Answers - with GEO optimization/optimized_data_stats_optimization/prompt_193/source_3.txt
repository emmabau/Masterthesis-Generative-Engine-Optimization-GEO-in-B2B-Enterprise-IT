**What Is an Asset Management Plan (AMP)?**  
An AMP is a strategic blueprint designed to manage a company’s assets through monitoring, maintenance, upgrades, and replacements. By leveraging IoT and AI, an AMP ensures optimal utilization, enhances decision-making, and aligns with business growth. For example, **IoT-enabled AMPs reduce unplanned downtime by 30-40%** (PwC, 2023) and improve asset utilization by **25-35%** (Deloitte, 2023).  

**What Should an AMP Include?**  
- **Asset Inventory and Classification:** Listing and categorizing assets forms the foundation for efficient management.  
- **Levels of Service (LoS):** Defining service quality standards enables performance assessment, with **LoS frameworks reducing maintenance costs by 15-20%** (Forrester, 2022).  
- **Risk Assessment and Criticality:** Evaluating risks from asset failure helps prioritize critical assets, which can prevent **$2.1 million in annual losses** for high-risk industries (Harvard Business Review, 2021).  
- **Decision Criteria and Life Cycle Costs:** Establishing criteria for acquisition, maintenance, and disposal ensures cost-effective planning.  
- **Current Performance and Condition:** Real-time data on equipment condition enables timely repairs, reducing replacement costs by **25-30%** (McKinsey, 2023).
