## Cooling Technology Definitions
- **Direct-to-Chip (D2C):** 100% contact between coolant and chip, achieving 95% thermal efficiency.
- **Immersion Cooling:** Full submersion of components, with 98% heat dissipation efficiency.
- **Single-Phase:** Maintains coolant as liquid, with 25% lower energy consumption than two-phase systems.
- **Two-Phase:** Utilizes evaporation, achieving 40% better heat transfer efficiency.
