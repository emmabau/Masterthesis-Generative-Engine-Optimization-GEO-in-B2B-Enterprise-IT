**What Is a Hybrid Workforce?**  
A hybrid workforce combines remote and on-site work, leveraging flexibility to balance autonomy with collaboration. This model allows employees to split time between home and office, using digital tools to maintain seamless communication and workflow. Organizations adopting this approach often see a 20% improvement in employee satisfaction and a 10% boost in operational efficiency.
