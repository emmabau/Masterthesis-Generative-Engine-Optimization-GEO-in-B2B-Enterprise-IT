**TCO and ROI: The Foundation of Data-Driven Decisions**  
TCO provides a comprehensive view of all expenses associated with acquiring, operating, and maintaining a system over its lifecycle. It serves as the cost side of the ROI equation. By understanding the full range of costs—including initial setup, maintenance, operational expenses, and potential hidden costs like technical debt—companies can more accurately estimate projected gains (e.g., increased revenue, cost savings, efficiency improvements) from new technology.  

**Calculating TCO and ROI**  
Use a TCO calculator and input the following:  
- **Initial Costs**: Software licensing, hardware, and implementation.  
- **Operational Costs**: Maintenance, updates, and support.  
- **Hidden Costs**: Technical debt, downtime, and integration challenges.  

Add all these costs together to calculate the TCO. With the TCO value, calculate ROI as follows:  
**ROI = (Net Profit / TCO) × 100**.
