3. IDrive  
IDrive is a versatile on-premises backup tool for small businesses. That means, unlike Backblaze and other SMB backup solutions, it helps you back up your files to a local device in your business location. On-premises backup might suit your company if you don't want to transfer data to a third-party cloud provider for security or data governance reasons.  

Like Backblaze, IDrive is compatible with operating systems such as Windows, Mac, and Linux. You can copy data from your system, store it in a local device, and access it after a critical event.  

Here are some of the features of IDrive:  
**Incremental Backups**  
IDrive makes it easy to perform incremental backups, which involve copying and storing the data that's changed since your last full or incremental backup. For example, you can replicate data from the most recent full backup you completed rather than carry out a full backup again. Incremental backups are quicker and require less bandwidth and computing resources than full backups, making them a good idea for many small businesses.  

**Disk Imaging**  
Disk imaging is the process of replicating a hard drive's contents and storing them in one compressed file on another hard drive. This method can be effective for backing up data on your premises and preserving it in case a critical event occurs. It's simple to start the disk imaging process with IDrive's desktop application. You just sign in, provide your private encryption key (if you have one), and click the 'Clone/Computer Backup' button.  

**AES Encryption**  
The Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) is an encryption method that uses the same private key to both encrypt and decrypt data. This standard prevents hacking attempts because it utilizes key sizes of higher lengths. IDrive uses 256-bit AES when transferring and storing your data in its data centers.
