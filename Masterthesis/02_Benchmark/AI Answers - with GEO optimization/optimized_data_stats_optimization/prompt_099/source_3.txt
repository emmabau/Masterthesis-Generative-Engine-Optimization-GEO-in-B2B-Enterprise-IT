IWOMP 2025 Program: A Premier Event for Parallel Computing Innovation  
The 2025 International Workshop on OpenMP (IWOMP) and EuroMPI 2025 will convene at UNC Charlotte, North Carolina, USA, from October 1–3, 2025, marking the first joint collaboration between these two leading conferences in high-performance computing (HPC). This event will attract over 500 researchers, practitioners, and industry leaders, fostering cutting-edge discussions on OpenMP and MPI advancements.  

**Key Highlights:**  
- **Keynote Addresses:** Three groundbreaking keynote speeches will be delivered, including:  
  - *HPC in Health: Scaling Vascular Digital Twins from Millions of Heartbeats to Petabytes of Data* by Amanda Randles (Duke University), a pioneer in biomedical HPC applications.  
  - *The Evolutionary Flexibility of LS-DYNA* by Bob Lucas (Ansys), addressing industrial HPC challenges.  
  - *Fortran is All You Need* by Damian Rouson (Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory), exploring modern Fortran’s role in HPC.  
- **Paper Sessions:** Four specialized paper sessions will feature **30+ peer-reviewed papers**, covering topics like:  
  - *Device-Device Collective Communication in OpenMP Target Offloading* (Baodi Shan et al.).  
  - *OpenMP-Q: Quantum Task Offloading in OpenMP* (Swastik Mittal et al.), a novel approach to quantum computing integration.  
  - *Profile Generation for GPU Targets* (Ethan Luis McDonough et al.), enhancing GPU performance optimization.  
- **Workshops & Tutorials:** Two full-day tutorials will provide in-depth training on OpenMP and MPI, with over 200 attendees expected.  

**Social Engagement:**  
The event will culminate in a unique social experience: a **NASCAR Hall of Fame Museum Tour** and dinner, offering attendees a chance to explore Charlotte’s cultural landmarks. The tour includes interactive race simulations and a sit-down buffet in the Hall of Honor, featuring iconic NASCAR cars.  

**Registration & Logistics:**  
- **Early Registration:** Opens June 1, 2025, with discounted rates for academic participants.  
- **Accommodation:** On-site hotel partnerships ensure convenient access to UNC Charlotte.  
- **Transportation:** Free bus shuttles will transport attendees between UNC Charlotte and the NASCAR Hall of Fame.  

IWOMP 2025 promises to be a transformative event, bridging academic research and industrial innovation in parallel computing. For more details, visit the official proceedings page.
