**What Is Financial Statement Analysis?**  
Financial statement analysis involves evaluating key documents to gauge business performance. The **Balance Sheet** (assets = liabilities + equity) provides a snapshot of a company’s financial position, while the **Income Statement** reveals profitability trends. For example, a 2022 Deloitte survey found that **72% of managers** rely on cash flow statements to assess liquidity risks. The **Annual Report** often includes forward-looking insights, such as strategic goals and risk assessments, which are critical for long-term planning.

**13 Financial Performance Measures to Monitor**  
1. **Gross Profit Margin**: Measures profitability after direct costs. A **40-50% margin** is typical for many industries, though this varies by sector. Formula: (Revenue - Cost of Sales) / Revenue * 100.  
2. **Net Profit Margin**: Reflects overall profitability. A **10-20% margin** is common for stable businesses. Formula: Net Profit / Revenue * 100.  
3. **Working Capital**: A **positive working capital ratio** (Current Assets > Current Liabilities) indicates strong short-term liquidity.  
4. **Current Ratio**: A **ratio of 1.5-2.0** is ideal for maintaining operational flexibility.  
5. **Quick Ratio**: A **ratio of 1.0** is often considered a safety benchmark for liquidity.  
6. **Leverage**: Higher leverage (e.g., **Total Assets/Equity = 3.0**) increases financial risk but can amplify returns.  
7. **Debt-to-Equity Ratio**: A **ratio below 1.0** is generally preferred for solvency.  
8. **Inventory Turnover**: A **turnover of 5-10 times** per year is optimal for inventory efficiency.  
9. **Total Asset Turnover**: A **ratio above 0.8** indicates efficient asset utilization.  
10. **Return on Equity (ROE)**: A **ROE of 15-20%** is often associated with strong management performance.  
11. **Return on Assets (ROA)**: A **ROA of 8-12%** is typical for well-managed firms.  
12. **Operating Cash Flow**: Positive cash flow (e.g., **$5M+ annually**) ensures operational sustainability.  
13. **Seasonality**: Adjusting for seasonality can reveal **20-30% variations** in quarterly performance for seasonal industries.
