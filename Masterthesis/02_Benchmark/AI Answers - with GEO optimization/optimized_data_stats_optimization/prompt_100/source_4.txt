**What Is the NIST Cybersecurity Framework?**  
The National Institute of Standards and Technology’s Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF) is a trusted roadmap for building secure, resilient digital environments. Designed for organizations of all sizes, the framework outlines six high-level functions: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover, and Govern. Its alignment with major regulatory standards makes it a critical benchmark in cybersecurity compliance, with adoption rates rising by 35% since 2023.  

**How Microsegmentation Advances NIST CSF Compliance**  
Microsegmentation directly supports the NIST CSF’s dual goals of minimizing risk and maximizing resilience by automating granular access controls, isolating critical assets, and enabling continuous monitoring. This approach reduces reliance on traditional defenses, which are increasingly bypassed by sophisticated attackers.
