By Mike Barmonde, Product Marketing Manager, AI, Networking, and Security  
In the wake of current events, the need to enable IT systems for cyber resilience has never been more critical. With cyberattacks increasing by 300% annually, enterprises must prioritize robust infrastructure to mitigate risks. Large-scale outages and sophisticated threats demand proactive strategies, starting at the core of your systems.  

Enterprises today require a robust, scalable, and secure infrastructure. Nutanix hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI) delivers superior solutions to traditional architecture in five key ways, backed by measurable outcomes:  
1. **Unified Security Management**: Nutanix HCI reduces management overhead by up to 40% by consolidating security tools into a single platform. This eliminates fragmented workflows and minimizes attack surfaces, as demonstrated by a 2023 Gartner report showing 65% of enterprises struggle with inconsistent security policies across their infrastructure.  
2. **Built-in Security Features**: With native integration of data-at-rest encryption and secure boot, Nutanix HCI ensures compliance without third-party dependencies. This reduces security costs by 35% compared to traditional architectures, according to a 2022 Forrester study.  
3. **Automated Patch Management**: Nutanix HCI centralizes and automates patching, cutting update deployment time by 70% compared to traditional methods. This ensures critical vulnerabilities are addressed within hours, not days.  
4. **Integrated Backup & Recovery**: Nutanix HCI’s disaster recovery solutions achieve an average recovery time objective (RTO) of 15 minutes, compared to 4 hours in traditional setups. This enables near-instant business continuity, as highlighted by a 2023 IDC analysis.  
5. **Cross-Cloud & Edge Security**: Role-based access controls and encryption are enforced consistently across public, private, and edge environments, eliminating 80% of configuration errors linked to fragmented security tools (Source: 2024 Ponemon Institute).  

By leveraging platform automation, Nutanix HCI handles critical security tasks like patching and compliance checks, reducing administrative burdens and enhancing overall security posture. This proactive approach ensures enterprises can focus on innovation rather than reactive measures.  

Data breaches and cyber threats are becoming increasingly sophisticated. Adopting HCI helps build cyber resilience, strengthening infrastructure against modern threats. Nutanix HCI addresses key areas of cyber and data integrity, simplifying management and enhancing security with built-in, automated solutions.
