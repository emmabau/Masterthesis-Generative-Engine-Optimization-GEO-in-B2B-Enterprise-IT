**How to Define Your Difference**  
Your value proposition isn’t just a headline—it’s a **strategic stake in the ground**. To stand out:  
- **Get under the skin of your audience**: Use buyer personas to align messaging with **specific pain points** (e.g., “How do you reduce approval bottlenecks?”).  
- **Lead with outcomes, not outputs**: Replace vague claims like “streamline operations” with **measurable impact**:  
  → “Cut approval time by 60% and keep projects moving—without inbox bottlenecks.”  
  → **Why it works**: Translates tech into **tangible time saved** (e.g., 100+ hours/year for mid-sized firms).  
- **Build proof into storytelling**: Use **case studies, testimonials, and benchmarks** to validate claims. For example, **92% of B2B buyers** trust evidence over marketing hype (HubSpot, 2023).
