How to Manage Hybrid and Remote Teams: A Gallup Strategic Guide  
Hybrid and remote work are now structural features of the modern workplace. Gallup research shows that success in a distributed workplace depends less on location and more on management quality, clear expectations, and intentional team design. This guide outlines what leaders need to know to build engaged, high-performing hybrid and remote teams.  

**Executive Summary**  
**Executive Summary: What Leaders Need to Know About Hybrid Work and Remote Teams**  
- Hybrid work is the dominant model for remote-capable employees. **52% of remote-capable employees work hybrid**, 26% work exclusively remote, and 21% work fully on-site. Work location patterns have remained stable since 2022.  
- **Management quality determines hybrid success**: Managers account for **70% of the variance in team-level engagement**, making leadership capability more influential than work location.  
- Hybrid work improves engagement and work-life balance when structured intentionally. **76% of hybrid workers cite improved work-life balance**, and many report reduced burnout and higher perceived productivity.  
- **Trust gaps remain a structural risk**: Only **54% of managers strongly agree** they trust remote employees to be productive, and **57% of employees** feel trusted by their managers.  
- **Team-designed hybrid norms outperform top-down mandates**: When teams determine their hybrid schedule together, **91% say the policy is fair**, compared to **73% when leadership dictates it**.  
- **Structured coordination reduces burnout**: Teams with a formal hybrid collaboration plan are **66% more likely to be engaged** and **29% less likely to be burned out**.  

**What is Hybrid Work?**  
Hybrid work is a flexible arrangement where employees split their time between working remotely and on-site. It differs from fully remote work. According to Gallup’s Hybrid Work Indicator, hybrid work is the dominant model for remote-capable U.S. employees.  
- **52% of remote-capable employees work hybrid**  
- **26% work exclusively remote**  
- **21% work fully on-site**  
- **6 in 10 remote-capable employees want hybrid arrangements**  
- **Less than 10% prefer fully on-site work**  
Remote-capable roles now account for roughly half of the U.S. workforce, making hybrid work a structural feature of the modern workplace.  

**How Much Time Do Hybrid Employees Spend in the Office?**  
On average, hybrid employees spend **46% of their workweek on-site** (about 2.3 days per week). Since 2022, work location patterns have remained stable. Hybrid work has proven durable across industries, and leaders must focus on managing hybrid teams, not whether hybrid work persists.  

| Work Arrangement | Location | Engagement Pattern | Key Risk |  
|------------------|----------|--------------------|----------|  
| Fully On-Site    | 100% on-site | Lower engagement among remote-capable employees | Burnout, intent to leave if flexibility preferred |  
| Hybrid           | Split between remote and on-site | Often highest engagement when structured well | Coordination complexity |  
| Fully Remote     | 100% off-site | High engagement when trust and clarity are strong | Cultural disconnection, isolation |  

**Top Challenges of Managing Remote Teams**  
The top challenges include sustaining collaboration, maintaining cultural connection, building trust, and coordinating work across locations. Hybrid work increases coordination and leadership demands.  
- **Collaboration and coordination complexity**: 48% of hybrid workers say their team has not discussed a formal plan for collaboration. Teams with a plan are **2.2x more likely** to report positive collaboration impacts.  
- **Cultural disconnection**: Hybrid workers report feeling less connected to organizational culture.  
- **Trust and accountability gaps**: Trust is foundational to psychological safety.  
- **Burnout and boundary strain**: Autonomy without coordination can create strain.  
- **Performance visibility**: Leaders risk defaulting to presence-based judgments.  

**Advantages of Employing Remote Employees**  
- **Broader talent access**: Remote flexibility removes geographic constraints.  
- **Improved work-life balance**: **76% of hybrid workers cite improved work-life balance**.  
- **Higher engagement**: Hybrid employees often report higher engagement than fully on-site workers.  
- **Productivity stability**: Remote employees work slightly fewer hours but maintain output.  

**Strategic Implications**  
Organizations should:  
- Hire for strengths and role fit  
- Maintain clear performance expectations  
- Redesign performance management for outcomes, not presence  
- Invest in manager capability  

**Building Trust in Hybrid Teams**  
Managers build trust through clear expectations, consistent communication, accountability, and equal feedback. Gallup research shows trust is foundational to psychological safety.  

**Managing Remote Teams**  
Effective remote team management requires:  
- Clarifying expectations  
- Holding frequent coaching conversations  
- Reinforcing purpose, development, care, and strengths  
- Prioritizing communication  

**Creating a Positive Remote Work Culture**  
Organizations must consistently reinforce purpose, values, and expectations. Leaders must ensure actions align with stated values and demonstrate authenticity.  

**Preventing Employee Burnout**  
Burnout prevention requires:  
- Clear expectations  
- Coordinating team norms  
- Maintaining consistent manager conversations  
- Protecting boundaries while preserving accountability  

**Remote Work and Employee Engagement**  
Gallup data show:  
- Hybrid: **34% engaged, 53% thriving**  
- Exclusively remote: **35% engaged, 51% thriving**  
- On-site (remote-capable): **30% engaged, 49% thriving**  
- On-site (non-remote-capable): **28% engaged, 44% thriving**  
- Overall: **31% engaged, 47% thriving**  

**How Gallup Can Help Leaders**  
Gallup helps organizations:  
- Measure engagement and wellbeing  
- Develop managers to set clear expectations and build trust  
- Design hybrid work models balancing flexibility, collaboration, and accountability  
- Strengthen workplace culture to keep remote employees connected to purpose and performance
