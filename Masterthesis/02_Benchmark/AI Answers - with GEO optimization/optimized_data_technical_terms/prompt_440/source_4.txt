The fundamental relationship between these metrics is governed by Little's Law: 
**Concurrency (QD) = IOPS × Latency**  
This equation reveals that system throughput is constrained by the product of IOPS and latency, with optimal performance achieved when QD is minimized while maintaining required throughput.

- **4+2 scheme**: Requires 2 reads + 3 writes per 4K partial-stripe write
- **Network amplification**: Bandwidth multiplies due to cross-node operations
