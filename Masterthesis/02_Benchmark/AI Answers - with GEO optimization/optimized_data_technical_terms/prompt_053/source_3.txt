These challenges are compounded by the proliferation of IoT devices, which introduce vulnerabilities such as insecure firmware updates and weak default credentials, creating attack surfaces that are often exploited via botnets or DDoS attacks.

Data security, as a critical component of information assurance, encompasses the protection of digital assets through cryptographic protocols (e.g., AES-256, TLS 1.3), access control mechanisms (RBAC, ABAC), and integrity verification via hashing algorithms (SHA-256). The core principles of confidentiality, integrity, and availability (CIA triad) are now under threat from quantum computing advancements, which could render current encryption standards obsolete through Shor's algorithm. This necessitates the adoption of post-quantum cryptography (PQC) standards, such as those proposed by NIST's PQC project, to future-proof cryptographic systems.

The evolution of data security challenges is marked by the rise of insider threats, which account for 60% of data breaches according to the 2023 IBM Cost of a Data Breach Report. Regulatory compliance frameworks such as GDPR (Article 30, data protection impact assessments), HIPAA (45 CFR 164.400), and CCPA (Cal. Civ.
