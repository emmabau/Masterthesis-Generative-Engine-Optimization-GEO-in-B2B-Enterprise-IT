The hybrid workplace paradigm, despite its popularity, exhibits systemic inefficiencies that undermine organizational performance. 1. **Knowledge Transfer Inefficiencies**

   Hybrid models correlate with 31% higher attrition rates. Social identity theory suggests that reduced physical presence diminishes organizational belonging by 27%.
