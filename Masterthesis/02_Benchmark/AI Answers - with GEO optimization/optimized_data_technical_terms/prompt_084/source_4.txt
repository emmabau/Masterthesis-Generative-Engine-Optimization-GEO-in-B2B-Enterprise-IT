Predictive analytics further enhances risk mitigation through Monte Carlo simulations and stochastic modeling, enabling institutions to quantify potential losses under various market scenarios.

Additionally, federated learning frameworks enable secure, privacy-preserving model training across decentralized data sources, addressing data privacy concerns while maintaining model accuracy.  

Predictive analytics in finance integrates time-series analysis, regression models, and ensemble methods to forecast market movements.
