Modern HPC systems leverage heterogeneous computing frameworks, integrating NVIDIA A100 GPUs, AMD EPYC CPUs, and Intel Xeon Scalable processors to achieve peak performance of up to 10^18 FLOPS (floating-point operations per second). 1. **Compute Resources**: Modern HPC clusters utilize heterogeneous architectures combining CPUs, GPUs, and FPGAs. For instance, the Summit supercomputer at Oak Ridge National Laboratory employs 4,320 IBM POWER9 CPUs and 27,648 NVIDIA V100 GPUs, achieving 200 petaflops of performance.
