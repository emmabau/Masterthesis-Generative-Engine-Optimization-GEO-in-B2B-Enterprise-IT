5 Hyperconverged Infrastructure Best Practices  
December 28, 2021  

According to IDC, hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI) accounted for 23.4% of the converged systems market growth in 2020, driven by its transition from virtual desktop infrastructure (VDI) to hybrid cloud and containerized workloads. TheNextPlatform highlights that HCI’s architectural evolution has enabled it to serve as a foundational platform for modern data center transformation, leveraging software-defined storage (SDS), software-defined networking (SDN), and virtualized compute resources.  

**Key HCI Best Practices for Enterprise Adoption**  
1) **Implement Deduplicated Backup Orchestration Frameworks**  
HCI systems require robust backup solutions compatible with storage virtualization layers. Cohesity’s CloudArray and Rubrik’s HyperConverged Backup Platform utilize HCI’s integrated storage fabric for snapshot-based recovery. Backup solutions must support deduplication, compression, and cross-platform replication to align with HCI’s resource abstraction model.  

2) **Enforce Zero-Trust Security Posture**  
HCI’s converged architecture necessitates microsegmentation and encryption at rest/in-transit. Security protocols must protect against ransomware attacks, insider threats, and lateral movement via vulnerabilities in VMware vSAN, Nutanix AHV, or KVM hypervisors. Regular patching of hypervisor kernels and integration with SIEM tools like Splunk are critical.  

3) **Enable Asymmetric Resource Scaling**  
Modern HCI platforms like Nutanix Xi Cloud and Dell EMC VxRail support decoupled scaling of compute, storage, and networking. This allows organizations to add storage nodes without overprovisioning compute resources, optimizing ROI for latency-sensitive workloads.  

4) **Leverage Virtualization Expertise with HCI Training**  
IT teams proficient in VMware vSphere or Hyper-V can transition to HCI by mastering storage virtualization, live migration, and resource pooling. Training should focus on managing VDI workloads, containerized applications, and hybrid cloud integration via Kubernetes.  

5) **Optimize for Edge Computing Latency**  
HCI’s unified management stack reduces deployment complexity for edge deployments, enabling sub-10ms latency for IoT applications. Solutions like Nutanix Edge and HPE Edgeline integrate with 5G networks to support real-time analytics and edge AI workloads.  

**Strategic HCI Adoption for Cloud-Native Environments**  
HCI’s ability to abstract physical infrastructure into logical pools makes it ideal for container orchestration and multi-cloud interoperability. Partnering with vendors like Nutanix, which provides end-to-end HCI solutions with AI-driven automation, ensures alignment with cloud-native requirements.
