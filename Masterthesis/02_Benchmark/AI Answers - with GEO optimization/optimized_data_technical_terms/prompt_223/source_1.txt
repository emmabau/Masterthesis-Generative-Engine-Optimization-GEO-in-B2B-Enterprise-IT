Sensor Types and Technical Specifications:  
1. **Vibration Sensors (Accelerometers)**  
   - **Piezoelectric (PZT) crystals** (e.g., PZT-5A) provide **wide dynamic range (0.01-100 g)** and **frequency response >20 kHz**.  
   - **Triaxial accelerometers** (X/Y/Z axes) are mounted on bearing housings, with **sampling rates 1-10 kHz** for rotating equipment (e.g., motors, compressors).  
   - **Applications**: Bearing wear detection, misalignment, imbalance, and resonance analysis per ISO 10816-3.  

2. **Temperature Sensors (Thermocouples/RTDs)**  
   - **Thermocouples** (e.g., K-type) offer **-200°C to +1,800°C range** with **±1-2°C accuracy**, while **RTDs** (e.g., Pt100) provide **-200°C to +850°C** with **±0.1-0.5°C precision**.  
   - **Applications**: Bearing overheating, motor overload, and thermal expansion monitoring per IEEE 1451.2.  

3. **Current Sensors (CTs)**  
   - **Non-invasive clamp-on CTs** measure **RMS current (0.1-1000A)**, **power factor**, and **harmonic distortion** (THD <5%).  
   - **Applications**: Motor winding degradation, phase imbalance, and cavitation detection in pumps.  

4. **Pressure Sensors (Transducers)**  
   - **Piezoresistive transducers** (0-30 PSI to 0-5,000 PSI) with **±0.25-1% accuracy** and **4-20mA analog output** for fluid systems.  
   - **Applications**: Filter clogging, pump wear, and leak detection in hydraulics and pneumatics.  

5. **Acoustic Sensors (Ultrasonic Detectors)**  
   - **Piezoelectric ultrasonic sensors** (20-100 kHz) detect **leaks, arcing, and bearing degradation** via **sound pressure level (SPL) analysis**.  
   - **Applications**: Compressed air leaks, steam trap failures, and vacuum system integrity.  

6. **Environmental Sensors**  
   - **Multi-parameter sensors** (humidity ±2-3%, temperature ±0.3-0.5°C, pressure ±1-2 mbar) monitor **dew point** and **corrosion risk** in HVAC and data centers.
