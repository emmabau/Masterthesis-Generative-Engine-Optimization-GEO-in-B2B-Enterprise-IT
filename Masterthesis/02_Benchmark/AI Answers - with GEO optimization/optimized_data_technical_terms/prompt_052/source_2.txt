Modern AI storage infrastructures integrate with open-source ML frameworks (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch) and proprietary tools via RESTful APIs, enabling seamless data pipeline orchestration. Key technical challenges include managing data locality in distributed systems, optimizing storage tiering (hot/warm/cold) for cost-performance trade-offs, and ensuring fault tolerance for AI training pipelines.
